## 1. History

**Q1.** [🔴 High Repeat] Who is known as the "Father of the Indian Constitution"?
(a) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar  (b) Jawaharlal Nehru  (c) Mahatma Gandhi  (d) Sardar Patel
**Answer: (a) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar**
*Explanation:* Dr. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly.
*Topic:* Modern History · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q2.** [🔴 High Repeat] The Battle of Plassey (1757) was fought between the British and the Nawab of Bengal, ____.
(a) Siraj-ud-Daulah  (b) Mir Jafar  (c) Mir Qasim  (d) Alivardi Khan
**Answer: (a) Siraj-ud-Daulah**
*Explanation:* Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah, aided by Mir Jafar's betrayal.
*Topic:* Modern History · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q3.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] The Dandi March (Salt Satyagraha) was led by Mahatma Gandhi in which year?
(a) 1930  (b) 1920  (c) 1942  (d) 1928
**Answer: (a) 1930**
*Explanation:* The 24-day Dandi March began on 12 March 1930 against the salt tax.
*Topic:* Freedom Struggle · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q4.** [🟢 Low Repeat] The ancient university of Nalanda was located in which present-day state?
(a) Bihar  (b) Uttar Pradesh  (c) West Bengal  (d) Odisha
**Answer: (a) Bihar**
*Explanation:* Nalanda Mahavihara, a great centre of learning, was in present-day Bihar.
*Topic:* Ancient History · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 2. Polity

**Q5.** [🔴 High Repeat] How many fundamental rights are currently guaranteed by the Indian Constitution?
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 5  (d) 8
**Answer: (a) 6**
*Explanation:* The Right to Property was removed in 1978; six fundamental rights remain.
*Topic:* Polity (Fundamental Rights) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q6.** [🔴 High Repeat] The Preamble of the Indian Constitution was amended by the 42nd Amendment to add which words?
(a) Socialist, Secular, Integrity  (b) Sovereign, Democratic  (c) Republic, Justice  (d) Liberty, Equality
**Answer: (a) Socialist, Secular, Integrity**
*Explanation:* The 42nd Amendment (1976) added "Socialist," "Secular," and "Integrity" to the Preamble.
*Topic:* Polity (Preamble) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q7.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Which Article of the Constitution deals with the Right to Constitutional Remedies?
(a) Article 32  (b) Article 19  (c) Article 21  (d) Article 14
**Answer: (a) Article 32**
*Explanation:* Article 32 (called the "heart and soul" of the Constitution by Ambedkar) provides the right to move the Supreme Court.
*Topic:* Polity (Articles) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q8.** [🟢 Low Repeat] The idea of the Directive Principles of State Policy in the Indian Constitution was borrowed from which country?
(a) Ireland  (b) USA  (c) UK  (d) Canada
**Answer: (a) Ireland**
*Explanation:* The DPSP were inspired by the Irish Constitution.
*Topic:* Polity (Sources) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 3. Geography

**Q9.** [🔴 High Repeat] Which is the longest river in India?
(a) Ganga  (b) Godavari  (c) Brahmaputra  (d) Yamuna
**Answer: (a) Ganga**
*Explanation:* The Ganga is the longest river flowing within India (~2,525 km).
*Topic:* Geography (Rivers) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q10.** [🔴 High Repeat] The Tropic of Cancer does NOT pass through which of these Indian states?
(a) Tamil Nadu  (b) Gujarat  (c) Madhya Pradesh  (d) West Bengal
**Answer: (a) Tamil Nadu**
*Explanation:* The Tropic of Cancer passes through 8 states; Tamil Nadu is south of it.
*Topic:* Geography (India) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q11.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Which is the highest mountain peak in India?
(a) Kanchenjunga  (b) Nanda Devi  (c) K2  (d) Everest
**Answer: (a) Kanchenjunga**
*Explanation:* Kanchenjunga (8,586 m) is the highest peak located within India. (K2 lies in the disputed PoK region; Everest is in Nepal.)
*Topic:* Geography (Mountains) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q12.** [🟢 Low Repeat] The Deccan Plateau is primarily made of which type of rock?
(a) Basalt (igneous)  (b) Limestone  (c) Sandstone  (d) Granite gneiss
**Answer: (a) Basalt (igneous)**
*Explanation:* The Deccan Trap is formed of basaltic lava from ancient volcanic eruptions.
*Topic:* Geography (Physical) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 4. Economics

**Q13.** [🔴 High Repeat] Which body regulates monetary policy in India?
(a) Reserve Bank of India  (b) SEBI  (c) NITI Aayog  (d) Finance Ministry
**Answer: (a) Reserve Bank of India**
*Explanation:* The RBI is India's central bank and controls monetary policy.
*Topic:* Economics (Banking) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q14.** [🔴 High Repeat] The term "GDP" stands for:
(a) Gross Domestic Product  (b) Gross Domestic Profit  (c) General Domestic Product  (d) Gross Demand Product
**Answer: (a) Gross Domestic Product**
*Explanation:* GDP is the total value of goods and services produced within a country's borders.
*Topic:* Economics (National Income) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q15.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] In economics, "repo rate" is the rate at which:
(a) RBI lends to commercial banks  (b) Banks lend to the public  (c) RBI borrows from banks  (d) Banks lend to each other
**Answer: (a) RBI lends to commercial banks**
*Explanation:* The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against securities.
*Topic:* Economics (Monetary) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q16.** [🟢 Low Repeat] The "Green Revolution" in India is most associated with the increased production of:
(a) Wheat and rice  (b) Milk  (c) Fish  (d) Cotton
**Answer: (a) Wheat and rice**
*Explanation:* The Green Revolution (1960s) boosted food-grain output, mainly wheat and rice. (The White Revolution refers to milk.)
*Topic:* Economics (Agriculture) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 5. General Science

**Q17.** [🔴 High Repeat] The chemical symbol for gold is:
(a) Au  (b) Ag  (c) Gd  (d) Go
**Answer: (a) Au**
*Explanation:* "Au" comes from the Latin name *aurum*. (Ag = silver.)
*Topic:* Chemistry · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q18.** [🔴 High Repeat] Which gas do plants primarily absorb from the atmosphere during photosynthesis?
(a) Carbon dioxide  (b) Oxygen  (c) Nitrogen  (d) Hydrogen
**Answer: (a) Carbon dioxide**
*Explanation:* Plants take in CO₂ and release O₂ during photosynthesis.
*Topic:* Biology · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q19.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] The SI unit of electric current is the:
(a) Ampere  (b) Volt  (c) Ohm  (d) Watt
**Answer: (a) Ampere**
*Explanation:* Current is measured in amperes (A); volt is potential difference.
*Topic:* Physics (Units) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q20.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Which vitamin is produced in the human body on exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin D  (b) Vitamin C  (c) Vitamin A  (d) Vitamin K
**Answer: (a) Vitamin D**
*Explanation:* Skin synthesises Vitamin D from sunlight (UV-B); its deficiency causes rickets.
*Topic:* Biology (Nutrition) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q21.** [🟢 Low Repeat] The powerhouse of the cell is:
(a) Mitochondria  (b) Nucleus  (c) Ribosome  (d) Golgi body
**Answer: (a) Mitochondria**
*Explanation:* Mitochondria generate ATP (energy) for the cell.
*Topic:* Biology (Cell) · *Difficulty:* Easy
