## History & Art-Culture

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the Mauryan administration:
1. The *Arthashastra* attributed to Kautilya describes an elaborate system of espionage and revenue administration.
2. The institution of *Dhamma Mahamatras* was created during the reign of Bindusara to spread moral teachings.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (a) 1 only**
*Solution:* The *Arthashastra* indeed details espionage, taxation and statecraft (statement 1 correct). The *Dhamma Mahamatras* were appointed by Ashoka, not Bindusara, to propagate Dhamma — so statement 2 is wrong.

---

**Q2.** With reference to Buddhist architecture in ancient India, consider the following:
1. A *stupa* is a hemispherical mound containing relics, surmounted by a *harmika* and *chhatra*.
2. A *chaitya* is a prayer hall while a *vihara* is a monastic residence.
3. The railing surrounding a stupa is called the *vedika*.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* All three are accurate. The stupa's relic-mound is topped by a harmika (square railing) and chhatra (umbrella); chaityas were worship halls and viharas were living quarters for monks; the encircling stone railing is the vedika.

---

**Q3.** Consider the following pairs of Bhakti/Sufi saints and their associated regions:
1. Guru Nanak — Punjab
2. Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — Bengal
3. Mirabai — Maharashtra
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Guru Nanak (Punjab) and Chaitanya (Bengal) are correctly matched. Mirabai is associated with Rajasthan (Mewar), not Maharashtra, so pair 3 is incorrect.

---

**Q4.** With reference to the early phase of the Indian National Congress (1885–1905), consider the following statements:
1. The Moderates believed in the method of constitutional agitation and "prayers and petitions".
2. Dadabhai Naoroji propounded the "Drain of Wealth" theory.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2  (b) 2 only  (c) 1 only  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (a) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The Moderates relied on petitions, resolutions and constitutional methods (statement 1 correct). Dadabhai Naoroji articulated the economic drain of India's wealth to Britain in his work *Poverty and Un-British Rule in India* (statement 2 correct).

---

**Q5.** Which one of the following pairs relating to Gandhian movements is correctly matched?
(a) Champaran Satyagraha (1917) — against indigo planters in Bihar
(b) Kheda Satyagraha (1918) — against textile mill owners in Ahmedabad
(c) Dandi March (1930) — protest against the Rowlatt Act
(d) Quit India (1942) — demand for separate electorates
**Answer: (a) Champaran Satyagraha (1917) — against indigo planters in Bihar**
*Solution:* Champaran (1917) was against the *tinkathia* system imposed by indigo planters in Bihar — correct. Kheda was a peasant agitation over land revenue (the Ahmedabad mill strike was separate); the Dandi March protested the salt law/tax; Quit India demanded an end to British rule, not separate electorates.

---

**Q6.** Consider the following statements about the Government of India Act, 1935:
1. It provided for the establishment of an All-India Federation of provinces and princely states.
2. It introduced provincial autonomy and abolished dyarchy at the provincial level.
3. It granted full universal adult franchise across British India.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The Act proposed an All-India Federation (statement 1 correct) and introduced provincial autonomy by ending provincial dyarchy and instead introducing dyarchy at the centre (statement 2 correct). However, the franchise was extended but remained limited — only about 10% of the population could vote, not universal adult franchise — so statement 3 is wrong.

## Indian Polity & Governance

**Q7.** Consider the following statements regarding the Fundamental Rights under the Indian Constitution:
1. The Right to Constitutional Remedies under Article 32 was described by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as the "heart and soul" of the Constitution.
2. The Right to Property was removed from the list of Fundamental Rights by the 44th Constitutional Amendment.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) Neither 1 nor 2  (c) 2 only  (d) Both 1 and 2
**Answer: (d) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* Ambedkar called Article 32 the heart and soul of the Constitution (statement 1 correct). The 44th Amendment Act, 1978 deleted the Right to Property from Part III and made it a legal/constitutional right under Article 300A (statement 2 correct).

---

**Q8.** With reference to the Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP), consider the following:
1. They are enforceable by the courts.
2. The concept of DPSP was borrowed from the Constitution of Ireland.
3. Article 44 deals with a Uniform Civil Code.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* DPSPs are non-justiciable, i.e., not enforceable by courts — so statement 1 is wrong. They were inspired by the Irish Constitution (statement 2 correct) and Article 44 directs the State to secure a Uniform Civil Code (statement 3 correct).

---

**Q9.** Consider the following pairs of constitutional bodies and the Article establishing them:
1. Election Commission — Article 324
2. Comptroller and Auditor General — Article 148
3. Finance Commission — Article 280
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* All three are correctly matched: the Election Commission (Art. 324), the CAG (Art. 148) and the Finance Commission (Art. 280) are each established by the respective Articles cited.

**Q10.** With reference to the Parliament of India, consider the following statements:
1. A Money Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha.
2. The Rajya Sabha can amend or reject a Money Bill.
3. The Speaker of the Lok Sabha certifies whether a Bill is a Money Bill.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* A Money Bill originates only in the Lok Sabha (statement 1 correct) and the Speaker certifies a Money Bill (statement 3 correct). The Rajya Sabha can only make recommendations within 14 days, which the Lok Sabha may accept or reject — it cannot amend or reject the bill itself, so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q11.** Consider the following statements about the office of the President of India:
1. The President is elected by an electoral college consisting of the elected members of both Houses of Parliament and the elected members of the State Legislative Assemblies.
2. The President can be removed from office through a process of impeachment for "violation of the Constitution".
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2  (b) 2 only  (c) 1 only  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (a) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The electoral college comprises elected MPs and elected MLAs (including those of Delhi and Puducherry) — statement 1 is correct. Under Article 61, the President can be impeached for violation of the Constitution — statement 2 is correct.

**Q12.** Which one of the following is NOT a feature of the federal structure of the Indian Constitution?
(a) Division of powers between the Union and the States
(b) An independent judiciary
(c) A single, unified citizenship for the whole country
(d) A written and rigid (for certain provisions) Constitution
**Answer: (c) A single, unified citizenship for the whole country**
*Solution:* Single citizenship is a *unitary* (non-federal) feature, as India provides only one citizenship unlike a typical federation. Division of powers, an independent judiciary, and a written Constitution are genuine federal features.

## Geography

**Q13.** Consider the following statements about the Indian monsoon:
1. The southwest monsoon normally sets in over Kerala around the first week of June.
2. The retreating monsoon brings most of the rainfall to the Tamil Nadu (Coromandel) coast.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) Neither 1 nor 2  (c) 2 only  (d) Both 1 and 2
**Answer: (d) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The southwest monsoon arrives over Kerala around 1 June (statement 1 correct). During the retreating (northeast) monsoon in October–December, the Coromandel coast of Tamil Nadu receives most of its annual rainfall (statement 2 correct).

**Q14.** Consider the following pairs of rivers and their sources/origin:
1. Godavari — Triambak Plateau (Western Ghats), Maharashtra
2. Krishna — Mahabaleshwar, Maharashtra
3. Kaveri — Brahmagiri Hills, Karnataka
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* The Godavari rises near Triambak in the Western Ghats; the Krishna originates at Mahabaleshwar; the Kaveri rises in the Brahmagiri Hills of the Western Ghats in Karnataka. All three pairs are correctly matched.

**Q15.** With reference to physical geography, consider the following statements:
1. The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight Indian states.
2. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands lie in the Arabian Sea.
3. The highest peak in peninsular India is Anamudi.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight states (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram) — statement 1 correct. Anamudi (in the Western Ghats, Kerala) is the highest peak of peninsular India — statement 3 correct. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands lie in the Bay of Bengal, not the Arabian Sea — statement 2 is wrong.

**Q16.** Which one of the following correctly describes a "continental shelf"?
(a) The deepest part of the ocean floor lying beyond the abyssal plain
(b) The gently sloping, shallow submerged margin of a continent extending into the sea
(c) A mid-ocean ridge formed by diverging tectonic plates
(d) A steep underwater canyon formed by turbidity currents
**Answer: (b) The gently sloping, shallow submerged margin of a continent extending into the sea**
*Solution:* The continental shelf is the shallow, gently sloping extension of the continent under the sea, ending at the shelf break. Option (a) refers to the ocean trench/abyss, (c) to a mid-oceanic ridge, and (d) to a submarine canyon.

**Q17.** Consider the following statements about world geography:
1. The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman.
2. The Suez Canal connects the Red Sea with the Mediterranean Sea.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman (and thence the Arabian Sea) — statement 1 correct. The Suez Canal connects the Red Sea to the Mediterranean Sea — statement 2 correct.

## Economy

**Q18.** Consider the following statements regarding Monetary Policy in India:
1. The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) is responsible for fixing the benchmark policy repo rate.
2. The MPC consists of six members, three of whom are appointed by the Central Government.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The MPC, constituted under the RBI Act (amended 2016), determines the policy repo rate (statement 1 correct). It has six members — three from the RBI (including the Governor) and three appointed by the central government — so statement 2 is correct.

**Q19.** With reference to the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India, consider the following:
1. GST is a destination-based consumption tax.
2. The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister.
3. Petroleum products and alcohol for human consumption are currently outside the ambit of GST.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) All three  (b) Only two  (c) Only one  (d) None
**Answer: (a) All three**
*Solution:* GST is levied at the place of consumption (destination-based) — correct. The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister — correct. Alcohol for human consumption is permanently excluded, and petroleum products are presently kept outside GST — correct.

**Q20.** Consider the following statements about the balance of payments:
1. The current account records trade in goods and services, income and current transfers.
2. Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) is recorded under the capital (financial) account.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The current account includes merchandise and services trade, primary income and current transfers (statement 1 correct). FDI, being a financial flow, is recorded in the capital/financial account (statement 2 correct).

**Q21.** Which one of the following best describes "Fiscal Deficit"?
(a) The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings
(b) The excess of revenue expenditure over revenue receipts
(c) The difference between primary expenditure and revenue receipts
(d) Total borrowings minus interest payments
**Answer: (a) The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings**
*Solution:* Fiscal deficit is the gap between the government's total expenditure and its total receipts excluding borrowings — it indicates the total borrowing requirement. Option (b) defines the revenue deficit; the others are incorrect characterisations.

## Environment & Ecology

**Q22.** Consider the following statements about wetlands and the Ramsar Convention:
1. The Ramsar Convention is an intergovernmental treaty for the conservation and wise use of wetlands.
2. Chilika Lake in Odisha is a designated Ramsar site.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) Both 1 and 2  (c) 2 only  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (b) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The Ramsar Convention (1971) promotes wise use of wetlands (statement 1 correct). Chilika Lake was among India's earliest Ramsar sites (statement 2 correct).

**Q23.** With reference to biodiversity conservation in India, consider the following:
1. A Biosphere Reserve typically has a core zone, a buffer zone and a transition zone.
2. The "Tiger" is listed as a Schedule I species under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.
3. Project Tiger was launched in 1973.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) None  (c) Only two  (d) All three
**Answer: (d) All three**
*Solution:* Biosphere Reserves are zoned into core, buffer and transition areas (correct). The tiger receives the highest protection under Schedule I (correct). Project Tiger was launched in 1973 (correct).

**Q24.** Consider the following pairs of environmental terms and their meaning:
1. Eutrophication — nutrient enrichment of water bodies leading to algal blooms
2. Biomagnification — increasing concentration of toxins up the food chain
3. Albedo — the proportion of solar radiation reflected by a surface
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* All three are correctly matched. Eutrophication is nutrient over-enrichment; biomagnification is the accumulation of toxins at higher trophic levels; albedo measures reflectivity of a surface.

**Q25.** Consider the following statements about climate change agreements:
1. The Paris Agreement (2015) aims to limit the rise in global average temperature to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels.
2. The Kyoto Protocol introduced legally binding emission-reduction targets for developed countries.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* The Paris Agreement targets keeping warming well below 2°C, pursuing efforts towards 1.5°C (statement 1 correct). The Kyoto Protocol (1997) set binding targets for Annex-I/developed nations (statement 2 correct).

## Science & Technology

**Q26.** Consider the following statements regarding vaccines:
1. mRNA vaccines work by introducing genetic instructions that prompt cells to produce a viral protein, triggering an immune response.
2. A "live attenuated" vaccine uses a weakened form of the pathogen.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) Both 1 and 2  (b) 2 only  (c) 1 only  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (a) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* mRNA vaccines deliver instructions for cells to make a viral antigen that stimulates immunity (statement 1 correct). Live attenuated vaccines (e.g., for measles) use a weakened pathogen (statement 2 correct).

**Q27.** With reference to space technology, consider the following:
1. A geostationary satellite has an orbital period equal to the Earth's rotation period and appears fixed over a point on the equator.
2. ISRO's PSLV is primarily used to place satellites into polar/sun-synchronous orbits.
3. The escape velocity from Earth is approximately 11.2 km/s.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) All three  (c) Only two  (d) None
**Answer: (b) All three**
*Solution:* A geostationary satellite orbits in ~24 hours over the equator and appears stationary (correct). The PSLV is the workhorse for polar and sun-synchronous launches (correct). Earth's escape velocity is about 11.2 km/s (correct).

**Q28.** Which one of the following statements about CRISPR-Cas9 is correct?
(a) It is a technique used for nuclear fission in reactors
(b) It is a gene-editing tool that can precisely modify DNA sequences
(c) It is a method of wireless data transmission using light
(d) It is a type of quantum computing algorithm
**Answer: (b) It is a gene-editing tool that can precisely modify DNA sequences**
*Solution:* CRISPR-Cas9 is a genome-editing technology enabling precise additions, deletions or alterations of DNA. The other options describe unrelated technologies.

## Current Affairs & International

**Q29.** With reference to international groupings, consider the following statements:
1. The Quad is a strategic dialogue among India, the United States, Japan and Australia.
2. India is a member of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO).
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
