## History & Art-Culture

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the Sangam literature of ancient South India:
1. It was composed primarily in the Tamil language.
2. The Tolkappiyam is a work on Tamil grammar belonging to this corpus.
3. It exclusively describes the political history of the Mauryan empire.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* Sangam literature was composed in early Tamil, and the Tolkappiyam is its foundational grammatical text, so statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — Sangam works depict the Chera, Chola and Pandya realms and everyday life of the Tamil region, not Mauryan political history.

**Q2.** With reference to the Bhakti movement in medieval India, consider the following pairs:
1. Kabir — Nirguna Bhakti
2. Mirabai — devotion to Krishna
3. Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — Gaudiya Vaishnavism
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* Kabir preached a formless (nirguna) god; Mirabai was a renowned Krishna devotee from Rajasthan; Chaitanya Mahaprabhu founded the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition centred in Bengal. All three pairs are correctly matched.

---

**Q3.** The rock-cut architecture at the Kailasa temple of Ellora was primarily patronised by which dynasty?
(a) Pallava  (b) Rashtrakuta  (c) Chalukya of Badami  (d) Western Ganga
**Answer: (b) Rashtrakuta**
*Solution:* The monolithic Kailasa temple (Cave 16) at Ellora was excavated under the Rashtrakuta ruler Krishna I in the 8th century CE. The Pallavas built at Mahabalipuram, while the Badami Chalukyas worked at Badami and Aihole.

**Q4.** Consider the following statements about the revenue system of the Mughal Empire:
1. The *zabt* system involved measurement of cultivated land to fix revenue.
2. Todar Mal's reforms were associated with land-revenue administration under Akbar.
3. The *mansabdari* system was a purely religious institution with no military function.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The zabt system relied on land measurement and average produce to assess revenue, and Todar Mal designed these reforms under Akbar — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; the mansabdari system was an administrative-military ranking system, not a religious one.

**Q5.** Consider the following events of the Indian freedom struggle:
1. Jallianwala Bagh massacre
2. Formation of the Indian National Congress
3. Dandi Salt March
What is the correct chronological order of these events (earliest first)?
(a) 2 - 1 - 3  (b) 1 - 2 - 3  (c) 2 - 3 - 1  (d) 1 - 3 - 2
**Answer: (a) 2 - 1 - 3**
*Solution:* The Indian National Congress was formed in 1885, the Jallianwala Bagh massacre occurred in 1919, and the Dandi Salt March took place in 1930. The correct order is therefore 2 - 1 - 3.

**Q6.** With reference to classical dance forms of India, consider the following pairs:
1. Sattriya — Assam
2. Mohiniyattam — Kerala
3. Kuchipudi — Odisha
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Sattriya originated in the Vaishnava monasteries (sattras) of Assam, and Mohiniyattam is a classical form of Kerala — both correct. Kuchipudi belongs to Andhra Pradesh, not Odisha (Odisha's form is Odissi), so pair 3 is wrong.

## Indian Polity & Governance

**Q7.** Consider the following statements regarding the Fundamental Duties in the Constitution of India:
1. They were added by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act.
2. They are enumerated in Part IV-A of the Constitution.
3. They are directly enforceable in a court of law.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* Fundamental Duties were inserted by the 42nd Amendment (1976) and placed in Part IV-A (Article 51A) — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; they are not directly enforceable by courts, though they may guide interpretation.

---

**Q8.** With reference to the office of the Governor of a State, which one of the following is correct?
(a) The Governor is elected by the people of the State.
(b) The Governor is appointed by the President of India.
(c) The Governor holds office for a fixed term of six years irrespective of the President's pleasure.
(d) The Governor must mandatorily be a resident of the State concerned.
**Answer: (b) The Governor is appointed by the President of India.**
*Solution:* Under Article 155, the Governor is appointed by the President and holds office during the President's pleasure (Article 156). The Governor is not elected, has no guaranteed fixed term against pleasure, and there is no constitutional residence requirement.

**Q9.** Consider the following statements about the Election Commission of India:
1. It is established under Article 324 of the Constitution.
2. The Chief Election Commissioner can be removed only in the manner provided for a Supreme Court judge.
3. It conducts elections to local panchayats and municipalities.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* Article 324 establishes the ECI, and the CEC enjoys removal protection akin to a Supreme Court judge — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; panchayat and municipal elections are conducted by the State Election Commissions, not the ECI.

**Q10.** Match the following constitutional provisions with their subjects:
1. Article 32 — Right to constitutional remedies
2. Article 21 — Protection of life and personal liberty
3. Article 19 — Abolition of untouchability
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Article 32 guarantees the right to move the Supreme Court for enforcement of fundamental rights, and Article 21 protects life and personal liberty — both correct. Article 19 covers freedoms such as speech and assembly; abolition of untouchability is Article 17, so pair 3 is wrong.

**Q11.** Consider the following statements regarding the Rajya Sabha:
1. It is a permanent House that is not subject to dissolution.
2. One-third of its members retire every second year.
3. Money Bills can originate in the Rajya Sabha.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The Rajya Sabha is a permanent body and one-third of its members retire every two years — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; Money Bills can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha (Article 110).

**Q12.** With reference to the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, consider the following statements:
1. It gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj Institutions.
2. It added the Eleventh Schedule to the Constitution.
3. It mandated reservation of seats for women in panchayats.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The 73rd Amendment (1992) constitutionalised Panchayati Raj, inserted the Eleventh Schedule listing 29 subjects, and provided for not less than one-third reservation of seats for women. All three statements are correct.

## Geography

**Q13.** Consider the following statements about the Western Ghats:
1. They run roughly parallel to the western coast of India.
2. They are a UNESCO-recognised biodiversity hotspot.
3. They are geologically younger and higher than the Himalayas.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The Western Ghats run parallel to the western coast and are recognised as a global biodiversity hotspot — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; the Western Ghats are older block mountains and far lower than the young, fold Himalayas.

**Q14.** Match the following rivers with the states where they originate:
1. Godavari — Maharashtra
2. Kaveri — Karnataka
3. Narmada — Madhya Pradesh
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* The Godavari rises at Trimbak in Maharashtra, the Kaveri at Talakaveri in Karnataka, and the Narmada at Amarkantak in Madhya Pradesh. All three pairs are correctly matched.

**Q15.** Which one of the following straits separates India from Sri Lanka?
(a) Palk Strait  (b) Strait of Malacca  (c) Bering Strait  (d) Strait of Hormuz
**Answer: (a) Palk Strait**
*Solution:* The Palk Strait, together with the Gulf of Mannar, separates the Indian state of Tamil Nadu from Sri Lanka. The Malacca, Bering and Hormuz straits lie in Southeast Asia, the Arctic-Pacific region, and the Persian Gulf respectively.

**Q16.** Consider the following statements regarding the El Niño phenomenon:
1. It is associated with the warming of central and eastern Pacific Ocean surface waters.
2. It can weaken the Indian summer monsoon.
3. It is a component of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO).
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* El Niño involves abnormal warming of the central-eastern equatorial Pacific, is often linked with a weaker Indian monsoon, and forms the warm phase of the ENSO cycle. All three statements are correct.

**Q17.** With reference to world geography, consider the following pairs of countries and the deserts located in them:
1. Chile — Atacama Desert
2. Mongolia/China — Gobi Desert
3. Australia — Sahara Desert
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* The Atacama lies in Chile and the Gobi spans Mongolia and northern China — both correct. The Sahara is in North Africa, not Australia (whose major desert is the Great Victoria Desert), so pair 3 is wrong.

## Economy

**Q18.** Consider the following statements about the repo rate in India:
1. It is the rate at which the Reserve Bank of India lends short-term funds to commercial banks.
2. An increase in the repo rate generally tends to reduce inflationary pressure.
3. It is decided by the Monetary Policy Committee.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The repo rate is the RBI's short-term lending rate to banks, a hike makes credit costlier and helps cool inflation, and it is set by the six-member Monetary Policy Committee. All three statements are correct.

---

**Q19.** Which one of the following best describes "fiscal deficit"?
(a) The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings.
(b) The excess of revenue expenditure over revenue receipts.
(c) The difference between exports and imports of goods.
(d) The total accumulated borrowings of the government over the years.
**Answer: (a) The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings.**
*Solution:* Fiscal deficit equals total expenditure minus total receipts other than borrowings; it indicates the borrowing requirement. Option (b) describes the revenue deficit, (c) the trade deficit, and (d) the public debt.

**Q20.** Consider the following statements regarding Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India:
1. It is a destination-based indirect tax.
2. The GST Council is a constitutional body.
3. Petroleum crude and petrol have been fully and permanently excluded from the constitutional ambit of GST.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* GST is a destination-based consumption tax and the GST Council is a constitutional body under Article 279A — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; petroleum products are constitutionally within GST but currently kept outside it, to be taxed from a date the Council recommends.

**Q21.** With reference to the Indian economy, consider the following:
1. Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)
2. Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI)
Which of the above is/are generally considered more stable and long-term in nature?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (a) 1 only**
*Solution:* FDI involves a lasting management interest in enterprises and is regarded as stable, long-term capital. FPI (investment in stocks and bonds) is more volatile and can exit quickly, so only statement 1 is correct.

## Environment & Ecology

**Q22.** Consider the following statements about the Ramsar Convention:
1. It is an international treaty for the conservation of wetlands.
2. Sites designated under it are called Ramsar Sites.
3. It is named after a city in Iran.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The Ramsar Convention (1971) is an intergovernmental treaty for the wise use and conservation of wetlands, its listed wetlands are "Ramsar Sites", and it is named after the Iranian city of Ramsar where it was signed. All three statements are correct.

**Q23.** With reference to in-situ and ex-situ conservation, consider the following pairs:
1. National Park — in-situ conservation
2. Botanical garden — ex-situ conservation
3. Gene bank — in-situ conservation
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* National parks conserve species in their natural habitat (in-situ), while botanical gardens conserve outside the natural habitat (ex-situ) — both correct. A gene bank is a form of ex-situ, not in-situ, conservation, so pair 3 is wrong.

**Q24.** Consider the following statements regarding Project Tiger in India:
1. It was launched in the 1970s.
2. The National Tiger Conservation Authority is a statutory body overseeing it.
3. Tiger reserves are notified under the Wildlife (Protection) Act.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* Project Tiger was launched in 1973, the NTCA constituted under the Wildlife (Protection) Act is a statutory authority that administers it, and tiger reserves are notified under that Act. All three statements are correct.

**Q25.** Which one of the following greenhouse gases has the highest global warming potential per molecule over a 100-year horizon among the options listed?
(a) Carbon dioxide  (b) Methane  (c) Nitrous oxide  (d) Sulphur hexafluoride
**Answer: (d) Sulphur hexafluoride**
*Solution:* Sulphur hexafluoride (SF6) has an extremely high global warming potential (in the tens of thousands times that of CO2 over 100 years), far exceeding methane and nitrous oxide. Carbon dioxide is the reference gas with a GWP of 1.

## Science & Technology

**Q26.** Consider the following statements regarding CRISPR-Cas9 technology:
1. It is a tool used for editing genes.
2. The "Cas9" component acts as molecular scissors that cut DNA.
3. It can only be used in plants and never in animals.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* CRISPR-Cas9 is a precise gene-editing tool and Cas9 is the enzyme that cleaves DNA at a targeted site — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; it is widely applied in animals, microbes and human cells, not only plants.

**Q27.** With reference to India's space programme, consider the following statements:
1. Chandrayaan-3 achieved a soft landing near the lunar south polar region in 2023.
2. ISRO's launch vehicle PSLV is used to place satellites into orbit.
3. The Aditya-L1 mission is aimed at studying Mars.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* Chandrayaan-3 made a successful soft landing near the Moon's south pole in August 2023, and the PSLV is ISRO's workhorse satellite launcher — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; Aditya-L1 is a solar observation mission, not a Mars mission.

---

**Q28.** Which one of the following is the correct full form of "mRNA" as used in certain vaccines?
(a) Multiple Ribonucleic Acid  (b) Messenger Ribonucleic Acid  (c) Modified Ribonucleic Acid  (d) Mitochondrial Ribonucleic Acid
**Answer: (b) Messenger Ribonucleic Acid**
*Solution:* mRNA stands for messenger ribonucleic acid, which carries genetic instructions for protein synthesis; mRNA vaccines deliver instructions for cells to produce a target antigen. The other expansions are not the meaning of mRNA.

## Current Affairs & International

**Q29.** Consider the following statements regarding the G20:
1. India held the G20 presidency in 2023.
2. The African Union was admitted as a permanent member of the G20 during the 2023 New Delhi Summit.
3. The G20 is a forum of only the seven most industrialised economies.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* India held the G20 presidency in 2023 and the African Union was inducted as a permanent member at the New Delhi Summit — statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong; the G20 comprises 19 countries, the EU and the AU, not just seven nations (that is the G7).

**Q30.** With reference to government welfare schemes, consider the following pairs:
1. PM-KISAN — income support to farmers
2. Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) — health insurance coverage
3. Ujjwala Yojana — free smartphones to students
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* PM-KISAN provides direct income support to farmer families, and Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) offers health insurance cover to eligible families — both correct. The Ujjwala Yojana provides LPG cooking-gas connections to poor households, not smartphones, so pair 3 is wrong.
