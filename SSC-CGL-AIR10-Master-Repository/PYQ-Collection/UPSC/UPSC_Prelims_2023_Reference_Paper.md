## History & Art-Culture

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the Buddhist site of Nalanda:
1. It functioned as a residential monastic university (Mahavihara) and attracted students from beyond India.
2. The Chinese pilgrim Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang) studied and stayed there in the 7th century CE.
3. It was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) None  (d) All three
**Answer: (d) All three**
*Solution:* Nalanda was a renowned residential Mahavihara that drew international scholars (1 correct). Xuanzang studied there in the 7th century and left detailed accounts (2 correct). The ruins of Nalanda Mahavihara were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2016 (3 correct).

**Q2.** With reference to the Bhakti and Sufi traditions of medieval India, consider the following pairs:
1. Kabir — Nirguna devotion
2. Mirabai — Devotion to Krishna
3. Chaitanya Mahaprabhu — Devotion to Shiva
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Kabir preached Nirguna (formless) devotion (1 correct). Mirabai was a devotee of Krishna (2 correct). Chaitanya Mahaprabhu was a Vaishnava saint devoted to Krishna, not Shiva (3 incorrect).

---

**Q3.** Which one of the following classical dance forms originated in Kerala and is traditionally performed by women?
(a) Kuchipudi  (b) Mohiniyattam  (c) Odissi  (d) Sattriya
**Answer: (b) Mohiniyattam**
*Solution:* Mohiniyattam ("dance of the enchantress") is a classical dance form of Kerala traditionally performed solo by women. Kuchipudi is from Andhra Pradesh, Odissi from Odisha, and Sattriya from Assam.

**Q4.** Consider the following statements about the revolt of 1857:
1. The immediate trigger involved the issue of greased cartridges for the new Enfield rifle.
2. Bahadur Shah Zafar was proclaimed the symbolic leader by the rebels at Delhi.
3. After the revolt, the administration of India was transferred from the East India Company to the British Crown.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) All three  (c) Only two  (d) None
**Answer: (b) All three**
*Solution:* The greased-cartridge controversy was the immediate spark (1 correct). The rebels proclaimed the Mughal emperor Bahadur Shah Zafar as their nominal head at Delhi (2 correct). The Government of India Act, 1858 transferred power from the Company to the Crown (3 correct).

**Q5.** With reference to the architecture of the Vijayanagara Empire, the Hazara Rama temple and the Vitthala temple with its iconic stone chariot are located at:
(a) Hampi  (b) Belur  (c) Thanjavur  (d) Madurai
**Answer: (a) Hampi**
*Solution:* Hampi, the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire, contains the Hazara Rama and Vitthala temples; the latter is famous for its stone chariot. Belur is a Hoysala site; Thanjavur and Madurai are associated with Chola and Pandya/Nayaka temples respectively.

**Q6.** Consider the following pairs of freedom-struggle movements and their associated years:
1. Champaran Satyagraha — 1917
2. Non-Cooperation Movement — 1920
3. Quit India Movement — 1942
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* Gandhi's Champaran Satyagraha began in 1917 (1 correct). The Non-Cooperation Movement was launched in 1920 (2 correct). The Quit India Movement began in August 1942 (3 correct).

## Indian Polity & Governance

**Q7.** Consider the following statements regarding the Fundamental Duties under the Indian Constitution:
1. They were added by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act.
2. They are listed in Article 51A of the Constitution.
3. They are legally enforceable in a court of law in the same manner as Fundamental Rights.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* Fundamental Duties were added by the 42nd Amendment (1976) (1 correct) and are contained in Article 51A (2 correct). However, they are not directly enforceable by courts (3 incorrect).

**Q8.** With reference to the office of the Governor of a State, consider the following statements:
1. The Governor is appointed by the President of India.
2. The Governor holds office during the pleasure of the President.
3. The minimum age to be appointed Governor is 30 years.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* The Governor is appointed by the President (Article 155) (1 correct) and holds office during the President's pleasure (Article 156) (2 correct). The minimum age to be a Governor is 35 years, not 30 (3 incorrect).

**Q9.** Which one of the following writs is issued by a court to a public official to perform a duty that he or she has failed to perform?
(a) Habeas Corpus  (b) Mandamus  (c) Certiorari  (d) Quo Warranto
**Answer: (b) Mandamus**
*Solution:* Mandamus ("we command") directs a public authority to perform its legal duty. Habeas Corpus deals with unlawful detention, Certiorari quashes an order of a lower court/tribunal, and Quo Warranto questions a person's right to hold a public office.

**Q10.** Consider the following statements about the Finance Commission of India:
1. It is a constitutional body established under Article 280.
2. It recommends the distribution of net tax proceeds between the Union and the States.
3. It is constituted by the President every five years or earlier.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) None  (d) All three
**Answer: (d) All three**
*Solution:* The Finance Commission is a constitutional body under Article 280 (1 correct), recommends the vertical and horizontal devolution of taxes (2 correct), and is constituted by the President every fifth year or earlier (3 correct).

**Q11.** Consider the following pairs of constitutional provisions and their subject matter:
1. Article 32 — Right to constitutional remedies
2. Article 21 — Protection of life and personal liberty
3. Article 14 — Freedom of religion
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Article 32 guarantees the right to constitutional remedies (1 correct) and Article 21 protects life and personal liberty (2 correct). Article 14 deals with equality before the law; freedom of religion is covered by Articles 25–28 (3 incorrect).

**Q12.** With reference to the Rajya Sabha, consider the following statements:
1. It is a permanent House and is not subject to dissolution.
2. One-third of its members retire every second year.
3. The Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) All three  (c) Only two  (d) None
**Answer: (b) All three**
*Solution:* The Rajya Sabha is a permanent body not subject to dissolution (1 correct); one-third of its members retire every two years (2 correct); and the Vice-President is its ex-officio Chairman (Article 64) (3 correct).

## Geography

**Q13.** Consider the following statements about the rivers of peninsular India:
1. The Narmada and the Tapi flow westward into the Arabian Sea.
2. The Godavari is the largest peninsular river by length.
3. The Mahanadi originates in Chhattisgarh.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The Narmada and Tapi are westward-flowing rivers draining into the Arabian Sea (1 correct). The Godavari, often called the "Dakshin Ganga," is the longest peninsular river (2 correct). The Mahanadi rises in the highlands of Chhattisgarh (3 correct).

---

**Q14.** Which one of the following straits separates India from Sri Lanka?
(a) Palk Strait  (b) Strait of Malacca  (c) Sunda Strait  (d) Bering Strait
**Answer: (a) Palk Strait**
*Solution:* The Palk Strait (along with the Gulf of Mannar) separates India from Sri Lanka. The Strait of Malacca lies between the Malay Peninsula and Sumatra, the Sunda Strait between Java and Sumatra, and the Bering Strait between Asia and North America.

**Q15.** Consider the following pairs of mountain passes and the States/UTs in which they are located:
1. Nathu La — Sikkim
2. Zoji La — Ladakh/Jammu & Kashmir region
3. Shipki La — Himachal Pradesh
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* Nathu La connects Sikkim with Tibet (1 correct). Zoji La lies on the Srinagar–Leh route in the J&K/Ladakh region (2 correct). Shipki La is in Himachal Pradesh near the Sutlej's entry into India (3 correct).

**Q16.** With reference to world geography, the Atacama Desert, one of the driest places on Earth, is located in:
(a) Egypt  (b) Australia  (c) Namibia  (d) Chile
**Answer: (d) Chile**
*Solution:* The Atacama Desert lies along the Pacific coast of northern Chile and is among the driest non-polar deserts on Earth. Egypt has the Sahara, Australia the Great Victoria Desert, and Namibia the Namib Desert.

**Q17.** Consider the following statements about the El Niño phenomenon:
1. It is associated with the abnormal warming of surface waters in the central and eastern Pacific Ocean.
2. It can weaken the Indian summer monsoon, leading to deficient rainfall.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Neither 1 nor 2  (d) Both 1 and 2
**Answer: (d) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* El Niño involves the warming of central and eastern equatorial Pacific surface waters (1 correct) and is often linked to a weaker Indian monsoon and below-normal rainfall (2 correct).

## Economy

**Q18.** Consider the following statements regarding the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India:
1. It is a destination-based indirect tax.
2. The GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister.
3. Petroleum crude and potable alcohol for human consumption were initially kept outside the GST regime.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* GST is a destination-based consumption tax (1 correct), the GST Council is chaired by the Union Finance Minister (2 correct), and items such as crude petroleum and potable alcohol were kept out of GST at the outset (3 correct).

**Q19.** Which one of the following best describes the term "Repo Rate" as used by the Reserve Bank of India?
(a) The rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against securities
(b) The rate at which banks pay interest to depositors
(c) The minimum percentage of deposits banks must keep as cash with the RBI
(d) The rate at which the government borrows from foreign institutions
**Answer: (a) The rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against securities**
*Solution:* The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends to commercial banks against government securities. Option (c) describes the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR). Options (b) and (d) are unrelated to the repo rate.

**Q20.** Consider the following statements about Gross Domestic Product (GDP):
1. GDP at market prices includes indirect taxes minus subsidies.
2. Real GDP is measured at constant prices and adjusts for inflation.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* GDP at market prices includes net indirect taxes (indirect taxes minus subsidies) (1 correct). Real GDP is valued at constant base-year prices to remove the effect of inflation (2 correct).

**Q21.** With reference to financial inclusion in India, the Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) primarily aims to:
(a) Provide basic banking accounts to the unbanked population
(b) Offer crop insurance to farmers
(c) Provide subsidized housing loans to urban poor
(d) Guarantee 100 days of wage employment in rural areas
**Answer: (a) Provide basic banking accounts to the unbanked population**
*Solution:* PMJDY, launched in 2014, aims to provide universal access to banking through basic (zero-balance) accounts with overdraft and insurance benefits. Crop insurance is PMFBY, and the rural wage-employment guarantee is MGNREGA.

## Environment & Ecology

**Q22.** Consider the following statements about wetlands and the Ramsar Convention:
1. The Ramsar Convention is an international treaty for the conservation and sustainable use of wetlands.
2. Wetlands designated under the Convention are known as Ramsar Sites.
3. India has more than one designated Ramsar Site.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The Ramsar Convention (1971) targets wetland conservation and wise use (1 correct); listed wetlands are called Ramsar Sites (2 correct); and India has many Ramsar Sites (well over 70) (3 correct).

**Q23.** With reference to Project Tiger in India, consider the following statements:
1. It was launched in 1973 to conserve the Bengal tiger.
2. Tiger reserves are notified and monitored by the National Tiger Conservation Authority.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* Project Tiger was launched in 1973 to protect the tiger (1 correct). The National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) oversees tiger reserves under the Wildlife (Protection) Act (2 correct).

**Q24.** Which one of the following greenhouse gases has the highest global warming potential per molecule over a 100-year period among the options listed?
(a) Carbon dioxide  (b) Sulphur hexafluoride  (c) Methane  (d) Water vapour
**Answer: (b) Sulphur hexafluoride**
*Solution:* Among the listed gases, sulphur hexafluoride (SF6) has an extremely high global warming potential, far exceeding that of carbon dioxide and methane. Water vapour is a natural greenhouse gas but is not assigned a comparable GWP value in this context.

**Q25.** Consider the following pairs of protected areas and the States in which they are located:
1. Kaziranga National Park — Assam
2. Gir National Park — Gujarat
3. Sundarbans National Park — West Bengal
Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* Kaziranga (one-horned rhinoceros) is in Assam (1 correct), Gir (Asiatic lion) is in Gujarat (2 correct), and the Sundarbans (Royal Bengal tiger, mangroves) is in West Bengal (3 correct).

## Science & Technology

**Q26.** Consider the following statements about the Chandrayaan-3 mission of ISRO:
1. It aimed to demonstrate a soft landing near the lunar south polar region.
2. It carried a rover to conduct in-situ experiments on the lunar surface.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* Chandrayaan-3 targeted a soft landing in the high-latitude southern region of the Moon (1 correct) and deployed the Pragyan rover for surface experiments (2 correct).

---

**Q27.** Which one of the following best describes "CRISPR" as used in modern biology?
(a) A gene-editing technology  (b) A type of vaccine adjuvant  (c) A satellite navigation system  (d) A renewable energy source
**Answer: (a) A gene-editing technology**
*Solution:* CRISPR (often with the Cas9 enzyme) is a precise gene-editing tool that allows targeted modification of DNA sequences. The other options are unrelated to CRISPR.

---

**Q28.** Consider the following statements about blockchain technology:
1. It is a distributed ledger maintained across a network of computers.
2. Once recorded, data in a block is generally resistant to retroactive alteration.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (c) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* Blockchain is a decentralized, distributed ledger (1 correct), and its cryptographic linking of blocks makes recorded data highly resistant to tampering (2 correct).

## Current Affairs & International

**Q29.** With reference to the G20 grouping, consider the following statements:
1. India held the G20 Presidency and hosted the Leaders' Summit in New Delhi in 2023.
2. The African Union was admitted as a permanent member of the G20 during India's presidency.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only 1  (b) Both 1 and 2  (c) Only 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
**Answer: (b) Both 1 and 2**
*Solution:* India held the G20 Presidency and hosted the summit in New Delhi in September 2023 (1 correct). The African Union was granted permanent membership of the G20 at that summit (2 correct).

**Q30.** Which one of the following international organizations released the "World Economic Outlook" report?
(a) International Monetary Fund (IMF)  (b) World Trade Organization (WTO)  (c) World Health Organization (WHO)  (d) International Labour Organization (ILO)
**Answer: (a) International Monetary Fund (IMF)**
*Solution:* The World Economic Outlook is a flagship publication of the IMF, providing analysis and projections of the global economy. The WTO, WHO, and ILO publish other reports unrelated to this title.
