## History & Art-Culture

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the Buddhist stupa architecture of ancient India:
1. The hemispherical dome of a stupa is known as the *anda*.
2. The *harmika* is the railing-like enclosure placed at the very base of the stupa.
3. The *chhatra* (umbrella) crowning the stupa symbolised honour and reverence.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The *anda* (dome) and *chhatra* (umbrella) are correctly described. The *harmika* is the small square railing/balcony placed on **top** of the dome, not at the base — the base structure is the *medhi* (drum) with the circumambulatory path (*pradakshina patha*). Hence statements 1 and 3 are correct.

**Q2.** With reference to the administration of the Mauryan Empire, consider the following:
1. The *Sannidhata* was the chief treasurer of the state.
2. The *Samaharta* was the highest officer in charge of revenue collection.
3. The *Pradeshika* functioned as a provincial governor directly appointed from royal princes only.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* The *Sannidhata* (treasurer) and *Samaharta* (collector-general of revenue) are correctly identified from the Arthashastra's administrative scheme. The *Pradeshika* was a district-level administrative officer overseeing revenue and order, not exclusively a royal prince; provinces (under *Kumaras*/princes) were a separate tier. Statement 3 is wrong.

**Q3.** Match the following Bhakti/Sufi figures with their associated traditions:
1. Ramananda      — A. Virashaiva (Lingayat) movement
2. Basavanna      — B. Chishti Sufi order
3. Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti — C. Bhakti tradition emphasising worship of Rama in North India
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
(a) 1-A, 2-B, 3-C  (b) 1-C, 2-A, 3-B  (c) 1-B, 2-C, 3-A  (d) 1-C, 2-B, 3-A
**Answer: (b) 1-C, 2-A, 3-B**
*Solution:* Ramananda preached devotion to Rama in North India (C). Basavanna founded/led the Virashaiva or Lingayat movement in Karnataka (A). Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti established the Chishti Sufi order at Ajmer (B).

**Q4.** Consider the following statements about the Revolt of 1857:
1. Mangal Pandey, a sepoy of the 34th Native Infantry, was among the earliest to rebel at Barrackpore.
2. Bahadur Shah Zafar was proclaimed the symbolic leader of the revolt at Delhi.
3. The Queen's Proclamation of 1858 transferred the governance of India from the East India Company to the British Crown.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* All three are historically accurate. Mangal Pandey's act at Barrackpore (March 1857) preceded the Meerut outbreak; Bahadur Shah Zafar was declared the figurehead emperor at Delhi; and the Government of India Act 1858 / Queen Victoria's Proclamation ended Company rule, vesting power in the Crown.

**Q5.** With reference to classical Indian dance forms and their states of origin, consider the following pairs:
1. Sattriya — Assam
2. Mohiniyattam — Kerala
3. Kuchipudi — Odisha
Which of the pairs given above is/are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Sattriya originated in the Vaishnava monasteries (sattras) of Assam, and Mohiniyattam is from Kerala — both correct. Kuchipudi originates from Andhra Pradesh, not Odisha; the classical dance of Odisha is Odissi. Hence pair 3 is wrong.

**Q6.** The term "Dhrupad," frequently encountered in the context of Indian heritage, refers to:
(a) A style of miniature painting developed under the Pahari schools
(b) One of the oldest surviving forms of Hindustani classical vocal music
(c) A type of stepwell architecture found mainly in Gujarat
(d) A ceremonial martial-art tradition of the southern coast
**Answer: (b) One of the oldest surviving forms of Hindustani classical vocal music**
*Solution:* Dhrupad is among the most ancient and austere genres of Hindustani classical vocal music, traditionally devotional and rendered with great structural discipline. The Gujarati stepwell is a *vav* (e.g., Rani-ki-Vav); Pahari refers to a painting school; the coastal martial art is Kalaripayattu (Kerala).

## Indian Polity & Governance

**Q7.** Consider the following statements regarding the Fundamental Duties under the Indian Constitution:
1. Fundamental Duties were added by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976.
2. They are enumerated in Article 51A under Part IV-A.
3. Fundamental Duties are directly enforceable by the courts, like Fundamental Rights.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* The 42nd Amendment (1976), based on the Swaran Singh Committee, inserted Fundamental Duties as Article 51A in a new Part IV-A. They are **not** directly justiciable/enforceable by courts (unlike Fundamental Rights), so statement 3 is incorrect.

**Q8.** With reference to the office of the Governor of a State, consider the following:
1. The Governor is appointed by the President under Article 155.
2. The Governor holds office during the pleasure of the President.
3. A person must have completed 30 years of age to be eligible for appointment as Governor.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* The Governor is appointed by the President (Art. 155) and holds office during the President's pleasure (Art. 156) — both correct. However, the minimum age to be appointed Governor is **35 years** (Art. 157), not 30. Hence statement 3 is wrong.

**Q9.** Match the following constitutional bodies with the Articles establishing them:
1. Election Commission        — A. Article 324
2. Finance Commission         — B. Article 280
3. Comptroller and Auditor General — C. Article 148
Select the correct answer:
(a) 1-A, 2-B, 3-C  (b) 1-B, 2-A, 3-C  (c) 1-C, 2-B, 3-A  (d) 1-A, 2-C, 3-B
**Answer: (a) 1-A, 2-B, 3-C**
*Solution:* The Election Commission is established under Article 324, the Finance Commission under Article 280, and the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) under Article 148. All pairings in option (a) are correct.

**Q10.** Consider the following statements about the Rajya Sabha:
1. It is a permanent House and is not subject to dissolution.
2. One-third of its members retire every second year.
3. The Vice-President of India is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* The Rajya Sabha is a permanent body not subject to dissolution; one-third of its members retire every two years (each member serves a six-year term); and the Vice-President is its ex-officio Chairman (Art. 64 and 89). All three statements are correct.

**Q11.** With reference to the amendment of the Indian Constitution under Article 368, consider the following:
1. Some amendments require ratification by at least half of the State Legislatures.
2. A Constitution Amendment Bill can be introduced in either House of Parliament.
3. The President is bound to give assent to a Constitution Amendment Bill duly passed.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* Provisions affecting federal structure (e.g., distribution of powers, representation of states) require ratification by half the state legislatures. Such bills can originate in either House. After the 24th Amendment (1971), the President is obligated to assent to a Constitution Amendment Bill passed in the prescribed manner. All three are correct.

**Q12.** The "Doctrine of Basic Structure" of the Constitution was firmly established by the Supreme Court in which of the following cases?
(a) A.K. Gopalan v. State of Madras
(b) Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala
(c) Golaknath v. State of Punjab
(d) Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India
**Answer: (b) Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala**
*Solution:* The Basic Structure doctrine — that Parliament cannot amend the Constitution so as to alter its fundamental framework — was laid down in *Kesavananda Bharati* (1973). *Golaknath* held rights non-amendable (later overruled in part); *Maneka Gandhi* expanded Article 21; *A.K. Gopalan* dealt with preventive detention.

## Geography

**Q13.** Consider the following statements about the El Niño phenomenon:
1. It is associated with the abnormal warming of surface waters in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean.
2. El Niño years are generally associated with weaker monsoon rainfall over the Indian subcontinent.
3. El Niño is the cold phase of the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO) cycle.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* El Niño is the warming of the central/eastern equatorial Pacific (1, correct) and tends to suppress the Indian monsoon (2, correct). It is the **warm** phase of ENSO; the cold phase is La Niña. Hence statement 3 is incorrect.

**Q14.** Match the following rivers with the states where they originate:
1. Godavari   — A. Maharashtra
2. Kaveri     — B. Karnataka
3. Narmada    — C. Madhya Pradesh
Select the correct answer:
(a) 1-A, 2-B, 3-C  (b) 1-B, 2-A, 3-C  (c) 1-C, 2-B, 3-A  (d) 1-A, 2-C, 3-B

*Solution:* The Godavari rises at Trimbak in Maharashtra; the Kaveri originates at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of Karnataka; the Narmada rises from the Amarkantak plateau in Madhya Pradesh. All pairings in (a) are correct.

**Q15.** With reference to the Strait of Hormuz, consider the following statements:
1. It connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman.
2. It lies between Iran to the north and the Musandam exclave of Oman / the UAE to the south.
3. It is a critical chokepoint for global crude oil shipments.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, lies between Iran (north) and the Omani Musandam peninsula/UAE (south), and is one of the world's most important oil-transit chokepoints. All three statements are correct.

**Q16.** Consider the following statements regarding black (regur) soil in India:
1. It is highly suitable for the cultivation of cotton.
2. It is rich in lime, iron, magnesia and alumina but generally poor in phosphorus.
3. It develops deep cracks during the dry season, which helps in aeration.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* Black soil (regur), formed mainly over the Deccan trap basalt, is ideal for cotton ("black cotton soil"). It is rich in lime, iron, magnesia and alumina but deficient in phosphorus, nitrogen and organic matter. Its clayey nature causes self-ploughing cracks when dry. All three are correct.

**Q17.** Which one of the following correctly describes the term "Doldrums" in physical geography?
(a) A zone of high pressure at the subtropical latitudes with descending dry air
(b) A low-pressure belt near the equator characterised by calm, light and variable winds
(c) The permanent ice sheets surrounding the polar regions
(d) The boundary between two converging tectonic plates
**Answer: (b) A low-pressure belt near the equator characterised by calm, light and variable winds**
*Solution:* The Doldrums refer to the equatorial low-pressure belt (Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone), known for calm and feeble, shifting winds due to intense heating and rising air. Subtropical high-pressure belts are the "Horse Latitudes," not the Doldrums.

## Economy

**Q18.** Consider the following statements regarding the repo rate in India:
1. It is the rate at which the Reserve Bank of India lends short-term funds to commercial banks against government securities.
2. An increase in the repo rate generally tends to reduce liquidity in the economy.
3. The repo rate is decided by the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC).
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The repo rate is the RBI's lending rate to banks against collateral securities; raising it makes borrowing costlier, tightening liquidity; and under the amended RBI Act, the repo rate is set by the six-member Monetary Policy Committee. All three statements are correct.

**Q19.** With reference to Gross Domestic Product (GDP) and related measures, consider the following:
1. Nominal GDP is measured at current market prices.
2. Real GDP is adjusted for inflation using a base year.
3. The GDP deflator is the ratio of real GDP to nominal GDP.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Nominal GDP uses current prices and real GDP is inflation-adjusted to a base year — both correct. The GDP deflator is (Nominal GDP / Real GDP) × 100, i.e., the ratio of nominal to real GDP, not the reverse. Hence statement 3 is wrong.

**Q20.** Consider the following statements about Treasury Bills (T-Bills) issued in India:
1. They are issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Government of India.
2. They are issued at a discount and redeemed at face value.
3. They carry a fixed periodic interest (coupon) payment.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (b) Only two**
*Solution:* T-Bills are short-term money-market instruments issued by the RBI for the central government, sold at a discount and redeemed at par. They are **zero-coupon** instruments — the return is the difference between issue price and face value, with no periodic interest. Hence statement 3 is wrong.

**Q21.** The "Fiscal Deficit" of the Government of India is best defined as:
(a) The excess of total expenditure over total revenue receipts excluding borrowings
(b) The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings
(c) The excess of revenue expenditure over revenue receipts
(d) The difference between interest payments and total revenue receipts
**Answer: (b) The excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding borrowings**
*Solution:* Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − total receipts other than borrowings; it indicates the total borrowing requirement of the government. Option (c) describes the revenue deficit, and option (d) approximates the primary deficit concept (fiscal deficit minus interest payments).

## Environment & Ecology

**Q22.** Consider the following statements about wetlands and the Ramsar Convention:
1. The Ramsar Convention is an international treaty for the conservation and wise use of wetlands.
2. "Montreux Record" is a list of Ramsar wetland sites facing or likely to face ecological change.
3. Chilika Lake in Odisha was India's first site to be designated under the Ramsar Convention.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* The Ramsar Convention (1971) promotes wetland conservation and wise use. The Montreux Record flags Ramsar sites undergoing or threatened by ecological change. Chilika Lake and Keoladeo were among India's first Ramsar sites (designated in 1981). All three statements are correct.

**Q23.** With reference to "carbon sequestration," consider the following statements:
1. Afforestation and reforestation are biological methods of carbon sequestration.
2. Oceans act as a natural sink that absorbs atmospheric carbon dioxide.
3. Carbon capture and storage (CCS) is an artificial method of removing CO₂ from emission sources.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (d) 1, 2 and 3**
*Solution:* Afforestation/reforestation (biological), oceanic absorption (natural sink), and CCS (engineered capture and underground storage) are all recognised mechanisms of carbon sequestration. All three statements are correct.

**Q25.** Consider the following statements about the ozone layer:
1. The ozone layer is concentrated mainly in the stratosphere.
2. It absorbs most of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet (UV) radiation.
3. The Montreal Protocol aims to phase out ozone-depleting substances such as chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs).

## Science & Technology

**Q26.** Consider the following statements about CRISPR-Cas9 technology:
1. It is a tool used for precise editing of genes (genome editing).
2. The Cas9 component acts as molecular "scissors" that cut the DNA at a targeted location.
3. A guide RNA helps direct the Cas9 enzyme to the specific DNA sequence.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**
*Solution:* CRISPR-Cas9 is a genome-editing system: a guide RNA locates the target DNA sequence, and the Cas9 enzyme cuts the DNA there, enabling precise additions or deletions. All three statements are accurate.

**Q27.** With reference to satellite navigation, the Indian regional system "NavIC" is associated with which of the following?
(a) A constellation of satellites providing regional positioning and timing services over India and surrounding regions
(b) A remote-sensing programme for crop monitoring only
(c) A deep-space mission to study the Sun's corona
(d) An undersea fibre-optic communication network
**Answer: (a) A constellation of satellites providing regional positioning and timing services over India and surrounding regions**
*Solution:* NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation), formerly IRNSS, is an independent regional satellite navigation system developed by ISRO, offering positioning and timing services over India and a region extending around it. The solar corona mission referenced is Aditya-L1, a separate programme.

**Q28.** Consider the following statements regarding monoclonal antibodies:
1. They are antibodies produced from a single clone of immune cells, targeting one specific antigen.
2. They are used in the treatment of certain cancers and autoimmune diseases.
3. They are a category of antibiotics effective against bacterial infections.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* Monoclonal antibodies are laboratory-produced from a single clone and bind a specific antigen, with major therapeutic uses in oncology and autoimmune disorders. They are **not** antibiotics; antibiotics are distinct chemical agents against bacteria. Hence statement 3 is wrong.

## Current Affairs & International

**Q29.** Consider the following statements about the BRICS grouping (as of 2024–2025):
1. BRICS originally comprised Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa.
2. The grouping expanded its membership with new countries joining from 1 January 2024.
3. The New Development Bank (NDB) is headquartered in Shanghai, China.
How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one  (b) Only two  (c) All three  (d) None
**Answer: (c) All three**

The New Development Bank, the BRICS-promoted multilateral bank, is headquartered in Shanghai. All three statements are correct.

**Q30.** With reference to the "Production Linked Incentive (PLI)" schemes of the Government of India, consider the following:
1. They aim to boost domestic manufacturing by offering incentives linked to incremental production/sales.
2. They cover sectors such as electronics, pharmaceuticals and automobiles.
3. They are administered exclusively by the Reserve Bank of India.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3
**Answer: (a) 1 and 2 only**
*Solution:* PLI schemes incentivise incremental domestic production/sales across multiple sectors including electronics, pharma, automobiles, textiles and more. They are implemented by various line ministries and departments (e.g., under the Government of India), **not** by the RBI. Hence statement 3 is wrong.
