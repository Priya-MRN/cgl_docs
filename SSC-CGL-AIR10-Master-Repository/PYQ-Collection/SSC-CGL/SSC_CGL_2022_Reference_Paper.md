## Section A — General Intelligence & Reasoning (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** Select the option that is related to the third term in the same way as the second is to the first: **Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?**
(a) Student  (b) School  (c) Book  (d) Class
**Answer: (b) School**
*Solution:* First works in the second (place of work). A doctor works in a hospital; a teacher works in a school.

**Q2.** Find the missing number: **7, 14, 28, 56, ?**
(a) 84  (b) 98  (c) 112  (d) 126
**Answer: (c) 112**
*Solution:* Each term is ×2 of the previous. 56 × 2 = **112**.

**Q3.** If `CAT = 24`, then `DOG = ?` (sum of letter positions)
(a) 26  (b) 28  (c) 30  (d) 32
**Answer: (a) 26**
*Solution:* C+A+T = 3+1+20 = 24 ✓. D+O+G = 4+15+7 = **26**.

**Q4.** Pointing to a photo, a man said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the girl related to the man?
(a) Sister  (b) Daughter  (c) Niece  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (a) Sister**
*Solution:* Grandfather's only son = the man's father. His father's daughter = the man's **sister**.
🔑 *Trick:* "Only son of grandfather" almost always resolves to "my father."

**Q5.** Select the odd one out: **121, 144, 169, 180**
(a) 121  (b) 144  (c) 169  (d) 180
**Answer: (d) 180**
*Solution:* 121=11², 144=12², 169=13². 180 is not a perfect square.

**Q6.** In a certain code, `MANGO` is written as `NBOHP`. How is `APPLE` written?
(a) BQQMF  (b) BQOMF  (c) BPQMF  (d) BQQNF
**Answer: (a) BQQMF**
*Solution:* Each letter shifts +1. A→B, P→Q, P→Q, L→M, E→F = **BQQMF**.

**Q7.** Arrange in a logical/dictionary order: 1. Rain 2. Cloud 3. Evaporation 4. Water 5. Vapour
(a) 4-3-5-2-1  (b) 4-5-3-2-1  (c) 3-4-5-1-2  (d) 4-3-2-5-1
**Answer: (a) 4-3-5-2-1**
*Solution:* Water → Evaporation → Vapour → Cloud → Rain (natural water cycle).

**Q8.** How many triangles are in a figure formed by a square with both diagonals drawn?
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 10
**Answer: (c) 8**
*Solution:* 4 small triangles + 4 larger half-square triangles = **8**.
🔑 *Trick:* Count smallest units first, then combinations.

**Q9.** If `+` means `×`, `−` means `+`, `×` means `÷`, and `÷` means `−`, then `16 × 4 + 5 − 2 = ?`
(a) 20  (b) 26  (c) 24  (d) 22
**Answer: (d) 22**
*Solution:* Substitute symbols first: `16 ÷ 4 × 5 + 2`. Apply BODMAS: 16÷4 = 4; 4×5 = 20; 20+2 = **22**.
🔑 *Trick:* Replace ALL symbols first, then apply BODMAS — never the other way round.

**Q10.** Which number completes the series: **3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?**
(a) 36  (b) 42  (c) 40  (d) 38
**Answer: (d) 38**
*Solution:* Differences: +3, +5, +7, +9, +11 → 27 + 11 = **38**.

## Section B — General Awareness (Q11–Q20)

**Q11.** Who is known as the "Father of the Indian Constitution"?
(a) Mahatma Gandhi  (b) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar  (c) Jawaharlal Nehru  (d) Sardar Patel
**Answer: (b) Dr. B. R. Ambedkar**
*Solution:* As Chairman of the Drafting Committee, Dr. Ambedkar is universally credited as the chief architect of the Constitution.

**Q12.** The "Tropic of Cancer" passes through how many Indian states?
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 8  (d) 9
**Answer: (c) 8**
*Solution:* It passes through Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram = **8**.

**Q13.** Which gas is most responsible for the greenhouse effect by volume of emissions?
(a) Oxygen  (b) Carbon dioxide  (c) Nitrogen  (d) Hydrogen
**Answer: (b) Carbon dioxide**
*Solution:* CO₂ is the principal anthropogenic greenhouse gas driving global warming.

**Q14.** The Battle of Plassey was fought in which year?
(a) 1757  (b) 1764  (c) 1857  (d) 1707
**Answer: (a) 1757**
*Solution:* Fought in 1757 between the British East India Company (Robert Clive) and Siraj-ud-Daulah — it laid the foundation of British rule in India.

**Q15.** Which Article of the Constitution deals with the Right to Equality?
(a) Article 14  (b) Article 19  (c) Article 21  (d) Article 32
**Answer: (a) Article 14**
*Solution:* Article 14 guarantees equality before the law and equal protection of the laws.

**Q16.** The currency of Japan is the:
(a) Won  (b) Yuan  (c) Yen  (d) Ringgit
**Answer: (c) Yen**
*Solution:* Japan's currency is the Yen; Won is Korean, Yuan is Chinese, Ringgit is Malaysian.

**Q17.** Which vitamin is produced in the human body upon exposure to sunlight?
(a) Vitamin A  (b) Vitamin C  (c) Vitamin D  (d) Vitamin K
**Answer: (c) Vitamin D**
*Solution:* UV-B radiation triggers Vitamin D synthesis in the skin.

**Q18.** "Sariska Tiger Reserve" is located in which state?
(a) Madhya Pradesh  (b) Maharashtra  (c) Gujarat  (d) Rajasthan
**Answer: (d) Rajasthan**
*Solution:* Sariska is in the Alwar district of Rajasthan.

**Q19.** Who wrote the national song "Vande Mataram"?
(a) Rabindranath Tagore  (b) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay  (c) Sarojini Naidu  (d) Subramania Bharati
**Answer: (b) Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**
*Solution:* It appears in his 1882 novel *Anandamath*.

**Q20.** The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is in:
(a) New York  (b) Geneva  (c) Paris  (d) Rome
**Answer: (b) Geneva**
*Solution:* WHO is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.

## Section C — Quantitative Aptitude (Q21–Q30)

**Q21.** If the cost price of 12 articles equals the selling price of 10 articles, find the profit %.
(a) 16⅔%  (b) 20%  (c) 25%  (d) 18%
**Answer: (b) 20%**
*Solution:* CP of 12 = SP of 10 → profit on 10 = 2 articles' CP → 2/10 = **20%**.

**Q22.** A sum doubles in 8 years at simple interest. In how many years will it triple?
(a) 12  (b) 24  (c) 20  (d) 16
**Answer: (d) 16**
*Solution:* Doubling → interest = principal in 8 yr → rate = 12.5%. To triple, interest = 2×principal → 16 years.

**Q23.** Find the average of the first 10 odd natural numbers.
(a) 9  (b) 10  (c) 11  (d) 100
**Answer: (b) 10**
*Solution:* Sum of first n odd numbers = n² = 100. Average = 100/10 = **10**.

**Q24.** A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 15 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
(a) 30  (b) 45  (c) 40  (d) 36
**Answer: (d) 36**
*Solution:* Speed = 150/15 = 10 m/s = 10 × 18/5 = **36 km/h**.

**Q25.** If 40% of a number is 240, what is 75% of that number?
(a) 360  (b) 420  (c) 450  (d) 480
**Answer: (c) 450**
*Solution:* Number = 240/0.40 = 600. 75% of 600 = **450**.

**Q26.** The simplified value of `(0.6 × 0.6 + 0.4 × 0.4 + 2 × 0.6 × 0.4)` is:
(a) 1  (b) 0.5  (c) 1.2  (d) 2
**Answer: (a) 1**
*Solution:* It is `(0.6 + 0.4)²` = `1² = 1`.
🔑 *Trick:* Recognize the `a² + b² + 2ab = (a+b)²` identity.

**Q27.** A can do a work in 12 days, B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days?
(a) 6.8  (b) 8  (c) 7.5  (d) 7.2
**Answer: (d) 7.2**
*Solution:* Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 → time = 36/5 = **7.2 days**.

**Q28.** The angles of a triangle are in ratio 2:3:4. The largest angle is:
(a) 40°  (b) 60°  (c) 80°  (d) 90°
**Answer: (c) 80°**
*Solution:* 2x+3x+4x = 180 → 9x = 180 → x = 20. Largest = 4×20 = **80°**.

**Q29.** Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% p.a. for 2 years.
(a) ₹2100  (b) ₹2000  (c) ₹2200  (d) ₹2050
**Answer: (a) ₹2100**
*Solution:* A = 10000(1.1)² = 12100 → CI = **₹2100**.

**Q30.** The perimeter of a square equals the circumference of a circle of radius 7 cm. Find the side of the square. (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 9 cm  (b) 22 cm  (c) 14 cm  (d) 11 cm
**Answer: (d) 11 cm**
*Solution:* Circumference = 2×(22/7)×7 = 44 cm = 4×side → side = **11 cm**.

## Section D — English Comprehension (Q31–Q40)

**Q31.** Choose the correct synonym of **"ABUNDANT"**:
(a) Scarce  (b) Plentiful  (c) Empty  (d) Rare
**Answer: (b) Plentiful**
*Solution:* Abundant = existing in large quantities = plentiful.

**Q32.** Choose the correct antonym of **"BENEVOLENT"**:
(a) Kind  (b) Generous  (c) Cruel  (d) Helpful
**Answer: (c) Cruel**
*Solution:* Benevolent = kind/charitable; its opposite is cruel.

**Q33.** Fill in the blank: *She has been working here ___ 2019.*
(a) since  (b) for  (c) from  (d) by
**Answer: (a) since**
*Solution:* "Since" is used with a point of time (2019); "for" is used with a period.
🔑 *Trick:* Since + point of time; For + duration.

**Q34.** Choose the correctly spelt word:
(a) Occassion  (b) Ocasion  (c) Occasion  (d) Occasson
**Answer: (c) Occasion**
*Solution:* Correct spelling = **Occasion** (double-c, single-s).

**Q35.** Identify the error: *One of my friend / is going / to the market / today.*
(a) One of my friend  (b) is going  (c) to the market  (d) today
**Answer: (a) One of my friend**
*Solution:* "One of" takes a plural noun → "one of my **friends**."

**Q36.** Choose the correct passive voice: *The teacher praised the student.*
(a) The student is praised by the teacher.  (b) The student praised the teacher.  (c) The student has praised the teacher.  (d) The student was praised by the teacher.
**Answer: (d) The student was praised by the teacher.**
*Solution:* Simple past active → simple past passive: was + praised + by.

**Q37.** Choose the meaning of the idiom **"to bite the dust"**:
(a) To eat quickly  (b) To fail or die  (c) To clean the floor  (d) To run fast
**Answer: (b) To fail or die**
*Solution:* "Bite the dust" = to be defeated, to fail, or to die.

**Q38.** Fill in the blank with the correct article: *He is ___ honest man.*
(a) a  (b) no article  (c) the  (d) an
**Answer: (d) an**
*Solution:* "Honest" begins with a silent 'h' (vowel sound), so it takes **an**.

**Q39.** Choose the one-word substitute: *A person who knows many languages.*
(a) Linguist  (b) Grammarian  (c) Polyglot  (d) Orator
**Answer: (c) Polyglot**
*Solution:* A polyglot speaks/knows many languages. (A linguist studies language scientifically.)

**Q40.** Choose the correct indirect speech: *He said, "I am tired."*
(a) He said that he is tired.  (b) He said that he was tired.  (c) He says that he was tired.  (d) He said that I was tired.
**Answer: (b) He said that he was tired.**
*Solution:* Reporting verb in past → present tense shifts to past: am → was.
