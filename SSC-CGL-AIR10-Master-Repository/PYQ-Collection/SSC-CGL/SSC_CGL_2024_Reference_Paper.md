## Section A — General Intelligence & Reasoning (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** Select the option that is related to the third term in the same way as the second term is related to the first term.
Bird : Nest :: Bee : ?
(a) Honey  (b) Hive  (c) Flower  (d) Wax
**Answer: (b) Hive**
*Solution:* A bird lives in a nest; analogously a bee lives in a hive. Honey and wax are products, and a flower is a food source — not the dwelling.

---

**Q2.** Select the number that will come next in the series.
3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?
(a) 36  (b) 38  (c) 40  (d) 42
**Answer: (b) 38**
*Solution:* Differences are +3, +5, +7, +9, so the next difference is +11. 27 + 11 = 38.
🔑 *Trick:* Consecutive odd-number gaps — each step adds the next odd number.

---

**Q3.** In a certain code language, "FROG" is written as "GSPH". How is "LION" written in that code?
(a) MJPO  (b) KJPO  (c) MHPO  (d) MJOO
**Answer: (a) MJPO**
*Solution:* Each letter is shifted +1: F→G, R→S, O→P, G→H. Applying +1 to LION: L→M, I→J, O→P, N→O = MJPO.

---

**Q4.** Pointing to a man, Rita said, "He is the only son of my mother's mother." How is the man related to Rita?
(a) Father  (b) Uncle (maternal)  (c) Brother  (d) Grandfather
**Answer: (b) Uncle (maternal)**
*Solution:* Rita's mother's mother is her grandmother. The grandmother's only son is Rita's mother's brother, i.e., Rita's maternal uncle.

---

**Q5.** In a row of 25 students facing north, Anil is 9th from the left end. What is his position from the right end?
(a) 16th  (b) 17th  (c) 18th  (d) 15th
**Answer: (b) 17th**
*Solution:* Position from right = Total − position from left + 1 = 25 − 9 + 1 = 17.
🔑 *Trick:* Left + Right = Total + 1.

---

**Q6.** Three of the following four are alike in a certain way and form a group. Which one does NOT belong?
(a) 25  (b) 49  (c) 64  (d) 50
**Answer: (d) 50**
*Solution:* 25 = 5², 49 = 7², 64 = 8² are perfect squares. 50 is not a perfect square.

---

**Q7.** Two statements are given followed by two conclusions. Assuming the statements true, decide which conclusion(s) logically follow.
Statements: All pens are markers. Some markers are inks.
Conclusions: (I) Some pens are inks. (II) Some markers are pens.
(a) Only I follows  (b) Only II follows  (c) Both follow  (d) Neither follows
**Answer: (b) Only II follows**
*Solution:* "All pens are markers" gives "Some markers are pens" (valid conversion), so II follows. The link between pens and inks is not established, so I does not follow.

---

**Q8.** A man walks 6 km north, turns right and walks 8 km, then turns right and walks 6 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 6 km  (b) 8 km  (c) 10 km  (d) 14 km
**Answer: (b) 8 km**
*Solution:* North 6, then east 8, then south 6 cancels the north 6. He ends 8 km east of start, so distance = 8 km.

---

**Q9.** If '+' means '×', '×' means '−', '−' means '÷', and '÷' means '+', then find the value of: 12 + 4 − 2 ÷ 3
(a) 27  (b) 24  (c) 21  (d) 30
**Answer: (a) 27**
*Solution:* Replace symbols: 12 × 4 ÷ 2 + 3 = (12 × 4) ÷ 2 + 3 = 48 ÷ 2 + 3 = 24 + 3 = 27.

---

**Q10.** How many triangles are there in a figure formed by a single rectangle with both of its diagonals drawn?
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 10
**Answer: (c) 8**
*Solution:* Both diagonals create 4 small triangles plus 4 larger triangles (each formed by two adjacent small ones / half-rectangles), giving 4 + 4 = 8 triangles in total.

## Section B — General Awareness (Q11–Q20)

**Q11.** Which part of the Indian Constitution is described as the "soul" of the Constitution and deals with Fundamental Rights?
(a) Part II  (b) Part III  (c) Part IV  (d) Part V
**Answer: (b) Part III**
*Solution:* Part III (Articles 12–35) of the Indian Constitution contains the Fundamental Rights and is often called the soul or conscience of the Constitution.

---

**Q12.** The ancient university of Nalanda was located in which present-day Indian state?
(a) Uttar Pradesh  (b) Bihar  (c) West Bengal  (d) Odisha
**Answer: (b) Bihar**
*Solution:* The ancient seat of learning, Nalanda Mahavihara, is located in the modern state of Bihar.

---

**Q13.** Which river is known as the "Sorrow of Bihar" due to its frequent flooding?
(a) Gandak  (b) Son  (c) Kosi  (d) Ganga
**Answer: (c) Kosi**
*Solution:* The Kosi River is historically called the "Sorrow of Bihar" because of its repeated devastating floods and shifting course.

---

**Q14.** In economics, which term refers to the total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's borders in a year?
(a) GNP  (b) GDP  (c) NNP  (d) Per Capita Income
**Answer: (b) GDP**
*Solution:* Gross Domestic Product (GDP) measures the market value of all final goods and services produced within a country's geographical boundaries in a given period.

---

**Q15.** Which gas is the most abundant component of Earth's atmosphere by volume?
(a) Oxygen  (b) Carbon dioxide  (c) Nitrogen  (d) Argon
**Answer: (c) Nitrogen**
*Solution:* Nitrogen makes up roughly 78% of the atmosphere by volume, far more than oxygen (~21%).

---

**Q16.** The chemical symbol "Na" stands for which element?
(a) Nitrogen  (b) Sodium  (c) Nickel  (d) Neon
**Answer: (b) Sodium**
*Solution:* The symbol Na comes from the Latin name "Natrium," which denotes sodium.

---

**Q17.** Who among the following is regarded as the "Father of the Indian Constitution"?
(a) B. R. Ambedkar  (b) Jawaharlal Nehru  (c) Rajendra Prasad  (d) Sardar Patel
**Answer: (a) B. R. Ambedkar**
*Solution:* Dr. B. R. Ambedkar, as Chairman of the Drafting Committee, is widely regarded as the chief architect and Father of the Indian Constitution.

---

**Q18.** The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?
(a) 6  (b) 7  (c) 8  (d) 9
**Answer: (c) 8**
*Solution:* The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight Indian states: Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, and Mizoram.

---

**Q19.** In which sport is the term "Grand Slam" associated with the four major championships including Wimbledon?
(a) Tennis  (b) Cricket  (c) Badminton  (d) Golf
**Answer: (a) Tennis**
*Solution:* In tennis, the Grand Slam refers to the four major tournaments: the Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, and US Open.

---

**Q20.** The "Jallianwala Bagh massacre" took place in which year?
(a) 1919  (b) 1916  (c) 1922  (d) 1930
**Answer: (a) 1919**
*Solution:* The Jallianwala Bagh massacre occurred on 13 April 1919 in Amritsar.

## Section C — Quantitative Aptitude (Q21–Q30)

**Q21.** The price of an article is increased by 25%. By what percentage must the new price be decreased to bring it back to the original price?
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 15%  (d) 22.5%
**Answer: (a) 20%**
*Solution:* Let original = 100, new = 125. Decrease needed = 25, so percentage = 25/125 × 100 = 20%.
🔑 *Trick:* For x% increase, reverse % = 100x/(100+x) = 2500/125 = 20%.

---

**Q22.** A shopkeeper sells an item for ₹460 and gains 15%. What was the cost price?
(a) ₹400  (b) ₹420  (c) ₹390  (d) ₹410
**Answer: (a) ₹400**
*Solution:* CP = SP / (1 + profit%) = 460 / 1.15 = ₹400.

---

**Q23.** Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 6% per annum for 3 years.
(a) ₹1,440  (b) ₹1,200  (c) ₹1,500  (d) ₹1,360
**Answer: (a) ₹1,440**
*Solution:* SI = (P × R × T)/100 = (8000 × 6 × 3)/100 = 144000/100 = ₹1,440.

---

**Q24.** Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years (compounded annually).
(a) ₹2,100  (b) ₹2,000  (c) ₹2,210  (d) ₹2,310
**Answer: (a) ₹2,100**
*Solution:* Amount = 10000 × (1.1)² = 10000 × 1.21 = ₹12,100. CI = 12100 − 10000 = ₹2,100.

---

**Q25.** A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, how many days will they take?
(a) 6 days  (b) 8 days  (c) 7.2 days  (d) 7.5 days
**Answer: (c) 7.2 days**
*Solution:* Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36 per day. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days.

---

**Q26.** A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 9 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
(a) 54 km/h  (b) 50 km/h  (c) 60 km/h  (d) 48 km/h
**Answer: (c) 60 km/h**
*Solution:* Speed = 150/9 m/s = 16.667 m/s. Convert: 16.667 × 18/5 = 60 km/h.
🔑 *Trick:* m/s → km/h: multiply by 18/5.

---

**Q27.** The average of five consecutive even numbers is 30. What is the largest number?
(a) 32  (b) 36  (c) 34  (d) 38
**Answer: (c) 34**
*Solution:* The middle (3rd) number equals the average = 30. The five numbers are 26, 28, 30, 32, 34; the largest is 34.

---

**Q28.** Two numbers are in the ratio 4 : 7 and their sum is 88. Find the larger number.
(a) 56  (b) 48  (c) 32  (d) 63
**Answer: (a) 56**
*Solution:* Total parts = 11, value per part = 88/11 = 8. Larger = 7 × 8 = 56.

---

**Q29.** The area of a circle is 154 cm². Find its radius. (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 5 cm  (b) 6 cm  (c) 7 cm  (d) 8 cm
**Answer: (c) 7 cm**
*Solution:* πr² = 154 → (22/7)r² = 154 → r² = 154 × 7/22 = 49 → r = 7 cm.

---

**Q30.** If x + 1/x = 5, find the value of x² + 1/x².
(a) 21  (b) 27  (c) 25  (d) 23
**Answer: (d) 23**
*Solution:* (x + 1/x)² = x² + 1/x² + 2, so 25 = x² + 1/x² + 2, giving x² + 1/x² = 23.
🔑 *Trick:* x² + 1/x² = (x + 1/x)² − 2.

## Section D — English Comprehension (Q31–Q40)

**Q31.** Select the word that is most nearly SYNONYMOUS with the given word: **ABUNDANT**
(a) Scarce  (b) Hidden  (c) Tiny  (d) Plentiful
**Answer: (d) Plentiful**
*Solution:* "Abundant" means existing in large quantity, which matches "plentiful." "Scarce" is the opposite.

---

**Q32.** Select the word that is the ANTONYM of the given word: **GENEROUS**
(a) Kind  (b) Lavish  (c) Stingy  (d) Helpful
**Answer: (c) Stingy**
*Solution:* "Generous" means giving freely; its opposite is "stingy," meaning unwilling to give or spend.

---

**Q33.** Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
She has been working here ____ 2015.
(a) for  (b) by  (c) from  (d) since
**Answer: (d) since**
*Solution:* With a specific point in time (2015) in the present perfect continuous, "since" is correct; "for" is used with durations.

---

**Q34.** Select the correctly spelt word.
(a) Occassion  (b) Ocasion  (c) Occasion  (d) Occasionn
**Answer: (c) Occasion**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "Occasion" — double 'c', single 's', single 'n'.

---

**Q35.** Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error.
"One of the boys (a)/ have forgotten (b)/ his umbrella (c)/ in the bus. (d)"
(a) part (a)  (b) part (d)  (c) part (c)  (d) part (b)
**Answer: (d) part (b)**
*Solution:* "One of the boys" takes a singular verb, so "have" should be "has." The error is in part (b).

---

**Q36.** Select the option that best expresses the sentence in PASSIVE voice.
"The teacher praised the students."
(a) The students are praised by the teacher.  (b) The students have been praised by the teacher.  (c) The students praised the teacher.  (d) The students were praised by the teacher.
**Answer: (d) The students were praised by the teacher.**
*Solution:* Simple past active "praised" becomes "were praised" in the passive, keeping the past tense.

---

**Q37.** Select the correct meaning of the idiom: **"To bite the bullet"**
(a) To eat quickly  (b) To waste money  (c) To get injured  (d) To face a difficult situation bravely
**Answer: (d) To face a difficult situation bravely**
*Solution:* "To bite the bullet" means to endure a painful or unpleasant situation with courage.

---

**Q38.** Select the most appropriate article to fill in the blank.
He is ____ honest man whom everyone respects.
(a) a  (b) no article  (c) the  (d) an
**Answer: (d) an**
*Solution:* "Honest" begins with a silent 'h', producing a vowel sound, so the article "an" is used.

---

**Q39.** Select the ONE-WORD substitution for: "A person who cannot read or write."
(a) Illegible  (b) Innocent  (c) Ignorant  (d) Illiterate
**Answer: (d) Illiterate**
*Solution:* "Illiterate" describes a person unable to read or write. "Illegible" refers to handwriting that cannot be read.

---

**Q40.** Select the correct INDIRECT (reported) speech.
He said, "I am busy now."
(a) He said that he is busy then.  (b) He says that he was busy then.  (c) He said that I was busy now.  (d) He said that he was busy then.
**Answer: (d) He said that he was busy then.**
*Solution:* In reported speech, "am" changes to "was" and "now" changes to "then" with the reporting verb in the past.
