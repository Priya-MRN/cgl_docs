## Section A — General Intelligence & Reasoning (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** Select the option that is related to the third term in the same way the second term is related to the first term.
Painter : Brush :: Tailor : ?
(a) Cloth  (b) Needle  (c) Shop  (d) Thread
**Answer: (b) Needle**
*Solution:* A painter works using a brush as the primary tool; similarly, a tailor works using a needle as the primary tool. Cloth and thread are materials, not the defining tool.

---

**Q2.** Select the number that will come next in the series.
3, 7, 16, 35, 74, ?
(a) 142  (b) 149  (c) 153  (d) 158
**Answer: (c) 153**
*Solution:* Each term follows ×2 + an increasing odd: 3×2+1=7, 7×2+2=16, 16×2+3=35, 35×2+4=74, 74×2+5=153.
🔑 *Trick:* Pattern is ×2 then add 1,2,3,4,5 in turn.

---

**Q3.** In a certain code language, "MORE" is written as "NPSF". How is "TIME" written in that code?
(a) UJNF  (b) UKNF  (c) SJND  (d) UJOF
**Answer: (a) UJNF**
*Solution:* Each letter shifts forward by 1: M→N, O→P, R→S, E→F. Apply to TIME: T→U, I→J, M→N, E→F = UJNF.

---

**Q4.** Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of the only son of my grandfather." How is the woman in the photograph related to the man?
(a) Mother  (b) Aunt  (c) Sister  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (c) Sister**
*Solution:* The only son of the man's grandfather is the man's father. The father's daughter is the man's sister.

---

**Q5.** Two statements are followed by two conclusions. Assuming the statements true, decide which conclusion(s) logically follow.
Statements: All pens are pencils. Some pencils are erasers.
Conclusions: (I) Some erasers are pens. (II) Some pencils are pens.
(a) Only I follows  (b) Both follow  (c) Neither follows  (d) Only II follows
**Answer: (d) Only II follows**
*Solution:* "All pens are pencils" converts to "Some pencils are pens," so II follows. No definite link exists between erasers and pens, so I does not follow.

---

**Q6.** A man walks 8 km North, turns right and walks 6 km, then turns right and walks 8 km. How far and in which direction is he from the starting point?
(a) 6 km East  (b) 6 km West  (c) 14 km East  (d) 8 km South
**Answer: (a) 6 km East**
*Solution:* North 8 then East 6 then South 8 returns to the starting latitude, 6 km to the East of the start.
🔑 *Trick:* Equal opposite legs (N8, S8) cancel; only the 6 km East remains.

---

**Q7.** Select the odd one out.
(a) 27  (b) 64  (c) 100  (d) 125
**Answer: (c) 100**
*Solution:* 27=3³, 64=4³, 125=5³ are perfect cubes, but 100=10² is a perfect square, not a cube.

---

**Q8.** How many triangles are there in a figure formed by a triangle with both of its base-vertices joined to a single point on the opposite side (one cevian drawn from the apex to the base)?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 5
**Answer: (b) 3**
*Solution:* A single cevian from the apex to the base splits the large triangle into 2 smaller triangles; together with the original whole triangle, the total is 3.

---

**Q9.** If "+" means "÷", "−" means "×", "×" means "+", and "÷" means "−", find the value of:
16 + 4 − 3 × 5 ÷ 2
(a) 13  (b) 17  (c) 19  (d) 15
**Answer: (d) 15**
*Solution:* Replace symbols: 16 ÷ 4 × 3 + 5 − 2 = 4 × 3 + 5 − 2 = 12 + 5 − 2 = 15.

---

**Q10.** Select the letter cluster that will replace the question mark.
AZ, CX, EV, GT, ?
(a) IR  (b) HR  (c) IS  (d) JR
**Answer: (a) IR**
*Solution:* First letters skip one forward: A, C, E, G, I. Second letters skip one backward: Z, X, V, T, R. So the next cluster is IR.

## Section B — General Awareness (Q11–Q20)

**Q11.** Which Article of the Indian Constitution abolishes untouchability?
(a) Article 17  (b) Article 14  (c) Article 19  (d) Article 21
**Answer: (a) Article 17**
*Solution:* Article 17 of the Constitution abolishes "untouchability" and forbids its practice in any form.

---

**Q12.** The Battle of Plassey, which laid the foundation of British political power in India, was fought in which year?
(a) 1757  (b) 1764  (c) 1707  (d) 1775
**Answer: (a) 1757**
*Solution:* The Battle of Plassey was fought in 1757 between the British East India Company and the Nawab of Bengal.

---

**Q13.** Which is the longest river that flows entirely within India?
(a) Godavari  (b) Krishna  (c) Narmada  (d) Mahanadi
**Answer: (a) Godavari**
*Solution:* The Godavari, often called "Dakshin Ganga," is the longest river flowing entirely within Indian territory.

**Q14.** In economics, the term "Repo Rate" refers to the rate at which:
(a) Commercial banks lend to the public
(b) Banks deposit funds with the RBI
(c) The government borrows from foreign markets
(d) The RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks
**Answer: (d) The RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks**
*Solution:* The repo rate is the interest rate at which the Reserve Bank of India provides short-term funds to commercial banks against securities.

---

**Q15.** Which gas is most responsible for the greenhouse effect and global warming due to human activity?
(a) Oxygen  (b) Nitrogen  (c) Carbon dioxide  (d) Hydrogen
**Answer: (c) Carbon dioxide**
*Solution:* Carbon dioxide released from burning fossil fuels is the principal anthropogenic greenhouse gas driving global warming.

---

**Q16.** The chemical symbol "Na" represents which element?
(a) Sodium  (b) Nickel  (c) Nitrogen  (d) Neon
**Answer: (a) Sodium**
*Solution:* "Na" comes from the Latin name *natrium*, the chemical symbol for sodium.

**Q17.** The Hornbill Festival, a famous cultural festival, is celebrated in which Indian state?
(a) Assam  (b) Manipur  (c) Nagaland  (d) Mizoram
**Answer: (c) Nagaland**
*Solution:* The Hornbill Festival is an annual cultural festival held in Nagaland, showcasing its tribal heritage.

**Q18.** Who among the following is conferred the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award?
(a) Decided by the Prime Minister alone
(b) Conferred by the President of India
(c) Awarded by the Chief Justice of India
(d) Granted by the Vice-President
**Answer: (b) Conferred by the President of India**
*Solution:* The Bharat Ratna is conferred by the President of India, typically on recommendations made to the Prime Minister.

**Q19.** In which sport is the term "Silly Point" used?
(a) Cricket  (b) Hockey  (c) Football  (d) Tennis
**Answer: (a) Cricket**
*Solution:* "Silly Point" is a close-in fielding position in the game of cricket.

**Q20.** Which mountain pass connects India and China and is located in Sikkim?
(a) Khyber Pass  (b) Nathula Pass  (c) Rohtang Pass  (d) Zoji La

## Section C — Quantitative Aptitude (Q21–Q30)

**Q21.** A shopkeeper marks an item 40% above cost and gives a 25% discount. What is his profit percent?
(a) 5%  (b) 10%  (c) 12.5%  (d) 15%
**Answer: (a) 5%**
*Solution:* Let CP = 100. MP = 140. SP = 140 × 0.75 = 105. Profit = 105 − 100 = 5, so profit percent = 5%.
🔑 *Trick:* Net factor = 1.40 × 0.75 = 1.05 → 5% profit.

---

**Q22.** If 35% of a number is 84, what is 60% of the same number?
(a) 124  (b) 132  (c) 144  (d) 156
**Answer: (c) 144**
*Solution:* Number = 84 ÷ 0.35 = 240. Then 60% of 240 = 144.

---

**Q23.** A sum of ₹8,000 is invested at 10% per annum compound interest. What is the compound interest after 2 years?
(a) ₹1,600  (b) ₹1,720  (c) ₹1,760  (d) ₹1,680
**Answer: (d) ₹1,680**
*Solution:* Amount = 8000 × (1.1)² = 8000 × 1.21 = 9680. CI = 9680 − 8000 = 1680.
🔑 *Trick:* For 2 years, CI% = 10 + 10 + (10×10)/100 = 21%; 21% of 8000 = 1680.

---

**Q24.** A can do a piece of work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish the work?
(a) 6.5 days  (b) 7 days  (c) 7.2 days  (d) 8 days
**Answer: (c) 7.2 days**
*Solution:* Combined one-day work = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days.

---

**Q25.** A train 180 m long crosses a pole in 9 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?
(a) 64  (b) 68  (c) 72  (d) 76
**Answer: (c) 72**
*Solution:* Speed = 180/9 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/h.
🔑 *Trick:* Multiply m/s by 18/5 to get km/h.

---

**Q26.** The average of 5 consecutive even numbers is 24. What is the largest of these numbers?
(a) 26  (b) 28  (c) 30  (d) 32
**Answer: (b) 28**
*Solution:* For consecutive numbers the average equals the middle term, so the middle number is 24. The numbers are 20, 22, 24, 26, 28; the largest is 28.

---

**Q27.** Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7. If their sum is 96, what is the larger number?
(a) 40  (b) 48  (c) 54  (d) 56
**Answer: (d) 56**
*Solution:* Total parts = 5 + 7 = 12; one part = 96/12 = 8. Larger = 7 × 8 = 56.

---

**Q28.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. What is the measure of the largest angle?
(a) 60°  (b) 70°  (c) 80°  (d) 90°
**Answer: (c) 80°**
*Solution:* Total parts = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9; one part = 180/9 = 20°. Largest = 4 × 20 = 80°.

---

**Q29.** The radius of a circle is 14 cm. What is its area? (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 596 cm²  (b) 616 cm²  (c) 628 cm²  (d) 644 cm²
**Answer: (b) 616 cm²**
*Solution:* Area = πr² = (22/7) × 14 × 14 = 22 × 28 = 616 cm².

---

**Q30.** If x + 1/x = 5, what is the value of x² + 1/x²?
(a) 21  (b) 25  (c) 27  (d) 23
**Answer: (d) 23**
*Solution:* (x + 1/x)² = x² + 1/x² + 2, so 25 = x² + 1/x² + 2, giving x² + 1/x² = 23.
🔑 *Trick:* Square the given value and subtract 2.

## Section D — English Comprehension (Q31–Q40)

**Q31.** Select the word most similar in meaning (synonym) to the word: **ABUNDANT**
(a) Scarce  (b) Plentiful  (c) Tiny  (d) Hidden
**Answer: (b) Plentiful**
*Solution:* "Abundant" means existing in large quantity, which matches "plentiful." "Scarce" is its opposite.

---

**Q32.** Select the word most opposite in meaning (antonym) to the word: **GENEROUS**
(a) Kind  (b) Wealthy  (c) Stingy  (d) Honest
**Answer: (c) Stingy**
*Solution:* "Generous" means giving freely; its antonym is "stingy," meaning unwilling to give or spend.

---

**Q33.** Select the option that correctly fills the blank.
She has been working here ____ 2015.
(a) for  (b) from  (c) until  (d) since
**Answer: (d) since**
*Solution:* "Since" is used with a specific point in time (2015) in the present perfect continuous tense.

---

**Q34.** Select the correctly spelt word.
(a) Embarass  (b) Embaras  (c) Embarras  (d) Embarrass
**Answer: (d) Embarrass**
*Solution:* The correct spelling has double "r" and double "s": E-m-b-a-r-r-a-s-s.

---

**Q35.** Identify the part of the sentence that contains an error.
"One of the students (a)/ have submitted (b)/ the assignment (c)/ on time. (d)"
(a) Part (a)  (b) Part (b)  (c) Part (c)  (d) Part (d)
**Answer: (b) Part (b)**
*Solution:* "One of the students" is singular, so the verb must be "has submitted," not "have submitted."

---

**Q36.** Select the correct passive voice form of the sentence:
"The teacher praised the student."
(a) The student was praised by the teacher.
(b) The student is praised by the teacher.
(c) The student has praised the teacher.
(d) The student was being praised the teacher.
**Answer: (a) The student was praised by the teacher.**
*Solution:* Simple past active becomes "was/were + past participle." The object "the student" becomes the subject.

---

**Q37.** Select the correct meaning of the idiom: **"To bite the bullet"**
(a) To eat quickly
(b) To make a careless mistake
(c) To argue loudly
(d) To face a difficult situation bravely
**Answer: (d) To face a difficult situation bravely**
*Solution:* "Bite the bullet" means to endure a painful or unpleasant situation with courage.

---

**Q38.** Fill in the blank with the correct article.
He is ____ honest officer respected by everyone.
(a) a  (b) an  (c) the  (d) no article
**Answer: (b) an**
*Solution:* "Honest" begins with a silent "h," so the word starts with a vowel sound, requiring "an."

---

**Q39.** Select the one word for the given phrase:
"A person who cannot read or write."
(a) Illegible  (b) Illiterate  (c) Ineligible  (d) Illegal
**Answer: (b) Illiterate**
*Solution:* "Illiterate" means unable to read or write. "Illegible" describes handwriting that cannot be read.

---

**Q40.** Select the correct indirect (reported) speech form:
He said, "I am busy now."
(a) He said that he is busy then.
(b) He said that I was busy now.
(c) He says that he was busy then.
(d) He said that he was busy then.
**Answer: (d) He said that he was busy then.**
*Solution:* In reported speech, present tense shifts to past ("am" → "was") and "now" changes to "then."
