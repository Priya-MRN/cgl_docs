### Answers & Explanations — Reasoning

**Q1. (b)** All pencils are erasers; some erasers are sharpeners — but the sharpeners may belong to the eraser part that is not pencils, so "some pencils are sharpeners" is not certain. Conclusion II ("some sharpeners are erasers") is the valid converse of "some erasers are sharpeners," so only II follows.

**Q2. (c)** All doctors are graduates and all graduates are voters, so all doctors are voters (I follows). Since doctors exist within voters, "some voters are doctors" is also valid (II follows). Both follow.

**Q3. (c)** Pattern: ×2 then +1, +2, +3, +4, +5. 3×2+1=7, 7×2+2=16, 16×2+3=35, 35×2+4=74, 74×2+5=153.

**Q4. (d)** Each term = previous ×2 + 1. 5×2+1=11, 11×2+1=23, 23×2+1=47, 47×2+1=95, 95×2+1=191.

**Q5. (b)** Each cluster advances by 4 letters: B(2)D(4) → F(6)H(8) → J(10)L(12) → N(14)P(16) → R(18)T(20) = RT.

**Q6. (a)** The shift pattern is +1, +2, +3, +4: F+1=G, R+2=T, O+3=R, G+4=K → GTRK. Apply to BIRD: B+1=C, I+2=K, R+3=U, D+4=H → CKUH.

**Q7. (d)** Each letter is replaced by its alphabet position number: D=4, E=5, S=19, K=11 → 4-5-19-11.

**Q8. (b)** Sum of positional values: W=23, O=15, R=18, K=11. 23+15+18+11 = 67.

**Q9. (c)** Pattern: n² + 1. 8² + 1 = 65. So 11² + 1 = 121 + 1 = 122.

**Q10. (a)** The shift pattern is +1, +2, +3: D+1=E, O+2=Q, G+3=J → EQJ. Apply to CAT: C+1=D, A+2=C, T+3=W → DCW.

**Q11. (b)** A pen is used to write; a knife is used to cut. The relationship is object-to-its-primary-function, so "Cut" fits.

**Q12. (d)** 36, 49, 64 are perfect squares (6², 7², 8²). 56 is not a perfect square — odd one out.

**Q13. (c)** Triangle, square and rectangle are figures made of straight lines (polygons). A circle is a curved figure with no straight sides — odd one out.

**Q14. (a)** "The only son of my grandfather" is the man's father. The daughter of the man's father is the man's sister.

**Q15. (b)** P is the son of Q; Q is the daughter of R; so R is P's grandparent. R is male (father of S), so R is P's grandfather, which makes P the grandson of R.

**Q16. (a)** Start facing North, walk 5 km N. Turn right → face East, walk 3 km. Turn right → face South, walk 5 km. The two 5 km legs (N then S) cancel, leaving 3 km East of start; he is now facing South and is 3 km away. Direction faced = South, distance = 3 km.

**Q17. (a)** East 4 m, turn left → North 3 m, turn left → West 4 m. The East and West 4 m legs cancel, leaving 3 m North of the start. So 3 m, North.

**Q18. (c)** Substitute: + → ×, × → −, − → ÷, ÷ → +. Expression 12 + 4 − 2 ÷ 6 × 3 becomes 12 × 4 ÷ 2 + 6 − 3 = (48 ÷ 2) + 6 − 3 = 24 + 6 − 3 = 27.

**Q19. (a)** Try ÷, ×, =: 18 ÷ 3 × 2 = 6 × 2 = 12 = 12. Balanced.

**Q20. (b)** Order facing North (left to right). T is left of P, P left of Q, R right of Q, S at extreme right: T, P, Q, R, S. The middle (3rd of 5) is Q.

**Q21. (c)** In each row, first × second = third: 4×6=24, 5×3=15, 7×8=56.
