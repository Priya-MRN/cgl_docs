## Section A — General Intelligence & Reasoning (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** Select the option that is related to the third term in the same way as the second term is related to the first term. Painter : Brush :: Tailor : ?
(a) Cloth  (b) Shop  (c) Thread  (d) Needle
**Answer: (d) Needle**
*Solution:* A painter's primary working tool is a brush; a tailor's primary working tool is a needle. Cloth and thread are materials, not the tool.

**Q2.** Select the number that will come next in the series: 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?
(a) 189  (b) 190  (c) 191  (d) 192
**Answer: (c) 191**
*Solution:* Each term follows the rule ×2 + 1: 5→11, 11→23, 23→47, 47→95, 95×2+1 = 191.
🔑 *Trick:* When gaps double (6,12,24,48,96), the rule is usually ×2 then +1.

**Q3.** In a certain code language, "LAMP" is written as "MBNQ". How is "DESK" written in that code?
(a) EFTL  (b) CDRJ  (c) EFTM  (d) EGTL
**Answer: (a) EFTL**
*Solution:* Each letter is shifted +1: D→E, E→F, S→T, K→L, giving EFTL.

**Q4.** Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Mother  (b) Sister  (c) Grandmother  (d) Aunt
**Answer: (a) Mother**
*Solution:* "The only daughter of my mother" is the woman herself. So the man's mother is the woman; she is his mother.

**Q5.** Select the odd one out.
(a) 64  (b) 125  (c) 216  (d) 100
**Answer: (d) 100**
*Solution:* 64 = 4³, 125 = 5³, 216 = 6³ are perfect cubes; 100 = 10² is not a perfect cube.

**Q6.** Statements: All pens are inks. Some inks are blue. Conclusions: I. Some pens are blue. II. Some inks are pens. Which conclusion follows?
(a) Only I  (b) Both I and II  (c) Neither I nor II  (d) Only II
**Answer: (d) Only II**
*Solution:* "All pens are inks" converts to "Some inks are pens," so II follows. No definite link exists between pens and blue, so I does not follow.

**Q7.** A man walks 8 km North, turns right and walks 6 km, then turns right and walks 8 km. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 6 km  (b) 8 km  (c) 14 km  (d) 22 km
**Answer: (a) 6 km**
*Solution:* North 8, East 6, South 8: the North and South cancel, leaving 6 km East of the start.
🔑 *Trick:* Equal opposite legs cancel; the answer is the leftover perpendicular distance.

**Q8.** Arrange the following words in the order they appear in an English dictionary: 1. Garden 2. Garage 3. Gather 4. Gallon
(a) 4, 2, 1, 3  (b) 2, 4, 1, 3  (c) 4, 1, 2, 3  (d) 2, 1, 4, 3
**Answer: (a) 4, 2, 1, 3**
*Solution:* Compare third letters: Gallon (l), Garage/Garden (r), Gather (t). Among "Gar-", Garage (a) before Garden (d). Order: Gallon, Garage, Garden, Gather.

**Q9.** How many triangles are there in a figure formed by a single square with both of its diagonals drawn?
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 10
**Answer: (c) 8**
*Solution:* Two diagonals create 4 small triangles, plus 4 larger triangles each formed by two halves (each side with the two half-diagonals): total 8.

**Q10.** If "+" means "×", "×" means "−", "−" means "÷", and "÷" means "+", then what is the value of 12 + 4 − 2 ÷ 6?
(a) 28  (b) 32  (c) 36  (d) 30
**Answer: (d) 30**
*Solution:* Replace symbols: 12 × 4 ÷ 2 + 6 = 48 ÷ 2 + 6 = 24 + 6 = 30.

## Section B — General Awareness (Q11–Q20)

**Q11.** Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the abolition of untouchability?
(a) Article 14  (b) Article 17  (c) Article 19  (d) Article 21
**Answer: (b) Article 17**
*Solution:* Article 17 abolishes untouchability and forbids its practice in any form, making it a punishable offence.

**Q12.** The Battle of Plassey (1757) was fought between the British East India Company and the Nawab of Bengal. Who was the defeated Nawab?
(a) Mir Jafar  (b) Mir Qasim  (c) Siraj-ud-Daulah  (d) Shuja-ud-Daulah
**Answer: (c) Siraj-ud-Daulah**
*Solution:* Siraj-ud-Daulah was defeated by Robert Clive at Plassey, largely due to the betrayal of his commander Mir Jafar.

**Q13.** Which is the longest river flowing entirely within India?
(a) Godavari  (b) Krishna  (c) Narmada  (d) Mahanadi
**Answer: (a) Godavari**
*Solution:* The Godavari, often called the "Dakshin Ganga," is the longest river that lies entirely within India.

**Q14.** In economics, "fiscal deficit" refers to the excess of total expenditure over total receipts excluding which of the following?
(a) Tax revenue  (b) Non-tax revenue  (c) Borrowings  (d) Disinvestment
**Answer: (c) Borrowings**
*Solution:* Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − total receipts (excluding borrowings). It indicates the total borrowing requirement of the government.

**Q15.** Which gas is most responsible for the formation of acid rain along with sulphur dioxide?
(a) Carbon dioxide  (b) Methane  (c) Nitrogen oxides  (d) Ozone
**Answer: (c) Nitrogen oxides**
*Solution:* Oxides of nitrogen (NOₓ) and sulphur dioxide (SO₂) dissolve in rainwater to form nitric and sulphuric acids, causing acid rain.

**Q16.** The chemical formula of washing soda is:
(a) NaHCO₃  (b) CaOCl₂  (c) NaOH  (d) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O
**Answer: (d) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O**
*Solution:* Washing soda is hydrated sodium carbonate, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O. NaHCO₃ is baking soda.

**Q17.** Which of these classical dance forms originates from the state of Kerala?
(a) Kuchipudi  (b) Odissi  (c) Kathakali  (d) Bharatanatyam
**Answer: (c) Kathakali**
*Solution:* Kathakali is a classical dance-drama form from Kerala known for elaborate costumes and facial make-up.

**Q18.** The Durand Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey  (b) Football  (c) Badminton  (d) Cricket
**Answer: (b) Football**
*Solution:* The Durand Cup is one of the oldest football tournaments in Asia, first held in 1888.

**Q19.** Who among the following is the constitutional head of a state in India?
(a) Chief Minister  (b) Governor  (c) Chief Justice of High Court  (d) Speaker
**Answer: (b) Governor**
*Solution:* The Governor is the constitutional (nominal) head of a state; the Chief Minister is the real executive head.

**Q20.** The "Nobel Prize" in which field is NOT awarded by a Swedish institution but by a Norwegian committee?
(a) Literature  (b) Physics  (c) Peace  (d) Chemistry
**Answer: (c) Peace**
*Solution:* The Nobel Peace Prize is awarded by the Norwegian Nobel Committee in Oslo, while the others are awarded in Sweden.

## Section C — Quantitative Aptitude (Q21–Q30)

**Q21.** A number is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. What is the net percentage change?
(a) No change  (b) 5% increase  (c) 5% decrease  (d) 10% increase
**Answer: (a) No change**
*Solution:* Net factor = 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.00, so the value is unchanged (0% change).
🔑 *Trick:* A 25% rise and 20% fall (since 1/4 up, 1/5 down) cancel exactly.

**Q22.** A shopkeeper sells an article for ₹920 at a profit of 15%. What was the cost price?
(a) ₹800  (b) ₹780  (c) ₹820  (d) ₹850
**Answer: (a) ₹800**
*Solution:* CP = 920 / 1.15 = ₹800. Check: 800 × 1.15 = 920.

**Q23.** Find the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually.
(a) ₹2,000  (b) ₹2,200  (c) ₹1,900  (d) ₹2,100
**Answer: (d) ₹2,100**
*Solution:* Amount = 10000 × (1.1)² = 10000 × 1.21 = ₹12,100. CI = 12,100 − 10,000 = ₹2,100.
🔑 *Trick:* For 2 years CI% = 2r + r²/100 = 20 + 1 = 21% of principal.

**Q24.** A can finish a piece of work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days will they complete it?
(a) 7.2 days  (b) 6.8 days  (c) 7.5 days  (d) 8 days
**Answer: (a) 7.2 days**
*Solution:* Combined one-day work = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days.

**Q25.** A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 9 seconds. What is its speed in km/h?
(a) 54 km/h  (b) 60 km/h  (c) 50 km/h  (d) 45 km/h
**Answer: (b) 60 km/h**
*Solution:* Speed = 150/9 m/s = 16.67 m/s. In km/h: 16.67 × 18/5 = 60 km/h.
🔑 *Trick:* m/s → km/h: multiply by 18/5.

**Q26.** The average of five consecutive even numbers is 32. What is the largest of these numbers?
(a) 34  (b) 38  (c) 36  (d) 40
**Answer: (c) 36**
*Solution:* The middle (3rd) number equals the average = 32. The five numbers are 28, 30, 32, 34, 36; the largest is 36.

**Q27.** Two numbers are in the ratio 5 : 7 and their sum is 96. Find the smaller number.
(a) 35  (b) 40  (c) 42  (d) 56
**Answer: (b) 40**
*Solution:* Total parts = 12; one part = 96/12 = 8. Smaller = 5 × 8 = 40.

**Q28.** The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find the largest angle.
(a) 40°  (b) 60°  (c) 80°  (d) 90°
**Answer: (c) 80°**
*Solution:* Total parts = 9; one part = 180/9 = 20°. Largest = 4 × 20 = 80°.

**Q29.** Find the area of a circle whose circumference is 44 cm. (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 154 cm²  (b) 144 cm²  (c) 164 cm²  (d) 121 cm²
**Answer: (a) 154 cm²**
*Solution:* 2πr = 44 ⇒ r = 44 × 7 / (2 × 22) = 7 cm. Area = πr² = (22/7) × 49 = 154 cm².

**Q30.** If x + 1/x = 4, find the value of x² + 1/x².
(a) 12  (b) 14  (c) 16  (d) 18
**Answer: (b) 14**
*Solution:* (x + 1/x)² = x² + 1/x² + 2 ⇒ 16 = x² + 1/x² + 2 ⇒ x² + 1/x² = 14.
🔑 *Trick:* x² + 1/x² = (x + 1/x)² − 2.

## Section D — English Comprehension (Q31–Q40)

**Q31.** Select the word most nearly SIMILAR in meaning (synonym) to the word: ABUNDANT
(a) Scarce  (b) Plentiful  (c) Empty  (d) Rare
**Answer: (b) Plentiful**
*Solution:* "Abundant" means existing in large quantities, i.e., plentiful. The others mean the opposite.

**Q32.** Select the word OPPOSITE in meaning (antonym) to the word: CONCEAL
(a) Hide  (b) Cover  (c) Reveal  (d) Bury
**Answer: (c) Reveal**
*Solution:* "Conceal" means to hide; its antonym is "reveal," meaning to make known.

**Q33.** Fill in the blank with the most appropriate word: She has been working here ____ 2015.
(a) for  (b) from  (c) by  (d) since
**Answer: (d) since**
*Solution:* With a point of time (2015) in the present perfect continuous, we use "since." "For" is used with a period of time.

**Q34.** Select the correctly spelt word.
(a) Definitely  (b) Definately  (c) Definitly  (d) Defenitely
**Answer: (a) Definitely**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "definitely" — root "finite," with no "a."

**Q35.** Identify the part that contains an error: "One of the boys / have not / submitted / his assignment."
(a) One of the boys  (b) have not  (c) submitted  (d) his assignment
**Answer: (b) have not**
*Solution:* The subject "One of the boys" is singular, so the verb should be "has not," not "have not."

**Q36.** Select the correct passive form: "The teacher praised the students."
(a) The students are praised by the teacher.  (b) The students praised the teacher.  (c) The students had been praised by the teacher.  (d) The students were praised by the teacher.
**Answer: (d) The students were praised by the teacher.**
*Solution:* Simple past active → simple past passive: object becomes subject, verb becomes "were + praised (past participle)."

**Q37.** Select the correct meaning of the idiom: "To bite the dust."
(a) To eat hurriedly  (b) To fail or be defeated  (c) To clean a surface  (d) To start afresh
**Answer: (b) To fail or be defeated**
*Solution:* "To bite the dust" idiomatically means to be defeated, to fail, or to die.

**Q38.** Fill in the blank with the correct article: He is ____ honest officer.
(a) a  (b) the  (c) no article  (d) an
**Answer: (d) an**
*Solution:* "Honest" begins with a silent "h," so the word starts with a vowel sound, requiring "an."

**Q39.** Select the one-word substitute for: "A person who speaks many languages."
(a) Linguist  (b) Polyglot  (c) Bilingual  (d) Orator
**Answer: (b) Polyglot**
*Solution:* A "polyglot" is a person who knows and uses several languages. A linguist studies language; bilingual means two languages only.

**Q40.** Select the correct indirect (reported) speech: He said, "I am tired."
(a) He said that he is tired.  (b) He says that he was tired.  (c) He said that I was tired.  (d) He said that he was tired.
**Answer: (d) He said that he was tired.**
*Solution:* In reported speech after "said," present tense "am" changes to past "was," and the pronoun "I" becomes "he."
