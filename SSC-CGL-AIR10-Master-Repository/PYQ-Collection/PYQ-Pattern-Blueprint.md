## 1. General Awareness

**History** — skip random dates.

| 🔴 Buddhism & Jainism | Teachings, councils, patrons |
|---|---|
| 🔴 Mauryan Empire | Ashoka & edicts, Chandragupta, Arthashastra |
| 🔴 Gupta Empire | Literature, science, administration |
| 🟡 Bhakti Movement | Saints, philosophies, impact |
| 🔴 Mughal **Administration** | Revenue, Mansabdari (not wars) |
| 🔴 Modern History | Freedom Movement (INC sessions, Swadeshi, NCM, CDM, Quit India), **Revolt of 1857**, Important Acts |

**Geography (NCERT basics)** — 🔴 Rivers · 🔴 Mountains · 🟡 National Parks · 🔴 Agriculture · 🔴 Climate/Monsoon · 🟡 Soil types

**Polity** — 🔴 Fundamental Rights/Duties · 🔴 DPSP · 🟡 Parliament · 🟡 President/PM · 🔴 Amendments (**42, 44, 73, 74, 86**)

**Science (Class 9–10 level)** — 🔴 Physics (Motion, Sound, Light, Electricity) · 🔴 Chemistry (Acids/Bases, Metals) · 🔴 Biology (Human systems, Vitamins/Diseases, Nutrition)

**Current Affairs** — only 5 things: 🔴 Awards · 🔴 Govt Schemes · 🔴 Sports · 🟡 Important Days · 🟡 Int'l Organizations. *Skip generic news.*

## 2. English — Grammar, not vocabulary

**8 core grammar topics:** 🔴 Tenses · 🔴 Articles · 🔴 Prepositions · 🟡 Conjunctions · 🔴 Pronouns · 🔴 **Subject-Verb Agreement** (most Qs) · 🔴 Voice · 🔴 Narration

**Error Detection** — errors hide in 5 spots: Articles, Tenses, Pronouns, Prepositions, S-V Agreement. *Read for logic, don't memorize.*

**Vocabulary** — 🟡 Synonyms/Antonyms · 🟡 One-Word · 🟢 Idioms (standard only). Maintain a notebook of words you forget.

**Cloze & RC** — 🔴 ~80% grammar + context; RC answers are inside the passage.

→ Drill: [PYQ_English.md](PYQ_English.md) · [English/](../English/) · [Grammar Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/English_Grammar_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## 3. Maths

**Arithmetic — 40–50% of the section. Master this first.**

🔴 Percentage · 🔴 Profit & Loss · 🔴 Ratio & Proportion · 🔴 Average · 🔴 SI/CI · 🔴 **Mixture & Alligation** · 🟡 Partnership

**Algebra** (rising weightage) — 🔴 Algebraic identities (x + 1/x) · 🔴 Linear/Quadratic equations · 🟡 Surds & Indices

**Geometry** (properties, not proofs) — 🔴 Triangles · 🔴 Circles · 🟡 Lines & Angles

**Mensuration** — fixed formulas = free marks. Cube, cuboid, cylinder, cone, sphere.

**Time-Work & Speed** — 🔴 Time & Work · 🔴 Pipes · 🔴 Boats & Streams · 🔴 Trains (one concept, many stories)

→ Drill: [PYQ_Quant.md](PYQ_Quant.md) · [Formula-Sheets/](../Formula-Sheets/) · [Speed-Book/](../Speed-Book/)

## 4. Reasoning — most scoring, most predictable

🔴 Analogy & Classification · 🔴 Series (Number/Alphabet — practice ~200 Qs) · 🔴 Coding-Decoding · 🔴 Blood Relations · 🔴 Direction Sense · 🔴 Puzzles & Seating · 🟡 Non-Verbal (Mirror/Water image, Paper folding)

*Blood Relations + Direction = easiest marks (<2 min). Non-Verbal = 0 memorization, pure visual thinking.*

## 5. Topper's Method

- **PYQs = the exam's blueprint.** Analyze them to learn the examiner's mindset — not just practice.
- **Smart revision:** revise old notes 10× instead of chasing new resources.
- **Error notebook:** log every mistake, revise weekly.
- **Measure progress by mock scores**, not hours or lectures.

**Homework:** Pick your weakest subject → pull 3 years of its PYQs → solve, understand the logic, note the pattern.

> Don't study more. Study exactly what SSC repeats — selection always comes from there.
