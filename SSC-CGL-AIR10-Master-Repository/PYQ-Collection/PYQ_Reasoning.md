## 1. Analogy

**Q1.** [🔴 High Repeat] CAT : DBU :: DOG : ?
(a) EPH  (b) EPI  (c) FPH  (d) EQH
**Answer: (a) EPH**
*Explanation:* Each letter +1: C→D, A→B, T→U gives DBU. So D→E, O→P, G→H = EPH.
*Topic:* Analogy (Letter) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q2.** [🔴 High Repeat] 6 : 42 :: 8 : ?
(a) 72  (b) 64  (c) 56  (d) 80
**Answer: (a) 72**
*Explanation:* Pattern is n × (n+1): 6 × 7 = 42, so 8 × 9 = 72.
*Topic:* Analogy (Number) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q3.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
(a) School  (b) Student  (c) Books  (d) Lesson
**Answer: (a) School**
*Explanation:* A doctor works in a hospital; a teacher works in a school (worker : workplace).
*Topic:* Analogy (Semantic) · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 2. Series

**Q4.** [🔴 High Repeat] Find the next term: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
(a) 42  (b) 40  (c) 44  (d) 36
**Answer: (a) 42**
*Explanation:* Differences are 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. 30 + 12 = 42. (Also n(n+1): 6×7=42.)
*Topic:* Number Series · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q5.** [🔴 High Repeat] Find the missing term: 3, 7, 15, 31, ?
(a) 63  (b) 62  (c) 64  (d) 47
**Answer: (a) 63**
*Explanation:* Each term = previous × 2 + 1: 3→7→15→31→63.
*Topic:* Number Series · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q6.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Complete the letter series: AZ, BY, CX, ?
(a) DW  (b) DV  (c) EW  (d) DX
**Answer: (a) DW**
*Explanation:* First letters go A,B,C,D forward; second letters Z,Y,X,W backward → DW.
*Topic:* Letter Series · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 3. Coding-Decoding

**Q7.** [🔴 High Repeat] If in a code TEACHER is written as VGCEJGT, how is STUDENT written?
(a) UVWFGPV  (b) UVWFGPW  (c) UWVFGPV  (d) UVWGFPV
**Answer: (a) UVWFGPV**
*Explanation:* Each letter +2: S→U, T→V, U→W, D→F, E→G, N→P, T→V = UVWFGPV.
*Topic:* Coding (Letter Shift) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q8.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] If RED = 27, then BLUE = ?
(a) 40  (b) 37  (c) 35  (d) 42
**Answer: (a) 40**
*Explanation:* Positional sum: R(18)+E(5)+D(4)=27. BLUE: B(2)+L(12)+U(21)+E(5)=40.
*Topic:* Coding (Number) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q9.** [🟢 Low Repeat] In a code, 'apple' is 'fruit', 'fruit' is 'tree', 'tree' is 'water'. Where does a bird sit?
(a) tree  (b) water  (c) fruit  (d) apple
**Answer: (b) water**
*Explanation:* A bird sits on a 'tree', and 'tree' is coded as 'water'.
*Topic:* Coding (Substitution) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 4. Blood Relations

**Q10.** [🔴 High Repeat] Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man?
(a) Mother  (b) Sister  (c) Aunt  (d) Grandmother
**Answer: (a) Mother**
*Explanation:* "Only daughter of my mother" = the woman herself. So the man's mother is the woman → she is his mother.
*Topic:* Blood Relations · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q11.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the sister of C. How is D related to B?
(a) Aunt  (b) Mother  (c) Sister  (d) Niece
**Answer: (a) Aunt**
*Explanation:* C is B's father (since C fathers A, who is B's brother). D is C's sister → D is B's aunt.
*Topic:* Blood Relations · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 5. Direction Sense

**Q12.** [🔴 High Repeat] A man walks 5 km North, then 3 km East, then 5 km South. How far and in which direction is he from the start?
(a) 3 km East  (b) 3 km West  (c) 5 km East  (d) 8 km East
**Answer: (a) 3 km East**
*Explanation:* North and South (5 each) cancel. Only 3 km East remains.
*Topic:* Direction Sense · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q13.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Ravi walks 4 km East, turns left and walks 3 km. How far is he from the start?
(a) 5 km  (b) 7 km  (c) 1 km  (d) 6 km
**Answer: (a) 5 km**
*Explanation:* Right-angle path; straight-line distance = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 km.
*Topic:* Direction Sense · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 6. Syllogism

**Q14.** [🔴 High Repeat] Statements: All cats are dogs. All dogs are animals. Conclusions: I. All cats are animals. II. Some animals are cats. Which follow?
(a) Both I and II  (b) Only I  (c) Only II  (d) Neither
**Answer: (a) Both I and II**
*Explanation:* All cats→dogs→animals, so all cats are animals (I). Hence some animals are cats (II, converse of an "all" that has members).
*Topic:* Syllogism · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q15.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Statements: Some books are pens. No pen is a pencil. Conclusions: I. Some books are not pencils. II. All pens are books. Which follow?
(a) Only I  (b) Only II  (c) Both  (d) Neither
**Answer: (a) Only I**
*Explanation:* Some books are pens, and no pen is a pencil → those books (which are pens) are not pencils → I follows. II is not guaranteed.
*Topic:* Syllogism · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 7. Non-Verbal Reasoning

**Q16.** [🔴 High Repeat] A cube is painted red on all faces and cut into 27 equal smaller cubes. How many small cubes have exactly one face painted?
(a) 6  (b) 8  (c) 12  (d) 1
**Answer: (a) 6**
*Explanation:* In a 3×3×3 cube, one-face-painted cubes are the centre of each face = 6.
*Topic:* Non-Verbal (Cube) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q17.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] A cube of side 3 cm painted on all faces is cut into 27 unit cubes. How many have no face painted?
(a) 1  (b) 8  (c) 6  (d) 0
**Answer: (a) 1**
*Explanation:* Only the innermost cube (centre) has no painted face = 1.
*Topic:* Non-Verbal (Cube) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q18.** [🟢 Low Repeat] How many triangles are there in a square with both its diagonals drawn?
(a) 8  (b) 4  (c) 6  (d) 10
**Answer: (a) 8**
*Explanation:* 4 small triangles + 4 large triangles (each formed by two adjacent small ones) = 8.
*Topic:* Non-Verbal (Figure Count) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 8. Classification (Odd One Out)

**Q19.** [🔴 High Repeat] Find the odd one out: 121, 144, 169, 200
(a) 200  (b) 169  (c) 144  (d) 121
**Answer: (a) 200**
*Explanation:* 121=11², 144=12², 169=13² are perfect squares; 200 is not.
*Topic:* Classification (Number) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q20.** [🔴 High Repeat] Find the odd one out: Rose, Lotus, Jasmine, Mango
(a) Mango  (b) Rose  (c) Lotus  (d) Jasmine
**Answer: (a) Mango**
*Explanation:* Rose, Lotus, Jasmine are flowers; Mango is a fruit.
*Topic:* Classification (Word) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q21.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Find the odd one out: DF, GI, JL, MP
(a) MP  (b) DF  (c) GI  (d) JL
**Answer: (a) MP**
*Explanation:* In DF, GI, JL the gap between letters is 1 (skip one). In MP the gap is 2 (M_ _P), so it is odd.
*Topic:* Classification (Letter) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 9. Mixed Logical

**Q22.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] If '+' means '×', '×' means '−', '−' means '÷', '÷' means '+', then 16 ÷ 4 − 2 × 3 = ?
(a) 15  (b) 12  (c) 18  (d) 9
**Answer: (a) 15**
*Explanation:* Translate: 16 + 4 ÷ 2 − 3 = 16 + 2 − 3 = 15.
*Topic:* Symbol Substitution · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q23.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] In a row of 40 students, Ravi is 12th from the left. What is his position from the right?
(a) 29th  (b) 28th  (c) 30th  (d) 27th
**Answer: (a) 29th**
*Explanation:* Position from right = (Total − left position) + 1 = (40 − 12) + 1 = 29th.
*Topic:* Ranking/Order · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q24.** [🟢 Low Repeat] Find the missing number: 5, 11, 23, 47, ?
(a) 95  (b) 94  (c) 96  (d) 71
**Answer: (a) 95**
*Explanation:* Each term = previous × 2 + 1: 5→11→23→47→95.
*Topic:* Number Series · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q25.** [🟢 Low Repeat] Some boys are standing in a circle facing the centre. Suresh is 5th to the left of Ramesh, who is 8th to the left of Suresh going the other way. How many boys are in the circle?
(a) 13  (b) 12  (c) 11  (d) 14
**Answer: (a) 13**
*Explanation:* The two counts between them cover the whole circle: 5 + 8 = 13 boys.
*Topic:* Circular Arrangement · *Difficulty:* Medium
