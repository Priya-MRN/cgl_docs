## 1. Percentage

**Q1.** [🔴 High Repeat] If the price of sugar is increased by 25%, by what percent must a household reduce its consumption so that the expenditure remains unchanged?
(a) 25%  (b) 20%  (c) 16⅔%  (d) 30%
**Answer: (b) 20%**
*Explanation:* Reduction % = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%.
*Topic:* Percentage · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q2.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] In an election between two candidates, the winner got 60% of the votes and won by 1600 votes. Find the total number of valid votes.
(a) 6000  (b) 8000  (c) 7500  (d) 9000
**Answer: (b) 8000**
*Explanation:* Margin = 60% − 40% = 20% of total = 1600 → total = 1600 × 100/20 = 8000.
*Topic:* Percentage · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q3.** [🟢 Low Repeat] The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and decreases by 10% in the second year. If the present population is 29700, what was it two years ago?
(a) 30000  (b) 29000  (c) 30300  (d) 31000
**Answer: (a) 30000**
*Explanation:* Net factor = 1.10 × 0.90 = 0.99. So P × 0.99 = 29700 → P = 30000.
*Topic:* Percentage · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 2. Profit & Loss

**Q4.** [🔴 High Repeat] A man sells an article at a profit of 20%. If he had bought it at 20% less and sold it for ₹10 less, he would have gained 25%. Find the cost price.
(a) ₹50  (b) ₹40  (c) ₹60  (d) ₹45
**Answer: (a) ₹50**
*Explanation:* Let CP = x. Old SP = 1.2x. New CP = 0.8x, new SP = 1.25 × 0.8x = x. So 1.2x − x = 10 → 0.2x = 10 → x = 50.
*Topic:* Profit & Loss · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q5.** [🔴 High Repeat] By selling 33 articles, a trader loses an amount equal to the selling price of 3 articles. Find his loss percent.
(a) 9%  (b) 10%  (c) 8⅓%  (d) 11%
**Answer: (c) 8⅓%**
*Explanation:* Let SP of each article = ₹1. Loss = SP of 3 = ₹3. CP of 33 = SP of 33 + loss = 33 + 3 = ₹36. Loss% = 3/36 × 100 = 8⅓%.
*Topic:* Profit & Loss · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q6.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] A shopkeeper marks his goods 40% above cost and gives a discount of 25%. Find his profit percent.
(a) 5%  (b) 10%  (c) 15%  (d) 12%
**Answer: (a) 5%**
*Explanation:* SP = 140 × 0.75 = 105 on CP 100 → profit = 5%.
*Topic:* Profit & Loss · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 3. Simple & Compound Interest

**Q7.** [🔴 High Repeat] The difference between compound and simple interest on a sum for 2 years at 10% per annum is ₹50. Find the sum.
(a) ₹4000  (b) ₹5000  (c) ₹5500  (d) ₹4500
**Answer: (b) ₹5000**
*Explanation:* Difference for 2 yrs = P(r/100)² = P × 0.01 = 50 → P = 5000.
*Topic:* Compound Interest · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q8.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] At what rate percent per annum will ₹2000 amount to ₹2420 in 2 years at compound interest?
(a) 10%  (b) 11%  (c) 12%  (d) 9%
**Answer: (a) 10%**
*Explanation:* 2420/2000 = 1.21 = (1.1)² → r = 10%.
*Topic:* Compound Interest · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q9.** [🟢 Low Repeat] A sum of money becomes ₹13380 after 3 years and ₹20070 after 6 years at compound interest. Find the sum.
(a) ₹8800  (b) ₹8920  (c) ₹9000  (d) ₹8600
**Answer: (b) ₹8920**
*Explanation:* (1+r)³ = 20070/13380 = 1.5. Sum × 1.5 = 13380 → Sum = 13380/1.5 = 8920.
*Topic:* Compound Interest · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 4. Time & Work

**Q10.** [🔴 High Repeat] A can do a work in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?
(a) 7.2 days  (b) 6 days  (c) 7.5 days  (d) 8 days
**Answer: (a) 7.2 days**
*Explanation:* Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36. Time = 36/5 = 7.2 days.
*Topic:* Time & Work · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q11.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] A and B together can do a work in 8 days, B alone in 12 days. In how many days can A alone do it?
(a) 24  (b) 20  (c) 16  (d) 18
**Answer: (a) 24**
*Explanation:* A = 1/8 − 1/12 = 3/24 − 2/24 = 1/24 → 24 days.
*Topic:* Time & Work · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q12.** [🟢 Low Repeat] 12 men can complete a work in 18 days. How many men are needed to finish the same work in 8 days?
(a) 27  (b) 24  (c) 30  (d) 25
**Answer: (a) 27**
*Explanation:* M₁D₁ = M₂D₂ → 12×18 = M₂×8 → M₂ = 216/8 = 27.
*Topic:* Time & Work · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 5. Time, Speed & Distance

**Q13.** [🔴 High Repeat] A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 15 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
(a) 36  (b) 40  (c) 45  (d) 30
**Answer: (a) 36**
*Explanation:* Speed = 150/15 = 10 m/s = 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h.
*Topic:* TSD (Trains) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q14.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] A boat goes 24 km downstream in 3 hours and returns in 4 hours. Find the speed of the stream.
(a) 1 km/h  (b) 1.5 km/h  (c) 2 km/h  (d) 0.5 km/h
**Answer: (a) 1 km/h**
*Explanation:* Downstream = 24/3 = 8, Upstream = 24/4 = 6. Stream = (8−6)/2 = 1 km/h.
*Topic:* TSD (Boats) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q15.** [🟢 Low Repeat] Two trains 120 m and 80 m long run in opposite directions at 42 km/h and 30 km/h. In how many seconds do they cross each other?
(a) 10 s  (b) 12 s  (c) 9 s  (d) 8 s
**Answer: (a) 10 s**
*Explanation:* Relative speed = 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Total length = 200 m. Time = 200/20 = 10 s.
*Topic:* TSD (Trains) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 6. Ratio & Proportion

**Q16.** [🔴 High Repeat] If A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5, find A:B:C.
(a) 8:12:15  (b) 2:3:5  (c) 8:12:10  (d) 6:9:15
**Answer: (a) 8:12:15**
*Explanation:* A:B = 2:3 = 8:12; B:C = 4:5 = 12:15. So A:B:C = 8:12:15.
*Topic:* Ratio · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q17.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] An amount of ₹7800 is divided among A, B, C in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. Find C's share.
(a) ₹1800  (b) ₹2400  (c) ₹3600  (d) ₹2000
**Answer: (a) ₹1800**
*Explanation:* Ratio = 6:4:3 (×12). Total parts = 13. C = 7800 × 3/13 = 1800.
*Topic:* Ratio · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 7. Geometry

**Q18.** [🔴 High Repeat] In a triangle ABC, the angles are in the ratio 2:3:4. Find the largest angle.
(a) 80°  (b) 60°  (c) 90°  (d) 40°
**Answer: (a) 80°**
*Explanation:* Sum = 180°, parts = 9. Largest = 4/9 × 180 = 80°.
*Topic:* Geometry (Triangles) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q19.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Two parallel chords of a circle of radius 13 cm are on opposite sides of the centre, of lengths 24 cm and 10 cm. Find the distance between them.
(a) 17 cm  (b) 14 cm  (c) 12 cm  (d) 7 cm
**Answer: (a) 17 cm**
*Explanation:* Distances from centre: √(13²−12²)=5 and √(13²−5²)=12. Opposite sides → 5+12 = 17 cm.
*Topic:* Geometry (Circles) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 8. Mensuration

**Q20.** [🔴 High Repeat] The radius of a sphere is 7 cm. Find its surface area. (Take π = 22/7)
(a) 616 cm²  (b) 154 cm²  (c) 308 cm²  (d) 770 cm²
**Answer: (a) 616 cm²**
*Explanation:* Surface area = 4πr² = 4 × 22/7 × 49 = 616 cm².
*Topic:* Mensuration (Sphere) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q21.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find its curved surface area. (π = 22/7)
(a) 440 cm²  (b) 880 cm²  (c) 220 cm²  (d) 660 cm²
**Answer: (a) 440 cm²**
*Explanation:* CSA = 2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm².
*Topic:* Mensuration (Cylinder) · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 9. Trigonometry

**Q22.** [🔴 High Repeat] If sin θ + cos θ = √2, find the value of sin θ · cos θ.
(a) ½  (b) 1  (c) √2  (d) 0
**Answer: (a) ½**
*Explanation:* Square both sides: 1 + 2 sinθcosθ = 2 → 2 sinθcosθ = 1 → sinθcosθ = ½.
*Topic:* Trigonometry (Identities) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q23.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from a point 30 m away from its base is 30°. Find the height of the tower.
(a) 10√3 m  (b) 30√3 m  (c) 15 m  (d) 20√3 m
**Answer: (a) 10√3 m**
*Explanation:* tan30° = h/30 → h = 30 × (1/√3) = 30/√3 = 10√3 m.
*Topic:* Trigonometry (Heights) · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 11. Algebra

**Q25.** [🔴 High Repeat] If x + 1/x = 3, find the value of x³ + 1/x³.
(a) 18  (b) 27  (c) 21  (d) 9
**Answer: (a) 18**
*Explanation:* x³ + 1/x³ = (x + 1/x)³ − 3(x + 1/x) = 27 − 9 = 18.
*Topic:* Algebra (Identities) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 12. Number System

**Q26.** [🔴 High Repeat] What is the largest 4-digit number exactly divisible by 88?
(a) 9944  (b) 9999  (c) 9856  (d) 9900
**Answer: (a) 9944**
*Explanation:* 9999 ÷ 88 = 113 remainder 55. 9999 − 55 = 9944. (88 × 113 = 9944.)
*Topic:* Number System · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q27.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] The HCF and LCM of two numbers are 12 and 144. If one number is 36, find the other.
(a) 48  (b) 60  (c) 24  (d) 72
**Answer: (a) 48**
*Explanation:* Product = HCF × LCM = 12 × 144 = 1728. Other = 1728/36 = 48.
*Topic:* Number System (HCF/LCM) · *Difficulty:* Easy
