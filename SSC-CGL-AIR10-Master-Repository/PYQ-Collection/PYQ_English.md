## 1. Error Spotting

**Q1.** [🔴 High Repeat] Spot the error: "One of my friend (a)/ is going (b)/ to the market (c)/ no error (d)."
(a) One of my friend  (b) is going  (c) to the market  (d) no error
**Answer: (a) One of my friend**
*Explanation:* "One of" is always followed by a plural noun → "One of my friends."
*Topic:* Error Spotting (Noun) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q2.** [🔴 High Repeat] Spot the error: "He is senior (a)/ than me (b)/ by three years (c)/ no error (d)."
(a) He is senior  (b) than me  (c) by three years  (d) no error
**Answer: (b) than me**
*Explanation:* "Senior" takes the preposition "to," not "than" → "senior to me."
*Topic:* Error Spotting (Preposition) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q3.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Spot the error: "The number of students (a)/ in the class (b)/ are increasing (c)/ no error (d)."
(a) The number of students  (b) in the class  (c) are increasing  (d) no error
**Answer: (c) are increasing**
*Explanation:* "The number of" takes a singular verb → "is increasing."
*Topic:* Error Spotting (Subject-Verb) · *Difficulty:* Medium

## 3. Antonyms

**Q7.** [🔴 High Repeat] Choose the antonym of **TRANSPARENT**.
(a) Opaque  (b) Clear  (c) Visible  (d) Bright
**Answer: (a) Opaque**
*Explanation:* Transparent (light passes through) ↔ opaque (light does not).
*Topic:* Antonym · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 5. One-Word Substitution

**Q12.** [🔴 High Repeat] One who does not believe in the existence of God.

*Explanation:* An atheist denies the existence of God; a theist believes; an agnostic is unsure.

**Answer: (a) Democracy**

*Topic:* One-Word Substitution · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q14.** [🟢 Low Repeat] A person who writes dictionaries.

## 6. Fill in the Blanks

**Q15.** [🔴 High Repeat] He has been living here ____ 2010.
(a) since  (b) for  (c) from  (d) by
**Answer: (a) since**
*Explanation:* "Since" is used with a point of time (a year); "for" with a duration.
*Topic:* Fill-Blanks (Preposition) · *Difficulty:* Easy

**Q16.** [🔴 High Repeat] Hardly had I reached the station ____ the train left.
(a) when  (b) than  (c) then  (d) that
**Answer: (a) when**
*Explanation:* The fixed structure is "Hardly had ... when ...."
*Topic:* Fill-Blanks (Conjunction) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q17.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] She is the most ____ student in the class.
(a) intelligent  (b) intelligence  (c) intelligently  (d) intelligential
**Answer: (a) intelligent**
*Explanation:* An adjective is needed to modify "student" → "intelligent."
*Topic:* Fill-Blanks (Adjective) · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 7. Sentence Improvement

**Q18.** [🔴 High Repeat] Improve the underlined part: "If I **was** you, I would accept the offer."
(a) were  (b) was  (c) am  (d) had been
**Answer: (a) were**
*Explanation:* In an unreal/hypothetical condition, the subjunctive "were" is used → "If I were you."
*Topic:* Sentence Improvement (Subjunctive) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q19.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Improve the underlined part: "He is one of the best player **who has** ever played."
(a) who have  (b) who has  (c) which has  (d) whom have
**Answer: (a) who have**
*Explanation:* "One of the best players" is plural, so the relative verb is plural → "who have." (Also "player" should be "players.")
*Topic:* Sentence Improvement (Agreement) · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q20.** [🟢 Low Repeat] Improve the underlined part: "She **didn't went** to school yesterday."
(a) didn't go  (b) didn't went  (c) doesn't go  (d) hasn't went
**Answer: (a) didn't go**
*Explanation:* After "did," the base form of the verb is used → "didn't go."
*Topic:* Sentence Improvement (Tense) · *Difficulty:* Easy

## 9. Mixed Grammar

**Q24.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Choose the correct passive voice: "They are building a new bridge."
(a) A new bridge is being built (by them).  (b) A new bridge was built.  (c) A new bridge is built.  (d) A new bridge has been built.
**Answer: (a) A new bridge is being built (by them).**
*Explanation:* Present continuous active → "is/are being + past participle" in passive.
*Topic:* Voice · *Difficulty:* Medium

**Q25.** [🟡 Medium Repeat] Choose the correct indirect speech: He said, "I am happy."
(a) He said that he was happy.  (b) He said that I am happy.  (c) He says that he is happy.  (d) He said that he is happy.
**Answer: (a) He said that he was happy.**
*Explanation:* In reported speech after "said," present tense shifts to past → "was," and "I" becomes "he."
*Topic:* Narration · *Difficulty:* Medium
