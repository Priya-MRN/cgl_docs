## Subject-Verb Agreement (high PYQ)
- Two subjects joined by **and** → plural verb. (Bread and butter **is** — single idea → singular)
- **Either/Neither/Each/Every/Everyone/Anyone/Nobody** → singular verb
- **Either…or / Neither…nor / Not only…but also** → verb agrees with the **nearer** subject
- "One of + plural noun + singular verb": *One of the boys **is**…*
- Collective nouns (team, jury, family) → singular (as unit)
- "The number of" → singular; "A number of" → plural
- Distances, amounts, time treated as a unit → singular (*Ten miles **is** far*)

## Tenses
- Since + point of time; For + period
- "Since" → present perfect / perfect continuous
- After "wish / if only / as if / it's time" → past tense (*I wish I **were**…*)
- Universal truth → simple present even in past reported speech

## Articles
- "A" before consonant sound (a university, a one-rupee); "An" before vowel sound (an hour, an MBA)
- "The" → unique things (the sun), superlatives, rivers, oceans, mountain ranges, holy books
- No article: proper nouns, languages, meals, abstract nouns (general sense)

## Prepositions (common PYQ pairs)
- **agree** to (a proposal) / with (a person) / on (a point)
- **die** of (disease) / from (overwork) / for (a cause)
- **differ** from (thing) / with (person)
- **comply with**, **abide by**, **deprived of**, **devoid of**, **adept at**
- **married to** (not with); **superior/inferior/senior/junior to**; **prefer X to Y**

## Voice (Active ↔ Passive)
- Passive: Object → Subject; verb → "be + V₃"; subject → "by + object"
- Modal: can → can be + V₃; must → must be + V₃
- Imperative active → "Let + obj + be + V₃"

## Narration (Direct ↔ Indirect)
- Present → Past; will → would; can → could; may → might; must → had to
- now→then, here→there, today→that day, tomorrow→the next day, ago→before
- Reporting verb: statement→said that; question→asked if/wh-; command→ordered/told to

## Conditionals
- Type 1 (real): If + present, will + V₁
- Type 2 (unreal present): If + past, would + V₁
- Type 3 (unreal past): If + had + V₃, would have + V₃

## Degree of Comparison
- "than any other" with comparative; "the most" with superlative
- No comparative/superlative of: unique, perfect, complete, supreme, ideal

## Confusables (One-Word & Spelling traps)
- accept (receive) / except (exclude)
- affect (verb) / effect (noun)
- principal (head/main) / principle (rule)
- stationary (still) / stationery (paper)
- complement (complete) / compliment (praise)
- Commonly misspelt: **accommodation, occurrence, embarrass, privilege, separate, maintenance, definitely, receive (i before e except after c)**

## Error-Spotting checklist (scan order)
1. Subject-verb agreement
2. Tense consistency
3. Preposition
4. Article
5. Pronoun (case/number)
6. Parallelism
7. Adjective/Adverb misuse
8. Redundancy (return back, repeat again, more better)

## High-frequency One-Word Substitutions
- One who eats everything → **Omnivore**
- One who cannot read/write → **Illiterate**
- Speech without preparation → **Extempore**
- Animals living in water → **Aquatic**
- Study of birds → **Ornithology**
- Government by the people → **Democracy**
- One who hates mankind → **Misanthrope**
- A cure for all diseases → **Panacea**
- Words written on a tomb → **Epitaph**
- One who loves books → **Bibliophile**
