**Q4. Who is the current Chief Justice of India?**

✅ **Answer:** **Justice Surya Kant** is the current Chief Justice of India (CJI). He became the **53rd Chief Justice of India** on **24 November 2025** and is expected to serve until **9 February 2027**. ([Supreme Court of India][1])

### Quick Facts

- **Current CJI:** Justice Surya Kant
- **Position:** Head of the Supreme Court of India
- **Assumed Office:** 24 November 2025
- **Retirement Date:** 9 February 2027 ([Supreme Court of India][1])

### TNPSC / SSC One-Liner

**Current Chief Justice of India (2026): Justice Surya Kant.** ([Supreme Court of India][1])

The **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award** and the **Arjuna Award** are both national sports awards of India, but they recognize different levels of achievement.

| Feature                | Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award                                                                | Arjuna Award                                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| **Status**             | Highest sporting award in India                                                                   | Second-highest sports award                          |
| **Purpose**            | Outstanding and most exceptional performance at the international level over the previous 4 years | Consistent good performance and leadership in sports |
| **Number of Awardees** | Usually very few each year                                                                        | Larger number of athletes each year                  |
| **Prestige**           | Most prestigious sports honor                                                                     | Highly prestigious, but below Khel Ratna             |
| **Named After**        | Major Dhyan Chand                                                                                 | Arjuna                                               |
| **Recipients**         | Elite athletes with extraordinary achievements                                                    | Athletes with notable and sustained achievements     |

### Easy Example

Suppose there are two athletes:

- Athlete A wins an Olympic gold medal and dominates internationally for several years.
  - May receive the **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award**.

- Athlete B consistently performs well in international competitions and wins medals.
  - May receive the **Arjuna Award**.

### Memory Trick

Think of it like this:

🥇 **Khel Ratna** = "Sports Person of the Year" (highest recognition)

🥈 **Arjuna Award** = "Outstanding Athlete Award"

### Other Important Sports Awards

1. Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award – Highest sports award.
2. Arjuna Award – Outstanding athlete.
3. Dronacharya Award – Best coaches.
4. Dhyan Chand Award – Lifetime achievement in sports.

**Q. Which is the highest sporting honour in India?**

A) Arjuna Award
B) Dronacharya Award
C) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award
D) Dhyan Chand Award

✅ **Answer:** C) Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award
