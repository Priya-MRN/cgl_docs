## 1. Ram Mandir – Ayodhya (Pran Pratishtha)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Event | *Pran Pratishtha* (consecration / "infusion of life" ceremony) of Ram Lalla idol |
| Date & time | **22 January 2024**, main ritual between 12:15–12:45 PM |
| Officiated by | Prime Minister **Narendra Modi** (chief patron of rituals) |
| Organised by | **Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra** Trust |
| Location | Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh, on the bank of river Sarayu |
| Idol | "Ram Lalla" (child form of Lord Ram), sculpted by **Arun Yogiraj** (Mysuru) |
| Architectural style | **Nagara** style (North Indian temple architecture) |
| Background | Built after the Supreme Court's 9 Nov 2019 verdict allotting the disputed site for the temple |

- UP Government earmarked **₹100 crore** for "Ramotsav" celebrations across 826 local bodies.
- PM Modi described the date as the start of a new *"kaal chakra"* (era).

## 2. Lok Sabha Elections 2024 (18th Lok Sabha)

- **7 phases** of voting (19 April – 1 June 2024); results declared **4 June 2024**.
- Conducted by the **Election Commission of India (ECI)**; Chief Election Commissioner then: **Rajiv Kumar**.
- Result: **NDA (National Democratic Alliance)** returned to power for a **third consecutive term**; Narendra Modi sworn in as PM for the 3rd time (equalling Jawaharlal Nehru's record).

## 3. Aditya-L1 Mission (India's First Solar Observatory)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Agency | **ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation)** |
| Launch | **2 September 2023**, **PSLV-XL (Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle – XL variant)** |
| Launch site | **SDSC-SHAR (Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota)** |
| Destination | **Lagrange Point 1 (L1)** of the Sun–Earth system, ~**1.5 million km** from Earth |
| Halo-orbit insertion | **6 January 2024** |
| Orbital period | ~178 days (~6 months) around L1 |
| Payloads | **7 total** — 4 remote-sensing + 3 in-situ |

**Key payloads:** Visible Emission Line Coronagraph (VELC – primary), Solar Ultraviolet Imaging Telescope (SUIT), Solar Low-Energy X-ray Spectrometer (SoLEXS), High-Energy L1 Orbiting X-ray Spectrometer (HEL1OS), Aditya Solar Wind Particle Experiment (ASPEX), Plasma Analyser Package for Aditya (PAPA), and a tri-axial magnetometer.

**Objective:** Study the Sun's corona, chromosphere, solar wind, and space weather. Advantage of L1: continuous, uninterrupted view of the Sun (no eclipses).

## 4. Gaganyaan Mission (India's First Human Spaceflight)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|

| Goal | Send a 3-member crew to **Low Earth Orbit (~400 km)** for up to 3 days, and return safely |

| First crewed flight (target) | **2027** (preceded by uncrewed test flights, first ~2026) |

## 10. Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (RVP) 2024

The **Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar (National Science Award)** was introduced in **2024**, replacing **300+** earlier science awards (including the **Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize**) for a unified recognition system. **33 awards** announced in the first edition.

### Four Categories

| Category | For | 2024 Highlight |
|---|---|---|
| **Vigyan Ratna (VR)** | Lifetime achievement | Dr. **Govindarajan Padmanabhan** (biochemist, ex-Director IISc Bengaluru) |
| **Vigyan Shri (VS)** | Distinguished contribution | 13 scientists |
| **Vigyan Yuva – Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar (VY-SSB)** | Young scientists | 18 young scientists |
| **Vigyan Team (VT)** | Teams of 3+ | **Chandrayaan-3** team (ISRO) |

## 17. Important Bills, Acts & Court Judgements (2024)

- **Three new criminal laws** came into force **1 July 2024**, replacing colonial-era codes:

| New Law | Replaces |
|---|---|
| **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** | Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860 |
| **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023** | Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC), 1973 |
| **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023** | Indian Evidence Act, 1872 |

- **Women's Reservation Act** (*Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam*) — 33% reservation for women in Lok Sabha & State Assemblies (to be implemented after delimitation).
- **Supreme Court** struck down the **Electoral Bonds** scheme as unconstitutional (Feb 2024), citing the citizen's right to information.
