# PART G — EXPLAINED: AWARDS, BOOKS, POSTS, DAYS & HIGH-YIELD TOPICS

## G-A. Awards (What each one IS)

| Award | What it is | Latest Winner(s) |
|---|---|---|
| **Jnanpith Award** | India's **highest literary honour** (since 1961), by the **Bharatiya Jnanpith** trust, for outstanding contribution to literature in any constitutionally-recognised Indian language. Prize: ₹11 lakh, *Vagdevi* (Saraswati) bronze, citation. | **58th (2023):** **Gulzar** (Urdu) & **Jagadguru Rambhadracharya** (Sanskrit) — conferred 16 May 2025. **59th (2024):** **Vinod Kumar Shukla** (Hindi) |
| **Sahitya Akademi Award** | Annual award by the **Sahitya Akademi (National Academy of Letters)** for the best literary work in each of **24 Indian languages**. Plaque + ₹1 lakh. | Announced annually across 24 languages |
| **Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna** | India's **highest sporting honour** (instituted 1991-92 as Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna; renamed 2021). Awarded by Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports. | **2024:** Manu Bhaker, D. Gukesh, Harmanpreet Singh, Praveen Kumar |
| **Arjuna Award** | India's **2nd-highest sporting honour** (since **1961**); named after Arjuna of the Mahabharata; for consistently good performance over **4 years**. Statuette of Arjuna + ₹15 lakh. | Announced annually (National Sports Awards) |

> Quick hierarchy of sports honours: **Khel Ratna** (highest) → **Arjuna** → **Dronacharya** (coaches) → **Dhyan Chand** (lifetime).
