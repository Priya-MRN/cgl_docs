🧠 Polity & History — Visual Mind-Maps for Easy Remembrance

SSC CGL Tier-I (General Awareness). Each topic is drawn as a boxed mind-map — a central idea in the middle with branches around it, exactly like a flow-chart you can picture in your head. Read the box, cover it, and redraw it from memory.

Sources in this repo: Polity & Constitution notes · History notes · GK Revision Cheat Sheet

How to use these maps

  1. Look at one box. Say the centre word aloud, then each branch.
  2. Cover it. Redraw the boxes on paper from memory.
  3. Use the 🔑 memory hook under each map — that's the line that makes it stick.

9. Key Amendments — The Timeline Ladder

1st  (1951) ── Ninth Schedule added (laws immune from review)
   │

   │
42nd (1976) ── "MINI CONSTITUTION": +Socialist, Secular, Integrity, Fundamental Duties
   │
44th (1978) ── Right to PROPERTY removed from Fundamental Rights → now Art. 300A
   │
52nd (1985) ── ANTI-DEFECTION law (10th Schedule)
   │
61st (1989) ── Voting age 21 → 18
   │
73rd & 74th (1992) ── PANCHAYATI RAJ & Urban local bodies
   │

   │
101st(2016) ── GST          103rd (2019) ── 10% EWS reservation

16. Freedom Struggle — The Date Line (memorise the years!)

1885 ── INC founded (A.O. Hume; 1st session Bombay, W.C. Bonnerjee)
1905 ── Partition of Bengal → SWADESHI movement
1919 ── Jallianwala Bagh massacre (13 Apr, Amritsar, Gen. Dyer)
1920 ── NON-COOPERATION (called off 1922 after Chauri Chaura)
1928 ── Simon Commission ("Simon Go Back"; Lala Lajpat Rai injured)
1929 ── Lahore Session → "PURNA SWARAJ" (Nehru president)
1930 ── DANDI / Salt March (12 Mar – 6 Apr) → Civil Disobedience
1942 ── QUIT INDIA ("Do or Die"; Bombay)
1947 ── INDEPENDENCE (15 Aug; Indian Independence Act / Mountbatten Plan)

🔑 Hook: "85 Congress, 05 Swadeshi, 19 Bagh, 20 Non-coop, 30 Salt, 42 Quit, 47 Free." 🔑 INA → Subhas Chandra Bose ("Dilli Chalo", "Jai Hind"). Quit India slogan = "Do or Die."
