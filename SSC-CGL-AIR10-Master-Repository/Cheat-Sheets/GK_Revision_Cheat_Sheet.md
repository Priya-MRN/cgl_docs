```

Art 32 → Right to Constitutional Remedies (Heart & Soul – Ambedkar)
Art 51A → Fundamental Duties (added by 42nd Amendment)

Art 280 → Finance Commission
Art 324 → Election Commission

Parts: III → Fundamental Rights; IV → DPSP; IVA → Fundamental Duties
Schedules: total 12
Fundamental Duties: 11 (added 42nd Amend 1976, 11th by 86th Amend)
```
