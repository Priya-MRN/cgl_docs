To remember all 28 states and 8 Union Territories (UTs) from bottom to top, it is easiest to split India into four logical geographic zones. [1, 2]
------------------------------
## 1. The South & West Zone (9 States)
Mnemonic: “Keep Traveling And Keep Moving, Go Read More!”

* Kerala (Bottom West)
* Tamil Nadu (Bottom East)
* Andhra Pradesh
* Karnataka
* Maharashtra
* Goa (The small link between Karnataka and Maharashtra)
* Gujarat
* Rajasthan
* Madhya Pradesh

------------------------------
## 2. The North Zone (7 States)
Mnemonic: “Please Help Us Understand Beautiful Journey Here.”

* Punjab
* Haryana
* Uttarakhand
* Uttar Pradesh
* Bihar
* Jharkhand
* Himachal Pradesh (The very top state) [3, 4]

------------------------------
## 3. The East & Northeast Zone (12 States)
Mnemonic: “We See Many Animals Near Mountains, Too Many Awesome Ones Chasing.”

* West Bengal
* Sikkim
* Meghalaya
* Assam
* Nagaland
* Manipur
* Tripura
* Mizoram
* Arunachal Pradesh (The highest Northeast peak)
* Odisha (Moving slightly down/center)
* Chhattisgarh
* Telangana (Finalizing the peninsular interior) [5]

------------------------------
## 4. The 8 Union Territories (UTs)
Mnemonic: “Let's Dance Cheerfully, Drink Juice, Love And Pray.”

* Ladakh (The highest point in the North)
* Delhi (National Capital)
* Chandigarh
* Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (Merged UT)
* Jammu & Kashmir
* Lakshadweep (Island group in the Arabian Sea)
* Andaman & Nicobar Islands (Island group in the Bay of Bengal)
* Puducherry [6, 7, 8, 9, 10]

------------------------------
