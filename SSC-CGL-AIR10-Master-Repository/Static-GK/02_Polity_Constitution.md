## Quick Notes

**Constitution Making**
- Constituent Assembly formed under the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946); first sitting 9 December 1946.
- Permanent Chairman/President: Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Temporary President: Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha.
- Drafting Committee Chairman: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar ("Father of the Indian Constitution").
- Constitution adopted: 26 November 1949 (Constitution/Law Day). Came into force: 26 January 1950 (Republic Day).
- Took 2 years, 11 months, 18 days to prepare. Originally 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules.
- The Preamble was inspired by the Objectives Resolution (Nehru). Words "Socialist, Secular, Integrity" added by the 42nd Amendment (1976).
- "Bag of borrowings": Fundamental Rights & Judicial review (USA); Parliamentary system & Rule of Law (UK); DPSP (Ireland); Emergency provisions (Germany); Concurrent List & trade (Australia); Fundamental Duties & Five-Year Plans (USSR); Liberty-Equality-Fraternity (France); Federation with strong Centre (Canada).

**Key Parts**
- Part I — Union & Territory; Part II — Citizenship; Part III — Fundamental Rights; Part IV — DPSP; Part IVA — Fundamental Duties; Part V — Union; Part VI — States; Part IX — Panchayats; Part IXA — Municipalities.

**Important Articles (high-yield)**
- Art. 1 — India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States.
- Art. 14 — Equality before law; Art. 15 — no discrimination; Art. 16 — equal opportunity in public employment.
- Art. 17 — abolition of untouchability; Art. 18 — abolition of titles.
- Art. 19 — six freedoms (speech, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession).
- Art. 21 — Protection of life and personal liberty; Art. 21A — Right to Education (6–14 yrs, added by 86th Amendment 2002).
- Art. 22 — protection against arrest; Art. 23–24 — against exploitation (child labour < 14).
- Art. 25–28 — freedom of religion; Art. 29–30 — cultural & educational rights of minorities.
- Art. 32 — Right to Constitutional Remedies ("heart and soul" — Ambedkar); writs.
- Art. 40 — Panchayati Raj (DPSP); Art. 44 — Uniform Civil Code; Art. 48 — cow protection; Art. 51 — international peace.
- Art. 51A — Fundamental Duties (originally 10, now 11; 11th added by 86th Amendment).
- Art. 72 — pardoning power of President; Art. 123 — Ordinance by President.
- Art. 143 — President can seek SC advisory opinion; Art. 148 — CAG; Art. 280 — Finance Commission.
- Art. 324 — Election Commission; Art. 343 — Hindi (Devanagari) official language; Art. 352/356/360 — National/State/Financial Emergency.
- Art. 368 — amendment procedure; Art. 370 — special status of J&K (abrogated 2019).

**📝 Exam Trick — Right to Equality (Articles 14–18)**
- **14 →** Equality before Law
- **15 →** No Discrimination (on religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth)
- **16 →** Equal Opportunity in Public Employment
- **17 →** Abolition of Untouchability
- **18 →** Abolition of Titles
- 🔑 *One-liner:* **Art. 17 (Abolition of Untouchability)** is enforced through the **Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955** (originally the Untouchability (Offences) Act, 1955; renamed in 1976).

**Fundamental Rights (Part III) — 6 rights**
1. Right to Equality (14–18); 2. Right to Freedom (19–22); 3. Right against Exploitation (23–24); 4. Right to Freedom of Religion (25–28); 5. Cultural & Educational Rights (29–30); 6. Right to Constitutional Remedies (32).
- Right to Property was removed as an FR by the 44th Amendment (1978); now a legal right under Art. 300A.
- Five writs: Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto.

**Directive Principles (DPSP, Part IV)** — non-justiciable; ideals for governance (Gandhian, Socialist, Liberal-Intellectual).

**Fundamental Duties (Part IVA, Art. 51A)** — added by the 42nd Amendment (1976) on the Swaran Singh Committee recommendation; 10 duties originally, 11th (education of children) added by 86th Amendment.

**Parliament**
- Bicameral: Lok Sabha (House of the People) + Rajya Sabha (Council of States) + President.
- Lok Sabha: max strength 552; term 5 years; presiding officer Speaker. Money Bills (Art. 110) originate only here.
- Rajya Sabha: max 250 (238 elected + 12 nominated); permanent house, 1/3 retire every 2 years; Chairman = Vice-President.
- A Money Bill requires the President's prior recommendation and the Speaker's certification.

**President (Art. 52–62)**
- Head of State; elected indirectly by an electoral college (elected MPs + elected MLAs) via single transferable vote (proportional representation).
- Qualification: citizen, 35+, eligible to be a Lok Sabha member. Term 5 years.
- Removed by impeachment (Art. 61) for "violation of the Constitution".

**Vice-President (Art. 63–71)** — ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha; elected by both Houses.

**Prime Minister & Council of Ministers (Art. 74–75)**
- PM appointed by President; real executive head. Council of Ministers collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha (Art. 75).

**Judiciary**
- Supreme Court (Art. 124): CJI + 33 judges (sanctioned strength 34). Judges retire at 65; High Court judges at 62.
- SC is the guardian of the Constitution and the apex court of appeal; has original, appellate, and advisory jurisdiction.
- Judicial Review allows courts to strike down unconstitutional laws.

**Key Amendments**
- 1st (1951) — added Ninth Schedule. 7th (1956) — reorganisation of states (linguistic).
- 42nd (1976) — "Mini Constitution"; added Socialist, Secular, Integrity, Fundamental Duties.
- 44th (1978) — Right to Property removed from FRs.
- 52nd (1985) — Anti-defection law (Tenth Schedule).
- 61st (1989) — voting age 21→18.
- 73rd & 74th (1992) — Panchayati Raj & Urban local bodies.
- 86th (2002) — Right to Education (Art. 21A).
- 101st (2016) — GST. 103rd (2019) — 10% EWS reservation.

**Schedules (12 in total)**
- 1st — States & UTs; 2nd — salaries/emoluments; 3rd — oaths; 4th — Rajya Sabha seats; 5th — Scheduled Areas; 6th — Tribal areas (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram); 7th — Union/State/Concurrent Lists; 8th — 22 official languages; 9th — laws immune from review; 10th — anti-defection; 11th — Panchayats (29 subjects); 12th — Municipalities (18 subjects).

## MCQs

**Q1.** The Indian Constitution came into force on —
(a) 26 November 1949  (b) 15 August 1947  (c) 26 January 1950  (d) 26 January 1930
**Answer: (c) 26 January 1950**

**Q2.** Who was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constitution?
(a) Rajendra Prasad  (b) Jawaharlal Nehru  (c) B. R. Ambedkar  (d) Sardar Patel
**Answer: (c) B. R. Ambedkar**

**Q3.** The words "Socialist" and "Secular" were added to the Preamble by which amendment?
(a) 24th  (b) 42nd  (c) 44th  (d) 52nd
**Answer: (b) 42nd Amendment (1976)**

**Q4.** Which Article is called the "heart and soul" of the Constitution?
(a) Art. 14  (b) Art. 19  (c) Art. 21  (d) Art. 32
**Answer: (d) Art. 32**

**Q5.** The Right to Education (Art. 21A) was added by which amendment?
(a) 42nd  (b) 44th  (c) 86th  (d) 73rd
**Answer: (c) 86th Amendment (2002)**

**Q6.** Money Bills can be introduced only in the —
(a) Rajya Sabha  (b) Lok Sabha  (c) Either House  (d) Joint Session
**Answer: (b) Lok Sabha**

**Q7.** The Directive Principles of State Policy were borrowed from the constitution of —
(a) USA  (b) UK  (c) Ireland  (d) Canada
**Answer: (c) Ireland**

**Q8.** Which Fundamental Right was removed by the 44th Amendment?
(a) Right to Equality  (b) Right to Property  (c) Right to Freedom  (d) Right to Religion
**Answer: (b) Right to Property**
*Note:* Now a legal right under Art. 300A.

**Q9.** The Fundamental Duties were added to the Constitution by the —
(a) 42nd Amendment  (b) 44th Amendment  (c) 52nd Amendment  (d) 1st Amendment
**Answer: (a) 42nd Amendment (1976)**

**Q10.** Who is the ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha?
(a) Speaker  (b) President  (c) Vice-President  (d) Prime Minister
**Answer: (c) Vice-President**

**Q11.** The President of India is elected by —
(a) the public directly  (b) the Lok Sabha only  (c) an electoral college  (d) the Council of Ministers
**Answer: (c) an electoral college (elected MPs + elected MLAs)**

**Q12.** The anti-defection law is contained in which Schedule?
(a) Eighth  (b) Ninth  (c) Tenth  (d) Twelfth
**Answer: (c) Tenth Schedule (52nd Amendment, 1985)**

**Q13.** How many languages are listed in the Eighth Schedule?
(a) 14  (b) 18  (c) 22  (d) 28
**Answer: (c) 22**

**Q14.** The 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments deal with —
(a) GST  (b) Local self-government  (c) Anti-defection  (d) Reservation
**Answer: (b) Local self-government (Panchayats & Municipalities)**

**Q15.** Which writ is issued to release a person from illegal detention?
(a) Mandamus  (b) Quo Warranto  (c) Habeas Corpus  (d) Certiorari
**Answer: (c) Habeas Corpus**
