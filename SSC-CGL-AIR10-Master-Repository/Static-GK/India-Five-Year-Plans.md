# India’s Five-Year Plans: Year, Theme, Motto and Description

## Brief Revision Table

| Plan      | Year    | Theme + Motto (combined)                                                | Descriptive Focus: How / Why / Where / When                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1st Plan  | 1951–56 | Agriculture and irrigation — growth through agriculture                 | Launched soon after independence to increase food production, improve irrigation and support rural development.              |
| 2nd Plan  | 1956–61 | Industrialization — rapid industrial growth                             | Focused on heavy industries, public sector growth and infrastructure to build a stronger industrial base.                    |
| 3rd Plan  | 1961–66 | Self-reliance and balanced growth — self-sufficiency                    | Aimed at reducing dependence on imports, strengthening basic industries and increasing agricultural output.                  |
| 4th Plan  | 1969–74 | Growth with stability — stability and growth                            | Addressed the need for stable growth during economic stress and gave importance to agriculture, industry and social justice. |
| 5th Plan  | 1974–79 | Poverty removal and self-reliance — removal of poverty                  | Emphasized employment, poverty reduction and rural development through programmes like NREP and the Minimum Needs Programme. |
| 6th Plan  | 1980–85 | Modernization and growth — growth with justice                          | Focused on modernization, technology, social welfare and rural development, especially for poor households.                  |
| 7th Plan  | 1985–90 | Food, employment and productivity — productivity and employment         | Aimed at raising agricultural output, creating jobs and improving productivity in both rural and urban sectors.              |
| 8th Plan  | 1992–97 | Human development and employment — human development                    | Emphasized education, health, employment and growth with social justice in the post-reform period.                           |
| 9th Plan  | 1997–02 | Growth with social justice — growth with equity                         | Focused on poverty alleviation, basic services, infrastructure and better access to education and health.                    |
| 10th Plan | 2002–07 | Poverty reduction and employment — growth with employment               | Aimed at faster economic growth, better employment opportunities and stronger social inclusion.                              |
| 11th Plan | 2007–12 | Inclusive growth — faster and more inclusive growth                     | Concentrated on education, health, rural development, infrastructure and livelihood security for all.                        |
| 12th Plan | 2012–17 | Faster, sustainable and more inclusive growth — sustainable development | Prioritized energy, infrastructure, skill development and balanced regional growth with sustainability in mind.              |

## Quick Memory Points

- 1st Plan: Community Development Programme
- 2nd Plan: KVIC for rural industries
- 3rd Plan: IADP for food production
- 5th Plan: Minimum Needs Programme and NREP
- 11th Plan: MGNREGA and NRHM
- 12th Plan: NFSA for food security

## Note

The 12th Five-Year Plan was the last official Five-Year Plan in India. After that, planning is guided by the NITI Aayog through long-term policy and development frameworks.
