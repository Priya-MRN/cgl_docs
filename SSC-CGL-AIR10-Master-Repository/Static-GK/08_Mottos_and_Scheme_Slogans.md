# Mottos & Slogans — Sporting Events & Government Schemes

> Quick links: [Static GK Index](README.md) · [Art, Culture, Days & Orgs](06_Art_Culture_Days_Orgs.md) · [GK Revision Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/GK_Revision_Cheat_Sheet.md)

High-yield "match the motto/slogan" GK for SSC CGL: official mottos of popular games & sporting events, and the catch-slogans (taglines) of popular government schemes. SSC frequently asks "Which scheme has the slogan …?" or "What is the motto of the Olympics?".

## A. Mottos of Popular Games & Sporting Events

| Event / Body | Motto / Slogan |
|---|---|
| **Olympic Games** | *Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter* — "Faster, Higher, Stronger – Together" (the word "Together" was added in 2021) |
| Olympic creed/spirit | "The most important thing is not to win but to take part." |
| **Paralympic Games** | "Spirit in Motion" |
| **Asian Games** | "Ever Onward" |
| Asian Games 2023 (Hangzhou) | "Heart to Heart, @Future" |
| **Commonwealth Games** | "Humanity, Equality, Destiny" |
| **FIFA World Cup 2022 (Qatar)** | "Now is All" (slogan) |
| **ICC Cricket** | "Cricket for Good" |
| **IPL (Indian Premier League)** | "Where Talent Meets Opportunity" (also popularised as *Manoranjan ka Baap*) |
| **Khelo India** | "Let's Play India" / *Khelo India – Khilega India* |
| **FIT India Movement** | "Fitness ka Dose, Aadha Ghanta Roz" |
| **National Sports Day** | Celebrated 29 August (birthday of Major Dhyan Chand) |
| **Special Olympics** | "Let me win. But if I cannot win, let me be brave in the attempt." |
| **Youth Olympic Games** | Shares the Olympic motto |

**Quick notes**
- The Olympic motto was coined by **Henri Didon**, proposed by **Pierre de Coubertin** (founder of the modern Olympics & IOC, 1894).
- Olympic **rings** (5): represent the five inhabited continents; colours — blue, yellow, black, green, red on white (every national flag has at least one of these colours).
- Olympic **flame/torch relay** symbolises continuity; the motto + rings + flame + creed make up the "Olympic symbols".

**National & general slogans (often clubbed in GK)**
- **National motto of India:** *Satyameva Jayate* — "Truth Alone Triumphs" (from the Mundaka Upanishad).
- **Indian Army:** "Service Before Self."
- **Indian Navy:** *Sham No Varunah* — "May the Lord of the Water be auspicious unto us."
- **Indian Air Force:** *Nabha Sparsham Deeptam* — "Touch the Sky with Glory."
- **DRDO:** *Balasya Mulam Vigyanam* — "Strength's origin is in science."
- **ISRO vision:** "Space technology in the service of humankind."
- **Doordarshan:** *Satyam Shivam Sundaram.*
- **All India Radio (Akashvani):** *Bahujana Hitaya Bahujana Sukhaya.*
- **LIC:** *Yogakshemam Vahamyaham* — "Your welfare is our responsibility."

## MCQs

**Q1.** The motto of the modern Olympic Games is —
(a) Ever Onward  (b) Citius, Altius, Fortius  (c) Spirit in Motion  (d) Humanity, Equality, Destiny
**Answer: (b) Citius, Altius, Fortius** ("Faster, Higher, Stronger"; "– Communiter/Together" added 2021)

**Q2.** "Spirit in Motion" is the motto of the —
(a) Asian Games  (b) Commonwealth Games  (c) Paralympic Games  (d) Youth Olympics
**Answer: (c) Paralympic Games**

**Q3.** The motto of the Asian Games is —
(a) Ever Onward  (b) Now is All  (c) Heart to Heart  (d) Cricket for Good
**Answer: (a) Ever Onward**

**Q4.** "Humanity, Equality, Destiny" is the motto of the —
(a) Olympics  (b) Commonwealth Games  (c) FIFA World Cup  (d) IPL
**Answer: (b) Commonwealth Games**

**Q5.** The slogan "Mera Khaata – Bhagya Vidhaata" is associated with which scheme?
(a) Ujjwala Yojana  (b) Jan Dhan Yojana  (c) Atal Pension Yojana  (d) Mudra Yojana
**Answer: (b) Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana**

**Q6.** "Swachh Indhan, Behtar Jeevan" is the slogan of —
(a) Swachh Bharat  (b) Ujjwala Yojana  (c) Saubhagya  (d) Namami Gange
**Answer: (b) Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana**

**Q7.** The slogan of the Digital India programme is —
(a) Power to Empower  (b) Make in India  (c) Vocal for Local  (d) Power for All
**Answer: (a) Power to Empower**

**Q8.** "Do Boond Zindagi Ki" is the slogan of which programme?
(a) Mission Indradhanush  (b) Pulse Polio  (c) Ayushman Bharat  (d) Janani Suraksha
**Answer: (b) Pulse Polio Programme**

**Q9.** The national motto of India, *Satyameva Jayate*, is taken from the —
(a) Rig Veda  (b) Mundaka Upanishad  (c) Bhagavad Gita  (d) Manusmriti
**Answer: (b) Mundaka Upanishad**

**Q10.** The motto of the Indian Air Force is —
(a) Service Before Self  (b) Nabha Sparsham Deeptam  (c) Sham No Varunah  (d) Touch the Sky
**Answer: (b) Nabha Sparsham Deeptam** ("Touch the Sky with Glory")

**Q11.** "Kaushal Bharat, Kushal Bharat" is the slogan of —
(a) Make in India  (b) Skill India  (c) Startup India  (d) Digital India
**Answer: (b) Skill India Mission**

**Q12.** Which sporting league uses the tagline "Where Talent Meets Opportunity"?
(a) ISL  (b) Pro Kabaddi  (c) IPL  (d) Ranji Trophy
**Answer: (c) Indian Premier League (IPL)**

**Answer: (a) Sham No Varunah**

(a) Swachh Bharat  (b) Make in India (manufacturing)  (c) Digital India  (d) Skill India
