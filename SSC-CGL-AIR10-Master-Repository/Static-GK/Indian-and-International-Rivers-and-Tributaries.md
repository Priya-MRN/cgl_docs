# Indian and International Rivers and Tributaries

## 1. Major Indian Rivers and Their Tributaries

### A. Himalayan Rivers

| River | Major Tributaries | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ganga | Yamuna, Son, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi, Gomti, Chambal, Betwa, Ken | Largest river system of North India |
| Brahmaputra | Dibang, Lohit, Subansiri, Jiabharali, Manas, Tista | Important river of Northeast India |
| Indus | Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej | Originates in Tibet, flows through India and Pakistan |

### B. Peninsular Rivers

| River | Major Tributaries | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Godavari | Pravara, Manjira, Wainganga, Wardha, Pranhita, Indravati, Sabari | Longest peninsular river |
| Krishna | Tungabhadra, Bhima, Koyna, Musi, Ghataprabha | Major river of South India |
| Kaveri | Kabini, Bhavani, Amaravati, Hemavati, Arkavati | Famous for Cauvery delta |
| Narmada | Tawa, Barna, Banjar, Hiran | West-flowing river |
| Tapi | Purna, Girna, Panjhara | West-flowing river |
| Mahanadi | Seonath, Jonk, Hasdeo, Ib, Tel | Important river of Chhattisgarh and Odisha |

## 3. Rivers Shared by India with Neighboring Countries

| River | Shared With | Important Note |
|---|---|---|
| Indus | Pakistan | Starts in Tibet, enters India and then flows to Pakistan |
| Ganga | Bangladesh | Shared river system; important for both India and Bangladesh |
| Brahmaputra | China, Bangladesh | Known as Yarlung Tsangpo in Tibet |
| Teesta | Bangladesh | Important transboundary river in Northeast India |
| Mahakali | Nepal | Forms the India-Nepal boundary |
| Kosi | Nepal | Also called the Sorrow of Bihar |
| Gandak | Nepal | Important river in northern Bihar and Nepal |
| Sutlej | Pakistan | Flows from Tibet through India into Pakistan |

## 4. Quick Memory Points

- The three major Himalayan river systems are Ganga, Brahmaputra, and Indus.
- Godavari, Krishna, and Kaveri are the major peninsular rivers.
- Narmada and Tapi are important west-flowing rivers.
- India shares important rivers with Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, and China.
