## C. Major Multi-Sport Games (incl. Commonwealth, Olympics, Asian)

| Games | First held | Frequency | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Olympic Games (Summer)** | 1896, Athens | Every 4 years | IOC (1894, HQ Lausanne, Switzerland); motto *Citius, Altius, Fortius – Communiter*; 5 rings = 5 continents |
| **Winter Olympics** | 1924, Chamonix (France) | Every 4 years | Held in the gap years from Summer Olympics since 1994 |
| **Paralympic Games** | 1960, Rome | Every 4 years | For athletes with disabilities; motto "Spirit in Motion" |
| **Commonwealth Games** | 1930, Hamilton (Canada) | Every 4 years | Among Commonwealth nations; motto "Humanity, Equality, Destiny"; once "British Empire Games" |
| **Asian Games (Asiad)** | 1951, **New Delhi (India)** | Every 4 years | Organised by OCA; motto "Ever Onward"; India hosted 1951 & 1982 (both Delhi) |
| **SAF / South Asian Games** | 1984, Kathmandu | ~biennial | South Asian nations |
| **Youth Olympic Games** | 2010, Singapore | Every 4 years | For ages 15–18 |

**Olympics quick facts**
- Modern Olympics founder: **Pierre de Coubertin**; IOC founded **1894**, HQ **Lausanne, Switzerland**.
- India first participated in 1900 (one athlete, Norman Pritchard); first as a team in 1920.
- India's national sport association for Olympics: **IOA** (Indian Olympic Association).

## MCQs

**Q1.** The Ranji Trophy is associated with which sport?
(a) Hockey  (b) Football  (c) Cricket  (d) Badminton
**Answer: (c) Cricket**

**Q2.** The Thomas Cup is the world team championship in badminton for —
(a) Women  (b) Men  (c) Mixed teams  (d) Juniors
**Answer: (b) Men** (Uber Cup → women; Sudirman Cup → mixed)

**Q3.** The Subroto Cup is associated with which sport?
(a) Cricket  (b) Football  (c) Hockey  (d) Volleyball
**Answer: (b) Football** (inter-school tournament)

**Q4.** The Santosh Trophy is contested in —
(a) Football  (b) Hockey  (c) Kabaddi  (d) Basketball
**Answer: (a) Football** (domestic, state teams)

**Q5.** The Davis Cup is associated with —
(a) Golf  (b) Tennis  (c) Polo  (d) Sailing
**Answer: (b) Tennis** (men's international team event)

**Q6.** The Swaythling Cup and Corbillon Cup belong to which sport?
(a) Badminton  (b) Squash  (c) Table Tennis  (d) Chess
**Answer: (c) Table Tennis** (men's & women's respectively)

**Q7.** The Ryder Cup is contested in —
(a) Golf  (b) Cricket  (c) Cycling  (d) Rowing
**Answer: (a) Golf**

**Q8.** The Durand Cup, one of the oldest football tournaments in the world, is held in —
(a) England  (b) India  (c) Brazil  (d) Germany
**Answer: (b) India** (started 1888; Asia's oldest football tournament)

**Q9.** The first modern Olympic Games (1896) were held in —
(a) Paris  (b) London  (c) Athens  (d) Rome
**Answer: (c) Athens, Greece**

**Q10.** The first Asian Games (1951) were hosted by —
(a) Tokyo  (b) New Delhi  (c) Bangkok  (d) Jakarta
**Answer: (b) New Delhi, India**

**Q11.** The Commonwealth Games were earlier known as —
(a) Empire Olympics  (b) British Empire Games  (c) Imperial Games  (d) Allied Games
**Answer: (b) British Empire Games**

**Q12.** The Border–Gavaskar Trophy is contested between India and —
(a) England  (b) Australia  (c) South Africa  (d) New Zealand
**Answer: (b) Australia** (cricket Tests)

**Q13.** The number of players in a Kabaddi team (on court) is —
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 7  (d) 9
**Answer: (c) 7**

**Q14.** The Webb Ellis Cup is awarded in which sport?
(a) Rugby  (b) American Football  (c) Cricket  (d) Hockey
**Answer: (a) Rugby (World Cup)**

**Q15.** The Sultan Azlan Shah Cup is associated with —
(a) Hockey  (b) Squash  (c) Badminton  (d) Football
**Answer: (a) Hockey**

## SSC PYQ Pattern

**Q1.** Which of the following trophies is NOT associated with cricket? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Duleep Trophy  (b) Irani Trophy  (c) Santosh Trophy  (d) Vijay Hazare Trophy
**Answer: (c) Santosh Trophy** (football)

**Q2.** The Uber Cup is associated with which sport? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Badminton  (b) Tennis  (c) Table Tennis  (d) Squash
**Answer: (a) Badminton (women's team)**

**Q3.** India hosted the Asian Games in 1951 and again in 1982 in which city? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Mumbai  (b) Kolkata  (c) New Delhi  (d) Chennai
**Answer: (c) New Delhi**

**Q4.** The Stanley Cup is the championship trophy of which sport? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Ice Hockey  (b) Baseball  (c) Basketball  (d) American Football
**Answer: (a) Ice Hockey**

**Q5.** The headquarters of the International Olympic Committee (IOC) is in — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Paris  (b) Lausanne  (c) Geneva  (d) Zurich
**Answer: (b) Lausanne, Switzerland**
