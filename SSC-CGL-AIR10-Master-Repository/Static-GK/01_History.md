## Quick Notes

**Ancient India — Indus Valley Civilisation (IVC)**
- Period: c. 2500–1700 BCE (Mature Harappan c. 2600–1900 BCE); Bronze Age, urban civilisation.
- Discovered: 1921 — Harappa (by Daya Ram Sahni); 1922 — Mohenjodaro (by R. D. Banerji), both on the Ravi and Indus respectively (now in Pakistan).
- Town planning: grid pattern, burnt-brick houses, Great Bath (Mohenjodaro), Great Granary, covered drainage.
- Key sites: Harappa (Ravi), Mohenjodaro (Indus), Lothal (Gujarat — dockyard), Kalibangan (Rajasthan — ploughed field, fire altars), Dholavira (Gujarat — water management), Rakhigarhi (Haryana — largest site in India).
- Script: pictographic, undeciphered, written right to left (boustrophedon). Worshipped Pashupati (proto-Shiva), Mother Goddess. No temples found.

**Vedic Period**
- Rigveda — oldest Veda; Samaveda (music), Yajurveda (rituals), Atharvaveda (magic/medicine).
- Gayatri Mantra is from the Rigveda (dedicated to Savitri/Sun).
- "Satyameva Jayate" — taken from the Mundaka Upanishad.

**Mahajanapadas, Buddhism & Jainism**
- 16 Mahajanapadas; Magadha became the most powerful.
- Gautama Buddha: born 563 BCE at Lumbini (Nepal); Enlightenment at Bodh Gaya; first sermon at Sarnath (Dharmachakra Pravartana); death (Mahaparinirvana) at Kushinagar. Founder of Buddhism. Four Noble Truths; Eightfold Path.
- Mahavira (24th Tirthankara): born at Kundagrama (Vaishali); attained Kaivalya; Three Jewels (Triratna) of Jainism. First Tirthankara: Rishabhanatha.

**Mauryan Empire (322–185 BCE)**
- Founder: Chandragupta Maurya (322 BCE), aided by Chanakya (Kautilya), author of *Arthashastra*. Capital: Pataliputra.
- Megasthenes (Greek ambassador of Seleucus) wrote *Indica*.
- Ashoka the Great (268–232 BCE): Kalinga War (261 BCE) led to his conversion to Buddhism. Edicts in Brahmi/Kharoshthi script. The Sarnath Lion Capital is India's National Emblem; the Dharmachakra is on the national flag.
- Last Mauryan ruler Brihadratha killed by Pushyamitra Shunga (founded Shunga dynasty).

**Gupta Empire (Golden Age, c. 320–550 CE)**
- Founder: Sri Gupta; Chandragupta I started the Gupta era (319–320 CE).
- Samudragupta — "Napoleon of India" (Allahabad/Prayag Prashasti by Harisena).
- Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya): Fa-Hien (Chinese pilgrim) visited; Navaratnas including Kalidasa (*Abhijnanasakuntalam*, *Meghaduta*).
- Aryabhata (concept of zero, value of pi), Varahamihira flourished. Iron Pillar of Delhi (Mehrauli).
- Nalanda University patronised by Kumaragupta I.

**Other Ancient**
- Harshavardhana (606–647 CE): capital Kannauj; Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang) visited; wrote *Nagananda*, *Ratnavali*.
- Cholas: Rajaraja I built Brihadeeswarar Temple (Thanjavur); strong navy.

**Medieval — Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526)**
- Slave/Mamluk dynasty (1206–1290): Qutb-ud-din Aibak (founder, began Qutub Minar). Iltutmish (real consolidator, introduced silver tanka & copper jital). Razia Sultan (first & only woman ruler of Delhi). Balban ("Zil-i-Ilahi").
- Khilji dynasty (1290–1320): Alauddin Khilji — market/price control, repelled Mongols; general Malik Kafur invaded the south.
- Tughlaq dynasty (1320–1414): Muhammad-bin-Tughlaq — transferred capital Delhi→Daulatabad, token (copper) currency. Firoz Shah Tughlaq — built canals, Firozabad.
- Sayyid (1414–1451) and Lodi (1451–1526) dynasties. Ibrahim Lodi defeated and killed by Babur at the First Battle of Panipat (1526).

**Vijayanagara & Bahmani**
- Vijayanagara founded 1336 by Harihara & Bukka (Sangama dynasty); Krishnadevaraya (peak); capital Hampi; defeated at the Battle of Talikota (1565).

**Mughal Empire (1526–1857)**
- Babur (1526) — founder; wrote *Baburnama* (Turki). First Battle of Panipat (1526).
- Humayun — defeated by Sher Shah Suri (Battle of Chausa 1539, Kannauj 1540); Sher Shah built the Grand Trunk Road, introduced the rupiya.
- Akbar (1556–1605): Second Battle of Panipat (1556); Din-i-Ilahi; Mansabdari system; abolished jizya; Navaratnas (Birbal, Tansen, Todar Mal, Abul Fazl who wrote *Akbarnama*/*Ain-i-Akbari*). Built Fatehpur Sikri, Buland Darwaza.
- Jahangir — Nur Jahan; English envoy Sir Thomas Roe.
- Shah Jahan — Taj Mahal (Agra), Red Fort & Jama Masjid (Delhi); peak of Mughal architecture.
- Aurangzeb (Alamgir) — last great Mughal; re-imposed jizya; greatest territorial extent; conflict with Marathas and Sikhs. Bahadur Shah Zafar was the last Mughal emperor.

**Marathas**
- Shivaji crowned 1674 at Raigad; founder of the Maratha empire; ashtapradhan (council of 8).

**Modern India — European Arrival & British Rule**
- Battle of Plassey (1757): Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah → British foothold in Bengal.
- Battle of Buxar (1764): British victory gave Diwani rights of Bengal, Bihar, Odisha (1765).

**Revolt of 1857 (First War of Independence)**
- Began 10 May 1857 at Meerut; immediate cause — greased cartridges (Enfield rifle). Mangal Pandey (Barrackpore) was an early figure.
- Leaders: Delhi — Bahadur Shah Zafar; Kanpur — Nana Sahib & Tantia Tope; Jhansi — Rani Lakshmibai; Lucknow — Begum Hazrat Mahal; Bihar — Kunwar Singh.
- Result: End of Company rule; Government of India Act 1858 transferred power to the British Crown.

**Indian National Congress (INC) & Sessions**
- Founded 1885 by A. O. Hume; first session Bombay 1885, president W. C. Bonnerjee.
- Surat Split (1907) — Moderates vs Extremists.
- Lahore Session (1929) — "Purna Swaraj" (complete independence); 26 Jan 1930 first Independence Day. President: Jawaharlal Nehru.
- Karachi Session (1931) — Fundamental Rights resolution.
- First woman INC president: Annie Besant (1917); first Indian woman: Sarojini Naidu (1925).

**Freedom Struggle — Movements & Dates**
- Partition of Bengal: 1905 → Swadeshi Movement.
- Home Rule Movement (1916): Tilak & Annie Besant.
- Jallianwala Bagh Massacre: 13 April 1919, Amritsar (General Dyer).
- Non-Cooperation Movement: 1920–22; called off after Chauri Chaura incident (Feb 1922).
- Simon Commission: 1928 ("Simon Go Back"); Lala Lajpat Rai injured.
- Civil Disobedience / Dandi (Salt) March: 12 March – 6 April 1930 (Sabarmati to Dandi).
- Round Table Conferences: 1930, 1931, 1932 (London). Gandhi attended the second.
- Poona Pact: 1932 (Gandhi–Ambedkar) on separate electorates for Depressed Classes.
- Quit India Movement: 8 August 1942 ("Do or Die"); Bombay session.
- INA (Azad Hind Fauj): led by Subhas Chandra Bose; slogan "Dilli Chalo", "Jai Hind".
- Independence: 15 August 1947 (Indian Independence Act 1947, Mountbatten Plan).

## MCQs

**Q1.** Which Indus Valley site is known for its dockyard?
(a) Harappa  (b) Lothal  (c) Kalibangan  (d) Dholavira
**Answer: (b) Lothal**

**Q2.** The author of *Arthashastra* was —
(a) Megasthenes  (b) Kalidasa  (c) Chanakya (Kautilya)  (d) Banabhatta
**Answer: (c) Chanakya (Kautilya)**

**Q3.** The Kalinga War was fought by which Mauryan ruler?
(a) Chandragupta Maurya  (b) Bindusara  (c) Ashoka  (d) Brihadratha
**Answer: (c) Ashoka**

**Q4.** Who is known as the "Napoleon of India"?
(a) Chandragupta II  (b) Samudragupta  (c) Skandagupta  (d) Kumaragupta
**Answer: (b) Samudragupta**

**Q5.** Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment at —
(a) Lumbini  (b) Sarnath  (c) Bodh Gaya  (d) Kushinagar
**Answer: (c) Bodh Gaya**
*Note:* Born at Lumbini, first sermon at Sarnath, death at Kushinagar.

**Q6.** The first and only woman ruler of the Delhi Sultanate was —
(a) Nur Jahan  (b) Razia Sultan  (c) Chand Bibi  (d) Rani Durgavati
**Answer: (b) Razia Sultan**

**Q7.** Who transferred the capital from Delhi to Daulatabad?
(a) Alauddin Khilji  (b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq  (c) Muhammad-bin-Tughlaq  (d) Balban
**Answer: (c) Muhammad-bin-Tughlaq**

**Q8.** The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between Babur and —
(a) Rana Sanga  (b) Ibrahim Lodi  (c) Sher Shah Suri  (d) Hemu
**Answer: (b) Ibrahim Lodi**

**Q9.** Who built the Grand Trunk Road and introduced the rupiya?
(a) Akbar  (b) Sher Shah Suri  (c) Babur  (d) Aurangzeb
**Answer: (b) Sher Shah Suri**

**Q10.** The Taj Mahal was built during the reign of —
(a) Akbar  (b) Jahangir  (c) Shah Jahan  (d) Aurangzeb
**Answer: (c) Shah Jahan**

**Q11.** The Battle of Plassey (1757) was won by the British under —
(a) Warren Hastings  (b) Robert Clive  (c) Lord Dalhousie  (d) Lord Wellesley
**Answer: (b) Robert Clive**

**Q12.** The Revolt of 1857 began at —
(a) Delhi  (b) Kanpur  (c) Meerut  (d) Jhansi
**Answer: (c) Meerut**

**Q13.** The Indian National Congress was founded in —
(a) 1857  (b) 1885  (c) 1905  (d) 1919
**Answer: (b) 1885**

**Q14.** The Jallianwala Bagh massacre took place in —
(a) 1909  (b) 1919  (c) 1929  (d) 1942
**Answer: (b) 1919**

**Q15.** "Do or Die" was the slogan associated with which movement?
(a) Non-Cooperation  (b) Civil Disobedience  (c) Quit India  (d) Swadeshi
**Answer: (c) Quit India (1942)**
