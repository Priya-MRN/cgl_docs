## A. Indian Rivers

**Himalayan (perennial, snow + rain fed) vs Peninsular (rain-fed, seasonal)**

| River | Source | Mouth / Ends in | Length (km, approx) | Key notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ganga | Gangotri glacier (as Bhagirathi) | Bay of Bengal (Sundarbans delta) | 2,525 | Longest river *within* India; Alaknanda + Bhagirathi → Devprayag |
| Indus (Sindhu) | Near Lake Mansarovar, Tibet | Arabian Sea (Pakistan) | 3,180 (total) | Indus Water Treaty 1960 |
| Brahmaputra | Angsi glacier / Mansarovar, Tibet | Bay of Bengal | 2,900 (total) | Tsangpo (Tibet) → Dihang (Arunachal); braided, floods Assam |
| Godavari | Trimbakeshwar, Nashik (Maharashtra) | Bay of Bengal | 1,465 | **Longest peninsular river** — "Dakshin Ganga" |
| Krishna | Mahabaleshwar (Maharashtra) | Bay of Bengal | 1,400 | 2nd longest peninsular |
| Yamuna | Yamunotri glacier (Uttarakhand) | Joins Ganga at Prayagraj (Sangam) | 1,376 | **Longest tributary of the Ganga** |
| Narmada | Amarkantak (MP) | Arabian Sea (estuary) | 1,312 | West-flowing, rift valley; no delta |
| Kaveri (Cauvery) | Talakaveri, Brahmagiri (Karnataka) | Bay of Bengal | 800 | "Dakshin Bharat ki Ganga"; Tn–Karnataka dispute |
| Tapi (Tapti) | Multai / Satpura (MP) | Arabian Sea (estuary) | 724 | West-flowing, rift valley (parallel to Narmada) |
| Mahanadi | Sihawa (Chhattisgarh) | Bay of Bengal | 851 | Hirakud Dam (one of longest dams) |
| Sabarmati | Aravalli Hills (Rajasthan) | Gulf of Khambhat | 371 | Ahmedabad on its banks |
| Luni | Aravallis (Pushkar) | Rann of Kutch (saline marsh) | 495 | Only major river of the Thar; "salt river" |

**Key river facts (most-asked)**
- **Longest river in India:** Ganga (~2,525 km within India).
- **Longest peninsular river:** Godavari.
- **Longest tributary of the Ganga:** Yamuna.
- **West-flowing (Arabian Sea) rivers** form **estuaries, not deltas:** Narmada & Tapi (both in rift valleys); also Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni.
- **East-flowing (Bay of Bengal) rivers** form **deltas:** Ganga, Brahmaputra, Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri.
- **World's largest delta:** Sundarbans (Ganga–Brahmaputra), shared by India & Bangladesh.
- **Indus Water Treaty (1960, brokered by World Bank):** Eastern rivers — **Ravi, Beas, Sutlej** to India; Western rivers — **Indus, Jhelum, Chenab** to Pakistan.
- Tributaries of Ganga: Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi ("Sorrow of Bihar"), Son, Ramganga.
- Damodar = "Sorrow of Bengal" (now controlled by DVC).
- Confluences: Ganga + Yamuna (+ mythical Saraswati) = **Triveni Sangam, Prayagraj**.

## B. India's Borders & Neighbours

**India shares land borders with 7 countries.** (Afghanistan border touches PoK.)

| Neighbour | Border length (km, approx) | Bordering Indian states (key) |
|---|---|---|
| Bangladesh | 4,096 | **Longest** — WB, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram |
| China | 3,488 | Ladakh, HP, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal |
| Pakistan | 3,323 | Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, J&K, Ladakh |
| Nepal | 1,751 | UP, Uttarakhand, Bihar, WB, Sikkim |
| Myanmar | 1,643 | Arunachal, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram |
| Bhutan | 699 | Sikkim, WB, Assam, Arunachal |
| Afghanistan | 106 | Touches Ladakh (PoK region) — shortest land border |

**Border lines & key facts**
- **Radcliffe Line** — India–Pakistan & India–Bangladesh (1947, drawn by Sir Cyril Radcliffe).
- **Line of Control (LoC)** — India–Pakistan (in J&K, post-1972 Shimla Agreement).
- **Line of Actual Control (LAC)** — India–China.
- **McMahon Line** — India (Arunachal) – China/Tibet (1914 Simla Convention).
- **Durand Line** — Afghanistan–Pakistan (often confused; NOT an India border).
- States with **most international borders:** all of UP, Bihar, WB, Sikkim, Arunachal etc.; **only WB shares borders with 3 countries** (Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan).
- **Maritime neighbours:** Sri Lanka (separated by **Palk Strait** & Gulf of Mannar) and Maldives.
- India has a coastline of ~7,517 km; **Gujarat has the longest coastline** among states.
- States not touching any international border are landlocked-interior (e.g., MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Telangana, Haryana).

## MCQs

**Q1.** The longest peninsular river of India is —
(a) Krishna  (b) Godavari  (c) Kaveri  (d) Mahanadi
**Answer: (b) Godavari** (Dakshin Ganga)

**Q2.** India shares its longest international land border with —
(a) China  (b) Pakistan  (c) Bangladesh  (d) Nepal
**Answer: (c) Bangladesh** (~4,096 km)

**Q3.** The boundary line between India and Pakistan is the —
(a) McMahon Line  (b) Radcliffe Line  (c) Durand Line  (d) Maginot Line
**Answer: (b) Radcliffe Line**

**Q4.** The McMahon Line demarcates the border between India and —
(a) Pakistan  (b) Nepal  (c) China  (d) Myanmar
**Answer: (c) China** (Arunachal Pradesh sector)

**Q5.** Which Indian state shares its border with three countries (Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan)?
(a) Assam  (b) Sikkim  (c) West Bengal  (d) Bihar
**Answer: (c) West Bengal**

**Q6.** The Indus Water Treaty (1960) allots which rivers to India?
(a) Indus, Jhelum, Chenab  (b) Ravi, Beas, Sutlej  (c) Ganga, Yamuna, Kosi  (d) Narmada, Tapi, Mahi
**Answer: (b) Ravi, Beas, Sutlej**

**Q7.** The longest tributary of the Ganga is the —
(a) Kosi  (b) Yamuna  (c) Son  (d) Gandak
**Answer: (b) Yamuna**

**Q8.** The world's tallest statue is located in —
(a) China  (b) USA  (c) India  (d) Japan
**Answer: (c) India** (Statue of Unity, 182 m, Gujarat)

**Q9.** The deepest and oldest freshwater lake in the world is —
(a) Caspian Sea  (b) Lake Baikal  (c) Lake Titicaca  (d) Lake Victoria
**Answer: (b) Lake Baikal (Russia)**

**Q10.** The largest hot desert in the world is the —
(a) Gobi  (b) Thar  (c) Sahara  (d) Kalahari
**Answer: (c) Sahara**

**Q11.** The tallest building in the world is —
(a) Shanghai Tower  (b) Burj Khalifa  (c) Petronas Towers  (d) Taipei 101
**Answer: (b) Burj Khalifa (Dubai, 828 m)**

**Q12.** Which is the largest country in the world by area?
(a) Canada  (b) China  (c) USA  (d) Russia
**Answer: (d) Russia**

**Q13.** The smallest country in the world (area) is —
(a) Monaco  (b) Nauru  (c) Vatican City  (d) San Marino
**Answer: (c) Vatican City**

**Q14.** India's shortest international land border is with —
(a) Bhutan  (b) Afghanistan  (c) Myanmar  (d) Nepal
**Answer: (b) Afghanistan** (~106 km, via PoK)

**Q15.** Which water body separates India from Sri Lanka?
(a) Palk Strait  (b) Bay of Bengal  (c) Gulf of Khambhat  (d) Andaman Sea
**Answer: (a) Palk Strait** (& Gulf of Mannar)

## SSC PYQ Pattern

**Q1.** The Narmada and Tapi rivers drain into the — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Bay of Bengal  (b) Arabian Sea  (c) Indian Ocean  (d) Gulf of Bengal
**Answer: (b) Arabian Sea** (west-flowing, form estuaries)

**Q2.** The Statue of Unity is dedicated to — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Mahatma Gandhi  (b) B.R. Ambedkar  (c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel  (d) Jawaharlal Nehru
**Answer: (c) Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel**

**Q3.** Which state of India has the longest coastline? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Tamil Nadu  (b) Andhra Pradesh  (c) Gujarat  (d) Maharashtra
**Answer: (c) Gujarat**

**Q4.** The highest mountain peak located entirely within India is — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Kanchenjunga  (b) Nanda Devi  (c) K2  (d) Everest
**Answer: (b) Nanda Devi** (Kanchenjunga is on the India–Nepal border)

**Q5.** "Sorrow of Bihar" refers to which river? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Son  (b) Kosi  (c) Gandak  (d) Damodar
**Answer: (b) Kosi** (Damodar = "Sorrow of Bengal")
