## 3. Summits & Diplomacy

### 2.1 G20 Summit
1. Date: 9–10 September 2023. Location: New Delhi, India. Importance: India hosted the G20 summit for the first time, giving a strong push to the voice of the Global South. Key details: The summit focused on inclusive growth, digital public infrastructure, food security, and green development. Important persons: Prime Minister Narendra Modi, President Joe Biden, President Emmanuel Macron, PM Rishi Sunak, and other world leaders.

### 2.2 BRICS Summit
2. Date: 22–24 August 2023 (Johannesburg) and 22–24 October 2024 (Kazan). Location: South Africa and Russia. Importance: BRICS remained an important platform for emerging economies and multilateral coordination. Key details: The grouping expanded its relevance in trade, development finance, and de-dollarisation debates. Important persons: Narendra Modi, Cyril Ramaphosa, Vladimir Putin, and Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva.

### 2.3 SCO Summit
3. Date: 4 July 2023. Location: New Delhi, India. Importance: The SCO summit highlighted security, connectivity, and regional cooperation in Eurasia. Key details: India promoted dialogue on terrorism, trade, and regional stability. Important persons: Prime Minister Narendra Modi, President Xi Jinping, President Vladimir Putin, and other SCO leaders.

### 2.4 COP Summits
4. Date: COP28 – 30 November to 12 December 2023; COP29 – 11 to 22 November 2024. Location: Dubai and Baku. Importance: Climate diplomacy remained central to global governance. Key details: The discussions focused on loss and damage funding, energy transition, and climate finance. Important persons: UN Climate Chief Simon Stiell, COP Presidencies, and national ministers from major economies.

### 2.5 Quad Meet
5. Date: 21 September 2024. Location: Wilmington, Delaware, USA. Importance: The Quad continued to deepen Indo-Pacific cooperation. Key details: The meeting discussed critical and emerging technologies, supply chains, infrastructure, and maritime security. Important persons: Prime Minister Narendra Modi, President Joe Biden, PM Anthony Albanese, and PM Fumio Kishida.

## 5. Research Data & Science

### 5.1 ISRO Missions
1. Date: 2023–2024. Location: India and space around Earth and the Moon. Importance: ISRO continued to strengthen India’s space capabilities. Key details: Missions like Chandrayaan-3 and Aditya-L1 were major milestones in lunar and solar research. Important persons: ISRO Chairman S. Somanath, scientists, and mission directors.

### 5.2 Chandrayaan-3
2. Date: 14 July 2023. Location: Moon’s south polar region. Importance: India became the first nation to land near the Moon’s south pole. Key details: The mission proved India’s precision landing and rover capabilities. Important persons: ISRO scientists, including S. Somanath and mission team members.

### 5.3 Aditya-L1
3. Date: 2 September 2023. Location: Sun-Earth L1 point. Importance: India launched its first dedicated solar observatory mission. Key details: The mission provided critical information about the Sun’s corona and space weather. Important persons: ISRO scientists and space physicists.

### 5.4 Genome Research and AI
4. Date: 2024–2025. Location: India and global research centres. Importance: India expanded research in genomics, AI, semiconductors, and digital health. Key details: These sectors are important for innovation, industry, and national competitiveness. Important persons: Indian scientists, tech leaders, and research institutions.
