## Quick Notes

**Physics — Motion & Force**
- Newton's Laws: 1st (inertia); 2nd (F = ma, force = rate of change of momentum); 3rd (action–reaction).
- Speed = distance/time (scalar); Velocity = displacement/time (vector); Acceleration = change in velocity/time.
- Momentum = mass × velocity (p = mv). Work = Force × displacement (W = F·d). Power = Work/time.
- Gravitational acceleration g ≈ 9.8 m/s². Escape velocity of Earth ≈ 11.2 km/s.

**Light & Optics**
- Light travels in straight lines; speed in vacuum ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
- Reflection (mirrors) and Refraction (bending in a medium). A concave mirror converges; convex mirror diverges (used as rear-view mirrors — wider field).
- A convex lens converges (used to correct hypermetropia/long-sightedness); a concave lens diverges (corrects myopia/short-sightedness).
- Dispersion: white light splits into VIBGYOR (Violet–Indigo–Blue–Green–Yellow–Orange–Red) through a prism. Red bends least, Violet most.
- The sky appears blue due to the scattering of light (Rayleigh scattering). Sunsets are red because red scatters least.

**Sound**
- Sound is a longitudinal mechanical wave; needs a medium (cannot travel in vacuum).
- Speed of sound in air ≈ 343 m/s (at 20 °C); faster in solids than liquids than gases.
- Audible range: 20 Hz – 20,000 Hz. Below 20 Hz = infrasonic; above 20 kHz = ultrasonic (used in SONAR).

**Electricity & Magnetism**
- Ohm's Law: V = IR (Voltage = Current × Resistance).
- Power: P = VI = I²R. Unit of electrical energy: kilowatt-hour (kWh) = 1 unit.
- Conductor (low resistance) vs Insulator. Fuse melts to protect circuits.
- AC (alternating) used for transmission; DC (direct) from batteries.

**Important SI Units**
- Force → Newton (N); Energy/Work → Joule (J); Power → Watt (W); Pressure → Pascal (Pa); Frequency → Hertz (Hz); Electric current → Ampere (A); Resistance → Ohm (Ω); Charge → Coulomb (C); Temperature → Kelvin (K); Magnetic flux density → Tesla (T); Radioactivity → Becquerel.

**Chemistry — Acids, Bases & Salts**
- Acids: sour, turn blue litmus red, pH < 7 (e.g., HCl, H₂SO₄, citric acid).
- Bases/Alkalis: bitter, slippery, turn red litmus blue, pH > 7 (e.g., NaOH, NH₄OH).
- pH scale: 0–14; pure water is neutral (pH 7). Acid + base → salt + water (neutralisation).
- Strongest acid (common): sulphuric acid ("King of Chemicals"). Baking soda = sodium bicarbonate (NaHCO₃); washing soda = sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃).

**Elements & Periodic Table**
- Mendeleev proposed the Periodic Table (by atomic mass); modern table by Moseley (by atomic number).
- Atom = protons (+), neutrons (neutral) in the nucleus, electrons (−) in shells. Atomic number = number of protons.
- Most abundant element in Earth's crust: Oxygen; in the universe: Hydrogen.
- Lightest element: Hydrogen; lightest metal: Lithium; heaviest naturally occurring: Uranium.
- Mercury (Hg) — only metal liquid at room temperature; Bromine — only liquid non-metal.
- Diamond & Graphite — allotropes of carbon (diamond hardest natural substance).
- Common formulas: Water H₂O; Common salt NaCl; CO₂ (carbon dioxide); Ozone O₃; Quicklime CaO; Slaked lime Ca(OH)₂.

**Biology — Human Body**
- Cell = basic unit of life (Robert Hooke coined "cell"). Mitochondria = "powerhouse of the cell"; Ribosomes = protein synthesis.
- Largest organ: Skin; largest internal organ & gland: Liver; smallest bone: Stapes (ear); longest bone: Femur (thigh); strongest muscle: Masseter (jaw).
- Total bones in an adult: 206 (a baby has ~300). Blood: plasma + RBCs (carry O₂ via haemoglobin) + WBCs (immunity) + platelets (clotting).
- Universal donor: O− ; universal recipient: AB+. Normal human body temperature: 37 °C (98.6 °F).
- The heart has 4 chambers; pumps blood; normal pulse ~72/min. Kidneys filter blood (form urine). Insulin (from the pancreas) regulates blood sugar.

**Vitamins & Deficiency Diseases**
- Vitamin A (Retinol) → night blindness; Vitamin B1 (Thiamine) → Beriberi; B3 → Pellagra; B12 → anaemia.
- Vitamin C (Ascorbic acid) → Scurvy; Vitamin D (Calciferol — "sunshine vitamin") → Rickets (children)/Osteomalacia; Vitamin K → poor blood clotting.
- Fat-soluble vitamins: A, D, E, K. Water-soluble: B, C.

**Diseases**
- Viral: AIDS, Influenza, Dengue, Chickenpox, Polio, Rabies, COVID-19, Measles, Mumps.
- Bacterial: Tuberculosis (TB), Cholera, Typhoid, Tetanus, Leprosy, Plague.
- Protozoan: Malaria (female Anopheles mosquito; Plasmodium), Kala-azar.
- Deficiency: Goitre (iodine deficiency), Anaemia (iron deficiency).

**Nutrition**
- Macronutrients: Carbohydrates (energy), Proteins (growth/repair — "body-building"), Fats (energy store).
- Proteins are made of amino acids. Vitamins & minerals are micronutrients.
- Plants make food by photosynthesis (chlorophyll + sunlight + CO₂ + water → glucose + O₂).

## MCQs

**Q1.** The SI unit of force is the —
(a) Joule  (b) Watt  (c) Newton  (d) Pascal
**Answer: (c) Newton**

**Q2.** Ohm's Law is expressed as —
(a) V = I/R  (b) V = IR  (c) I = VR  (d) R = VI
**Answer: (b) V = IR**

**Q3.** The bending of light as it passes from one medium to another is called —
(a) reflection  (b) refraction  (c) dispersion  (d) diffraction
**Answer: (b) refraction**

**Q4.** The sky appears blue due to —
(a) reflection  (b) refraction  (c) scattering of light  (d) absorption
**Answer: (c) scattering of light**

**Q5.** The only metal that is liquid at room temperature is —
(a) Bromine  (b) Mercury  (c) Sodium  (d) Lithium
**Answer: (b) Mercury**

**Q6.** The pH value of a neutral solution is —
(a) 0  (b) 7  (c) 10  (d) 14
**Answer: (b) 7**

**Q7.** Which gas turns lime water milky?
(a) Oxygen  (b) Hydrogen  (c) Carbon dioxide  (d) Nitrogen
**Answer: (c) Carbon dioxide**

**Q8.** Diamond and graphite are allotropes of —
(a) Sulphur  (b) Carbon  (c) Phosphorus  (d) Silicon
**Answer: (b) Carbon**

**Q9.** The powerhouse of the cell is the —
(a) Nucleus  (b) Ribosome  (c) Mitochondria  (d) Golgi body
**Answer: (c) Mitochondria**

**Q10.** The number of bones in an adult human body is —
(a) 196  (b) 206  (c) 216  (d) 300
**Answer: (b) 206**

**Q11.** Deficiency of Vitamin C causes —
(a) Rickets  (b) Scurvy  (c) Night blindness  (d) Beriberi
**Answer: (b) Scurvy**

**Q12.** Which vitamin is known as the "sunshine vitamin"?
(a) Vitamin A  (b) Vitamin C  (c) Vitamin D  (d) Vitamin K
**Answer: (c) Vitamin D**

**Q13.** Malaria is caused by which organism transmitted by the female Anopheles mosquito?
(a) Bacteria  (b) Virus  (c) Plasmodium (protozoan)  (d) Fungus
**Answer: (c) Plasmodium (protozoan)**

**Q14.** Which blood group is the universal donor?
(a) AB+  (b) O−  (c) A+  (d) B−
**Answer: (b) O−**

**Q15.** The hormone that regulates blood sugar is —
(a) Adrenaline  (b) Thyroxine  (c) Insulin  (d) Oestrogen
**Answer: (c) Insulin**

## SSC PYQ Pattern

**Q1.** The unit of electrical energy consumed (1 unit) is the — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Watt  (b) Volt  (c) Kilowatt-hour  (d) Ampere
**Answer: (c) Kilowatt-hour (kWh)**

**Q2.** Which of the following is a bacterial disease? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Dengue  (b) Tuberculosis  (c) AIDS  (d) Influenza
**Answer: (b) Tuberculosis**
*Note:* Dengue, AIDS, and Influenza are viral.

**Q3.** The most abundant element in the Earth's crust is — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Silicon  (b) Oxygen  (c) Aluminium  (d) Iron
**Answer: (b) Oxygen**

**Q4.** Goitre is caused by the deficiency of — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Iron  (b) Iodine  (c) Calcium  (d) Vitamin D
**Answer: (b) Iodine**

**Q5.** Which mirror is used as a vehicle rear-view mirror? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Concave  (b) Convex  (c) Plane  (d) Cylindrical
**Answer: (b) Convex (gives a wider field of view)**
