Exam tip: the classic trap is a term shared across sports — Smash (tennis/badminton/volleyball/TT), Hat-trick (cricket/football/hockey), Bunker (golf/polo), Deuce (tennis/badminton/volleyball/TT). When asked "which sport uses X", pick the sport where the term is most specific (e.g. Googly → only Cricket; Chukker → only Polo; En passant → only Chess).
