## Quick Notes

**Classical Dances of India (8 recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi)**
- Bharatanatyam — Tamil Nadu (oldest classical dance).
- Kathak — Uttar Pradesh / North India.
- Kathakali — Kerala (elaborate face make-up & masks; story-play).
- Mohiniyattam — Kerala (solo, graceful, by women).
- Kuchipudi — Andhra Pradesh.
- Odissi — Odisha.
- Manipuri — Manipur.
- Sattriya — Assam (introduced by saint Sankardev).
- Folk dances: Bhangra/Giddha (Punjab), Garba/Dandiya (Gujarat), Bihu (Assam), Lavani (Maharashtra), Ghoomar (Rajasthan), Chhau (Jharkhand/Odisha/WB), Yakshagana (Karnataka), Kalbelia (Rajasthan).

**Festivals (state association)**
- Onam, Vishu — Kerala; Pongal — Tamil Nadu; Bihu — Assam; Baisakhi/Lohri — Punjab.
- Hornbill Festival — Nagaland; Hemis Festival — Ladakh; Chhath Puja — Bihar; Rann Utsav — Gujarat; Suras (Konark Dance) — Odisha.
- Pushkar Fair — Rajasthan; Thrissur Pooram — Kerala.

**Important Days (National & International)**

- 8 Mar — International Women's Day; 22 Mar — World Water Day; 21 Mar — World Forestry Day.
- 7 Apr — World Health Day (WHO foundation); 22 Apr — Earth Day.

### Important Days — Detailed Reference Table

> Source: improvised and expanded from [gknow.in — Important Days Current Affairs](https://gknow.in/category/current-affairs/important-days-current-affairs/). High-yield for SSC CGL General Awareness.

| Date | Day Name | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 4 January | World Braille Day | Observed to mark the birth anniversary of Louis Braille, the inventor of the Braille script. The day raises awareness about Braille as a vital means of communication for blind and visually impaired people. It highlights the importance of accessibility, inclusion, and equal rights for the visually challenged in education and society. |
| 9 January | Pravasi Bharatiya Diwas | Celebrates the contribution of the overseas Indian community to the development of India. The date commemorates the return of Mahatma Gandhi from South Africa to India in 1915. It strengthens the engagement of the Indian diaspora with their homeland and recognises their achievements abroad. |
| 12 January | National Youth Day | Marks the birth anniversary of Swami Vivekananda, the great spiritual leader and reformer. The day inspires the youth of India through his ideals of character-building, self-confidence, and nation-building. Various seminars, debates, and cultural events are organised to channel youth energy towards national development. |
| 14 January | Armed Forces Veterans' Day | Honours the service and sacrifice of India's military veterans. It commemorates the retirement of the first Indian Commander-in-Chief, Field Marshal K.M. Cariappa, in 1953. The day pays tribute to ex-servicemen and acknowledges their lifelong dedication to the nation. |
| 15 January | Army Day | Celebrates the Indian Army and its valour, discipline, and service to the nation. It marks the day in 1949 when Field Marshal K.M. Cariappa took over as the first Indian Commander-in-Chief from the British. Parades and military displays are held, especially at Delhi Cantonment. |
| 19 January | NDRF Raising Day | Commemorates the establishment of the National Disaster Response Force in 2006. The NDRF is a specialised force for disaster relief and emergency response during natural and man-made calamities. The day recognises the courage of NDRF personnel in saving lives during floods, earthquakes, and other disasters. |
| 23 January | Parakram Diwas | Observed on the birth anniversary of Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose. The day is a tribute to his courage, patriotism, and leadership in India's freedom struggle. It inspires citizens, especially the youth, to imbibe his spirit of selfless service to the nation. |
| 2 February | World Wetlands Day | Highlights the ecological importance of wetlands for biodiversity, water purification, and climate regulation. It marks the signing of the Ramsar Convention on Wetlands in 1971 in Iran. The day promotes conservation and sustainable use of wetland ecosystems worldwide. |
| 17 February | Global Tourism Resilience Day | Promotes the development of tourism that can withstand crises, disasters, and pandemics. It emphasises sustainable and resilient tourism practices for economic stability. The day was recognised by the United Nations to strengthen the tourism sector globally. |
| 20 February | World Day of Social Justice | Focuses on tackling poverty, unemployment, inequality, and social exclusion. It promotes fairness, human dignity, and equal opportunity for all. The day calls for collective action to remove barriers based on gender, age, race, religion, or culture. |
| 28 February | National Science Day | Commemorates the discovery of the "Raman Effect" by Sir C.V. Raman in 1928, for which he won the Nobel Prize in Physics. The day promotes scientific temper and awareness among students and the public. Science exhibitions, lectures, and quizzes are organised across the country. |
| 3 March | World Wildlife Day | Celebrates the world's wild fauna and flora and the need for their conservation. It marks the signing of the CITES convention (Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species) in 1973. The day raises awareness about protecting endangered species and combating wildlife crime. |
| 10 March | CISF Raising Day | Marks the founding of the Central Industrial Security Force. The CISF protects critical infrastructure such as airports, ports, metros, and government installations. The day honours the discipline and dedication of its personnel in safeguarding the nation's assets. |
| 21 March | Parsi New Year (Navroz) | Celebrated by the Zoroastrian community to mark the spring equinox and renewal of nature. "Navroz" means "new day" and symbolises fresh beginnings, hope, and prosperity. It is observed with prayers, feasts, and family gatherings. |
| 24 March | World Tuberculosis Day | Raises public awareness about the health, social, and economic impact of tuberculosis. It commemorates the discovery of the TB-causing bacterium by Dr. Robert Koch in 1882. The day aims to accelerate global efforts to end the TB epidemic. |
| 30 March | International Day of Zero Waste | Promotes sustainable consumption and responsible waste management. It encourages the 3Rs principle — Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle — to minimise waste generation. The day supports a circular economy and protection of the environment. |
| 7 April | World Health Day | Promotes global health awareness and access to healthcare for all. It marks the founding anniversary of the World Health Organization (WHO) in 1948. Each year focuses on a specific health theme of global concern. |
| 10 April | World Homoeopathy Day | Observed on the birth anniversary of Dr. Samuel Hahnemann, the founder of homoeopathy. The day celebrates the achievements and contributions of homoeopathic medicine. It promotes awareness about this alternative system of healing. |
| 14 April | Ambedkar Jayanti | Celebrates the birth anniversary of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, the chief architect of the Indian Constitution. The day honours his fight against social discrimination and for the upliftment of the marginalised. He is revered as a symbol of social justice and equality. |
| 21 April | World Creativity and Innovation Day | Encourages creative thinking and innovation to solve global challenges. It highlights the role of new ideas in social, economic, and sustainable development. The day inspires individuals and organisations to think differently and innovate. |
| 22 April | World Earth Day | Raises awareness about environmental protection and the health of the planet. It encourages climate action, sustainable living, and conservation of natural resources. The day mobilises people worldwide to protect the Earth for future generations. |
| 24 April | National Panchayati Raj Day | Marks the implementation of the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act in 1993, which gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj institutions. The day celebrates grassroots democracy and decentralised local governance. It recognises the role of Panchayats in rural development. |
| 21 May | International Tea Day | Recognises the cultural, economic, and historical significance of tea worldwide. It supports the livelihoods of millions of tea workers and farmers. The day promotes sustainable production and fair trade in the tea industry. |
| 22 May | International Day for Biological Diversity | Promotes awareness about the importance of biodiversity for life on Earth. It encourages conservation efforts at community, national, and global levels. The day highlights the threats of habitat loss, climate change, and species extinction. |
| 25 May | World Football Day | Celebrates football as a global sport that unites people across cultures. It marks the sport's appearance in the 1924 Paris Olympics. The day promotes the values of teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship. |
| 31 May | World No Tobacco Day | Raises awareness about the dangers of tobacco and nicotine addiction. It highlights the health risks of smoking and exposure to second-hand smoke. The day focuses on protecting youth from tobacco and e-cigarette use. |
| 5 June | World Environment Day | The largest global platform for environmental awareness, led by the UNEP. It encourages action to protect nature and combat pollution and climate change. Each year a host country and a specific environmental theme are chosen. |
| 21 June | International Day of Yoga | Celebrates the physical, mental, and spiritual benefits of yoga, an ancient Indian practice. It is observed on the summer solstice, the longest day of the year. Mass yoga sessions are held globally, with India leading the initiative since 2015. |
| 26 December | Veer Baal Diwas | Honours the supreme sacrifice of the younger sons (Sahibzades) of Guru Gobind Singh. The day commemorates their courage, faith, and martyrdom at a very young age. It inspires citizens to value bravery, sacrifice, and devotion to principles. |

## MCQs

**Q1.** Bharatanatyam is the classical dance form of —
(a) Kerala  (b) Tamil Nadu  (c) Odisha  (d) Andhra Pradesh
**Answer: (b) Tamil Nadu**

**Q2.** Kathakali is associated with which state?
(a) Karnataka  (b) Manipur  (c) Kerala  (d) Assam
**Answer: (c) Kerala**

**Q3.** Sattriya, a classical dance form, originated in —
(a) Assam  (b) Odisha  (c) West Bengal  (d) Manipur
**Answer: (a) Assam**

**Q4.** The Onam festival is celebrated mainly in —
(a) Tamil Nadu  (b) Kerala  (c) Karnataka  (d) Andhra Pradesh
**Answer: (b) Kerala**

**Q5.** International Yoga Day is observed on —
(a) 5 June  (b) 21 June  (c) 1 July  (d) 11 July
**Answer: (b) 21 June**

**Q6.** National Sports Day in India is celebrated on the birthday of —
(a) Sachin Tendulkar  (b) Major Dhyan Chand  (c) Milkha Singh  (d) Kapil Dev
**Answer: (b) Major Dhyan Chand (29 August)**

**Q7.** The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is in —
(a) New York  (b) Paris  (c) Geneva  (d) Rome
**Answer: (c) Geneva, Switzerland**

**Q8.** The headquarters of UNESCO is located in —

**Q9.** Who wrote the book *Wings of Fire*?

**Answer: (b) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam**

**Q10.** *Gitanjali* was written by —
(a) Rabindranath Tagore  (b) Munshi Premchand  (c) Khushwant Singh  (d) R. K. Narayan

**Q11.** Teachers' Day in India is celebrated on the birthday of —
