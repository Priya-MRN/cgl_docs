## Quick Notes

**Classical Dances of India (8 recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi)**
- Bharatanatyam — Tamil Nadu (oldest classical dance).
- Kathak — Uttar Pradesh / North India.
- Kathakali — Kerala (elaborate face make-up & masks; story-play).
- Mohiniyattam — Kerala (solo, graceful, by women).
- Kuchipudi — Andhra Pradesh.
- Odissi — Odisha.
- Manipuri — Manipur.
- Sattriya — Assam (introduced by saint Sankardev).
- Folk dances: Bhangra/Giddha (Punjab), Garba/Dandiya (Gujarat), Bihu (Assam), Lavani (Maharashtra), Ghoomar (Rajasthan), Chhau (Jharkhand/Odisha/WB), Yakshagana (Karnataka), Kalbelia (Rajasthan).

**Festivals (state association)**
- Onam, Vishu — Kerala; Pongal — Tamil Nadu; Bihu — Assam; Baisakhi/Lohri — Punjab.
- Hornbill Festival — Nagaland; Hemis Festival — Ladakh; Chhath Puja — Bihar; Rann Utsav — Gujarat; Suras (Konark Dance) — Odisha.
- Pushkar Fair — Rajasthan; Thrissur Pooram — Kerala.

**Important Days (National & International)**
- 12 Jan — National Youth Day (Swami Vivekananda's birthday); 26 Jan — Republic Day.
- 8 Mar — International Women's Day; 22 Mar — World Water Day; 21 Mar — World Forestry Day.
- 7 Apr — World Health Day (WHO foundation); 22 Apr — Earth Day.
- 1 May — Labour Day; 11 May — National Technology Day; 31 May — Anti-Tobacco Day.
- 5 Jun — World Environment Day; 21 Jun — International Yoga Day.
- 1 Jul — National Doctors' Day; 11 Jul — World Population Day.
- 15 Aug — Independence Day; 29 Aug — National Sports Day (Dhyan Chand's birthday).
- 5 Sep — Teachers' Day (S. Radhakrishnan); 8 Sep — World Literacy Day; 14 Sep — Hindi Diwas; 16 Sep — Ozone Day.
- 2 Oct — Gandhi Jayanti / International Day of Non-Violence; 24 Oct — UN Day; 31 Oct — National Unity Day (Sardar Patel).
- 14 Nov — Children's Day (Nehru's birthday); 26 Nov — Constitution Day; 1 Dec — World AIDS Day; 10 Dec — Human Rights Day.

**International Organizations & Headquarters**
- UN (United Nations) — New York, USA (founded 24 Oct 1945).
- WHO (World Health Organization) — Geneva, Switzerland.
- WTO (World Trade Organization) — Geneva, Switzerland.
- ILO, WMO, WIPO, UNHCR, Red Cross (ICRC) — Geneva, Switzerland.
- IMF & World Bank — Washington, D.C., USA.
- UNESCO — Paris, France; Interpol — Lyon, France.
- UNICEF — New York, USA; FAO — Rome, Italy; IAEA — Vienna, Austria; OPEC — Vienna, Austria.
- NATO — Brussels, Belgium; ICJ (International Court of Justice) — The Hague, Netherlands.
- ASEAN — Jakarta, Indonesia; SAARC — Kathmandu, Nepal; OPEC — Vienna.
- Asian Development Bank (ADB) — Manila, Philippines.

**Indian Organizations & HQ**
- RBI — Mumbai; SEBI — Mumbai; LIC — Mumbai; ISRO — Bengaluru; DRDO — New Delhi; BARC — Mumbai.
- Election Commission — New Delhi; Supreme Court — New Delhi; NABARD — Mumbai.

**Books & Authors (high-yield)**

- *Discovery of India*, *Glimpses of World History* — Jawaharlal Nehru.
- *Wings of Fire*, *India 2020*, *Ignited Minds* — A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (with Arun Tiwari for Wings of Fire).
- *My Experiments with Truth* (autobiography) — M. K. Gandhi.

- *Geet Govind* — Jayadeva; *Abhijnanasakuntalam*, *Meghaduta* — Kalidasa.
- *A Brief History of Time* — Stephen Hawking; *The Argumentative Indian* — Amartya Sen.

- *God of Small Things* — Arundhati Roy; *Midnight's Children* — Salman Rushdie.

## MCQs

**Q1.** Bharatanatyam is the classical dance form of —
(a) Kerala  (b) Tamil Nadu  (c) Odisha  (d) Andhra Pradesh
**Answer: (b) Tamil Nadu**

**Q2.** Kathakali is associated with which state?
(a) Karnataka  (b) Manipur  (c) Kerala  (d) Assam
**Answer: (c) Kerala**

**Q3.** Sattriya, a classical dance form, originated in —
(a) Assam  (b) Odisha  (c) West Bengal  (d) Manipur
**Answer: (a) Assam**

**Q4.** The Onam festival is celebrated mainly in —
(a) Tamil Nadu  (b) Kerala  (c) Karnataka  (d) Andhra Pradesh
**Answer: (b) Kerala**

**Q5.** International Yoga Day is observed on —
(a) 5 June  (b) 21 June  (c) 1 July  (d) 11 July
**Answer: (b) 21 June**

**Q6.** National Sports Day in India is celebrated on the birthday of —
(a) Sachin Tendulkar  (b) Major Dhyan Chand  (c) Milkha Singh  (d) Kapil Dev
**Answer: (b) Major Dhyan Chand (29 August)**

**Q7.** The headquarters of the World Health Organization (WHO) is in —
(a) New York  (b) Paris  (c) Geneva  (d) Rome
**Answer: (c) Geneva, Switzerland**

**Q8.** The headquarters of UNESCO is located in —
(a) New York  (b) Paris  (c) Vienna  (d) Geneva
**Answer: (b) Paris, France**

**Q9.** Who wrote the book *Wings of Fire*?
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru  (b) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam  (c) Amartya Sen  (d) Arundhati Roy
**Answer: (b) A. P. J. Abdul Kalam**

**Q10.** *Gitanjali* was written by —
(a) Rabindranath Tagore  (b) Munshi Premchand  (c) Khushwant Singh  (d) R. K. Narayan
**Answer: (a) Rabindranath Tagore**

**Q11.** Teachers' Day in India is celebrated on the birthday of —
(a) Jawaharlal Nehru  (b) S. Radhakrishnan  (c) Swami Vivekananda  (d) Mahatma Gandhi
**Answer: (b) Dr. S. Radhakrishnan (5 September)**

**Q12.** The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is located at —
(a) Geneva  (b) New York  (c) The Hague  (d) Vienna
**Answer: (c) The Hague, Netherlands**

**Q13.** Lavani is a folk dance of which state?
(a) Gujarat  (b) Rajasthan  (c) Maharashtra  (d) Punjab
**Answer: (c) Maharashtra**

**Q14.** The headquarters of OPEC is in —
(a) Geneva  (b) Vienna  (c) Manila  (d) Jakarta
**Answer: (b) Vienna, Austria**

**Q15.** The Hornbill Festival is celebrated in —
(a) Nagaland  (b) Manipur  (c) Mizoram  (d) Sikkim
**Answer: (a) Nagaland**
