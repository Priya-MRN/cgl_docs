G20 2024 — Rio de Janeiro (19th)

  • First G20 hosted by Brazil. Brazil's 3 priorities: (1) social inclusion & fight against hunger/poverty; (2) sustainable development, energy transition & climate; (3) reform of global-governance institutions.
  • Flagship outcome: Launch of the Global Alliance Against Hunger and Poverty (148 founding members incl. 82 countries, AU, EU). Landmark agreement to cooperate on taxing ultra-high-net-worth individuals. Task Force on a Global Mobilization Against Climate Change.
  • Putin declined (represented by FM Lavrov).
PM Modi (Session on Hunger & Poverty): Cited India lifting 250 million people out of poverty in a decade, free foodgrains to 800 million, health cover to 550 million, and credit to 300 million women entrepreneurs. Highlighted that the African Union became a permanent G20 member during India's own 2023 New Delhi presidency, and endorsed Lula's Hunger Alliance.
Host — Pres. Lula: "Hunger and poverty are not the result of scarcity… they are the product of political decisions." Drove the taxing-the-rich and Hunger Alliance agendas.

G20 2025 — Johannesburg (20th)

  • First G20 summit ever held on African soil.
  • US President Trump BOYCOTTED the summit; no US delegation accredited. Xi Jinping absent (Premier Li Qiang attended); Putin absent (ICC warrant).
  • Johannesburg Leaders' Declaration (122 paragraphs) adopted on the opening day despite the US boycott. Argentina (Milei) was the only member to decline to sign.
  • Focus: food security, debt relief, climate/energy-transition finance, global-governance reform, critical minerals.
  • US handover controversy: The US will host the 2026 G20 in Miami. Washington proposed only a junior embassy official receive the ceremonial gavel; South Africa rejected this — no US official was present for the handover, an unprecedented close.
Host — Pres. Ramaphosa: Framed it as "Africa's G20," centering Global-South solidarity, debt relief, climate justice and a just energy transition. Modi attended in person, continuing India's Global-South advocacy.

BRICS 2024 — Kazan (16th)

  • First summit of the expanded BRICS. Full members: Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa + Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE.
  • New "Partner Country" category created — 13 nations invited (Belarus, Bolivia, Cuba, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, Nigeria, Thailand, Turkey, Uganda, Uzbekistan, Vietnam, Algeria).
  • Kazan Declaration: urged an immediate Gaza ceasefire; condemned unilateral sanctions; advanced BRICS Pay (cross-border payments alternative to SWIFT); pushed de-dollarisation.
PM Modi (key quotes): "We must move forward in a time-bound manner on reforms in global institutions such as the UN Security Council…" Cautioned that BRICS "must be careful to ensure it does not acquire the image of one trying to replace global institutions, instead of one that wishes to reform them." On terrorism: "There is no place for double standards." Framed BRICS as "non-West, not anti-West."

BRICS 2025 — Rio de Janeiro (17th)

  • Xi Jinping ABSENT — first BRICS he skipped since 2012 (Premier Li Qiang represented China). Putin virtual; FM Lavrov attended. Indonesia attended as a new full member (joined Jan 2025).
  • Rio de Janeiro Declaration (126 commitments) + three special documents: Framework Declaration on Climate Finance, Declaration on Global Governance of AI, and Partnership for the Elimination of Socially Determined Diseases.
  • India confirmed as BRICS Chair for 2026 — will host the 18th BRICS Summit.
PM Modi (four proposals): Called the April 2025 Pahalgam terror attack "not just an attack on India, but on all of humanity." Memorable line: Global-South institutions without representation are "like a mobile phone with a SIM card but no network." Four proposals: (1) demand-driven NDB lending; (2) a BRICS Science & Research Repository; (3) secure critical-mineral supply chains; (4) responsible AI.
⚠️ Exam trap: Two "Rio de Janeiro Declarations" — G20 (Nov 2024) vs. BRICS (July 2025), both hosted by Brazil/Lula in Rio, but different bodies and years.

SCO 2024 — Astana (24th)

  • Belarus formally admitted as the 10th member. Adopted SCO Development Strategy until 2035.
  • Modi skipped (Parliament session); Jaishankar represented India.
India (Jaishankar): Emphasised fighting the "three evils — terrorism, separatism, extremism." Invoked India's 2023 theme "SECURE SCO" and stressed respect for sovereignty and territorial integrity.

SCO 2025 — Tianjin (25th)

  • Largest SCO summit in history. Modi's first China visit in ~7 years. Tianjin Declaration + new SCO Development Strategy 2026–2035; decision to establish an SCO Development Bank.
PM Modi: Sought action under three pillars — Security, Connectivity, Opportunity. Urged "firm and decisive action against terrorism"; backed Chabahar Port and INSTC. On the sidelines, Modi–Xi held a bilateral (first on Chinese soil in 7 years) pledging to be "partners not rivals" — a visible India–China thaw post-Galwan.

COP29 — Baku ("The Finance COP")

  • Headline: New Collective Quantified Goal (NCQG) — developed nations to raise at least $300 billion/year by 2035 (replacing the $100 bn goal), within a broader aspiration of $1.3 trillion/year by 2035.
  • India formally REJECTED the deal. Delivered by Chandni Raina (Dept of Economic Affairs), who called $300 bn "abysmally poor," "paltry," "an optical illusion." India argued it violates CBDR & equity.

COP30 — Belém ("Global Mutirão")

  • Theme "Global Mutirão" (collective community effort); the Belém Political Package bundled contested tracks into consensus.
  • Outcomes: call to triple adaptation finance by 2035; "Belém Mission to 1.5"; Tropical Forests Forever Facility (TFFF) (~$6.7 bn pledged). Formal fossil-fuel phase-out language FAILED.
  • India spoke for BASIC (Brazil, South Africa, India, China) and LMDC, pressing equity and CBDR.

G7 2024 — Apulia, Italy (50th)

  • Under Italy's presidency (PM Giorgia Meloni). Leaders agreed $50 billion in Ukraine funding from frozen Russian sovereign assets. First-ever attendance by a Pope (Francis, on AI ethics). Modi attended as an outreach guest (his first foreign trip of his third term).

Ukraine Peace Summit 2024 — Bürgenstock, Switzerland

  • 92 nations + 8 organisations; Russia not invited. A Joint Communiqué on a Peace Framework was issued.
  • India attended (Secretary-West Pavan Kapoor, not Modi) but did NOT sign the communiqué — maintaining that peace requires both parties at the table.

UN Summit of the Future 2024 (UNGA 79)

  • World leaders adopted the Pact for the Future, with two annexes: the Global Digital Compact and the Declaration on Future Generations. India pressed for UNSC reform and Global-South representation.

G7 2025 — Kananaskis, Canada (51st)

  • Under PM Mark Carney. Modi invited and attended as an outreach guest despite the strained India–Canada relationship — read as a diplomatic reset. First G7 for Trump's second term.
