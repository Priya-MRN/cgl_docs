## Quick Notes

**Basic Concepts**
- Economics: study of how scarce resources are allocated. Branches: Microeconomics (individual units) & Macroeconomics (aggregate economy).
- Types of economy: Capitalist (market-driven), Socialist (state-controlled), Mixed (India — both public & private sectors).
- Factors of production: Land, Labour, Capital, Entrepreneurship.
- Sectors: Primary (agriculture, mining), Secondary (manufacturing/industry), Tertiary (services). India's GDP is dominated by the services (tertiary) sector.

**National Income / GDP**
- GDP (Gross Domestic Product): market value of all final goods & services produced within a country in a year.
- GNP = GDP + net factor income from abroad. NNP = GNP − depreciation.
- NNP at factor cost = National Income.
- Per Capita Income = National Income / Population.
- GDP at Market Price vs at Factor Cost (= MP − indirect taxes + subsidies).
- Calculated/released in India by the NSO (National Statistical Office) under MoSPI. Base year currently 2011–12.

**Inflation**
- Inflation: sustained rise in the general price level (fall in money's purchasing power).
- Types: Demand-pull (too much demand) and Cost-push (rising input costs).
- Measured by: WPI (Wholesale Price Index — base 2011-12) and CPI (Consumer Price Index — retail; RBI's monetary policy targets CPI).
- Deflation = falling prices; Disinflation = falling rate of inflation; Stagflation = inflation + stagnation/unemployment together.
- RBI's CPI inflation target: 4% (±2%), i.e., 2–6%.

**Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**
- Established: 1 April 1935 (under the RBI Act, 1934); nationalised 1949. Headquarters: Mumbai (initially Calcutta). First Governor: Sir Osborne Smith; first Indian Governor: C. D. Deshmukh.
- Functions: central bank; sole issuer of currency (except ₹1 note & coins — issued by the Ministry of Finance / Govt.); banker to the government; banker's bank; lender of last resort; controller of credit; manager of forex (FEMA).
- The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) sets the repo rate (6-member committee).

**Monetary Policy Tools (Quantitative)**
- Repo Rate: rate at which RBI lends to commercial banks (against securities).
- Reverse Repo Rate: rate at which RBI borrows from banks.
- CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio): % of deposits banks keep with RBI (in cash).
- SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio): % of deposits kept in liquid assets (gold, govt. securities) by the bank itself.
- Bank Rate, MSF (Marginal Standing Facility), OMO (Open Market Operations).
- To control inflation → RBI raises repo/CRR/SLR (tight money policy).

**Banking**
- First bank of India: Bank of Hindustan (1770). State Bank of India: formed 1955 (from Imperial Bank of India).
- Nationalisation of banks: 14 banks in 1969, 6 more in 1980 (under Indira Gandhi).
- NABARD (1982) — apex bank for agriculture & rural development. SIDBI — small industries. EXIM Bank — export-import.
- Types: Scheduled (in 2nd Schedule of RBI Act) & Non-Scheduled; Commercial, Cooperative, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Payment Banks, Small Finance Banks.
- NPA = Non-Performing Asset (loan overdue 90+ days).

**Budget & Fiscal Policy**
- Union Budget — Annual Financial Statement (Art. 112). Presented on 1 February.
- Revenue Budget (revenue receipts + expenditure) and Capital Budget.
- Fiscal Deficit = Total Expenditure − Total Receipts (excluding borrowings). Indicates govt. borrowing.
- Revenue Deficit = Revenue Expenditure − Revenue Receipts. Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest payments.
- Direct taxes: Income Tax, Corporate Tax (paid by bearer). Indirect taxes: GST, Customs Duty (shifted to consumer).
- GST (Goods and Services Tax): implemented 1 July 2017 (101st Amendment); "One Nation, One Tax"; types CGST, SGST, IGST.
- Finance Commission (Art. 280) — recommends tax distribution between Centre and States.
- NITI Aayog (2015) replaced the Planning Commission (1950). Five-Year Plans (1951–2017; 12 plans). First plan based on Harrod-Domar model; Second on Mahalanobis model.

**Important Economic Terms**
- Repo Rate, Fiscal Deficit, GDP, Inflation, FDI/FII, Disinvestment, Balance of Payments (BoP), Current Account, Devaluation, SEZ.
- BPL (Below Poverty Line); Gini Coefficient (income inequality); HDI (Human Development Index — UNDP; life expectancy, education, per-capita income).
- Repo > Reverse Repo always. CRR is maintained in cash; SLR in liquid assets.

## MCQs

**Q1.** The Reserve Bank of India was established in the year —
(a) 1934  (b) 1935  (c) 1949  (d) 1955
**Answer: (b) 1935**

**Q2.** The headquarters of the RBI is located in —
(a) New Delhi  (b) Kolkata  (c) Mumbai  (d) Chennai
**Answer: (c) Mumbai**

**Q3.** The rate at which the RBI lends money to commercial banks is the —
(a) Bank Rate  (b) Repo Rate  (c) Reverse Repo Rate  (d) SLR
**Answer: (b) Repo Rate**

**Q4.** GST was implemented in India on —
(a) 1 April 2017  (b) 1 July 2017  (c) 1 January 2017  (d) 1 February 2018
**Answer: (b) 1 July 2017**

**Q5.** Which of the following is a direct tax?
(a) GST  (b) Customs Duty  (c) Income Tax  (d) Excise Duty
**Answer: (c) Income Tax**

**Q6.** A situation of high inflation combined with stagnation/unemployment is called —
(a) Deflation  (b) Disinflation  (c) Stagflation  (d) Reflation
**Answer: (c) Stagflation**

**Q7.** NABARD is associated with —
(a) Industry  (b) Agriculture & rural development  (c) Foreign trade  (d) Insurance
**Answer: (b) Agriculture & rural development**

**Q8.** The Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) is maintained by banks with the —
(a) Government  (b) SBI  (c) RBI  (d) NABARD
**Answer: (c) RBI**

**Q9.** GNP minus depreciation equals —
(a) GDP  (b) NNP  (c) National Income  (d) Per Capita Income
**Answer: (b) NNP (Net National Product)**

**Q10.** Bank nationalisation in India first took place in —
(a) 1955  (b) 1969  (c) 1980  (d) 1991
**Answer: (b) 1969 (14 banks)**

**Q11.** NITI Aayog replaced which body?
(a) Finance Commission  (b) Planning Commission  (c) RBI  (d) SEBI
**Answer: (b) Planning Commission (in 2015)**

**Q12.** Fiscal deficit is the difference between total expenditure and total receipts excluding —
(a) taxes  (b) borrowings  (c) subsidies  (d) interest
**Answer: (b) borrowings**

**Q13.** Which index does the RBI primarily target for its monetary policy?
(a) WPI  (b) CPI  (c) IIP  (d) Sensex
**Answer: (b) CPI (Consumer Price Index)**

**Q14.** The sector contributing the largest share to India's GDP is the —
(a) Primary  (b) Secondary  (c) Tertiary  (d) Quaternary
**Answer: (c) Tertiary (services) sector**

**Q15.** The Union Budget is presented under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Art. 110  (b) Art. 112  (c) Art. 280  (d) Art. 360
**Answer: (b) Art. 112 (Annual Financial Statement)**

## SSC PYQ Pattern

**Q1.** Who was the first Governor of the Reserve Bank of India? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) C. D. Deshmukh  (b) Sir Osborne Smith  (c) Benegal Rama Rau  (d) Raghuram Rajan
**Answer: (b) Sir Osborne Smith**
*Note:* C. D. Deshmukh was the first Indian Governor.

**Q2.** The Repo Rate is decided by which body of the RBI? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Board of Directors  (b) Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)  (c) Finance Ministry  (d) SEBI
**Answer: (b) Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)**

**Q3.** GST is an example of which type of tax? (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) Direct tax  (b) Indirect tax  (c) Wealth tax  (d) Corporate tax
**Answer: (b) Indirect tax**

**Q4.** The HDI (Human Development Index) is published by — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) World Bank  (b) IMF  (c) UNDP  (d) WTO
**Answer: (c) UNDP**

**Q5.** SLR (Statutory Liquidity Ratio) is maintained in the form of — (SSC CGL pattern)
(a) cash with RBI  (b) liquid assets like gold and govt. securities  (c) foreign currency  (d) loans
**Answer: (b) liquid assets like gold and govt. securities**
