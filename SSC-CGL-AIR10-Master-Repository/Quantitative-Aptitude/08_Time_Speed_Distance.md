# Time Speed Distance, Trains, Boats & Streams — Solved Question Bank

> Navigation: [README.md](README.md) | [Quant Formula Sheet](../Formula-Sheets/Quant_Formula_Sheet.md)

## Key Formulas

- Basic: `Speed = Distance / Time`
- Conversion: `km/hr → m/s : multiply by 5/18` (and `m/s → km/hr : multiply by 18/5`)
- Average speed (equal distances): `2xy / (x + y)`
- Train crossing a pole: `Time = L / S` (L = train length)
- Train crossing a platform: `Time = (L + P) / S` (P = platform length)
- Relative speed — opposite directions: `S1 + S2`; same direction: `S1 − S2`
- Boats: `Downstream = b + s`, `Upstream = b − s`
- Back-solve: `b = (down + up) / 2`, `s = (down − up) / 2`

## EASY

**Q1.** A car covers 240 km in 4 hours. Find its speed.
(a) 50 km/hr  (b) 55 km/hr  (c) 60 km/hr  (d) 65 km/hr
**Answer: (c) 60 km/hr**
*Solution:* Speed = Distance / Time = 240 / 4 = 60 km/hr.

**Q2.** Convert 72 km/hr into m/s.
(a) 18  (b) 20  (c) 22  (d) 25
**Answer: (b) 20**
*Solution:* m/s = km/hr × 5/18 = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
🔑 *Trick:* For multiples of 18, just multiply by 5 and drop the 18 quickly: 72/18 = 4, 4 × 5 = 20.

**Q3.** A man walks at 5 km/hr. How far does he go in 2 hours 30 minutes?
(a) 10 km  (b) 11.5 km  (c) 12.5 km  (d) 15 km
**Answer: (c) 12.5 km**
*Solution:* Time = 2.5 hr. Distance = Speed × Time = 5 × 2.5 = 12.5 km.

**Q4.** A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 10 seconds. Find its speed in m/s.
(a) 12  (b) 15  (c) 18  (d) 20
**Answer: (b) 15**
*Solution:* Crossing a pole: Speed = L / Time = 150 / 10 = 15 m/s.

**Q5.** A boat's speed in still water is 8 km/hr and the stream flows at 2 km/hr. Find the downstream speed.
(a) 6 km/hr  (b) 8 km/hr  (c) 10 km/hr  (d) 12 km/hr
**Answer: (c) 10 km/hr**
*Solution:* Downstream = b + s = 8 + 2 = 10 km/hr.

**Q6.** Convert 25 m/s into km/hr.
(a) 80  (b) 85  (c) 90  (d) 95
**Answer: (c) 90**
*Solution:* km/hr = m/s × 18/5 = 25 × 18/5 = 90 km/hr.

## MEDIUM

**Q7.** A man covers half a journey at 40 km/hr and the other half at 60 km/hr. Find his average speed.
(a) 48 km/hr  (b) 50 km/hr  (c) 52 km/hr  (d) 45 km/hr
**Answer: (a) 48 km/hr**
*Solution:* Equal distances → average = 2xy/(x + y) = (2 × 40 × 60)/(40 + 60) = 4800/100 = 48 km/hr.
🔑 *Trick:* For equal distances never take the simple mean; always use 2xy/(x+y).

**Q8.** A train 200 m long crosses a platform 300 m long in 25 seconds. Find its speed in km/hr.
(a) 68  (b) 70  (c) 72  (d) 75
**Answer: (c) 72**
*Solution:* Total distance = L + P = 200 + 300 = 500 m. Speed = 500 / 25 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/hr.

**Q9.** Two trains run towards each other at 54 km/hr and 36 km/hr from stations 180 km apart. After how long do they meet?
(a) 1.5 hr  (b) 2 hr  (c) 2.5 hr  (d) 3 hr
**Answer: (b) 2 hr**
*Solution:* Opposite directions → relative speed = 54 + 36 = 90 km/hr. Time = 180 / 90 = 2 hr.
🔑 *Trick:* Closing in from opposite sides? Add the speeds, then divide the gap.

**Q10.** A boat goes 30 km downstream in 2 hours and returns the same distance upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
(a) 12.5 km/hr  (b) 13 km/hr  (c) 12 km/hr  (d) 14 km/hr
**Answer: (a) 12.5 km/hr**
*Solution:* Downstream speed = 30/2 = 15 km/hr; upstream = 30/3 = 10 km/hr. b = (down + up)/2 = (15 + 10)/2 = 12.5 km/hr.

**Q11.** A train running at 60 km/hr crosses a man standing on the platform in 9 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 120 m  (b) 135 m  (c) 150 m  (d) 165 m
**Answer: (c) 150 m**
*Solution:* Speed = 60 × 5/18 = 50/3 m/s. Length = Speed × Time = (50/3) × 9 = 150 m.

**Q12.** Two trains 120 m and 80 m long run in the same direction at 50 km/hr and 32 km/hr. How long does the faster take to completely pass the slower?
(a) 36 s  (b) 40 s  (c) 42 s  (d) 45 s
**Answer: (b) 40 s**
*Solution:* Same direction → relative speed = 50 − 32 = 18 km/hr = 18 × 5/18 = 5 m/s. Combined length = 120 + 80 = 200 m. Time = 200 / 5 = 40 s.
🔑 *Trick:* To "pass" another train, the distance is the SUM of both lengths.

**Q13.** A boat takes twice as long to go upstream as downstream over the same distance. If the stream speed is 3 km/hr, find the boat's speed in still water.
(a) 6 km/hr  (b) 8 km/hr  (c) 9 km/hr  (d) 12 km/hr
**Answer: (c) 9 km/hr**
*Solution:* Time up = 2 × time down ⇒ downstream speed = 2 × upstream speed. So (b + 3) = 2(b − 3) ⇒ b + 3 = 2b − 6 ⇒ b = 9 km/hr.

## HARD

**Q14.** A train crosses a pole in 15 seconds and a 100 m platform in 25 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 120 m  (b) 140 m  (c) 150 m  (d) 160 m
**Answer: (c) 150 m**
*Solution:* Speed is constant. Pole: L = S × 15. Platform: L + 100 = S × 25. Subtract: 100 = S × 10 ⇒ S = 10 m/s. Then L = 10 × 15 = 150 m.
🔑 *Trick:* The extra time (25 − 15 = 10 s) covers exactly the platform length, giving speed directly.

**Q15.** Two trains start at the same time from A and B towards each other at 20 m/s and 30 m/s. They meet 60 km from A. Find the distance AB.
(a) 120 km  (b) 140 km  (c) 150 km  (d) 160 km
**Answer: (c) 150 km**
*Solution:* They travel for the same time, so distance ratio = speed ratio = 20 : 30 = 2 : 3. Train from A covers 2 parts = 60 km ⇒ 1 part = 30 km. Total = 5 parts = 150 km.
🔑 *Trick:* For bodies starting together and meeting, distances are in the ratio of their speeds.

**Q16.** A man can row 6 km/hr in still water. In a river flowing at 2 km/hr it takes him 2 hours to row to a place and back. How far is the place?
(a) 5 km  (b) 5.33 km  (c) 6 km  (d) 4 km
**Answer: (b) 5.33 km**
*Solution:* Downstream = 8 km/hr, upstream = 4 km/hr. Let distance = d. d/8 + d/4 = 2 ⇒ (d + 2d)/8 = 2 ⇒ 3d = 16 ⇒ d = 16/3 ≈ 5.33 km.

**Q17.** A train travelling at 54 km/hr crosses another train of equal length running in the opposite direction at 36 km/hr in 8 seconds. Find the length of each train.
(a) 80 m  (b) 90 m  (c) 100 m  (d) 110 m
**Answer: (c) 100 m**
*Solution:* Opposite directions → relative speed = 54 + 36 = 90 km/hr = 25 m/s. Total distance = 25 × 8 = 200 m = sum of both lengths. Each (equal) = 200/2 = 100 m.

**Q18.** A boat covers 30 km downstream in 2 hours and 18 km upstream in 3 hours. Find the speed of the stream.
(a) 3 km/hr  (b) 4 km/hr  (c) 4.5 km/hr  (d) 5 km/hr
**Answer: (c) 4.5 km/hr**
*Solution:* Downstream speed = 30/2 = 15 km/hr; upstream speed = 18/3 = 6 km/hr. Stream s = (down − up)/2 = (15 − 6)/2 = 9/2 = 4.5 km/hr.
🔑 *Trick:* Convert each leg to a speed first, then apply s = (down − up)/2 in one step.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN

**Q19.** (SSC CGL pattern) A train 180 m long is running at 72 km/hr. How long will it take to cross a bridge 270 m long?
(a) 20 s  (b) 22 s  (c) 22.5 s  (d) 25 s
**Answer: (c) 22.5 s**
*Solution:* Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. Distance = L + P = 180 + 270 = 450 m. Time = 450 / 20 = 22.5 s.

**Q20.** (SSC CGL pattern) A person covers a distance at 5 km/hr and reaches 10 minutes late; at 6 km/hr he reaches 5 minutes early. Find the distance.
(a) 6 km  (b) 7 km  (c) 7.5 km  (d) 8 km
**Answer: (c) 7.5 km**
*Solution:* Time difference = 10 + 5 = 15 min = 1/4 hr. Let distance = d. d/5 − d/6 = 1/4 ⇒ (6d − 5d)/30 = 1/4 ⇒ d/30 = 1/4 ⇒ d = 7.5 km.
🔑 *Trick:* Late + early times add to give the total time gap between the two speeds.

**Q21.** (SSC CGL pattern) The downstream speed of a boat is 15 km/hr and its upstream speed is 9 km/hr. Find the speed of the stream.
(a) 2 km/hr  (b) 3 km/hr  (c) 4 km/hr  (d) 6 km/hr
**Answer: (b) 3 km/hr**
*Solution:* s = (down − up)/2 = (15 − 9)/2 = 6/2 = 3 km/hr.

**Q22.** (SSC CGL pattern) Two trains of lengths 137 m and 163 m run on parallel tracks in the same direction at 42 km/hr and 30 km/hr. How long will they take to cross each other?
(a) 80 s  (b) 85 s  (c) 90 s  (d) 95 s
**Answer: (c) 90 s**
*Solution:* Same direction → relative speed = 42 − 30 = 12 km/hr = 12 × 5/18 = 10/3 m/s. Total length = 137 + 163 = 300 m. Time = 300 / (10/3) = 300 × 3/10 = 90 s.

**Q23.** (SSC CGL pattern) A car travels the first 60 km at 30 km/hr and the next 60 km at 60 km/hr. Find the average speed for the whole journey.
(a) 40 km/hr  (b) 42 km/hr  (c) 45 km/hr  (d) 48 km/hr
**Answer: (a) 40 km/hr**
*Solution:* Equal distances → average = 2xy/(x + y) = (2 × 30 × 60)/(30 + 60) = 3600/90 = 40 km/hr.
🔑 *Trick:* Equal distances always use the harmonic-mean formula 2xy/(x+y), never the simple average.

### 🔑 Exam Tricks Box

- Memorise km/hr → m/s as ×5/18 and the reverse as ×18/5; most train sums need exactly one conversion.
- "Crossing a pole" uses only the train length; "crossing a platform/bridge" adds the platform length.
- Opposite directions add speeds, same direction subtract — and the distance to clear another train is the SUM of both lengths.
- For equal-distance average speed always apply 2xy/(x+y); the plain mean is a trap option.
- Lock down the boat back-solve: b = (down + up)/2 and s = (down − up)/2; these crack most stream questions in one line.

> Drill the conversion factor and the pole-vs-platform distinction first; they unlock the majority of SSC CGL speed-distance and train questions.

## BOOSTER SET — Extra Hard & PYQ Pattern

**B1.** Convert 90 km/hr into m/s.
(a) 20  (b) 22  (c) 25  (d) 30
**Answer: (c) 25**
*Solution:* 90 × 5/18 = **25 m/s**.
🔑 *Trick:* 90/18 = 5, 5 × 5 = 25.

**B2.** A train 250 m long crosses a platform 350 m long in 30 seconds. Find its speed in km/hr.
(a) 68  (b) 70  (c) 72  (d) 75
**Answer: (c) 72**
*Solution:* Distance = 250 + 350 = 600 m. Speed = 600/30 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = **72 km/hr**.

**B3.** Two trains run towards each other at 60 km/hr and 40 km/hr from stations 250 km apart. After how long do they meet?
(a) 2 hr  (b) 2.5 hr  (c) 3 hr  (d) 1.5 hr
**Answer: (b) 2.5 hr**
*Solution:* Relative speed = 60 + 40 = 100 km/hr. Time = 250/100 = **2.5 hr**.

**B4.** A boat goes 36 km downstream in 3 hours and returns the same distance upstream in 4 hours. Find the speed of the boat in still water.
(a) 10.5 km/hr  (b) 10 km/hr  (c) 11 km/hr  (d) 12 km/hr
**Answer: (a) 10.5 km/hr**
*Solution:* Downstream = 36/3 = 12; upstream = 36/4 = 9. b = (12 + 9)/2 = **10.5 km/hr**.

**B5.** A man covers half his journey at 30 km/hr and the other half at 70 km/hr. Find his average speed.
(a) 42 km/hr  (b) 45 km/hr  (c) 48 km/hr  (d) 50 km/hr
**Answer: (a) 42 km/hr**
*Solution:* Equal distances → 2xy/(x+y) = (2×30×70)/(30+70) = 4200/100 = **42 km/hr**.
🔑 *Trick:* Equal-distance average is always harmonic, never the plain mean (50 here is the trap).

**B6.** Two trains of length 150 m and 100 m run in the same direction at 65 km/hr and 47 km/hr. How long does the faster take to pass the slower completely?
(a) 48 s  (b) 50 s  (c) 52 s  (d) 45 s
**Answer: (b) 50 s**
*Solution:* Relative speed = 65 − 47 = 18 km/hr = 5 m/s. Total length = 150 + 100 = 250 m. Time = 250/5 = **50 s**.

**B7.** A train running at 72 km/hr crosses a man standing on the platform in 12 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 200 m  (b) 220 m  (c) 240 m  (d) 250 m
**Answer: (c) 240 m**
*Solution:* Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. Length = 20 × 12 = **240 m**.

**B8.** A boat takes thrice as long to go upstream as downstream over the same distance. If the stream speed is 4 km/hr, find the boat's speed in still water.
(a) 6 km/hr  (b) 8 km/hr  (c) 10 km/hr  (d) 12 km/hr
**Answer: (b) 8 km/hr**
*Solution:* Time up = 3 × time down → downstream speed = 3 × upstream → (b + 4) = 3(b − 4) → b + 4 = 3b − 12 → 2b = 16 → b = **8 km/hr**.

**B9.** A train crosses a pole in 12 seconds and a 120 m platform in 18 seconds. Find the length of the train.
(a) 200 m  (b) 220 m  (c) 240 m  (d) 250 m
**Answer: (c) 240 m**
*Solution:* Extra time 18 − 12 = 6 s covers 120 m → speed = 120/6 = 20 m/s. Length = 20 × 12 = **240 m**.
🔑 *Trick:* (Platform length)/(extra time) gives speed directly.

**B10.** Two trains start at the same time from A and B towards each other at 30 m/s and 45 m/s. They meet 90 km from A. Find the distance AB.
(a) 200 km  (b) 210 km  (c) 225 km  (d) 240 km
**Answer: (c) 225 km**
*Solution:* Distance ratio = speed ratio = 30 : 45 = 2 : 3. Train from A covers 2 parts = 90 km → 1 part = 45 km. Total = 5 parts = **225 km**.

**B11.** (SSC CGL pattern) A train 200 m long running at 90 km/hr crosses a bridge 250 m long. How long does it take?
(a) 16 s  (b) 18 s  (c) 20 s  (d) 22 s
**Answer: (b) 18 s**
*Solution:* Speed = 90 × 5/18 = 25 m/s. Distance = 200 + 250 = 450 m. Time = 450/25 = **18 s**.

**B12.** (SSC CGL pattern) A person reaches 12 minutes late at 4 km/hr and 8 minutes early at 5 km/hr. Find the distance.
(a) 6 km  (b) 6.5 km  (c) 6⅔ km  (d) 7 km
**Answer: (c) 6⅔ km**
*Solution:* Time gap = 12 + 8 = 20 min = 1/3 hr. d/4 − d/5 = 1/3 → (5d − 4d)/20 = 1/3 → d/20 = 1/3 → d = 20/3 = **6⅔ km**.
🔑 *Trick:* Late + early = total time difference between the two speeds.
