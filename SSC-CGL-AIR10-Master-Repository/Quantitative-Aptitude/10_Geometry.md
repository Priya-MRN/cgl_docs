## Key Formulas

- Sum of angles of a triangle: `A + B + C = 180°`
- Exterior angle = sum of two opposite interior angles: `ext = A + B`
- Pythagoras: `hyp² = base² + height²`; common triples `(3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17), (7,24,25)`
- Centroid divides each median in ratio `2 : 1`
- Angle at centre `= 2 × angle at circumference` (same arc)
- Angle in a semicircle `= 90°`
- Cyclic quadrilateral: opposite angles sum to `180°`
- Tangent from external point: `tangent² = (distance to centre)² − radius²`
- Two tangents from an external point are equal in length
- Similar triangles: corresponding sides proportional; area ratio `= (side ratio)²`
- Alternate segment theorem: angle between tangent and chord = angle in alternate segment

## EASY

**Q1.** Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. Find the third angle.
(a) 70°  (b) 80°  (c) 60°  (d) 110°
**Answer: (a) 70°**
*Solution:* Third angle `= 180° − (50° + 60°) = 180° − 110° = 70°`.

**Q2.** In a right triangle, the legs are 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
(a) 10 cm  (b) 12 cm  (c) 14 cm  (d) 9 cm
**Answer: (a) 10 cm**
*Solution:* `hyp = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10` (a 3-4-5 triple scaled by 2).

🔑 *Trick:* Memorise triples — 6-8-10 is just 3-4-5 doubled; no calculation needed.

**Q3.** An exterior angle of a triangle is 120° and one interior opposite angle is 45°. Find the other interior opposite angle.
(a) 75°  (b) 65°  (c) 80°  (d) 60°
**Answer: (a) 75°**
*Solution:* Exterior angle = sum of opposite interiors, so other angle `= 120° − 45° = 75°`.

**Q4.** The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is 80°. Find the angle subtended at the circumference (same arc).
(a) 40°  (b) 80°  (c) 160°  (d) 20°
**Answer: (a) 40°**
*Solution:* Angle at circumference `= ½ × angle at centre = ½ × 80° = 40°`.

**Q5.** In a cyclic quadrilateral, one angle is 110°. Find its opposite angle.
(a) 70°  (b) 110°  (c) 90°  (d) 80°
**Answer: (a) 70°**
*Solution:* Opposite angles sum to 180°, so the opposite angle `= 180° − 110° = 70°`.

## MEDIUM

**Q6.** The medians of a triangle meet at the centroid G. If a median has length 18 cm, find the distance from the vertex to G.
(a) 12 cm  (b) 9 cm  (c) 6 cm  (d) 15 cm
**Answer: (a) 12 cm**
*Solution:* Centroid divides median in ratio 2:1 from vertex. Vertex part `= (2/3) × 18 = 12 cm`.

**Q7.** A tangent is drawn from a point 13 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 5 cm. Find the tangent length.
(a) 12 cm  (b) 8 cm  (c) 18 cm  (d) 10 cm
**Answer: (a) 12 cm**
*Solution:* `tangent = √(13² − 5²) = √(169 − 25) = √144 = 12 cm`.

**Q8.** In a circle, an angle in a semicircle is:
(a) 90°  (b) 60°  (c) 180°  (d) 45°
**Answer: (a) 90°**
*Solution:* The angle subtended by a diameter at the circumference is always 90° (angle at centre = 180°, half = 90°).

**Q9.** Two similar triangles have sides in ratio 3 : 5. Find the ratio of their areas.
(a) 9 : 25  (b) 3 : 5  (c) 6 : 10  (d) 27 : 125
**Answer: (a) 9 : 25**
*Solution:* Area ratio = (side ratio)² = `3² : 5² = 9 : 25`.

**Q10.** The angles of a triangle are in ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find the largest angle.
(a) 80°  (b) 60°  (c) 90°  (d) 70°
**Answer: (a) 80°**
*Solution:* Sum of parts `= 2 + 3 + 4 = 9`. Each part `= 180°/9 = 20°`. Largest `= 4 × 20° = 80°`.

**Q11.** Two tangents from an external point P touch a circle at A and B. If PA = 7 cm, find PB.
(a) 7 cm  (b) 14 cm  (c) 3.5 cm  (d) cannot be found
**Answer: (a) 7 cm**
*Solution:* Tangents from the same external point are equal, so `PB = PA = 7 cm`.

🔑 *Trick:* Equal-tangent property is a frequent free-mark question — tangents from one point are always equal.

## HARD

**Q12.** In triangle ABC, the incentre I is equidistant from all three sides. This equal distance is the radius of which circle?
(a) Incircle  (b) Circumcircle  (c) Excircle  (d) Nine-point circle
**Answer: (a) Incircle**
*Solution:* The incentre is the centre of the inscribed circle; its equal perpendicular distance to each side is the inradius.

**Q13.** The circumcentre of a right triangle lies at:
(a) midpoint of the hypotenuse  (b) the right-angle vertex  (c) centroid  (d) outside the triangle
**Answer: (a) midpoint of the hypotenuse**
*Solution:* In a right triangle the hypotenuse is the diameter of the circumcircle, so its midpoint (equidistant from all vertices) is the circumcentre.

**Q14.** Two chords AB and CD of a circle intersect inside at P. If PA = 4, PB = 6, PC = 3, find PD.
(a) 8  (b) 6  (c) 12  (d) 9
**Answer: (a) 8**
*Solution:* Intersecting chords: `PA × PB = PC × PD`, so `4 × 6 = 3 × PD`, giving `PD = 24/3 = 8`.

**Q15.** In triangle ABC, AB = 6, AC = 9, and AD bisects angle A meeting BC at D. Find BD : DC.
(a) 2 : 3  (b) 3 : 2  (c) 6 : 9 reversed  (d) 1 : 1
**Answer: (a) 2 : 3**
*Solution:* Angle bisector theorem: `BD/DC = AB/AC = 6/9 = 2/3`, i.e. `2 : 3`.

🔑 *Trick:* Angle bisector splits the opposite side in the ratio of the two adjacent sides.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN

**Q16.** (SSC CGL pattern) An equilateral triangle has each angle equal to:
(a) 60°  (b) 90°  (c) 45°  (d) 120°
**Answer: (a) 60°**
*Solution:* All three angles equal and sum to 180°, so each `= 180°/3 = 60°`.

**Q17.** (SSC CGL pattern) The angle subtended by a chord at the centre is 130°. A point on the major arc subtends what angle?
(a) 65°  (b) 130°  (c) 50°  (d) 115°
**Answer: (a) 65°**
*Solution:* Angle at circumference `= ½ × central angle = ½ × 130° = 65°`.

**Q18.** (SSC CGL pattern) The sides of a triangle are 7 cm, 24 cm, 25 cm. The triangle is:
(a) right-angled  (b) acute  (c) obtuse  (d) equilateral
**Answer: (a) right-angled**
*Solution:* `7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25²`, so it satisfies Pythagoras — right-angled.

**Q19.** (SSC CGL pattern) In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, ∠A = 70° and ∠B = 95°. Find ∠C and ∠D.
(a) ∠C = 110°, ∠D = 85°  (b) ∠C = 70°, ∠D = 95°  (c) ∠C = 85°, ∠D = 110°  (d) ∠C = 90°, ∠D = 90°
**Answer: (a) ∠C = 110°, ∠D = 85°**
*Solution:* Opposite angles sum to 180°: `∠C = 180° − 70° = 110°` and `∠D = 180° − 95° = 85°`.

🔑 *Trick:* In any cyclic quad, pair opposite angles and subtract from 180° — done in one step.

### 🔑 Exam Tricks Box

- Memorise Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25) and their multiples to skip square-root work.
- Angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference; angle in a semicircle = 90° — two of the most-tested circle facts.
- Centroid 2:1, circumcentre at hypotenuse-midpoint (right triangle), incentre = incircle centre — know each "centre" property.
- Use the angle-bisector theorem and intersecting-chord product (`PA·PB = PC·PD`) for fast ratio/length answers.

> ✅ Geometry rewards memorised properties over long proofs — drill the centre/circle/triangle facts until they are reflexive.

## BOOSTER SET — Extra Hard & PYQ Pattern

**B1.** Two angles of a triangle are in ratio 3 : 5 and the third angle is 100°. Find the smallest angle.
(a) 30°  (b) 25°  (c) 35°  (d) 40°
**Answer: (a) 30°**
*Solution:* The two angles sum to 180° − 100° = 80°, in ratio 3 : 5 → 8 parts = 80° → 1 part = 10°. Smallest = 3 × 10° = **30°**.

**B2.** In a right triangle the two legs are 9 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
(a) 13 cm  (b) 15 cm  (c) 16 cm  (d) 14 cm
**Answer: (b) 15 cm**
*Solution:* √(9² + 12²) = √(81 + 144) = √225 = **15 cm** (3-4-5 scaled ×3).
🔑 *Trick:* 9-12-15 is just 3-4-5 tripled.

**B3.** The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is 140°. Find the angle it subtends at the circumference on the major arc.
(a) 60°  (b) 70°  (c) 80°  (d) 75°
**Answer: (b) 70°**
*Solution:* Angle at circumference = ½ × central angle = ½ × 140° = **70°**.

**B4.** A tangent is drawn from a point 17 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 8 cm. Find the tangent length.
(a) 12 cm  (b) 15 cm  (c) 14 cm  (d) 13 cm
**Answer: (b) 15 cm**
*Solution:* tangent = √(17² − 8²) = √(289 − 64) = √225 = **15 cm** (8-15-17 triple).
🔑 *Trick:* Recognise 8-15-17 → answer is 15 with no square-root work.

**B5.** Two chords AB and CD of a circle meet inside at P. If PA = 5, PB = 8, PC = 4, find PD.
(a) 8  (b) 9  (c) 10  (d) 12
**Answer: (c) 10**
*Solution:* Intersecting chords: PA × PB = PC × PD → 5 × 8 = 4 × PD → PD = 40/4 = **10**.

**B6.** In triangle ABC, AB = 8, AC = 12, and AD bisects angle A meeting BC at D. Find BD : DC.
(a) 2 : 3  (b) 3 : 2  (c) 1 : 2  (d) 4 : 5
**Answer: (a) 2 : 3**
*Solution:* Angle bisector theorem: BD/DC = AB/AC = 8/12 = **2 : 3**.

**B7.** Two similar triangles have areas in ratio 16 : 49. Find the ratio of their corresponding sides.
(a) 4 : 7  (b) 16 : 49  (c) 2 : 7  (d) 8 : 21
**Answer: (a) 4 : 7**
*Solution:* Side ratio = √(area ratio) = √16 : √49 = **4 : 7**.

**B8.** A median of a triangle is 21 cm long. Find the distance from the centroid to the midpoint of the opposite side.
(a) 7 cm  (b) 14 cm  (c) 10.5 cm  (d) 6 cm
**Answer: (a) 7 cm**
*Solution:* Centroid divides median 2 : 1 from vertex, so the part toward the side = (1/3) × 21 = **7 cm**.

**B9.** In a cyclic quadrilateral PQRS, ∠P = 85° and ∠Q = 100°. Find ∠R and ∠S.
(a) ∠R = 95°, ∠S = 80°  (b) ∠R = 85°, ∠S = 100°  (c) ∠R = 80°, ∠S = 95°  (d) ∠R = 90°, ∠S = 90°
**Answer: (a) ∠R = 95°, ∠S = 80°**
*Solution:* Opposite angles sum to 180°: ∠R = 180° − 85° = 95°, ∠S = 180° − 100° = **80°**.

**B10.** The sides of a triangle are 8 cm, 15 cm and 17 cm. The triangle is:
(a) acute  (b) right-angled  (c) obtuse  (d) equilateral
**Answer: (b) right-angled**
*Solution:* 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289 = 17² → satisfies Pythagoras → **right-angled**.

**B11.** (SSC CGL pattern) Two tangents from an external point P touch a circle, meeting at an angle of 70° at P. Find the angle subtended by the two points of contact at the centre.
(a) 100°  (b) 110°  (c) 120°  (d) 90°
**Answer: (b) 110°**
*Solution:* The radius–tangent angles are each 90°, so in quadrilateral the central angle = 180° − 70° = **110°**.
🔑 *Trick:* Angle at centre + angle between tangents = 180° (radii ⟂ tangents).

**B12.** (SSC CGL pattern) In triangle ABC, ∠B = 90°, AB = 5 cm and BC = 12 cm. Find the length of the median from B to AC.
(a) 6 cm  (b) 6.5 cm  (c) 7 cm  (d) 5.5 cm
**Answer: (b) 6.5 cm**
*Solution:* AC = √(5² + 12²) = √169 = 13 cm. In a right triangle the median to the hypotenuse = half the hypotenuse = 13/2 = **6.5 cm**.
🔑 *Trick:* Median to the hypotenuse always equals half the hypotenuse (circumradius of a right triangle).
