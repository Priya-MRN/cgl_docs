## Key Formulas

- `Percentage = (part / total) × 100`
- `Average = (sum of values) / (number of values)`
- `Ratio = value₁ : value₂` (reduce to lowest terms)
- `Percentage change = (new − old)/old × 100`
- `Difference = larger value − smaller value`

## EASY

**Q1.** (Set A) How many students are enrolled in Centre R in total?
(a) 400  (b) 300  (c) 350  (d) 450
**Answer: (a) 400**
*Solution:* From the Total column, Centre R = `100 + 100 + 200 = 400`.

**Q2.** (Set B) What amount is allocated to Rent?
(a) ₹10,800  (b) ₹9,000  (c) ₹7,200  (d) ₹5,400
**Answer: (a) ₹10,800**
*Solution:* Rent = 30% of 36,000 = `0.30 × 36,000 = ₹10,800`.

**Q3.** (Set C) What were the sales in 2023?
(a) ₹60 lakh  (b) ₹50 lakh  (c) ₹75 lakh  (d) ₹40 lakh
**Answer: (a) ₹60 lakh**
*Solution:* Directly read from the bar chart: 2023 = ₹60 lakh.

**Q4.** (Set A) What is the total number of Maths students across all centres?
(a) 650  (b) 500  (c) 550  (d) 600
**Answer: (a) 650**
*Solution:* `120 + 150 + 100 + 80 + 200 = 650`.

**Q5.** (Set B) How much is allocated to Savings?
(a) ₹3,600  (b) ₹7,200  (c) ₹5,400  (d) ₹9,000
**Answer: (a) ₹3,600**
*Solution:* Savings = 10% of 36,000 = `₹3,600`.

## MEDIUM

**Q6.** (Set A) What percentage of Centre P's students study Reasoning?
(a) 33.33%  (b) 40%  (c) 25%  (d) 30%
**Answer: (a) 33.33%**
*Solution:* `(100 / 300) × 100 = 33.33%`.

**Q7.** (Set C) What is the average yearly sales over the four years?
(a) ₹56.25 lakh  (b) ₹55 lakh  (c) ₹60 lakh  (d) ₹50 lakh
**Answer: (a) ₹56.25 lakh**
*Solution:* Average `= (40 + 50 + 60 + 75)/4 = 225/4 = 56.25 lakh`.

**Q8.** (Set B) What is the ratio of the Food budget to the Transport budget?
(a) 5 : 3  (b) 3 : 5  (c) 25 : 10  (d) 2 : 1
**Answer: (a) 5 : 3**
*Solution:* `9000 : 5400 = 90 : 54 = 5 : 3` (or directly `25% : 15% = 5 : 3`).

🔑 *Trick:* In a pie chart, ratios of amounts equal ratios of percentages — skip the rupee conversion.

**Q9.** (Set A) What is the difference between total English students and total Reasoning students?
(a) 50  (b) 100  (c) 150  (d) 500
**Answer: (a) 50**
*Solution:* English total `= 80+90+100+120+110 = 500`; Reasoning total `= 100+60+200+100+90 = 550`. Difference `= 550 − 500 = 50`.

**Q10.** (Set C) What is the percentage increase in sales from 2021 to 2024?
(a) 87.5%  (b) 75%  (c) 100%  (d) 35%
**Answer: (a) 87.5%**
*Solution:* `(75 − 40)/40 × 100 = 35/40 × 100 = 87.5%`.

**Q11.** (Set A) Which centre has the highest number of Reasoning students?
(a) R  (b) P  (c) T  (d) S
**Answer: (a) R**
*Solution:* Reasoning values: P=100, Q=60, R=200, S=100, T=90. Highest is R with 200.

## HARD

**Q12.** (Set A) Maths students across all centres form what percentage of the grand total (1700)?
(a) ≈ 38.2%  (b) ≈ 30%  (c) ≈ 45%  (d) ≈ 35%
**Answer: (a) ≈ 38.2%**
*Solution:* Maths total = 650. `(650 / 1700) × 100 ≈ 38.24%`.

**Q13.** (Set B) Rent and Education together exceed Food and Transport together by how much?
(a) ₹3,600  (b) ₹1,800  (c) ₹5,400  (d) ₹7,200
**Answer: (a) ₹3,600**
*Solution:* Rent + Education `= 10,800 + 7,200 = 18,000`. Food + Transport `= 9,000 + 5,400 = 14,400`. Difference `= 18,000 − 14,400 = ₹3,600`.

**Q14.** (Set C) By what percent did sales grow from 2022 to 2023?
(a) 20%  (b) 16.67%  (c) 25%  (d) 10%
**Answer: (a) 20%**
*Solution:* `(60 − 50)/50 × 100 = 10/50 × 100 = 20%`.

**Q15.** (Set A) What is the average number of Reasoning students per centre?
(a) 110  (b) 100  (c) 120  (d) 90
**Answer: (a) 110**
*Solution:* Reasoning total = 550 over 5 centres. Average `= 550/5 = 110`.

🔑 *Trick:* Compute a column total once, then reuse it for both percentage and average questions.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN

**Q16.** (SSC CGL pattern) (Set B) The central angle of the Rent sector in the pie chart is:
(a) 108°  (b) 90°  (c) 120°  (d) 72°
**Answer: (a) 108°**
*Solution:* Rent = 30%. Central angle `= 30% × 360° = 0.30 × 360 = 108°`.

**Q17.** (SSC CGL pattern) (Set A) The ratio of total students in Centre P to Centre R is:
(a) 3 : 4  (b) 4 : 3  (c) 1 : 1  (d) 2 : 3
**Answer: (a) 3 : 4**
*Solution:* P = 300, R = 400. Ratio `= 300 : 400 = 3 : 4`.

**Q18.** (SSC CGL pattern) (Set C) What is the total sales over the four years?
(a) ₹225 lakh  (b) ₹200 lakh  (c) ₹250 lakh  (d) ₹215 lakh
**Answer: (a) ₹225 lakh**
*Solution:* `40 + 50 + 60 + 75 = 225 lakh`.

**Q19.** (SSC CGL pattern) (Set B) Education spending is what percent of Savings?
(a) 200%  (b) 150%  (c) 50%  (d) 250%
**Answer: (a) 200%**
*Solution:* Education = ₹7,200, Savings = ₹3,600. `(7200/3600) × 100 = 200%`.

🔑 *Trick:* "A is what percent of B" = `(A/B) × 100` — keep the order strictly A over B.

### 🔑 Exam Tricks Box

- Compute each row/column total once and reuse it for percentage, ratio, and average sub-questions.
- In pie charts, percentages map directly to amounts and angles (1% = 3.6°) — convert only when forced.
- "A is what percent of B" is always `(A/B) × 100`; never reverse the order.
- For percentage change use `(new − old)/old × 100` — divide by the OLD value, a common trap.
