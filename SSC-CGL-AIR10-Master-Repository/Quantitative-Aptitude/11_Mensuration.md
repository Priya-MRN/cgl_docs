## Key Formulas

**2D (use π = 22/7):**
- Rectangle: `Area = l × b`, `Perimeter = 2(l + b)`
- Square: `Area = a²`, `Diagonal = a√2`, `Perimeter = 4a`
- Circle: `Area = πr²`, `Circumference = 2πr`
- Triangle: `Area = ½ × base × height`; Equilateral `Area = (√3/4)a²`
- Trapezium: `Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height`

**3D:**
- Cube: `Volume = a³`, `Total surface area = 6a²`, `Diagonal = a√3`
- Cuboid: `Volume = l·b·h`, `TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl)`
- Cylinder: `Volume = πr²h`, `CSA = 2πrh`, `TSA = 2πr(r + h)`
- Cone: `Volume = ⅓πr²h`, `CSA = πrl`, `TSA = πr(r + l)`, slant `l = √(r² + h²)`
- Sphere: `Volume = (4/3)πr³`, `Surface area = 4πr²`
- Hemisphere: `Volume = (2/3)πr³`, `CSA = 2πr²`, `TSA = 3πr²`

## EASY

**Q1.** Find the area of a rectangle of length 12 cm and breadth 5 cm.
(a) 60 cm²  (b) 34 cm²  (c) 17 cm²  (d) 50 cm²
**Answer: (a) 60 cm²**
*Solution:* `Area = l × b = 12 × 5 = 60 cm²`.

**Q2.** Find the perimeter of a square of side 9 cm.
(a) 36 cm  (b) 81 cm  (c) 18 cm  (d) 27 cm
**Answer: (a) 36 cm**
*Solution:* `Perimeter = 4a = 4 × 9 = 36 cm`.

**Q3.** Find the area of a circle of radius 7 cm (π = 22/7).
(a) 154 cm²  (b) 44 cm²  (c) 22 cm²  (d) 308 cm²
**Answer: (a) 154 cm²**
*Solution:* `Area = πr² = (22/7) × 7 × 7 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm²`.

🔑 *Trick:* With radius 7 (or its multiples), the 7 cancels π's denominator — instant clean answers.

**Q4.** Find the volume of a cube of edge 4 cm.
(a) 64 cm³  (b) 16 cm³  (c) 96 cm³  (d) 48 cm³
**Answer: (a) 64 cm³**
*Solution:* `Volume = a³ = 4³ = 64 cm³`.

**Q5.** Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.
(a) 30 cm²  (b) 60 cm²  (c) 16 cm²  (d) 15 cm²
**Answer: (a) 30 cm²**
*Solution:* `Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 10 × 6 = 30 cm²`.

## MEDIUM

**Q6.** Find the total surface area of a cuboid 5 × 4 × 3 cm.
(a) 94 cm²  (b) 60 cm²  (c) 47 cm²  (d) 120 cm²
**Answer: (a) 94 cm²**
*Solution:* `TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(5·4 + 4·3 + 3·5) = 2(20 + 12 + 15) = 2 × 47 = 94 cm²`.

**Q7.** A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Find its volume (π = 22/7).
(a) 1540 cm³  (b) 440 cm³  (c) 770 cm³  (d) 2200 cm³
**Answer: (a) 1540 cm³**
*Solution:* `V = πr²h = (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 10 = 22 × 7 × 10 = 1540 cm³`.

**Q8.** Find the area of a trapezium with parallel sides 8 cm and 12 cm, height 5 cm.
(a) 50 cm²  (b) 100 cm²  (c) 40 cm²  (d) 60 cm²
**Answer: (a) 50 cm²**
*Solution:* `Area = ½ × (8 + 12) × 5 = ½ × 20 × 5 = 50 cm²`.

**Q9.** Find the curved surface area of a cone with radius 3 cm and slant height 5 cm (π = 22/7).
(a) 330/7 cm²  (b) 47.1 cm²  (c) 15 cm²  (d) 660/7 cm²
**Answer: (a) 330/7 cm²**
*Solution:* `CSA = πrl = (22/7) × 3 × 5 = 330/7 ≈ 47.14 cm²`.

**Q10.** Find the surface area of a sphere of radius 7 cm (π = 22/7).
(a) 616 cm²  (b) 308 cm²  (c) 154 cm²  (d) 1232 cm²
**Answer: (a) 616 cm²**
*Solution:* `SA = 4πr² = 4 × (22/7) × 49 = 4 × 22 × 7 = 616 cm²`.

**Q11.** A cone has radius 6 cm and height 8 cm. Find its slant height.
(a) 10 cm  (b) 14 cm  (c) 12 cm  (d) 9 cm
**Answer: (a) 10 cm**
*Solution:* `l = √(r² + h²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 cm` (a 6-8-10 triple).

🔑 *Trick:* Cone slant height is just a Pythagoras triple on (r, h) — spot 6-8-10, 5-12-13 etc.

## HARD

**Q12.** Find the volume of a hemisphere of radius 3 cm (π = 22/7).
(a) 594/7 cm³  (b) 88/7 cm³  (c) 198/7 cm³  (d) 1188/7 cm³
**Answer: (a) 594/7 cm³**
*Solution:* `V = (2/3)πr³ = (2/3) × (22/7) × 27 = (2 × 22 × 27)/(3 × 7) = 1188/21 = 594/7 ≈ 84.86 cm³`.

**Q13.** A solid metal sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and recast into a cube. Find the cube's edge (π = 22/7), to one decimal.
(a) ≈ 4.8 cm  (b) ≈ 3.0 cm  (c) ≈ 6.0 cm  (d) ≈ 2.4 cm
**Answer: (a) ≈ 4.8 cm**
*Solution:* Sphere volume `= (4/3)(22/7)(27) = 2376/21 ≈ 113.14 cm³`. Cube edge `= ∛113.14 ≈ 4.84 cm`.

**Q14.** The diagonal of a cube is `6√3` cm. Find its volume.
(a) 216 cm³  (b) 108 cm³  (c) 343 cm³  (d) 144 cm³
**Answer: (a) 216 cm³**
*Solution:* Diagonal `= a√3 = 6√3`, so `a = 6`. Volume `= a³ = 6³ = 216 cm³`.

**Q15.** A cylinder and a cone have equal radius and equal height. Find the ratio of their volumes.
(a) 3 : 1  (b) 1 : 3  (c) 1 : 1  (d) 2 : 1
**Answer: (a) 3 : 1**
*Solution:* `V_cyl = πr²h`, `V_cone = ⅓πr²h`. Ratio `= πr²h : ⅓πr²h = 3 : 1`.

🔑 *Trick:* Same r and h → cone is always one-third the cylinder; remember 3:1.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN

**Q16.** (SSC CGL pattern) The diagonal of a square is `10√2` cm. Find its area.
(a) 100 cm²  (b) 200 cm²  (c) 50 cm²  (d) 141 cm²
**Answer: (a) 100 cm²**
*Solution:* Diagonal `= a√2 = 10√2`, so `a = 10`. Area `= a² = 100 cm²`.

**Q17.** (SSC CGL pattern) Find the area of an equilateral triangle of side 8 cm.
(a) `16√3 cm²`  (b) `64√3 cm²`  (c) `8√3 cm²`  (d) `32√3 cm²`
**Answer: (a) 16√3 cm²**
*Solution:* `Area = (√3/4)a² = (√3/4) × 64 = 16√3 cm²`.

**Q18.** (SSC CGL pattern) A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 20 cm. Find its curved surface area (π = 22/7).
(a) 880 cm²  (b) 440 cm²  (c) 1540 cm²  (d) 308 cm²
**Answer: (a) 880 cm²**
*Solution:* `CSA = 2πrh = 2 × (22/7) × 7 × 20 = 2 × 22 × 20 = 880 cm²`.

**Q19.** (SSC CGL pattern) If the radius of a sphere is doubled, by what factor does its volume increase?
(a) 8 times  (b) 4 times  (c) 2 times  (d) 6 times
**Answer: (a) 8 times**
*Solution:* `V ∝ r³`. Doubling r gives `2³ = 8` times the volume.

🔑 *Trick:* Volume scales with the cube of any linear scale factor — doubling → ×8, tripling → ×27.

### 🔑 Exam Tricks Box

- Always plug `π = 22/7` and choose radii that are multiples of 7 to cancel cleanly.
- Cone slant height and "is it right-angled" questions reduce to Pythagorean triples — memorise them.
- Equal radius + equal height: cone : cylinder : sphere relationships are fixed — cone = ⅓ cylinder.
- Linear scale k changes area by k² and volume by k³ — a one-line shortcut for "doubled/tripled" questions.
