6 · 📊 Tangent & Circle Theorems — Visual Diagrams

① Tangent ⟂ Radius

Radius meets the tangent at the point of contact T.

OT ⟂ tangent → angle = 90°

② Two Tangents from a Point

Two tangents drawn from one external point P.

PA = PB (equal lengths)

③ Tangent Length (PT)

Length of tangent from external point, distance d = OP.

PT = √(d² − r²)

④ Angle at Centre = 2 × Circumference

Arc AB subtends ∠ at centre (O) and on circumference (P).

∠AOB = 2 × ∠APB

⑤ Angle in Semicircle = 90°

AB is a diameter; C is any point on the circle.

∠ACB = 90°

⑥ Cyclic Quadrilateral

All four vertices A, B, C, D lie on the circle.

∠A + ∠C = 180°  and  ∠B + ∠D = 180°

⑦ Intersecting Chords

Two chords AB and CD cross inside the circle at P.

PA × PB = PC × PD

⑧ Tangent–Secant (Power of Point)

From external P: one tangent PT and one secant cutting at A, B.

PT² = PA × PB

⑨ Alternate Segment Theorem

Angle between tangent & chord TA = angle in the alternate segment (∠B).

∠(tangent, chord) = ∠ in alternate segment

⑩ Perpendicular from Centre Bisects Chord

Perpendicular OM dropped from centre O to chord AB.

AM = MB (M is midpoint)

⑪ Two ⟂ Chords → Radius (5-12-13 PYQ)

Two chords cross at 90°; distances from centre are d₁, d₂.

R = √(d₁² + d₂²) = √(5²+12²) = 13 cm
