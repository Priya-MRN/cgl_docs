# Percentage — Solved Question Bank

> ⭐ Top-priority topic. Feeds into P&L, SI/CI, DI. Master this first.
> [Quant Index](README.md) · [Formula Sheet](../Formula-Sheets/Quant_Formula_Sheet.md)

## Key Formulas
- `x% of y = y% of x`
- A is x% more than B → B is `x/(100+x)×100`% less than A
- A is x% less than B → B is `x/(100−x)×100`% more than A
- Successive a%, b% → `a + b + ab/100`
- +x% then −x% → net `−x²/100`% (loss)

**Fraction–% table (memorize):**
`1/2=50, 1/3=33⅓, 1/4=25, 1/5=20, 1/6=16⅔, 1/7=14²⁄₇, 1/8=12½, 1/9=11⅑, 1/11=9¹⁄₁₁, 1/12=8⅓, 1/16=6¼`

## EASY

**Q1.** What is 35% of 280?
(a) 88  (b) 98  (c) 108  (d) 78
**Answer: (b) 98**
*Solution:* 10% of 280 = 28 → 35% = 28×3.5 = 98.

**Q2.** If 60% of a number is 240, the number is:
(a) 360  (b) 400  (c) 420  (d) 480
**Answer: (b) 400**
*Solution:* Number = 240 × 100/60 = 400.

**Q3.** 25 is what percent of 200?
(a) 10%  (b) 12.5%  (c) 15%  (d) 20%
**Answer: (b) 12.5%**
*Solution:* 25/200 × 100 = 12.5%. (Shortcut: 1/8 = 12.5%.)

**Q4.** A number increased by 20% becomes 540. The original number is:
(a) 432  (b) 450  (c) 460  (d) 500
**Answer: (b) 450**
*Solution:* 540 × 100/120 = 450.

**Q5.** Convert 0.65 to a percentage.
(a) 6.5%  (b) 65%  (c) 0.65%  (d) 650%
**Answer: (b) 65%**

## MEDIUM

**Q6.** The price of sugar rises by 25%. By what percent must a family reduce consumption to keep expenditure unchanged?
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 16⅔%  (d) 33⅓%
**Answer: (a) 20%**
*Solution:* Reduction = `x/(100+x)×100 = 25/125×100 = 20%`.
🔑 *Trick:* Rise 25% = 1/4 → reduce by 1/(4+1) = 1/5 = 20%.

**Q7.** A's salary is 25% more than B's. By what percent is B's salary less than A's?
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 16⅔%  (d) 30%
**Answer: (a) 20%**
*Solution:* `25/(100+25)×100 = 20%`.

**Q8.** In an election between two candidates, the winner got 56% votes and won by 7200 votes. Total valid votes?
(a) 54000  (b) 60000  (c) 64000  (d) 72000
**Answer: (b) 60000**
*Solution:* Winner 56%, loser 44%, margin = 12% = 7200 → total = 7200/12 × 100 = 60000.

**Q9.** If the length of a rectangle increases by 20% and breadth decreases by 20%, the area changes by:
(a) no change  (b) −4%  (c) +4%  (d) −2%
**Answer: (b) −4%**
*Solution:* Net = `20 − 20 − (20×20)/100 = −4%`.

**Q10.** A student scored 30% and failed by 45 marks. Another scored 42% and got 9 marks more than passing. Find total marks.
(a) 450  (b) 500  (c) 550  (d) 600
**Answer: (a) 450**
*Solution:* Passing mark = 30% + 45 = 42% − 9 → 12% = 54 → total = 54/12×100 = 450. (Pass = 30%×450+45 = 135+45 = 180.)

**Q11.** Two numbers are 20% and 50% more than a third number. What % is the first of the second?
(a) 70%  (b) 80%  (c) 90%  (d) 75%
**Answer: (b) 80%**
*Solution:* Let third = 100 → first = 120, second = 150 → 120/150×100 = 80%.

**Q12.** The population of a town increases 10% in year 1 and decreases 10% in year 2. Net change over two years?
(a) 0%  (b) −1%  (c) +1%  (d) −2%
**Answer: (b) −1%**
*Solution:* `+10 −10 −(10×10)/100 = −1%`.

## HARD

**Q13.** A man spends 75% of income. His income rises 20% and expenditure rises 10%. By what % do his savings increase?
(a) 40%  (b) 50%  (c) 45%  (d) 60%
**Answer: (b) 50%**
*Solution:* Income 100 → save 25, spend 75. New income 120, new spend 75×1.1 = 82.5 → new save = 37.5. Increase = (37.5−25)/25 ×100 = **50%**.

**Q14.** 40% of the students passed in Maths, 30% in English, 10% in both. What % failed in both?
(a) 30%  (b) 40%  (c) 50%  (d) 60%
**Answer: (b) 40%**
*Solution:* Passed at least one = 40+30−10 = 60% → failed both = 40%.

**Q15.** The salary of a worker is first increased by 10% and then decreased by 5%. If the present salary is ₹20,900, find the original.
(a) ₹19,000  (b) ₹20,000  (c) ₹21,000  (d) ₹18,000
**Answer: (b) ₹20,000**
*Solution:* Factor = 1.10 × 0.95 = 1.045 → original = 20900/1.045 = 20000.

**Q16.** If A's income is 25% less than B's, and B's is 25% more than C's, then A's income compared to C's is:
(a) equal  (b) 6.25% less  (c) 6.25% more  (d) 5% less
**Answer: (b) 6.25% less**
*Solution:* C=100 → B=125 → A=125×0.75 = 93.75 → 6.25% less than C.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN

**Q17.** (SSC CGL pattern) The price of an article is reduced by 20%. To restore the original price, the new price must be increased by:
(a) 20%  (b) 25%  (c) 22%  (d) 16⅔%
**Answer: (b) 25%**
*Solution:* `20/(100−20)×100 = 25%`. 🔑 −20% = ×4/5 → restore = ×5/4 = +25%.

**Q18.** (SSC CGL pattern) In a class, 60% are boys. 80% of boys and 75% of girls passed. What % of the class failed?
(a) 21%  (b) 22%  (c) 23%  (d) 24%
**Answer: (b) 22%**
*Solution:* Boys 60 → passed 48, failed 12. Girls 40 → passed 30, failed 10. Failed = 22%.

**Q19.** (SSC CGL pattern) If 8% of x = 4% of y, then 20% of x equals what % of y?
(a) 10%  (b) 12%  (c) 15%  (d) 8%
**Answer: (a) 10%**
*Solution:* 8x = 4y → x = y/2 → 20% of x = 0.2×(y/2) = 0.1y = 10% of y.

**Q20.** (SSC CGL pattern) A's marks were wrongly entered as 83 instead of 63. Average of the class rose by ½. Number of students?
(a) 20  (b) 30  (c) 40  (d) 10
**Answer: (c) 40**
*Solution:* Excess = 83−63 = 20 spread over n students raises avg by 20/n = ½ → n = 40.

### 🔑 Exam Tricks Box
- Convert % to fractions instantly (table above) — turns multiplication into division.
- "Restore after x% fall" and "consumption cut after x% rise" both use `x/(100±x)`.
- Successive % → always `a+b+ab/100` (sign-aware). Equal +x/−x → `−x²/100`.
- Net effect of two opposite 20% changes is **always −4%**; two 10% → −1%.

## BOOSTER SET — Extra Hard & PYQ Pattern

**B1.** The price of an article is increased by 25% and then decreased by 20%. The net percentage change in price is:
(a) +5%  (b) −5%  (c) 0%  (d) +1%
**Answer: (c) 0%**
*Solution:* Net = `25 − 20 − (25×20)/100 = 5 − 5 = 0%`.
🔑 *Trick:* +1/4 then −1/5 → ×5/4 × 4/5 = 1 → no change.

**B2.** The price of petrol rises by 20%. By what percent must a driver cut consumption to keep his fuel bill unchanged?
(a) 16⅔%  (b) 20%  (c) 25%  (d) 15%
**Answer: (a) 16⅔%**
*Solution:* Reduction = `x/(100+x)×100 = 20/120×100 = 16⅔%`.
🔑 *Trick:* +20% = +1/5 → cut by 1/(5+1) = 1/6 = 16⅔%.

**B3.** In an election between two candidates, 20% of votes were invalid. The winner got 65% of the valid votes and won by 2400 votes. Find the total number of votes cast.
(a) 10000  (b) 12000  (c) 15000  (d) 16000
**Answer: (a) 10000**
*Solution:* Winner 65%, loser 35% of valid → margin = 30% of valid = 2400 → valid = 8000. Valid = 80% of total → total = 8000/0.8 = **10000**.
🔑 *Trick:* Margin% of valid = 65 − 35 = 30%; valid = 2400/0.3 = 8000; then divide by 0.8.

**B4.** A student scored 36% in an exam and failed by 24 marks. Another scored 43% and got 18 marks more than the passing mark. Find the maximum marks.
(a) 500  (b) 600  (c) 550  (d) 450
**Answer: (b) 600**
*Solution:* Pass mark = 36% + 24 = 43% − 18 → 7% = 42 → max = 42/7 × 100 = 600. (Check pass = 36% of 600 + 24 = 216 + 24 = 240 = 43% of 600 − 18 = 258 − 18 = 240 ✓.)

**B5.** A's income is 20% more than B's, and B's income is 25% more than C's. By what percent is A's income more than C's?
(a) 45%  (b) 50%  (c) 55%  (d) 40%
**Answer: (b) 50%**
*Solution:* C = 100 → B = 125 → A = 125 × 1.20 = 150 → A is 50% more than C.

**B6.** The population of a town increases by 10% in the first year and by 20% in the second year. If the present population is 26,400, what was it two years ago?
(a) 20000  (b) 22000  (c) 24000  (d) 21000
**Answer: (a) 20000**
*Solution:* Factor = 1.10 × 1.20 = 1.32 → original = 26400/1.32 = 20000.

**B7.** In a mixture of 60 litres, the ratio of milk to water is 2 : 1. What percent of the mixture is water?
(a) 25%  (b) 30%  (c) 33⅓%  (d) 40%
**Answer: (c) 33⅓%**
*Solution:* Water = 1 part of 3 = 1/3 = 33⅓%. (Water volume = 20 L of 60 L = 33⅓%.)

**B8.** If the numerator of a fraction is increased by 20% and the denominator decreased by 20%, the fraction becomes 3/4. Find the original fraction.
(a) 1/2  (b) 8/15  (c) 2/5  (d) 3/8
**Answer: (a) 1/2**
*Solution:* New = (1.2N)/(0.8D) = 1.5 × (N/D) = 3/4 → N/D = (3/4)/1.5 = 1/2.

**B9.** 30% of a number exceeds 25% of it by 12. Find the number.
(a) 200  (b) 240  (c) 250  (d) 300
**Answer: (b) 240**
*Solution:* (30 − 25)% = 5% = 12 → number = 12/5 × 100 = 240.

**B10.** A man spends 75% of his income. If his income increases by 20% and his expenditure increases by 16%, by what percent do his savings increase?
(a) 30%  (b) 32%  (c) 34%  (d) 36%
**Answer: (b) 32%**
*Solution:* Income 100 → save 25, spend 75. New income 120, new spend 75 × 1.16 = 87 → new save = 120 − 87 = 33. Increase = (33 − 25)/25 × 100 = 8/25 × 100 = **32%**.

**B11.** (SSC CGL pattern) If 15% of A = 20% of B, then A : B is:
(a) 3 : 4  (b) 4 : 3  (c) 15 : 20  (d) 2 : 3
**Answer: (b) 4 : 3**
*Solution:* 15A = 20B → A/B = 20/15 = 4/3 → A : B = 4 : 3.

**B12.** (SSC CGL pattern) A number is first increased by 10% and then by 10% again. What single percentage increase is equivalent?
(a) 20%  (b) 21%  (c) 22%  (d) 19%
**Answer: (b) 21%**
*Solution:* Successive `10 + 10 + (10×10)/100 = 21%`.
🔑 *Trick:* Two equal +x% successive changes → `2x + x²/100`.
