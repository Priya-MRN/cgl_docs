## 🎯 TARGET POST: Assistant Section Officer (ASO)

> **The aspirant is applying for ASO (Assistant Section Officer — e.g. CSS, MEA, AFHQ).**
> Per the notification **paras 13.9.2 & 13.9.3**, the papers each post takes differ:
>
> | Paper | Who takes it | Applies to ASO? |
> | --- | --- | :-: |
> | **Tier-I** (all 4 sections) | **Every** candidate | ✅ Yes |
> | **Tier-II Paper-I** (Maths, Reasoning, English, GA, Computer) | **Compulsory for ALL posts** (13.9.2) | ✅ Yes |
> | **Tier-II Paper-II (Statistics)** | **Only JSO / Statistical Investigator** | ❌ **No — out of scope** |
> | **Tier-II Paper-III (Finance & Economics)** | **Only Assistant Audit/Accounts Officer (AAO)** | ❌ **No — out of scope** |
>
> **ASO exam path → Tier-I + Tier-II Paper-I only.**
> ➡️ For ASO, **both Paper-II (Statistics) and Paper-III (Finance & Economics) are out of scope.**
> Focus entirely on the common **Paper-I**: Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English Language &
> Comprehension, General Awareness, and Computer Knowledge.
> Note: ASO additionally requires a **DEST (Data Entry Skill Test)** — see para 13.9.10 of the notification.

## TIER-I — Indicative Syllabus (Para 13.10)

> Four sections. **Part C (Quant) = 10th-standard level. Parts A, B, D = Graduation level** (Para 13.10.5).

### A. General Intelligence & Reasoning (Para 13.10.1)
Both **verbal and non-verbal** type. Topics:
- Semantic Analogy · Symbolic/Number Analogy · Figural Analogy
- Semantic Classification · Symbolic/Number Classification · Figural Classification
- Semantic Series · Number Series · Figural Series
- Problem Solving · Word Building
- Coding & Decoding · Numerical Operations · Symbolic Operations
- Trends · Space Orientation · Space Visualization · Venn Diagrams
- Drawing Inferences · Punched hole/pattern – folding & unfolding
- Figural Pattern – folding and completion
- Indexing · Address matching · Date & city matching
- Classification of centre codes/roll numbers
- Small & Capital letters/numbers coding, decoding and classification
- Embedded Figures · Critical Thinking · Emotional Intelligence · Social Intelligence
- (also: analogies, similarities & differences, space visualization, spatial orientation,
  problem solving, analysis, judgment, decision making, visual memory, discrimination,
  observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, figural classification,
  arithmetic number series, non-verbal series, statement-conclusion, syllogistic reasoning)

### B. General Awareness (Para 13.10.2)
- General awareness of the **environment** and its **application to society**.
- **Current events** + everyday observation & experience in their **scientific aspect**.
- India and its neighbouring countries, especially: **History, Culture, Geography,
  Economic Scene, General Policy & Scientific Research.**

### C. Quantitative Aptitude (Para 13.10.3) — *10th standard level*
- Computation of whole numbers, decimals, fractions, relationships between numbers
- Percentage · Ratio & Proportion · Square roots · Averages
- Interest · Profit and Loss · Discount · Partnership Business
- Mixture and Alligation · Time and Distance · Time & Work
- Basic algebraic identities of School Algebra & Elementary surds · Graphs of Linear Equations
- Triangle and its various kinds of centres · Congruence and similarity of triangles
- Circle and its chords, tangents, angles subtended by chords, common tangents to two or more circles
- Triangle · Quadrilaterals · Regular Polygons · Circle · Right Prism · Right Circular Cone
- Right Circular Cylinder · Sphere · Hemispheres · Rectangular Parallelepiped
- Regular Right Pyramid with triangular or square base
- Trigonometric ratio · Degree and Radian Measures · Standard Identities · Complementary angles
- Heights and Distances
- Histogram · Frequency polygon · Bar diagram · Pie chart

### D. English Comprehension (Para 13.10.4)
- Ability to understand correct English, **basic comprehension and writing ability**.

## TIER-II — Indicative Syllabus (Para 13.11)

> **Levels (Para 13.11.8):** Paper-I Part A (Maths) = **Matriculation**; Paper-I Part A (English) =
> **10+2**; Paper-II and Paper-III = **Graduation**.

### PAPER-I

#### Section-I, Part A — Mathematical Abilities (Para 13.11.1)
- **Number Systems (13.11.1.1):** Computation of Whole Number, Decimal and Fractions, Relationship between numbers.
- **Fundamental Arithmetical Operations (13.11.1.2):** Percentages, Ratio and Proportion, Square roots, Averages, Interest (Simple and Compound), Profit and Loss, Discount, Partnership Business, Mixture and Alligation, Time and distance, Time and work.
- **Algebra (13.11.1.3):** Basic algebraic identities of School Algebra and Elementary surds (simple problems) and Graphs of Linear Equations.
- **Geometry (13.11.1.4):** Elementary geometric figures and facts: Triangle and its various kinds of centres, Congruence and similarity of triangles, Circle and its chords, tangents, angles subtended by chords, common tangents to two or more circles.
- **Mensuration (13.11.1.5):** Triangle, Quadrilaterals, Regular Polygons, Circle, Right Prism, Right Circular Cone, Right Circular Cylinder, Sphere, Hemispheres, Rectangular Parallelepiped, Regular Right Pyramid with triangular or square Base.
- **Trigonometry (13.11.1.6):** Trigonometric ratios, Complementary angles, Height and distances (simple problems only), Standard Identities like sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, etc.
- **Statistics & Probability (13.11.1.7):** Use of Tables and Graphs — Histogram, Frequency polygon, Bar-diagram, Pie-chart; Measures of central tendency — mean, median, mode, standard deviation; calculation of simple probabilities.

#### Section-I, Part B — Reasoning & General Intelligence (Para 13.11.2)
Verbal and non-verbal: Semantic Analogy, Symbolic operations, Symbolic/Number Analogy, Trends, Figural Analogy, Space Orientation, Semantic Classification, Venn Diagrams, Symbolic/Number Classification, Drawing inferences, Figural Classification, Punched hole/pattern-folding & unfolding, Semantic Series, Figural Pattern-folding and completion, Number Series, Embedded figures, Figural Series, Critical Thinking, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building, Social Intelligence, Coding and de-coding, Numerical operations, Other sub-topics if any.

#### Section-II, Part A — English Language & Comprehension (Para 13.11.3)
Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms and their correct usage; **Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Homonyms, Antonyms, Spellings/Detecting mis-spelt words, Idioms & Phrases, One word substitution, Improvement of Sentences, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect narration, Shuffling of Sentence parts, Shuffling of Sentences in a passage, Cloze Passage, Comprehension Passage.**
- For comprehension: **three or more paragraphs**; at least one simple (book/story), the other two on **current affairs / report / editorial**.

#### Section-II, Part B — General Awareness (Para 13.11.4)
Same scope as Tier-I GA: environment & application to society, current events, scientific aspect of everyday experience, and **India & neighbours — History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General policy and scientific research.**

#### Section-III — Computer Knowledge / Proficiency (Para 13.11.5)
- **Computer Basics (13.11.5.1):** Organization of a computer, CPU, input/output devices, computer memory, memory organization, back-up devices, PORTs, Windows Explorer, Keyboard shortcuts.
- **Software (13.11.5.2):** Windows OS; basics of MS Office — MS Word, MS Excel, PowerPoint, etc.
- **Internet & e-mail (13.11.5.3):** Web Browsing & Searching, Downloading & Uploading, Managing an E-mail Account, e-Banking.
- **Networking & cyber security (13.11.5.4):** Networking devices and protocols; network & information security threats (hacking, virus, worms, Trojan, etc.) and preventive measures.

### PAPER-II — Statistics (Para 13.11.6)  ·  ❌ NOT for ASO (JSO/SI only)

> ⚠️ **Skip for the ASO target post.** This paper is taken only by Junior Statistical Officer /
> Statistical Investigator candidates (para 13.9.3). Kept below for completeness only.

1. **Collection, Classification & Presentation of Data (13.11.6.1)** — primary/secondary data, methods of collection, tabulation, graphs & charts, frequency distributions, diagrammatic presentation.
2. **Measures of Central Tendency (13.11.6.2)** — mean, median, mode; partition values (quartiles, deciles, percentiles).
3. **Measures of Dispersion (13.11.6.3)** — range, quartile deviation, mean deviation, standard deviation; relative dispersion.
4. **Moments, Skewness & Kurtosis (13.11.6.4)** — types of moments & relationships; skewness & kurtosis measures.
5. **Correlation & Regression (13.11.6.5)** — scatter diagram, simple correlation coefficient, simple regression lines, Spearman's rank correlation, association of attributes, multiple regression, multiple & partial correlation (three variables only).
6. **Probability Theory (13.11.6.6)** — meaning & definitions, conditional & compound probability, independent events, Bayes' theorem.
7. **Random Variable & Probability Distributions (13.11.6.7)** — random variable, probability functions, expectation & variance, higher moments; Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential; joint distribution of two discrete variables.
8. **Sampling Theory (13.11.6.8)** — population & sample, parameter & statistic, sampling/non-sampling errors; probability & non-probability sampling (simple random, stratified, multistage, multiphase, cluster, systematic, purposive, convenience, quota); sampling distribution (statement only); sample size decisions.
9. **Statistical Inference (13.11.6.9)** — point & interval estimation, properties of a good estimator, methods of estimation (moments, MLE, least squares), testing of hypothesis, small & large sample tests (Z, t, Chi-square, F), confidence intervals.
10. **Analysis of Variance (13.11.6.10)** — one-way and two-way classified data.
11. **Time Series Analysis (13.11.6.11)** — components, trend determination, seasonal variation measurement.
12. **Index Numbers (13.11.6.12)** — meaning, construction problems, types, formulae, base shifting & splicing, cost-of-living index, uses.

### PAPER-III — General Studies (Finance & Economics) (Para 13.11.7)  ·  ❌ NOT for ASO (AAO only)

> ⚠️ **Skip for the ASO target post.** This paper is taken only by Assistant Audit Officer /
> Assistant Accounts Officer (AAO) candidates (para 13.9.3). Kept below for completeness only.
> Finance & Accounts (80 marks) + Economics & Governance (120 marks).


#### Part A — Finance and Accounts — 80 marks (13.11.7.1)
**Fundamental principles & basic concept of Accounting:**
- **Financial Accounting (13.11.7.1.1.1):** nature & scope, limitations, basic concepts & conventions, Generally Accepted Accounting Principles.
- **Basic concepts of accounting (13.11.7.1.1.2):** single & double entry, books of original entry, bank reconciliation, journal, ledgers, trial balance, rectification of errors, manufacturing/trading/P&L appropriation accounts, balance sheet, capital vs revenue expenditure, depreciation accounting, valuation of inventories, non-profit organisation accounts, receipts & payments and income & expenditure accounts, bills of exchange, self-balancing ledgers.

#### Part B — Economics and Governance — 120 marks (13.11.7.2)
1. **Comptroller & Auditor General of India (13.11.7.2.1)** — constitutional provisions, role & responsibility.
2. **Finance Commission (13.11.7.2.2)** — role & functions.
3. **Basic Concept of Economics & Micro Economics (13.11.7.2.3)** — definition, scope & nature; methods of economic study; central problems; production possibilities curve.
4. **Theory of Demand & Supply (13.11.7.2.4)** — demand & determinants, law of demand & elasticity (price, income, cross); consumer behaviour (Marshallian & indifference curve); supply & determinants, law of supply & elasticity.
5. **Theory of Production & Cost (13.11.7.2.5)** — factors of production; law of variable proportions; laws of returns to scale.
6. **Forms of Market & Price Determination (13.11.7.2.6)** — Perfect Competition, Monopoly, Monopolistic Competition, Oligopoly; price determination in each.
7. **Indian Economy (13.11.7.2.7):**
   - Nature of the Indian Economy — role of Agriculture, Industry, Services; their problems & growth (13.11.7.2.7.1)
   - National Income — concepts & methods of measuring (13.11.7.2.7.2)
   - Population — size, growth rate & implications (13.11.7.2.7.3)
   - Poverty & unemployment — absolute/relative poverty; types, causes, incidence of unemployment (13.11.7.2.7.4)
   - Infrastructure — Energy, Transportation, Communication (13.11.7.2.7.5)
8. **Economic Reforms in India (13.11.7.2.8)** — reforms since 1991; Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation, Disinvestment.
9. **Money & Banking (13.11.7.2.9):**
   - Monetary/Fiscal policy — role & functions of RBI; functions of commercial banks/RRB/Payment Banks (13.11.7.2.9.1)
   - Budget, Fiscal deficits & Balance of Payments (13.11.7.2.9.2)
   - Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management (FRBM) Act, 2003 (13.11.7.2.9.3)
10. **Role of Information Technology in Governance (13.11.7.2.10).**
