# Current Affairs — Science & Technology

> Source: [gknow.in › Current Affairs › Science and Technology](https://gknow.in/category/current-affairs/science-and-technology/). Figures as of mid-2026 — verify before exam use.

## Revision Notes

### Space Missions
- **Chang'e-7 (China)** — extracted/analysed water molecules from Moon's south pole.
- **Artemis II (NASA)** — crewed lunar flyby; reached 252,756 miles from Earth (Apr 2026).
- **Gaganyaan (India)** — uncrewed G1 test flight Dec 2025; crewed mission targeted 2027.
- **GSAT-7R** — India's heaviest comm satellite (4,410 kg), launched via **LVM3** for Navy.
- **Mission Drishti** — world's first **OptoSAR** satellite (optical + radar imaging).

### Energy & Transport
- **Hydrogen Train** — India's first indigenous hydrogen train (Jind–Sonipat); 1,200 kW fuel cell.
- **PFBR** — Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor achieved criticality at **Kalpakkam**.

### Defence
- **Agni with MIRV** — multiple independently targetable warheads tested.
- **SFDR** — Solid Fuel Ducted Ramjet propulsion demonstrated.

### AI & Digital
- **BHASHINI** — 350+ AI models; 36 text + 23 voice languages.
- **Google AI Hub** — $15 bn data centre at **Visakhapatnam** (1 GW, renewables).
- **Vikram-3201** — India's first fully indigenous semiconductor chip (ISRO).
- **Starlink** — satellite internet approved in India (₹33,000 kit, ₹3,000/month).
