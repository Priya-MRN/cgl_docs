## Revision Notes

### Major Tournaments
- **FIFA World Cup 2026** — hosted by **Canada, Mexico, USA** (first 3-nation host); 48 teams, 104 matches.
- **ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026** — India beat New Zealand in the final (8 Mar).
- **ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026** — England & Wales; 12 teams; India captained by Harmanpreet Kaur.
- **IPL 2026** — **RCB** won (beat Gujarat Titans by 5 wickets); Kohli unbeaten 75 in final.

### Records
- **R. Praggnanandhaa** — first Indian to win **Norway Chess** title.
- **Gurindervir Singh** — first Indian sprinter under 10.10 s in 100 m.
- **Tejaswin Shankar** — indoor decathlon national record (5,993 pts).
- **Sabastian Sawe (Kenya)** — first to officially run a marathon under 2 hours (1:59:30).

### Other Winners
- **French Open 2026** — Alexander Zverev (men), Mirra Andreeva (women).
- **Satwik–Chirag** — Singapore Open men's doubles.
- **Ranji Trophy 2025-26** — Jammu & Kashmir's first-ever title.
