# Current Affairs — Environment

> Source: [gknow.in › Current Affairs › Environment](https://gknow.in/category/current-affairs/environment-current-affairs/). Figures as of mid-2026 — verify before exam use.

## Revision Notes

### Wetlands & Ramsar Sites
- **Surha Tal (UP)** — India's **100th Ramsar site** (5 June 2026); 3,400 ha.
- **Shekha Jheel (Aligarh)** — India's 99th Ramsar site (Apr 2026).

### Wildlife & Protected Areas
- **Asiatic Lions** — Gujarat census recorded **891 lions** (May 2025).
- **Riverine Dolphins** — 6,327 counted across 28 rivers / 8 states.
- **Project Cheetah** — Botswana handed 8 cheetahs to India (Nov 2025).
- **Madhav National Park** — new tiger reserve in Shivpuri, MP.

### Climate
- **El Niño 2026** — rainfall deficit up to 64% (4-15 June 2026); monsoon ~90% of LPA.

### Green Energy & Forests
- **Bio-bitumen** — India first to begin commercial production (Jan 2026).
- **Green Hydrogen** — 1 MW plant at Kandla Port (Jul 2025).
- India ranks **9th globally** in forest area; **3rd** in annual forest gain (FAO 2025).
- **Cold Desert Biosphere Reserve** added to UNESCO network — India's total now **13**.
