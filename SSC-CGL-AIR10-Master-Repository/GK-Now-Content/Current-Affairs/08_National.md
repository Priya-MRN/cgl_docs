## Revision Notes

### Defence & Security
- **Indigenous Air Cushion Vehicle (ACV)** inducted by Indian Coast Guard (18 June 2026); 50% indigenous.
- **PRAHAAR** — MHA's proactive counter-terrorism framework (Feb 2026).
- India declared **"completely free from Naxalism"** (19 May 2026) — ending a 55-year insurgency.

### Infrastructure
- **Ganga Expressway** — 594 km, inaugurated 29 Apr 2026 (Meerut–Prayagraj).
- **Delhi–Dehradun Corridor** — ₹11,868 cr; travel down to 2.5 hours.
- **Noida International Airport** — Phase I inaugurated 28 Mar 2026 (12 million capacity).

### Railways & Water
- **South Coast Railway** — India's **18th railway zone** (HQ Visakhapatnam).
- **Kishau Dam** — six states agreed (₹15,000 cr) for Yamuna rejuvenation; 236 m gravity dam.

### Governance
- **West Bengal Elections** — record **92.47% turnout** (Apr 2026), highest since Independence.
- **Census 2027** — India's **first fully digital census**, beginning 1 Apr 2026.
- **UPSC CSE 2025** — Anuj Agnihotri AIR 1.
