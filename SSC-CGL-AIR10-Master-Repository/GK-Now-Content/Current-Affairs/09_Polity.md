## Revision Notes

### Constitutional & Parliamentary
- **Women's Reservation** — 106th Amendment (2023) mandates **33%** reservation in Lok Sabha & State Assemblies; target 2029.
- **Supreme Court Strength** — Cabinet approved raising judges from 33 to 37 (excl. CJI).
- **Jan Vishwas Bill 2026** — decriminalised **717 provisions** across 79 central laws.
- **Central Armed Police Forces Bill 2026** — uniform recruitment; 100% deputation of DG/Special DG from IPS.

### Judiciary Milestones
- **Passive Euthanasia** approved (Mar 2026) — right to die with dignity under **Article 21**.
- **Menstrual Hygiene** declared part of Article 21 (Jan 2026) — free napkins in schools.

### Executive & States
- **Kerala → "Keralam"** — Cabinet approved renaming (pending Parliament).
- **Amaravati** made Andhra Pradesh's permanent capital.
- **UCC** — Gujarat became 2nd state (after Uttarakhand 2024) to adopt Uniform Civil Code.
- **NITI Aayog 11th Council** — themed "Inclusive Human Development for Viksit Bharat @2047".

### Records
- **PM Modi** — longest-serving elected PM (4,399 consecutive days), surpassing Nehru.
