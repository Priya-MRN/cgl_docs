# Current Affairs — Economy

> Source: [gknow.in › Current Affairs › Economy](https://gknow.in/category/current-affairs/economy-current-affairs/). Figures as of mid-2026 — verify before exam use.

## Revision Notes

### Growth
- **FY 2025-26 real GDP** — 7.4% (Q2 at 8.2%); manufacturing 9.1% in Q2.
- OECD: India remains the **fastest-growing major economy**.

### RBI & Monetary Policy
- **Repo rate** held at **5.25%** (June 2026).
- Four rate cuts in 2025 totalling **125 bps**.
- Record **₹2.86 lakh crore** surplus transfer to Centre (FY26).

### Inflation & Prices
- **CPI inflation** projected at ~2% for FY26 (historic low).
- **WPI base year** revised 2011-12 → **2022-23** (eff. 15 June 2026).

### External Sector & Trade
- Current account **surplus $7.1 bn** in Q4 FY26; FDI $6.9 bn.
- **India–EU FTA** concluded Jan 2026 (99% export market access).
- **India–US trade deal** — tariffs cut to 18% (Feb 2026).

### Banking & Payments
- **UPI** — world's largest real-time payment system (129.3 bn transactions).
- **Paytm Payments Bank** licence cancelled (Apr 2026).
