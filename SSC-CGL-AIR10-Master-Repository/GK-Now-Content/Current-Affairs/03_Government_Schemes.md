# Current Affairs — Government Schemes

## Revision Notes

| Scheme | Authority | Purpose / Key Features |
|--------|-----------|------------------------|
| **ULLAS – Nav Bharat Saaksharta Karyakram** | Education Ministry | Adult literacy (15+); launched 2022. Sikkim declared fully literate (99.82%). |
| **VB-G RAM G Act 2025** | Central Govt | Replaces MGNREGA; **125 days** guaranteed rural employment; ₹95,692 cr budget; childcare at worksites. |
| **JANANI Platform** | Health Ministry | Maternal & child health; digital MCH cards with QR codes; 1.34 cr registrations. |
| **BHAVYA Scheme** | Union Cabinet | 100 plug-and-play industrial parks; ₹33,660 cr; 15 lakh jobs target. |
| **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** | Ministry of Power | Rooftop solar; up to **300 free units/month**; ₹75,021 cr; 7,075 MW installed. |
| **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)** | Finance Ministry | Old-age security; ₹1,000–₹5,000/month pension; 8.66 cr subscribers; extended to 2030-31. |
| **PM RAHAT Scheme** | MoRTH | Cashless treatment for road-accident victims; ₹1.5 lakh cover; 7-day window. |
| **White Revolution 2.0** | Ministry of Cooperation | 75,000 new dairy societies; 70% workforce women. |
| **National Cooperative Policy 2025** | Ministry of Cooperation | 20-year roadmap (2025–2045); 2 lakh new PACS by 2026. |
| **Startup India** | DPIIT | 2.10 lakh recognised startups; 120+ unicorns ($350 bn); 3rd-largest ecosystem. |
| **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** | Union Cabinet | 100 low-productivity districts; 6-year scheme; converges 36 central schemes. |
| **Skill India Mission** | Skill Development Ministry | 6 cr youth trained; 45% women; job-readiness 34%→51%. |
