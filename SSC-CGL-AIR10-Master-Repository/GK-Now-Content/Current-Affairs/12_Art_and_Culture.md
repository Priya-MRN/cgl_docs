## Revision Notes

### UNESCO Recognitions
- **Deepavali** inscribed on UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage list — India's **16th** element (Dec 2025).
- **UNESCO World Heritage Sites** — India now has **44** (36 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed); recent: Maratha Military Landscapes, Moidams of Assam.
- **Cold Desert Biosphere Reserve** added to UNESCO network.

### Heritage & Pilgrimage
- **Bhojshala Complex (MP)** — declared temple of Goddess Saraswati; ASI granted Hindu access (May 2026).
- **Kailash Manasarovar Yatra 2026** — June–Aug; 2,000 pilgrims via lottery; routes via Lipulekh & Nathu La.
- **Somnath Temple** — Swabhiman Parv (Jan 2026), marking 1,000 years since Mahmud of Ghazni's 1026 attack.

### Art & Film
- **Raja Ravi Varma's "Yashoda and Krishna"** sold for **₹167.2 crore** — costliest Indian artwork (Apr 2026).
- **56th IFFI (Goa, Nov 2025)** — Golden Peacock to *Skin of Youth*; honoured Rajinikanth.
- **Venice 2025** — Anuparna Roy won Best Director for *Songs of Forgotten Trees*.

### Festivals
- **Hornbill Festival (Nagaland)** — Dec 1-10, 2025; 17 tribes; 6 international partners.
- **Kashi-Tamil Sangamam 4.0** — Dec 2025.
