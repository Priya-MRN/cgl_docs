# GK Topic — Polity & Constitution

> Source: [gknow.in › GK MCQ › Indian Polity & Constitution](https://gknow.in/indian-polity-and-constitution-mcq-for-competitive-exams/).

## Key Revision Facts
- India = **"Union of States"** (not a federation in name).
- **Single citizenship** adopted from England.
- **DPSP** inspired by Ireland; **equality before law** from Britain.
- **Fundamental Duties** — added by 42nd Amendment, **1976**; now **11** (originally 10).
- Constitution came into force **26 January 1950**; voting age **18**.
- Parliamentary system = "fusion of executive and legislature".

> Note: gknow lists "12 Fundamental Duties" — the correct, exam-standard count is **11** (10 original + 1 added by the 86th Amendment, 2002). Use **11**.
