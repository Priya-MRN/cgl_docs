## Key Revision Facts
- **First British fort in Calcutta** — Fort William.
- **Portuguese HQ in India** — Goa; first European traders to arrive.
- **Khalsa** founded **1699** by Guru Gobind Singh.
- **First Governor-General** — Warren Hastings; **Last Viceroy** — Lord Mountbatten.
- **1857 outcome** — power transferred from East India Company to British Crown.
- **Ryotwari System** — direct land revenue payment by peasants to government.
- **Indian Councils Act 1909** = Minto-Morley Reforms.
