## Key Revision Facts
- **Total area** — ~3,287,590 sq km; India is the **7th-largest** country.
- **Coastline** — ~7,500 km; **Gujarat** has the longest coastline.
- **Southernmost point** — Indira Point (Andaman & Nicobar).
- **Indian desert** — Thar.
- **Highest waterfall** — Kunchikal Falls.
- **Survey of India HQ** — Dehradun.
- **Aravallis** are NOT part of the Himalayas; **Tethys Sea** preceded the Himalayas.
