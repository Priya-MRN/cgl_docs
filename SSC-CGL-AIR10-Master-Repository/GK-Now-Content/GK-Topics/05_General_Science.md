# GK Topic — General Science

> Source: [gknow.in › GK MCQ › Science](https://gknow.in/biology-gk-mcqs-for-competitive-exams/) (Biology / Chemistry / Physics).

## Key Revision Facts
- **Vitamin D** deficiency → rickets; **Vitamin C** → scurvy.
- **RBCs, WBCs, platelets** produced in **bone marrow**.
- **Blood pH** ≈ **7.4** (slightly alkaline).
- **Pancreas** produces digestive enzymes (and insulin).
- Digestion begins in the **mouth** (salivary amylase).
- **DNA** = Deoxyribonucleic Acid; stores genetic information.
- **CO₂** is used in photosynthesis; **O₂** released.
- Normal body temperature ≈ **98.6°F (37°C)**.
