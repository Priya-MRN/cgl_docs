## Key Revision Facts
- **RBI** established **1935**; nationalised **1949**.
- **14 banks** nationalised in **1969** (6 more in 1980).
- **SEBI** established **1988** (statutory 1992).
- **Decimal currency** adopted **1957**.
- **MSP** for food grains introduced **1974**.
- **NABARD** = National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development.
- RBI is the **lender of last resort**; one-rupee notes issued by the **Government of India**.
- "Garibi Hatao" — Fifth Five-Year Plan.
