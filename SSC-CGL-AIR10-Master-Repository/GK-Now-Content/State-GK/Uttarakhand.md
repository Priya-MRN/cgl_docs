# State GK — Uttarakhand

> Source: [gknow.in › State GK › Uttarakhand](https://gknow.in/uttarakhand-gk-questions-for-competitive-exams/).

## Key Facts
- **Statehood** — 9 November 2000 (27th state); originally **Uttaranchal**, renamed Uttarakhand in Jan 2007.
- **Highest peak** — Nanda Devi (~7,816 m), in Chamoli district.
- **Tehri Dam** — on the **Bhagirathi** river; India's highest & largest (260.5 m).
- **Jim Corbett National Park** — established 1936 (originally Hailey NP; renamed 1957).
- **State tree** — Buransh (*Rhododendron arboreum*).
- **Chipko Movement** — began **1973** (anti-deforestation).
- **Nainital** — "City of Lakes" (Naini Lake).
- **Doon** — narrow longitudinal Himalayan valleys (e.g. Dehradun).
