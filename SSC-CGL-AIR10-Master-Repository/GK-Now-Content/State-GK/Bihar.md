# State GK — Bihar

> Source: [gknow.in › State GK › Bihar](https://gknow.in/bihar-gk-mcqs-for-competitive-exams/).

## Key Facts
- **Bihar Day** — 22 March (formation in 1912, separated from Bengal).
- **Ancient name of Patna** — Pataliputra (Maurya Empire capital).
- **Jharkhand** separated from Bihar on **15 November 2000**.
- **Nalanda University** — founded by **Kumaragupta I**.
- **Bhagalpur** — the "Silk City".
- **State animal** — Ox; **state tree** — Peepal.
- **38 districts** in 9 divisions; monsoon June–September.
