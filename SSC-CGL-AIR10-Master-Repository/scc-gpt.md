## Q8. Painted Cube

A cube of side 5 cm is painted on all faces and cut into 1 cm cubes. How many small cubes have exactly two faces painted?

- (a) 24
- (b) 30
- (c) 36
- (d) 48

**Answer:** (c) 36

**Formula:** `12(n − 2)`
