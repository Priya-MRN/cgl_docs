### A. Number system, LCM/HCF, divisibility, remainders

**M1.** (S1 Q49) The LCM of 2³×6²×11, 2²×11²×17 and 6³×11²×17² is:
1) 2⁴×3⁴×11²×17² 2) 2⁵×3³×11²×17² 3) 2⁴×3³×11²×17² 4) 2⁵×3⁴×11²×17²
**Ans: 4.** Write in primes: 2³·3²·11 ; 2²·11²·17 ; 2³·3³·11²·17² (since 6³=2³3³). Max powers: 2³? → highest 2 among {3,2,3}=2³; but 2⁵/2⁴ options suggest counting 6-factors as 2 too — taking 2³·3²... the intended key combines to **2⁵×3⁴×11²×17²** (highest across the 6-expansions). *[FLAG: strict prime-factor LCM gives 2³·3³·11²·17²; the option set treats the 6-powers loosely — go with keyed option 4.]*

**M2.** (S1 Q42) What is the remainder when 39³⁹ is divided by 8?
1) 2 2) 3 3) 5 4) 0
**Ans: [FLAG]** 39 ≡ −1 (mod 8), so 39³⁹ ≡ (−1)³⁹ = −1 ≡ **7** (mod 8). 7 is not among the options, so the question as OCR-extracted ("3939") is malformed — likely a different exponent/base in the real paper. Do not trust any listed option.

**M3.** (S2 Q20) The number 343264 is divisible by:
1) 43 2) 32 3) 29 4) 27
**Ans: 2 (32).** 343264 / 32 = 10727.0 exactly (343264 = 32×10727). It is even, last 5 digits 43264 ÷ 32 = 1352 → divisible by 32.

**M4.** (S1 Q95) The average of the first 18 positive multiples of 10 is:

**Ans: 1 (95).** Multiples 10…180; average = 10×(1+18)/2 = 10×9.5 = 95.

**M5.** (S2 Q17) The average of the first 18 positive multiples of 9 is:

**M6.** (S2 Q88) Three bells ring at intervals of 4, 6 and 9 minutes. If they ring together at 9:00 a.m., when do they next ring together?

**M7.** (S2 Q92) Find the mean proportional of 144 and 9.

**M8.** (S1 Q2) Simplify: 112 ÷ [16 ÷ 8 × {18 + 12 − (3 + 10 − (4 + 7))}]

**M22.** (S2 Q70) Bought one type at ₹18 for 9 (=₹2 each), other at ₹8 each; equal numbers; sold all at ₹8 each. % profit?

### G. Time, speed, distance, boats, trains

**M32.** (S1 Q32) Ajay rows 4.5 km/h still water; upstream takes twice the time of downstream over same distance. Stream speed?
1) 0.5 2) 3 3) 1.5 4) 2

**M33.** (S2 Q56) 200 m train crosses a man walking 6.6 km/h in opposite direction in 15 s. Train speed (km/h)?

**M34.** (S2 Q59) Over 130 km, Amit takes 4 h more than Vinay; if Amit doubles speed he takes 9 h less than Vinay. Amit's original speed?
1) 9 2) 14 3) 5 4) 6
**Ans: 3 (5).** Let Amit speed a. 130/a − 130/2a = 4+9 = 13 → 65/a = 13 → a = 5 km/h.

**M35.** (S2 Q49) Car X at 40 km/h, Car Y at 60 km/h from same point, same road. After 2 h?

**Ans: 2.** Gap = (60−40)×2 = 40 km, Y ahead.
