## A. Ancient & Medieval India

**Indus Valley Civilization (c. 2500–1900 BCE)**
- Bronze Age, urban; sites: **Harappa, Mohenjo-daro** (on Indus/Ravi), Lothal & Dholavira (Gujarat), Kalibangan (Rajasthan), Rakhigarhi (Haryana, largest).
- Features: grid town planning, Great Bath (Mohenjo-daro), drainage, standardised weights; script **undeciphered**; no temples; worship of Mother Goddess & Pashupati.

**Vedic Age → Mahajanapadas → Mauryas & Guptas**
- **Mauryas:** Chandragupta Maurya (guided by **Chanakya/Kautilya**, author of *Arthashastra*); **Ashoka** (Kalinga war → Buddhism; rock edicts; Lion Capital = National Emblem).
- **Guptas (Golden Age):** Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya); **Aryabhata, Kalidasa, Varahamihira**; decimal system & concept of zero; **Nalanda University**.

**Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526):** Slave (Qutb-ud-din Aibak → Qutb Minar), Khilji (Alauddin), Tughlaq (Muhammad bin Tughlaq), Sayyid, Lodi.

**Mughals (1526–1857):** Babur (1st Battle of Panipat 1526) → Humayun → **Akbar** (Din-i-Ilahi, Mansabdari, Navaratnas) → Jahangir → Shah Jahan (Taj Mahal) → Aurangzeb (last great Mughal).

**Marathas:** **Chhatrapati Shivaji** (founder, guerrilla warfare); later the Peshwas.

**Vijayanagara & Bahmani:** Vijayanagara (1336, Harihara & Bukka; **Krishnadevaraya** the greatest; capital Hampi) vs the Bahmani Sultanate of the Deccan.

## C. National Renaissance & Early Uprisings

- **Socio-religious reform:** Raja Ram Mohan Roy (Brahmo Samaj, against Sati), Dayananda Saraswati (Arya Samaj), Swami Vivekananda (Ramakrishna Mission), Jyotiba Phule.
- **Revolt of 1857** ("First War of Independence") — began at Meerut; sparked by greased cartridges; leaders: Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai, Tantia Tope, Bahadur Shah Zafar.

## D. Indian National Movement

**Indian National Congress (founded 1885, A.O. Hume).**
- **Moderates** (1885–1905): Dadabhai Naoroji ("Drain Theory"), Gokhale.
- **Extremists** (1905–): Bal Gangadhar Tilak ("Swaraj is my birthright"), Lala Lajpat Rai, Bipin Chandra Pal (Lal-Bal-Pal).
- **Partition of Bengal (1905)** → **Swadeshi Movement**.

**Gandhian Era (1915 onwards)**
| Movement | Year | Trigger / Note |
|---|---|---|
| Champaran Satyagraha | 1917 | First Satyagraha in India (indigo farmers) |
| Rowlatt Satyagraha / Jallianwala Bagh | 1919 | Massacre at Amritsar (Gen. Dyer) |
| Non-Cooperation | 1920–22 | Called off after Chauri Chaura |
| Civil Disobedience / **Dandi Salt March** | 1930 | Breaking the salt law |
| Quit India | 1942 | "Do or Die"; final mass movement |

- **Independence: 15 August 1947** (Mountbatten Plan / Indian Independence Act 1947). Partition → India & Pakistan.

**Key leaders (syllabus-named):**
B.R. Ambedkar (Father of the Constitution, Dalit rights), Bhagat Singh (HSRA, martyr 1931), Bharathiar & V.O. Chidambaranar (TN — see Unit VI), Jawaharlal Nehru (1st PM), Kamarajar (TN, "Kingmaker"), Mahatma Gandhi, Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, Periyar (Self-Respect Movement), Rajaji (1st Indian Governor-General), Subhas Chandra Bose (INA, "Give me blood..."), Rabindranath Tagore (Nobel 1913, *Jana Gana Mana*).

## E. Indian Culture — Unity in Diversity

- **Unity in Diversity:** many races, languages (22 in 8th Schedule), religions, customs — yet a shared civilisational identity. India = **secular state** (no state religion).
- **Social harmony**, composite culture, classical arts (Bharatanatyam – TN, Kathakali – Kerala, etc.), music (Carnatic & Hindustani).

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Why was the Non-Cooperation Movement called off?**
Gandhi suspended it in **1922 after the Chauri Chaura incident** (a mob burned a police station), because the movement had turned violent, contradicting *ahimsa*.

**Example 2 — Why is the Gupta period called the "Golden Age"?**
Peak achievements in **mathematics (zero, decimals — Aryabhata), astronomy, literature (Kalidasa), art and science**, with political stability and prosperity.

## Previous-Year-Style MCQs

**1.** The largest Indus Valley site discovered in India is:
(a) Lothal (b) Kalibangan (c) **Rakhigarhi** (d) Dholavira
> **Ans: (c)** — Rakhigarhi (Haryana).

**2.** The author of *Arthashastra* was:
(a) Kalidasa (b) **Chanakya (Kautilya)** (c) Aryabhata (d) Megasthenes
> **Ans: (b)**

**3.** The Indian National Congress was founded in:
(a) 1857 (b) **1885** (c) 1905 (d) 1919
> **Ans: (b)** — by A.O. Hume.

**4.** The First Battle of Panipat (1526) was fought between:
(a) Akbar and Hemu (b) **Babur and Ibrahim Lodi** (c) Humayun and Sher Shah (d) Akbar and Rana Pratap
> **Ans: (b)**

**5.** The slogan "Swaraj is my birthright" was given by:
(a) Gandhi (b) Nehru (c) **Bal Gangadhar Tilak** (d) Bose
> **Ans: (c)**

**6.** The Dandi March (1930) was part of the:
(a) Non-Cooperation Movement (b) **Civil Disobedience Movement** (c) Quit India Movement (d) Swadeshi Movement
> **Ans: (b)**

**7.** "Father of the Indian Constitution" is:
(a) Nehru (b) **B.R. Ambedkar** (c) Rajendra Prasad (d) Patel
> **Ans: (b)**

**8.** The National Emblem is adapted from the Lion Capital at:
(a) Bodh Gaya (b) **Sarnath** (c) Sanchi (d) Amaravati
> **Ans: (b)**

**9.** The greatest ruler of the Vijayanagara Empire was:
(a) Harihara (b) **Krishnadevaraya** (c) Bukka (d) Rama Raya
> **Ans: (b)**

**10.** Who founded the Brahmo Samaj?
(a) Dayananda Saraswati (b) Vivekananda (c) **Raja Ram Mohan Roy** (d) Jyotiba Phule
> **Ans: (c)**

**11.** The Quit India Movement was launched in:
(a) 1930 (b) 1920 (c) **1942** (d) 1947
> **Ans: (c)** — slogan "Do or Die".

**12.** The author of *Vande Mataram* (National Song) is:
(a) Tagore (b) **Bankim Chandra Chatterjee** (c) Bharathiar (d) Iqbal
> **Ans: (b)**

**13.** Rani Lakshmibai was associated with the revolt of:
(a) 1947 (b) 1905 (c) **1857** (d) 1942
> **Ans: (c)**

**14.** The first Satyagraha by Gandhi in India was at:
(a) Kheda (b) **Champaran** (c) Ahmedabad (d) Dandi

**15.** The Brihadeeswarar (Big) Temple at Thanjavur was built by:
(a) Rajendra Chola (b) **Rajaraja Chola I** (c) Krishnadevaraya (d) Pallava Narasimhavarman
> **Ans: (b)**
