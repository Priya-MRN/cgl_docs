**The largest single block (50 Q).** Covers the nature of the Indian economy, planning (Five-Year Plans → NITI Aayog), public finance (RBI, fiscal/monetary policy, Finance Commission, GST), agriculture & industry, social problems, and — heavily — **Tamil Nadu's development administration, HDI, welfare schemes and e-Governance**.

---

## A. Nature of the Indian Economy & Planning

- India = a **mixed economy** (public + private). A **developing** economy; services-led GDP.
- **Five-Year Plans (1951–2017):** based on the Soviet model; the **Planning Commission (1950)** designed them. 1st Plan (Harrod-Domar, agriculture), 2nd Plan (Mahalanobis, heavy industry). The **12th Plan** (2012–17) was the last.
- **NITI Aayog (2015)** replaced the Planning Commission — a **policy think-tank** promoting cooperative & competitive federalism (PM = Chairman; CEO; Governing Council of CMs).

---

## B. Public Finance & Banking

**Reserve Bank of India (RBI, est. 1935)** — the central bank.
- Functions: issues currency, banker to government, controls credit, **monetary policy**.
- **Monetary policy tools:** Repo rate, Reverse repo, CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio), SLR, OMO. Set by the **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)**, inflation target **4% (±2%)**.

**Fiscal policy** = government taxation & spending (Finance Ministry; Union Budget).
- **Direct taxes:** income tax, corporate tax. **Indirect taxes:** GST, customs.

**Goods and Services Tax (GST)** — "**One Nation One Tax**", from **1 July 2017** (101st Constitutional Amendment).
- Slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%. **GST Council** (Union FM + state FMs) decides rates.
- Components: CGST, SGST (intra-state), IGST (inter-state).

**Finance Commission (Art. 280)** — recommends **resource/tax sharing between the Union and States** every 5 years (vertical & horizontal devolution).

---

## C. Sectors, Land Reforms & Employment

- **Land reforms:** abolition of zamindari, tenancy reforms, land ceiling, consolidation.
- **Green Revolution** (food-grain self-sufficiency); science & technology in agriculture.
- **Industrial growth:** PSUs, MSMEs, "Make in India".
- **Employment & welfare schemes:** MGNREGA (100 days' wage work), PM Awas, PM-KISAN, Jan Dhan, Skill India.
- **Social problems:** population, poverty, unemployment, education & health gaps.
- **Human Development Indicators (HDI):** life expectancy, education, per-capita income (UNDP). **Tamil Nadu ranks among the top Indian states in HDI.**

---

## D. Development Administration in Tamil Nadu (high-yield)

**TN's social-reform foundation**
- Impact of the **Self-Respect & Dravidian Movements** on socio-economic development — social justice, anti-caste reform, reservation, women's empowerment.
- **Reservation policy:** TN has **69% reservation** (protected under the 9th Schedule), among the highest, to ensure access to social resources for backward classes, SC/ST.

**Landmark TN welfare schemes (historic & continuing — confirm current names/figures with latest CA):**
- **Mid-Day Meal Scheme** — pioneered by **K. Kamaraj** (free noon meal); expanded by **M.G. Ramachandran (MGR)** as the **Nutritious Noon Meal Programme** — a global model that boosted enrolment & nutrition.
- **Free education**, free bus passes/bicycles, free laptops to students.
- **Public Distribution System (universal PDS)** — TN runs a near-universal, subsidised PDS.
- Maternity/old-age/widow pensions; **Amma** branded schemes (canteens, salt, mineral water, pharmacies); cradle-baby scheme; girl-child protection schemes.
- **Magalir Urimai Thogai** / women's entitlement & breakfast scheme (recent) — verify current details with latest current affairs.

**TN achievements & administration**
- High **literacy, low infant & maternal mortality, strong primary-health network** (PHCs), industrial diversification (automobile "Detroit of India" – Chennai, textiles – Tiruppur/Coimbatore, leather – Vellore).
- **e-Governance:** TN e-Sevai centres, online certificates, ELCOT/TNeGA initiatives; citizen-service delivery.
- **Geography & growth:** long coastline (ports — Chennai, Ennore, Tuticorin), Neyveli lignite, Cauvery delta ("rice bowl"), wind & solar energy leadership.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Why was the Nutritious Noon Meal Programme so impactful?**
By providing a **free cooked midday meal**, it tackled child malnutrition *and* school dropout simultaneously, sharply raising enrolment & retention — especially of girls & poor children — making it a landmark in human-development administration.

**Example 2 — How does GST work for an inter-state sale?**
For goods sold from TN to Karnataka, **IGST** is levied (collected by the Centre and apportioned to the destination state). For a sale *within* TN, **CGST + SGST** apply, split between Centre and State.

(a) SBI (b) **RBI** (c) NABARD (d) NITI Aayog
