## A. Scientific Temper & Nature of Science

- **Scientific temper** (Article 51A(h), a Fundamental Duty): a way of thinking based on observation, reason, evidence and questioning — vs. **rote learning** (memorising without understanding).
- **Conceptual learning** = understanding *why* (cause–effect, principles); valued over rote recall.
- Science as a tool to understand **past** (fossils, carbon dating), **present** (observation), **future** (prediction/modelling).

---

## B. Physics — General Laws & Mechanics

**Newton's Laws of Motion**
- **First law (Inertia):** a body stays at rest / uniform motion unless acted on by external force.
- **Second law:** Force = mass × acceleration (**F = ma**); rate of change of momentum.
- **Third law:** every action has an equal & opposite reaction (rocket propulsion, recoil of gun).

**Key quantities & units (SI)**

| Quantity | Formula | SI Unit |
|---|---|---|
| Force | F = ma | newton (N) |
| Work / Energy | W = F·d | joule (J) |
| Power | P = W/t | watt (W) |
| Pressure | P = F/A | pascal (Pa) |
| Momentum | p = mv | kg·m/s |

**Properties of matter & everyday applications**
- **Archimedes' principle:** upthrust = weight of fluid displaced → ships float, hydrometer.
- **Pascal's law:** pressure applied to enclosed fluid transmits equally → hydraulic brakes, lift.
- **Bernoulli's principle:** faster fluid → lower pressure → aeroplane lift, spin of ball.
- **Surface tension:** drops are spherical, capillary rise.

**Light, Sound, Heat**
- **Light:** reflection, refraction (mirage, twinkling of stars), dispersion (rainbow), total internal reflection (optical fibre, diamond sparkle). Speed of light ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s.
- **Sound:** longitudinal wave; needs medium (no sound in vacuum); speed ≈ 343 m/s in air. Audible range 20 Hz–20,000 Hz. **Ultrasound** (>20 kHz) → SONAR, medical imaging.
- **Heat:** transfer by **conduction, convection, radiation**. Latent heat (no temperature change during state change).

**Nuclear & modern physics**
- **Nuclear fission** (splitting; atom bomb, reactors) vs **fusion** (joining; the Sun, hydrogen bomb).
- **LASER** = Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation.
- **Electronics/communication:** semiconductors (Si, Ge), diode, transistor; optical fibre, satellite communication.

## C. Chemistry

- **Elements vs compounds vs mixtures.** Atom = smallest unit of an element.
- **Acids** (pH < 7, turn blue litmus red, sour) vs **Bases** (pH > 7, turn red litmus blue, bitter, slippery). **Salt** = acid + base (neutralisation). **pH 7 = neutral** (pure water).
- **Petroleum products:** petrol, diesel, kerosene, LPG, bitumen (from fractional distillation of crude oil).
- **Fertilizers:** NPK — Nitrogen (urea), Phosphorus, Potassium. **Pesticides:** insecticides, herbicides, fungicides.
- Common gases: CO₂ (greenhouse), CO (toxic, from incomplete combustion), O₃ (ozone, UV shield).

---

## D. Life Science

- **Classification:** Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species (binomial nomenclature by **Carl Linnaeus**).
- **Cell:** basic unit of life; **mitochondrion** = powerhouse; **ribosome** = protein synthesis.
- **Genetics:** **DNA** carries heredity; gene = unit of heredity; **Gregor Mendel** = Father of Genetics.
- **Evolution:** **Charles Darwin** — natural selection ("survival of the fittest").
- **Nutrition:** carbohydrates (energy), proteins (growth), fats, vitamins, minerals. Vitamin deficiency: A (night blindness), C (scurvy), D (rickets), B1 (beri-beri).
- **Human diseases:**
  - **Bacterial:** TB, cholera, typhoid, tetanus.
  - **Viral:** dengue, COVID-19, rabies, polio, hepatitis, AIDS.
  - **Protozoan:** malaria (Plasmodium, via female Anopheles mosquito).
  - **Deficiency:** goitre (iodine), anaemia (iron).

## E. Environment & Ecology

- **Ecosystem** = biotic + abiotic components. **Food chain / food web.**
- **Greenhouse gases:** CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, water vapour → **global warming**.
- **Ozone depletion:** by CFCs; **Montreal Protocol** (1987).
- **Biodiversity hotspots in India:** Western Ghats, Eastern Himalaya, etc.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Why does a rocket move forward?**
By **Newton's Third Law**: hot gases are expelled backward (action); the rocket is pushed forward (reaction). Conservation of momentum applies.

**Example 2 — Why is the sky blue?**
**Scattering of light** (Rayleigh scattering): shorter wavelengths (blue) scatter more than longer (red), so the daytime sky appears blue.

**Example 3 — Why does ice float on water?**
Water is densest at **4 °C**; on freezing it *expands* (anomalous expansion), so ice is **less dense** than water and floats.

## Previous-Year-Style MCQs

**1.** The SI unit of force is:
(a) joule (b) watt (c) **newton** (d) pascal
> **Ans: (c)** — Force = mass × acceleration; unit newton (kg·m/s²).

**2.** Which principle explains why ships float?
(a) Pascal's law (b) **Archimedes' principle** (c) Bernoulli's principle (d) Newton's first law
> **Ans: (b)** — Upthrust equals the weight of water displaced.

**3.** Malaria is caused by:
(a) bacteria (b) virus (c) **protozoa (Plasmodium)** (d) fungus
> **Ans: (c)** — Plasmodium, transmitted by the female Anopheles mosquito.

**4.** The pH value of a neutral solution is:
(a) 0 (b) **7** (c) 14 (d) 1
> **Ans: (b)** — pH 7 is neutral (pure water); <7 acidic, >7 basic.

**5.** "Father of Genetics" is:
(a) Charles Darwin (b) **Gregor Mendel** (c) Linnaeus (d) Watson
> **Ans: (b)** — Mendel's pea-plant experiments established laws of inheritance.

**6.** Deficiency of Vitamin C causes:
(a) night blindness (b) rickets (c) **scurvy** (d) beri-beri
> **Ans: (c)** — Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) deficiency causes scurvy.

**7.** LASER stands for Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of:
(a) Reflection (b) Refraction (c) **Radiation** (d) Resonance
> **Ans: (c)**

**8.** Energy of the Sun is produced by:
(a) nuclear fission (b) **nuclear fusion** (c) combustion (d) chemical reaction
> **Ans: (b)** — Fusion of hydrogen nuclei into helium.

**9.** Which gas is mainly responsible for global warming?
(a) Oxygen (b) Nitrogen (c) **Carbon dioxide** (d) Hydrogen
> **Ans: (c)**

**10.** Sound cannot travel through:
(a) solids (b) liquids (c) gases (d) **vacuum**
> **Ans: (d)** — Sound is a mechanical wave and needs a medium.

**11.** Scientific temper is enshrined in the Constitution as a:
(a) Fundamental Right (b) **Fundamental Duty** (c) Directive Principle (d) Preamble value
> **Ans: (b)** — Article 51A(h).

**12.** The instrument used to measure atmospheric pressure is:
(a) thermometer (b) **barometer** (c) hydrometer (d) ammeter
> **Ans: (b)**
