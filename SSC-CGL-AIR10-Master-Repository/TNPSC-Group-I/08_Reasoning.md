# Unit VIII — Reasoning (SSLC Standard, 10 Questions)

> Quick links: [TNPSC Group I Index](README.md) · [Aptitude](07_Aptitude.md) · [repo Reasoning](../Reasoning/) · [repo Cheat-Sheets](../Cheat-Sheets/)

Part B, SSLC level. Covers Logical reasoning, Puzzles, Dice, Visual reasoning, Alpha-numeric reasoning and Number series. **Method + worked example + PYQ-style MCQ** for each.

---

## A. Number Series

Find the pattern (add/subtract/multiply, squares, primes, alternating).
- **Example:** 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ? → differences 4, 6, 8, 10 → next diff 12 → **42** (pattern n²+n).
- **Example:** 3, 6, 12, 24, ? → ×2 each → **48**.

---

## B. Alpha-Numeric / Letter Series

Map letters to positions (A=1 … Z=26). Look for skips.
- **Example:** A, C, E, G, ? → skip one letter → **I**.
- **Coding:** if CAT = 24 (3+1+20), then DOG = 4+15+7 = **26**.

---

## C. Logical Reasoning (Analogy, Odd-One-Out, Syllogism)

- **Analogy:** Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ? → **School**.
- **Odd one out:** Rose, Lotus, Lily, **Mango** → Mango is a fruit, others are flowers.
- **Syllogism:** "All cats are animals; all animals are living" → "All cats are living" (valid).

---

## D. Puzzles & Seating / Ordering

Translate clues into a line/table.
- **Example:** A is taller than B; C is shorter than B. Tallest? → **A** (A > B > C).

---

## E. Dice

- Opposite faces of a standard die sum to **7** (1–6, 2–5, 3–4).
- **Example:** If 2 is on top, the bottom face is **5**.
- Adjacent faces never appear opposite; use two views to deduce hidden faces.

---

## F. Visual / Non-Verbal Reasoning

- **Mirror image:** left–right reversed. **Water image:** top–bottom reversed.
- Figure series: track rotation (clockwise/anticlockwise), addition/removal of elements, shading.
- **Example:** A clock at 3:00 in a mirror reads **9:00**.

---

## Worked Example (mixed)

**If in a code FRIEND is written as GSJFOE, how is MOTHER written?**
Each letter is shifted **+1**: F→G, R→S, … So MOTHER → **NPUIFS**.

## Previous-Year-Style MCQs

**1.** Find the next term: 5, 10, 20, 40, ?
(a) 60 (b) **80** (c) 50 (d) 100
> **Ans: (b)** — ×2 each.

**2.** Find the missing term: 1, 4, 9, 16, ?
(a) 20 (b) **25** (c) 24 (d) 36
> **Ans: (b)** — squares (5² = 25).

**3.** Complete the series: B, D, F, H, ?
(a) I (b) **J** (c) K (d) G
> **Ans: (b)** — skip one letter each.

**4.** Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
(a) Book (b) **School** (c) Student (d) Class
> **Ans: (b)**

**5.** Find the odd one out: Apple, Banana, Carrot, Mango
(a) Apple (b) Banana (c) **Carrot** (d) Mango
> **Ans: (c)** — Carrot is a vegetable; others are fruits.

**6.** On a standard die, if 3 is on top, the number at the bottom is:
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) **4** (d) 6
> **Ans: (c)** — opposite faces sum to 7.

**7.** The mirror image of the time 4:00 shows:
(a) 6:00 (b) **8:00** (c) 10:00 (d) 2:00
> **Ans: (b)** — mirror time = 12:00 − 4:00 = 8:00.

**8.** If FRIEND = GSJFOE (each letter +1), then CAT = ?
(a) DBU (b) **DBU** (c) BCS (d) ABS
> **Ans: (a/b)** — C→D, A→B, T→U = **DBU**.

**9.** Find the next number: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, ?
(a) 12 (b) **13** (c) 14 (d) 15
> **Ans: (b)** — prime numbers.

**10.** Pointing to a man, a woman said, "He is the son of my mother's only son." How is the man related to the woman?
(a) Brother (b) Father (c) **Nephew** (d) Uncle
> **Ans: (c)** — "My mother's only son" = the woman's brother; his son is therefore her **nephew**. (Relation puzzles hinge on tracing each step — here the speaker is female, so the brother's son is her nephew.)

**11.** Which is the water image of the digit 3?
(a) E (b) **Ɛ (vertical flip)** (c) 8 (d) m
> **Ans: (b)** — water image flips top–bottom.

**12.** All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Conclusion: "All roses fade quickly" is:
(a) True (b) **Not necessarily true** (c) Always false (d) Cannot be a rose
> **Ans: (b)** — "some" does not allow a universal conclusion about roses.
