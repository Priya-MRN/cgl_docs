## A. Location & Physical Features

- India lies between **8°4′N – 37°6′N** latitude and **68°7′E – 97°25′E** longitude.
- **Tropic of Cancer (23½°N)** passes through **8 states** (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram).
- **Standard Meridian: 82°30′E** (passes through Mirzapur, UP) → IST = UTC + 5:30.
- Area-wise **7th largest**; population-wise **largest** in the world.

**Physical divisions**
1. **The Himalayas** (Greater/Himadri – Lesser/Himachal – Outer/Shiwaliks). Highest Indian peak: **Kangchenjunga (Sikkim)**.
2. **Northern (Indo-Gangetic) Plains** — alluvial, fertile.
3. **Peninsular Plateau** — Deccan Plateau, oldest landmass; Western & Eastern Ghats.
4. **Indian Desert (Thar)** — Rajasthan.
5. **Coastal Plains** — Western (Konkan, Malabar) & Eastern (Coromandel).
6. **Islands** — Andaman & Nicobar (Bay of Bengal), Lakshadweep (Arabian Sea).

> **Western Ghats** (Sahyadris) are continuous & higher; **Eastern Ghats** are broken by rivers. Highest peak of Peninsular India: **Anamudi (Kerala)**.

## B. Monsoon, Rainfall & Climate

- India has a **tropical monsoon climate**.
- **Southwest (summer) monsoon** (June–Sept): brings ~75% of annual rainfall; two branches — Arabian Sea & Bay of Bengal.
- **Northeast (retreating/winter) monsoon** (Oct–Dec): rains the **Coromandel coast (Tamil Nadu)**.
- **Mawsynram/Cherrapunji (Meghalaya):** wettest place on Earth.

---

## C. Rivers & Water Resources

- **Himalayan rivers** (perennial): Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Indus.
- **Peninsular rivers** (rain-fed, seasonal): Godavari (longest peninsular, "Dakshin Ganga"), Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi, Narmada & Tapi (west-flowing, form estuaries).
- See [Rivers & Borders notes](../Static-GK/07_Rivers_Borders_Superlatives.md) for the full river table.

## D. Soils, Minerals & Natural Resources

| Soil | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Alluvial | Northern plains, deltas | Most fertile, most widespread |
| Black (Regur) | Deccan trap (Maharashtra, MP) | Best for **cotton** |
| Red | Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, eastern plateau | Iron oxide; less fertile |
| Laterite | Western Ghats, NE | Heavy rain leaching; tea, coffee, cashew |
| Desert | Rajasthan | Sandy, saline |

- **Minerals:** Coal (Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh — Damodar valley), Iron ore (Odisha, Jharkhand, Karnataka), Bauxite, Mica (Jharkhand), Manganese.
- **Tamil Nadu:** lignite (**Neyveli**), bauxite, magnesite, limestone.

## E. Forests, Wildlife & Agriculture

- **Forest cover** ~21–24% of land (target: 33% per National Forest Policy).
- **Project Tiger (1973)**, **Project Elephant (1992)**. Biosphere reserves: Nilgiri (first), Gulf of Mannar (TN).
- **Agriculture:** Kharif (monsoon — rice, cotton, jute), Rabi (winter — wheat, gram), Zaid (summer).
- **Green Revolution** → wheat & rice (Punjab, Haryana); **White Revolution** → milk (Operation Flood, Verghese Kurien).

---

## F. Population, Transport & Communication

- **Census 2011:** population ~121 crore; **density** ~382/km²; **literacy** ~74%; sex ratio 943.
- Most populous state: UP; densest: Bihar; least dense: Arunachal Pradesh.
- **Transport:** Indian Railways (largest in Asia); **NH network**; major ports — Chennai, Tuticorin (TN), Mumbai/JNPT, Kolkata.

---

## G. Disasters, Pollution, Climate Change & Green Energy

- **NDMA** (National Disaster Management Authority, 2005) — PM is chairman.
- **Pollution:** air (vehicles, industry), water (sewage, effluents), soil.
- **Climate change:** rising temperature, sea-level rise; India's commitments under **Paris Agreement (2015)**; **Net-zero by 2070**.
- **Green/renewable energy:** solar (National Solar Mission), wind (TN, Gujarat lead), hydro, biomass; **International Solar Alliance (ISA)** HQ at Gurugram, India.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Why does Tamil Nadu get most of its rain in Oct–Dec?**
Because TN's Coromandel coast lies in the **rain-shadow of the SW monsoon** but is directly exposed to the moisture-laden **Northeast (retreating) monsoon** that picks up water over the Bay of Bengal.

**Example 2 — Why is black soil best for cotton?**
Black (regur) soil has high **clay & moisture-retention** capacity and is rich in lime, iron, magnesium — ideal for cotton in the Deccan trap region.

**1.** The Standard Meridian of India passes through:
(a) Delhi (b) **Mirzapur (82°30′E)** (c) Kolkata (d) Bhopal
> **Ans: (b)**

**2.** The Tropic of Cancer passes through how many Indian states?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) **8** (d) 9

**3.** Which monsoon brings rainfall to Tamil Nadu's Coromandel coast?
(a) Southwest monsoon (b) **Northeast (retreating) monsoon** (c) Western disturbances (d) Loo winds

(a) black soil (b) red soil (c) **alluvial soil** (d) laterite soil
> **Ans: (c)**

**5.** Lignite is mined in Tamil Nadu at:
(a) Salem (b) **Neyveli** (c) Madurai (d) Tuticorin

(a) Kangchenjunga (b) **Anamudi** (c) Doddabetta (d) Nilgiri
