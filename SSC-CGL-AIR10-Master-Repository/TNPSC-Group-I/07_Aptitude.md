# Unit VII — Aptitude (SSLC Standard, 15 Questions)

> Quick links: [TNPSC Group I Index](README.md) · [Reasoning](08_Reasoning.md) · [repo Quantitative-Aptitude](../Quantitative-Aptitude/) · [repo Formula-Sheets](../Formula-Sheets/)

Part B, SSLC (10th-standard) level. Covers Simplification, Percentage, HCF & LCM, Ratio & Proportion, Simple & Compound Interest, Area, Volume, and Time & Work. **Brief method + worked example + PYQ-style MCQ** for each.

---

## A. Simplification (BODMAS)

**Rule order:** **B**rackets → **O**rders (powers/roots) → **D**ivision → **M**ultiplication → **A**ddition → **S**ubtraction.

**Example:** `12 + 6 ÷ 2 × 3 − 4`
= 12 + 3×3 − 4 = 12 + 9 − 4 = **17**.

---

## B. Percentage

- x% of N = (x/100)×N. Increase/decrease % = (change ÷ original)×100.
- **Example:** 15% of 240 = (15/100)×240 = **36**.
- **Successive change trap:** +20% then −20% ≠ 0; net = −4% (use multipliers 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96).

---

## C. HCF & LCM

- **HCF** (Highest Common Factor) = largest number dividing all; **LCM** = smallest number divisible by all.
- **Key identity:** **HCF × LCM = product of the two numbers.**
- **Example:** HCF & LCM of 12 and 18. 12 = 2²×3, 18 = 2×3². HCF = 2×3 = **6**; LCM = 2²×3² = **36**. Check: 6×36 = 216 = 12×18. ✓

---

## D. Ratio & Proportion

- Ratio a:b; in proportion a:b = c:d → **a×d = b×c** (product of means = product of extremes).
- **Example:** Divide ₹600 in ratio 2:3. Parts = 5 → one part = 120 → shares **₹240 and ₹360**.

---

## E. Simple Interest (SI)

- **SI = (P × R × T) / 100**; Amount = P + SI.
- **Example:** P = ₹5000, R = 8%, T = 3 yr → SI = (5000×8×3)/100 = **₹1200**; Amount = ₹6200.

---

## F. Compound Interest (CI)

- **A = P(1 + R/100)ᵀ**; CI = A − P.
- **Example:** P = ₹1000, R = 10%, T = 2 yr → A = 1000×(1.1)² = 1000×1.21 = ₹1210 → **CI = ₹210** (vs SI ₹200; the extra ₹10 is interest-on-interest).

---

## G. Area

| Shape | Area |
|---|---|
| Square | side² |
| Rectangle | length × breadth |
| Triangle | ½ × base × height |
| Circle | πr² |
| Parallelogram | base × height |

**Example:** Circle of radius 7 cm → Area = (22/7)×7×7 = **154 cm²**.

---

## H. Volume

| Solid | Volume |
|---|---|
| Cube | side³ |
| Cuboid | l × b × h |
| Cylinder | πr²h |
| Sphere | (4/3)πr³ |
| Cone | (1/3)πr²h |

**Example:** Cube of side 5 cm → Volume = 5³ = **125 cm³**.

---

## I. Time & Work

- If A does a job in *n* days, A's 1-day work = **1/n**. Combine rates by adding.
- **Example:** A does a job in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6 → **6 days**.

## Previous-Year-Style MCQs

**1.** Simplify: 8 + 2 × 5 − 4 ÷ 2
(a) 14 (b) **16** (c) 18 (d) 20
> **Ans: (b)** — 8 + 10 − 2 = 16 (BODMAS).

**2.** 25% of 480 = ?
(a) 100 (b) **120** (c) 96 (d) 130
> **Ans: (b)** — (25/100)×480 = 120.

**3.** The HCF of 24 and 36 is:
(a) 6 (b) **12** (c) 4 (d) 72
> **Ans: (b)** — 24 = 2³×3, 36 = 2²×3² → HCF = 2²×3 = 12.

**4.** The LCM of 4, 6 and 8 is:
(a) 12 (b) **24** (c) 48 (d) 16
> **Ans: (b)**

**5.** Divide ₹350 in the ratio 3:4. The larger share is:
(a) 150 (b) **200** (c) 175 (d) 210
> **Ans: (b)** — 7 parts; one part = 50 → 150 & 200.

**6.** SI on ₹2000 at 5% for 4 years:
(a) ₹200 (b) **₹400** (c) ₹500 (d) ₹100
> **Ans: (b)** — (2000×5×4)/100 = 400.

**7.** CI on ₹5000 at 10% for 2 years:
(a) ₹1000 (b) **₹1050** (c) ₹500 (d) ₹1100
> **Ans: (b)** — A = 5000×1.21 = 6050 → CI = 1050.

**8.** Area of a rectangle 12 m × 8 m:
(a) 20 m² (b) 40 m² (c) **96 m²** (d) 24 m²
> **Ans: (c)**

**9.** Volume of a cylinder, r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm (π = 22/7):
(a) 1540 cm³ (b) **1540 cm³** (c) 770 cm³ (d) 220 cm³
> **Ans: (a/b)** — πr²h = (22/7)×49×10 = **1540 cm³**.

**10.** A finishes work in 12 days, B in 12 days. Together they finish in:
(a) 12 (b) **6** (c) 24 (d) 8
> **Ans: (b)** — 1/12 + 1/12 = 1/6 → 6 days.

**11.** If +10% then −10% is applied to a price, the net change is:
(a) 0% (b) +1% (c) **−1%** (d) −10%
> **Ans: (c)** — 1.1 × 0.9 = 0.99 → −1%.

**12.** Area of a triangle, base 10 cm, height 6 cm:
(a) 60 (b) **30** (c) 16 (d) 32
> **Ans: (b)** — ½×10×6 = 30 cm².
