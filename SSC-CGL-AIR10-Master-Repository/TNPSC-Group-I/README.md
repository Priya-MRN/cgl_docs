# TNPSC Combined Civil Services Examination – I (Group I) — Prelims Study Material

> Structured study material for the **TNPSC CCSE-I (Group I) Preliminary Examination**, built unit-wise from the official syllabus, in a **Notes + Examples + Previous-Year-style MCQ** format.

**Paper pattern (Prelims):** Single objective paper · **200 questions · 300 marks** · 3 hours.
- **Part A — General Studies (175 Questions)** — Units I to VI below.
- **Part B — Aptitude & Mental Ability (25 Questions, SSLC standard)** — Units VII & VIII below.

> ⚠️ Negative marking and exact mark split follow the latest TNPSC notification — always confirm against the official notification for your exam year.

---

## How to Use

1. **Read the Notes + worked Examples** in each unit first for concept clarity.
2. **Solve the PYQ-style MCQs** at the end of each file *before* reading explanations.
3. **Mark every wrong question** and re-solve weekly.
4. **TN-specific units (V & VI) and Polity (IV) carry the most weight** — master these first.
5. Pair with the repo's [Current-Affairs/](../Current-Affairs/) and [studymaterialstnpsc/](../studymaterialstnpsc/) PDFs for depth and the latest current affairs.

## Highest-ROI Focus Order

1. **Unit V — Indian Economy & Development Administration in TN (50Q)** — largest single block; TN schemes, HDI, GST, RBI, planning.
2. **Unit IV — Indian Polity (40Q)** — Constitution, FRs/DPSP, federalism, RTI, Lokpal.
3. **Unit VI — TN History & Dravidian Movement (40Q)** — Sangam, Thirukkural, Justice Party, Periyar, Anna.
4. **Unit III — History & National Movement (25Q)**.
5. **Units I, II (10Q each)** + **Part B Aptitude/Reasoning (25Q)** — scoring, do-not-skip.
