# Unit VI — History, Culture, Heritage & Socio-Political Movements in Tamil Nadu (40 Questions)

> Quick links: [TNPSC Group I Index](README.md) · [Economy & TN Development](05_Indian_Economy_TN_Development.md) · [History & National Movement](03_History_Culture_National_Movement.md)

The most **TN-unique** unit. Covers Tamil society & archaeology, Sangam to modern Tamil literature, **Thirukkural**, TN's role in the freedom struggle, and the **Justice Party / Self-Respect / Dravidian movements** (Periyar & Anna).

---

## A. Tamil Society & Archaeology

- Ancient Tamil society = the **Tamilakam**, ruled by three crowned kings: **Chera, Chola, Pandya** (the "Moovendar").
- **Archaeological discoveries:**
  - **Keeladi (Sivaganga district)** — excavations indicating an urban, literate Sangam-age (Vaigai valley) civilisation; pushed back the antiquity of Tamil writing/urbanism.
  - **Adichanallur** (urn burials), **Arikamedu** (Roman trade port near Pondicherry — Indo-Roman trade).
- Megalithic burials, Tamil-Brahmi inscriptions evidence early literacy.

---

## B. Tamil Literature — Sangam to Contemporary

- **Sangam Age (c. 300 BCE – 300 CE):** three Sangams (literary academies) at Madurai.
  - **Ettuthogai** (Eight Anthologies) & **Pathupattu** (Ten Idylls) — the major Sangam corpus.
  - **Tholkappiyam** — oldest extant Tamil grammar (by Tholkappiyar).
  - Concepts of **Aham** (love/inner) and **Puram** (war/public/outer) poetry; five landscapes (**Thinai**).
- **Post-Sangam / Epics:** **Silappathikaram** (Ilango Adigal — Kannagi) and **Manimekalai** (Sathanar) — the twin Tamil epics.
- **Bhakti age:** **Nayanmars** (Shaivite) & **Alvars** (Vaishnavite); Thevaram, Thiruvasagam.
- **Modern:** **Subramania Bharathi (Bharathiar)** — national poet, freedom-struggle songs; Bharathidasan, and contemporary Tamil writers.

---

## C. Thirukkural (high-yield)

- **Author: Thiruvalluvar.** A secular ethical classic of **1330 couplets (kurals)** in **133 chapters (athikarams)** of 10 couplets each.
- **Three sections (paals):**
  1. **Aram (Virtue/Dharma)**
  2. **Porul (Wealth/Polity/Artha)**
  3. **Inbam / Kamam (Love)**
- **Significance:** secular (no religion-specific bias), universal values, relevance to **everyday life** and **socio-politico-economic affairs**; philosophical depth; translated into many languages. Often called the "Tamil Veda" / a universal scripture.

---

## D. Tamil Nadu in the Freedom Struggle

- **Early agitations & leaders:**
  - **V.O. Chidambaranar (V.O.C.)** — "Kappalottiya Tamizhan" (the Tamil who launched a ship): founded the **Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company** to challenge the British shipping monopoly.
  - **Subramania Bharathi (Bharathiar)** — patriotic poet; women's freedom & social reform.
  - **Subramania Siva, Vanchinathan**, and the **Tirunelveli uprising**.
- **Role of women:** participation in Salt Satyagraha & Civil Disobedience in TN.

---

## E. Socio-Political Movements (19th–20th century)

**Justice Party (South Indian Liberal Federation, 1916)**
- Founded by **Dr. C. Natesan, T.M. Nair, P. Theagaraya Chetty** — championed **non-Brahmin** representation; introduced **communal G.O.** (reservation in jobs/education).

**Self-Respect Movement (1925) & Rationalism — Periyar**
- **Thanthai Periyar (E.V. Ramasamy)** founded the **Self-Respect Movement** — against caste oppression, untouchability, superstition; for **rationalism, women's equality, widow remarriage, self-respect marriages** (no priest).
- Later led the **Dravidar Kazhagam (DK, 1944)**.

**Dravidian Movement**
- Emphasised Tamil/Dravidian identity, social justice, anti-Brahminism, and rationalism.
- **Perarignar Anna (C.N. Annadurai)** — founded the **Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK, 1949)**; became the **first non-Congress Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu (1967)**; renamed Madras State to **Tamil Nadu (1969)**. Noted orator and writer.

**Principles underlying both movements:** social equality, eradication of caste & superstition, rationalism, self-respect, women's empowerment, and Tamil cultural pride.

**Contributions — Periyar & Anna**
- **Periyar:** ideological father of social reform & rationalism in TN; anti-caste & anti-superstition campaigns; women's rights.
- **Anna:** translated the movement into electoral politics & governance; champion of Tamil language & federalism; welfare administration.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1 — Why is Thirukkural described as "secular literature"?**
Because it teaches **universal ethics** (virtue, governance, love) without binding itself to any one religion or deity — its values apply to all humanity, which is why it is studied as a guide to everyday and socio-political life.

**Example 2 — Why is V.O. Chidambaranar called "Kappalottiya Tamizhan"?**
He launched the **Swadeshi Steam Navigation Company** to run Indian-owned ships, directly challenging the British shipping monopoly — an economic blow to colonial dominance, earning him the title "the Tamil who launched a ship".
