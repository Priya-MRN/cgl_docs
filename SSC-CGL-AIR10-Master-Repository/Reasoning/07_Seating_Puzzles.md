# Seating & Puzzles — Solved Question Bank

> Quick links: [Reasoning Index](README.md) · [Reasoning Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/Reasoning_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Concepts
- **Linear seating**: people in a row; fix "facing" direction to read left/right correctly.
- **Circular seating**: facing centre → a person's right is your anticlockwise; facing outward reverses it.
- Start with the most definite clue (ends, fixed positions) and pin it first.
- For ordering puzzles (taller/older/ranks), draw a single line from least to greatest.
- Convert each clue to a small note, then merge — never juggle all clues mentally.

## EASY

**Q1.** Five friends A, B, C, D, E sit in a row facing North. A is at the extreme left and E at the extreme right. C is exactly in the middle. Who can sit between A and C?
(a) Only B  (b) Only D  (c) B and D  (d) E
**Answer: (c) B and D**
*Solution:* Order is A _ C _ E. The two middle gaps (positions 2 and 4) are filled by B and D, so the person between A and C is one of B or D.

**Q2.** In a row of 5 people facing North, P is to the immediate right of Q. Who is to the left?
(a) P  (b) Q  (c) Both  (d) Cannot say
**Answer: (b) Q**
*Solution:* For people facing North, the observer's right is the seated person's... here "P is to the immediate right of Q" means P sits on Q's right side, so Q is to the left of P.

**Q3.** Six people sit around a circular table facing the centre. If R is to the immediate left of S, then S is to R's ____?
(a) Left  (b) Right  (c) Opposite  (d) Cannot say
**Answer: (b) Right**
*Solution:* "Left/right" are mutual: if R is to the immediate left of S, then S is to the immediate right of R.

**Q4.** A is taller than B, and B is taller than C. Who is the shortest?
(a) A  (b) B  (c) C  (d) Cannot say
**Answer: (c) C**
*Solution:* Order A > B > C, so C is the shortest.

**Q5.** Four people W, X, Y, Z stand in a queue. W is just ahead of X, and Y is just ahead of W. Z is last. Who is first?
(a) W  (b) X  (c) Y  (d) Z
**Answer: (c) Y**
*Solution:* Y → W → X, with Z last gives Y, W, X, Z. Y is first.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** Seven boys sit in a row facing North. M is 3rd from the left and N is 2nd from the right. There are 7 boys. How many sit between M and N?
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 4
**Answer: (b) 2**
*Solution:* M is at position 3; N is 2nd from right in a row of 7 = position 6. Between positions 3 and 6 are positions 4 and 5 = 2 boys.

**Q2.** Five people A, B, C, D, E sit around a circle facing the centre. A is between B and C. D is to the immediate right of B. Where is E?
(a) Right of C  (b) Left of C  (c) Right of A  (d) Cannot say
**Answer: (a) Right of C**
*Solution:* Going clockwise (each person's left when facing centre is the next clockwise seat): place D, B, A, C with A between B and C. The remaining seat for E is next to C, on C's right.

**Q3.** In a class, Ravi ranks 7th from the top and 26th from the bottom. How many students are in the class?
(a) 31  (b) 32  (c) 33  (d) 34
**Answer: (b) 32**
*Solution:* Total = (rank from top) + (rank from bottom) − 1 = 7 + 26 − 1 = 32.

**Q4.** Six people sit in a row facing North: the order from left is P, Q, R, S, T, U. Who is exactly in the middle two seats?
(a) Q and R  (b) R and S  (c) S and T  (d) Q and T
**Answer: (b) R and S**
*Solution:* With 6 seats, the middle two are positions 3 and 4 = R and S.

**Q5.** Around a circular table, six friends sit facing the centre. If A is opposite D, and B is opposite E, then who is opposite C?
(a) A  (b) B  (c) F  (d) D
**Answer: (c) F**
*Solution:* Three opposite pairs exist among A,B,C,D,E,F. With A–D and B–E paired, the only remaining pair is C–F, so C is opposite F.

**Q6.** Five students are ranked by marks: P > Q, R > P, S < Q, T is highest. Who is second highest?
(a) P  (b) Q  (c) R  (d) S
**Answer: (c) R**
*Solution:* T is highest. Among the rest, R > P > Q > S. So the order is T > R > P > Q > S; the second highest is R.

## HARD

**Q1.** Eight people sit around a circular table facing the centre. A is 3rd to the left of B, and C is 2nd to the right of B. How many people sit between A and C going the shorter way (through B)?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 1
**Answer: (a) 2**
*Solution:* Place B. A is 3rd to B's left; C is 2nd to B's right. Going from A through B to C, B lies between them along with one more seat on A's side, giving 2 people between A and C on that arc.

**Q2.** In a row of 40 students facing North, Anu is 11th from the left and Banu is 31st from the left. How many students are between them?
(a) 18  (b) 19  (c) 20  (d) 21
**Answer: (b) 19**
*Solution:* Students strictly between positions 11 and 31 are positions 12 to 30 = 30 − 12 + 1 = 19.

**Q3.** Five houses in a row are painted Red, Blue, Green, White, Yellow (left to right unknown). Red is at one end. Green is exactly in the middle. Blue is immediately right of Red. The White house is at the other end. Which house is 4th from the left?
(a) Blue  (b) Yellow  (c) White  (d) Red
**Answer: (b) Yellow**
*Solution:* Red at left end (pos 1), Blue next (pos 2), Green middle (pos 3), White at right end (pos 5). The only colour left, Yellow, takes position 4.

**Q4.** Six friends sit around a hexagonal table, one on each side, all facing the centre. P is opposite S. Q is between P and R. T is to the immediate left of P. Who sits opposite Q?
(a) T  (b) R  (c) U  (d) S
**Answer: (a) T**
*Solution:* Fix P; T is to P's immediate left and Q is to P's immediate right (Q between P and R). Opposite pairs are P–S, and the remaining seats pair Q with T's opposite. Working the hexagon, Q's opposite seat is occupied by T.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN (SSC CGL pattern)

**Q1.** In a class, Sita's rank is 12th from the top and 18th from the bottom. How many students are there?
(a) 28  (b) 29  (c) 30  (d) 31
**Answer: (b) 29**
*Solution:* Total = 12 + 18 − 1 = 29 students.

**Q2.** Five persons A, B, C, D, E are sitting in a row facing North. C is to the immediate left of D and to the immediate right of B. A is at the right end. Who is at the left end?
(a) B  (b) C  (c) E  (d) D
**Answer: (c) E**
*Solution:* B C D are consecutive (B-C-D). A is at the right end. The remaining person E takes the left end: E B C D A.

**Q3.** Six people sit around a round table facing the centre. If the seat directly opposite to seat 1 is seat 4, which seat is opposite seat 3?
(a) 5  (b) 6  (c) 2  (d) 1
**Answer: (b) 6**
*Solution:* In a 6-seat circle, opposite seats differ by 3: 1↔4, 2↔5, 3↔6. So seat 3 is opposite seat 6.

**Q4.** In a queue, Raju is 9th from the front and 13th from the back. How many people are in the queue?
(a) 20  (b) 21  (c) 22  (d) 23
**Answer: (b) 21**
*Solution:* Total = 9 + 13 − 1 = 21 people.

### 🔑 Trick Box
- Rank/total formula: Total = (position from top) + (position from bottom) − 1.
- People strictly between positions m and n = |n − m| − 1.
- Facing centre: a person's left is the next clockwise seat; their right is anticlockwise (mirror it if facing outward).
- In an even circle, opposite seat = current seat ± (n/2).

> Pin the most fixed clue first (an end seat or "opposite" pair), then build outward.
