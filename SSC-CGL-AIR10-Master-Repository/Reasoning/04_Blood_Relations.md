# Blood Relations — Solved Question Bank

> Quick links: [Reasoning Index](README.md) · [Reasoning Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/Reasoning_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Concepts
- **Pointing puzzles**: "pointing to a photo, he said…" — start from the speaker and trace outward.
- **Coded relations**: A + B (e.g. A is father of B), A − B, A × B — read the legend each time.
- Draw a quick family tree; use △ for male, ○ for female, = for married couple.
- "Only son / only daughter" of someone's parent often points back to that person.
- Generations: grandparent → parent → self → child → grandchild.

## EASY

**Q1.** Pointing to a man, Reena said, "He is the son of my mother." How is the man related to Reena?
(a) Father  (b) Brother  (c) Uncle  (d) Son
**Answer: (b) Brother**
*Solution:* The son of Reena's mother is Reena's brother.

**Q2.** A is B's father. How is B related to A?
(a) Son or daughter  (b) Father  (c) Uncle  (d) Brother
**Answer: (a) Son or daughter**
*Solution:* If A is the father of B, then B is A's child — son or daughter (gender not given).

**Q3.** My mother's brother is my ____?
(a) Uncle  (b) Father  (c) Cousin  (d) Nephew
**Answer: (a) Uncle**
*Solution:* The brother of one's mother is one's (maternal) uncle.

**Q4.** P is the mother of Q. Q is the sister of R. How is P related to R?
(a) Sister  (b) Mother  (c) Aunt  (d) Daughter
**Answer: (b) Mother**
*Solution:* Q and R are siblings; P is Q's mother, so P is also R's mother.

**Q5.** X is the husband of Y. Z is the daughter of X. How is Y related to Z?
(a) Sister  (b) Mother  (c) Aunt  (d) Daughter
**Answer: (b) Mother**
*Solution:* X and Y are married; Z is X's daughter, so Y (X's wife) is Z's mother.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** Pointing to a photo, a woman said, "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." How is the girl in the photo related to the woman?
(a) Sister  (b) Cousin  (c) Niece  (d) Aunt
**Answer: (a) Sister**
*Solution:* Grandfather's only son = the woman's father. His daughter is the woman's sister.

**Q2.** If A + B means A is the mother of B, and A − B means A is the brother of B, then in A + B − C, how is A related to C?
(a) Mother  (b) Sister  (c) Aunt  (d) Grandmother
**Answer: (a) Mother**
*Solution:* A + B → A is mother of B; B − C → B is brother of C. So B and C are siblings, and A (mother of B) is also the mother of C.

**Q3.** Pointing to a boy, Ravi (male) said, "He is the son of my mother's only son." How is the boy related to Ravi?
(a) Son  (b) Brother  (c) Nephew  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (a) Son**
*Solution:* Ravi's mother's only son is Ravi himself. So the boy is Ravi's son.

**Q4.** If P × Q means P is the father of Q, and P + Q means P is the wife of Q, then in M + N × O, how is M related to O?
(a) Mother  (b) Aunt  (c) Sister  (d) Daughter
**Answer: (a) Mother**
*Solution:* N × O → N is father of O; M + N → M is wife of N. The wife of O's father is O's mother.

**Q5.** A is the sister of B. B is the son of C. C is the mother of D. How is A related to D?
(a) Sister  (b) Mother  (c) Aunt  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (a) Sister**
*Solution:* A and B are children of C, and D is also C's child. So A, B and D are siblings — A is D's sister.

## HARD

**Q1.** Pointing to a portrait, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." Whose portrait is it?
(a) Her son  (b) Her father  (c) Her brother  (d) Her nephew
**Answer: (a) Her son**
*Solution:* The woman is the only daughter of her own mother. So the portrait's mother is the woman herself, making the portrait her son.

**Q2.** A and B are siblings. B is the wife of E and has one son F. How is F related to A?
(a) Son  (b) Nephew  (c) Brother  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (b) Nephew**
*Solution:* B is A's sibling and F is B's son. Your sibling's son is your nephew, so F is A's nephew.

**Q3.** Pointing to a lady, Raj said, "She is the daughter of the only son of my grandmother." How is the lady related to Raj?
(a) Sister  (b) Aunt  (c) Mother  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (a) Sister**
*Solution:* The only son of Raj's grandmother is Raj's father. The father's daughter is Raj's sister.

**Q4.** A is the brother of B. C is the father of A. D is the brother of C. How is D related to B?
(a) Uncle  (b) Father  (c) Brother  (d) Grandfather
**Answer: (a) Uncle**
*Solution:* C is the father of A and B (siblings). D is C's brother, so D is the paternal uncle of B.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN (SSC CGL pattern)

**Q1.** Introducing a man, a woman said, "His wife is the only daughter of my father." How is the man related to the woman?
(a) Brother  (b) Father  (c) Husband  (d) Uncle
**Answer: (c) Husband**
*Solution:* The only daughter of the woman's father is the woman herself. The man's wife is the woman, so the man is her husband.

**Q2.** If A + B means A is the son of B, and A − B means A is the wife of B, then P + Q − R means?
(a) P is the son of R  (b) P is the daughter of R  (c) R is the husband of P  (d) P is the husband of R
**Answer: (a) P is the son of R**
*Solution:* P + Q → P is the son of Q; Q − R → Q is the wife of R, so R is the father of Q's child P. Thus P is the son of R.

**Q3.** Pointing to a girl, Mohan said, "She is the daughter of my mother's only child." How is the girl related to Mohan?
(a) Daughter  (b) Sister  (c) Niece  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (a) Daughter**
*Solution:* Mohan's mother's only child is Mohan himself. The girl is the daughter of Mohan = his daughter.

**Q4.** A is the brother of B. C is the mother of A. D is the daughter of C. How is B related to D?
(a) Brother or sister  (b) Father  (c) Uncle  (d) Cousin
**Answer: (a) Brother or sister**
*Solution:* C is the mother of A, B and D, so all are siblings. B (gender not stated) is therefore the brother or sister of D.

### 🔑 Trick Box
- Always start from the *speaker* and decode "my / his / her" relative to that person.
- "Only daughter of my mother" / "only son of my father" usually points back to the speaker (if gender matches).
- Maternal vs paternal: trace through mother's side or father's side respectively.
- Build a tree with symbols — never hold a 4-link chain in your head.

> In coded relations, re-read the legend each question; symbols change between questions.
