# Non-Verbal & Misc — Solved Question Bank

## Key Concepts
- **Mirror image** (vertical mirror on the side): left and right swap, top and bottom stay; letters like A, H, I, M, O, T, U, V, W, X, Y look the same.
- **Water image** (mirror below): top and bottom swap, left and right stay; letters like B, C, D, E, H, I, O, X read the same.
- **Paper folding/punching**: each fold doubles the holes by symmetry; unfold step by step.
- **Embedded figures**: the target shape hides inside the larger figure without rotation.
- **Counting figures**: count smallest units first, then combinations (pairs, larger composites).
- **Venn diagram**: choose the diagram whose overlaps match real-category relationships.

## EASY

**Q1.** What is the mirror image of the letter "b" (reflected in a vertical mirror placed on its right)?
(a) d  (b) p  (c) q  (d) b
**Answer: (a) d**
*Solution:* A vertical mirror swaps left and right, turning "b" into "d" (the bowl flips to the other side).

**Q2.** Which capital letter looks the same as its mirror image (vertical mirror)?
(a) A  (b) B  (c) F  (d) G
**Answer: (a) A**
*Solution:* "A" is symmetric about a vertical axis, so its left-right mirror image is identical.

**Q3.** The water image of the digit "8" is?
(a) 8  (b) 3  (c) 6  (d) 0
**Answer: (a) 8**
*Solution:* "8" is symmetric top-to-bottom, so flipping it vertically (water image) leaves it unchanged.

**Q4.** How many triangles are there in a single triangle divided by one line from a vertex to the midpoint of the opposite side?
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 4
**Answer: (c) 3**
*Solution:* The two smaller triangles plus the whole original triangle make 3 triangles in total.

**Q5.** In a Venn diagram for "Dogs, Animals, Cats," which arrangement is correct?
(a) Dogs and Cats are separate circles, both inside Animals  (b) All three overlap  (c) Animals inside Dogs  (d) Cats inside Dogs
**Answer: (a) Dogs and Cats are separate circles, both inside Animals**
*Solution:* Dogs and cats are different kinds of animals (no overlap with each other) but both are subsets of animals.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** The mirror image of the word "CODE" (vertical mirror) reads, from the viewer's side, as the letters in reverse order. Which option shows this?
(a) EDOC reversed shapes  (b) CODE  (c) DECO  (d) OCDE
**Answer: (a) EDOC reversed shapes**
*Solution:* A vertical mirror reverses the order of letters left-to-right, so CODE appears as E-D-O-C with each letter also flipped.

**Q2.** A square sheet is folded once along the vertical centre line, and a single hole is punched near the fold. On unfolding, how many holes appear?
(a) 1  (b) 2  (c) 3  (d) 4
**Answer: (b) 2**
*Solution:* One fold creates a mirror copy of the punch, so unfolding shows 2 holes symmetric about the fold line.

**Q3.** A paper folded twice (into four layers) with one punch shows how many holes when unfolded?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 8
**Answer: (c) 4**
*Solution:* Each fold doubles the holes: 1 punch through 4 layers gives 4 holes on unfolding.

**Q4.** How many squares are there in a 2×2 grid (a big square divided into four equal small squares)?
(a) 4  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 8
**Answer: (b) 5**
*Solution:* Four small squares plus the one large enclosing square = 5 squares.

**Q5.** Which capital letter has the same water image as itself?
(a) E  (b) A  (c) N  (d) R
**Answer: (a) E**
*Solution:* "E" is symmetric about a horizontal axis, so its top-bottom (water) reflection is identical.

**Q6.** Statement: All successful people work hard. Conclusion: Hard work alone guarantees success.
(a) Follows  (b) Does not follow  (c) Either-or  (d) Cannot say
**Answer: (b) Does not follow**
*Solution:* The statement says successful people work hard, not that hard work alone causes success. The reverse implication is not given, so it does not follow.

## HARD

**Q1.** How many triangles are in a square with both diagonals drawn?
(a) 4  (b) 6  (c) 8  (d) 10
**Answer: (c) 8**
*Solution:* The two diagonals create 4 small triangles, plus 4 larger triangles each made of two small ones (one per side of the square) = 4 + 4 = 8.

**Q2.** A circular paper is folded into quarters and one notch is cut at the curved edge of the folded corner. On unfolding, how many notches appear around the circle?
(a) 2  (b) 3  (c) 4  (d) 8
**Answer: (c) 4**
*Solution:* Folding into quarters stacks 4 layers; one cut passes through all 4, giving 4 symmetric notches around the circle.

**Q3.** In the word-arrangement, the mirror image of "1 2 3" (vertical mirror) appears in what left-to-right order of the digit shapes?
(a) reversed: 3 2 1 with each digit flipped  (b) 1 2 3 unchanged  (c) 3 2 1 unflipped  (d) 2 1 3
**Answer: (a) reversed: 3 2 1 with each digit flipped**
*Solution:* A vertical mirror both reverses the order and flips each character, so "1 2 3" becomes flipped "3 2 1."

**Q4.** Statements: Some doctors are writers. All writers are creative. Conclusion: Some doctors are creative.
(a) Follows  (b) Does not follow  (c) Either-or  (d) Cannot say
**Answer: (a) Follows**
*Solution:* The doctors who are writers are all creative (since all writers are creative), so "some doctors are creative" follows.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN (SSC CGL pattern)

**Q1.** Select the correct mirror image of the capital letter "L" when the mirror is on the right side.
(a) Reversed L (foot points left)  (b) L unchanged  (c) T  (d) J
**Answer: (a) Reversed L (foot points left)**
*Solution:* A vertical mirror flips left-right, so the foot of "L" that points right now points left.

**Q2.** A transparent sheet has a pattern. Its water image will differ from the original by?
(a) Top and bottom getting swapped  (b) Left and right getting swapped  (c) No change  (d) Rotation by 90°
**Answer: (a) Top and bottom getting swapped**
*Solution:* A water image is reflection in a horizontal mirror below the figure, so top and bottom interchange while left-right stay.

**Q3.** How many rectangles (including squares) are there in a 1×3 strip divided into three equal cells?
(a) 3  (b) 5  (c) 6  (d) 4
**Answer: (c) 6**
*Solution:* Single cells: 3; pairs of adjacent cells: 2; all three together: 1. Total = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 rectangles.

**Q4.** Which diagram best represents the relation among "Women, Mothers, Doctors"?
(a) Mothers inside Women; Doctors overlapping both  (b) All three completely separate  (c) Doctors inside Mothers  (d) Women inside Doctors
**Answer: (a) Mothers inside Women; Doctors overlapping both**
*Solution:* All mothers are women (mothers ⊆ women), while doctors can be women, mothers, or neither — so the doctors' circle overlaps both.

### 🔑 Trick Box
- Mirror (vertical) = left-right swap; water (horizontal) = top-bottom swap.
- Each fold doubles punched holes: n folds → 2ⁿ holes from one punch.
- Count figures in layers: smallest units, then 2-unit combos, then the whole.
- For Venn relations, ask "is every X a Y?" (subset) vs "can they just overlap?" before drawing.

> For mirror images of words, remember to reverse the letter order *and* flip each letter.
