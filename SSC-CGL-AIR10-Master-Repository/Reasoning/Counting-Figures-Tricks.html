🧭 The systematic counting method ↑ top

Don't count randomly — you will miss some. Steps:
1. Count the smallest (single-part) figures and label them 1, 2, 3…
2. Count figures made of 2 parts, then 3 parts, and so on.
3. Add all levels. For standard grids/fans, use the formulas below instead.

🔺 Counting Triangles ↑ top

(a) Big triangle divided by lines from the apex to the base. If the base is divided into n parts (n cevians create n small triangles):

        /\
       /  \           Base split into n segments by lines from apex.
      /|  |\          Number of triangles = n + (n-1) + ... + 1
     / |  | \                            = n(n+1)/2
    /__|__|__\
   1   2   3          e.g. n = 3  ->  3·4/2 = 6 triangles

(b) Triangle made of a grid of rows (like a triangular wall of small triangles). For a big triangle divided into smaller ones with n rows:

⬛ Counting Squares ↑ top

   |_|_|_|               Squares of size 3×3 : 1
   |_|_|_|               TOTAL = 9 + 4 + 1 = 14
   |_|_|_|               = 1² + 2² + 3²

▭ Counting Rectangles ↑ top

In an m × n grid, total rectangles (squares included):

   Total rectangles = [m(m+1)/2] × [n(n+1)/2]
   (choose 2 vertical lines × 2 horizontal lines)

   e.g. 3 × 2 grid  =  (3·4/2) × (2·3/2) = 6 × 3 = 18 rectangles
   Chessboard 8×8   =  (8·9/2)² = 36² = 1296 rectangles

🪄 Only rectangles that are NOT squares = total rectangles − total squares.

🎨 Painted Cube problems (SSC favourite) ↑ top

A cube is painted on all 6 faces, then cut into n³ small cubes. How many small cubes have paint on 3, 2, 1, or 0 faces? Memorise these 4 formulas.
            ___________
           /  CORNER  /|        • CORNERS get 3 faces painted
          /__________/ |        • EDGES (non-corner) get 2
         |  EDGE     | |        • FACE-CENTRES get 1
         |   FACE    | |        • INNER (hidden) cubes get 0
         |  CENTRE   | /
         |___________|/
Paint on…WhereFormulan = 3n = 4
3 facesthe 8 corners8 (always)88
2 facesalong the 12 edges12 × (n − 2)1224
1 facecentre of the 6 faces6 × (n − 2)²624
0 faceshidden inside(n − 2)³18
Check: total= n³2764

🪄 Sanity check: 8 + 12(n−2) + 6(n−2)² + (n−2)³ must equal n³. For n = 4: 8 + 24 + 24 + 8 = 64 = 4³ ✔

⚠️ Special cases SSC asks: if only some faces are painted, or two opposite faces left unpainted, recompute — the corner/edge counts change. Also: at least one face painted = n³ − (n−2)³.
