# Direction Sense — Solved Question Bank

> Quick links: [Reasoning Index](README.md) · [Reasoning Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/Reasoning_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Concepts
- Fix directions: North up, South down, East right, West left.
- A **right turn** rotates clockwise; a **left turn** rotates anticlockwise.
- **Net displacement**: combine North–South and East–West separately, then use Pythagoras for straight-line distance: √(x² + y²).
- **Shadows**: at sunrise the sun is in the East, so shadows fall West; at sunset the sun is in the West, so shadows fall East. At noon shadows are minimal.
- Always sketch the path; track facing direction after each turn.

## EASY

**Q1.** A man walks 5 km North, then turns right and walks 5 km. Which direction is he facing now?
(a) North  (b) South  (c) East  (d) West
**Answer: (c) East**
*Solution:* Facing North, a right (clockwise) turn makes him face East.

**Q2.** Ravi walks 3 km East, then turns left. Which direction does he face?
(a) North  (b) South  (c) East  (d) West
**Answer: (a) North**
*Solution:* Facing East, a left (anticlockwise) turn points him North.

**Q3.** The sun rises in the East. In the morning, a pole's shadow falls towards which direction?
(a) East  (b) West  (c) North  (d) South
**Answer: (b) West**
*Solution:* With the sun in the East, shadows are cast in the opposite direction — West.

**Q4.** A boy walks 4 km South and then 4 km North. How far is he from the start?
(a) 8 km  (b) 4 km  (c) 0 km  (d) 2 km
**Answer: (c) 0 km**
*Solution:* The two equal, opposite moves cancel, returning him to the starting point — distance 0.

**Q5.** Facing West, you turn 180°. Which direction do you face?
(a) North  (b) South  (c) East  (d) West
**Answer: (c) East**
*Solution:* A 180° turn reverses direction; the opposite of West is East.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** A person walks 4 km East, turns left and walks 3 km. How far is he from the start (straight line)?
(a) 5 km  (b) 7 km  (c) 6 km  (d) 4 km
**Answer: (a) 5 km**
*Solution:* He moves 4 km East and 3 km North — perpendicular. Straight distance = √(4² + 3²) = √25 = 5 km.

**Q2.** Starting facing North, a man turns right, then right again, then left. Which way does he face?
(a) North  (b) South  (c) East  (d) West
**Answer: (c) East**
*Solution:* North → right = East → right = South → left = East.

**Q3.** A car goes 6 km North, 8 km East. The shortest distance back to start is?
(a) 14 km  (b) 10 km  (c) 12 km  (d) 9 km
**Answer: (b) 10 km**
*Solution:* North 6 and East 8 are perpendicular: √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10 km.

**Q4.** At sunset, a man faces the sun. His shadow falls in which direction?
(a) East  (b) West  (c) North  (d) South
**Answer: (a) East**
*Solution:* At sunset the sun is in the West and the man faces West, so his shadow falls behind him — towards the East.

**Q5.** A man walks 10 m South, turns left, walks 10 m, turns left, walks 10 m. Which direction is he from start?
(a) East  (b) West  (c) North  (d) South
**Answer: (a) East**
*Solution:* South 10 → (left) East 10 → (left) North 10. The South and North legs cancel, leaving him 10 m East of start.

**Q6.** Facing East, a man turns 90° clockwise twice. Which direction does he face?
(a) North  (b) South  (c) East  (d) West
**Answer: (d) West**
*Solution:* East → 90° CW = South → 90° CW = West.

## HARD

**Q1.** A man walks 3 km North, 4 km East, 3 km North again. How far is he from the start?
(a) 5 km  (b) 10 km  (c) √52 km  (d) 7 km
**Answer: (c) √52 km**
*Solution:* Net North = 3 + 3 = 6 km; net East = 4 km. Distance = √(6² + 4²) = √(36 + 16) = √52 ≈ 7.2 km.

**Q2.** A man walks 5 km East, 5 km South, 5 km West, 3 km North. How far is he from start and in which direction?
(a) 2 km South  (b) 2 km North  (c) 5 km South  (d) 2 km East
**Answer: (a) 2 km South**
*Solution:* East 5 and West 5 cancel. South 5 minus North 3 = 2 km net South. He ends 2 km South of start.

**Q3.** Two men start from the same point. One walks 12 km North, the other 5 km East. How far apart are they?
(a) 17 km  (b) 13 km  (c) 7 km  (d) 15 km
**Answer: (b) 13 km**
*Solution:* Their paths are perpendicular: distance = √(12² + 5²) = √169 = 13 km.

**Q4.** A man walks 8 km West, turns right and walks 6 km, turns right and walks 8 km. Final distance from start?
(a) 6 km  (b) 8 km  (c) 14 km  (d) 10 km
**Answer: (a) 6 km**
*Solution:* West 8 → (right) North 6 → (right) East 8. West and East cancel, leaving 6 km North = 6 km from start.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN (SSC CGL pattern)

**Q1.** A boy goes 15 m East, then turns right and goes 20 m. How far is he from the starting point?
(a) 25 m  (b) 35 m  (c) 30 m  (d) 20 m
**Answer: (a) 25 m**
*Solution:* East 15 then South 20 (perpendicular): √(15² + 20²) = √625 = 25 m.

**Q2.** Rahul starts facing North, turns 135° clockwise, then 180° anticlockwise. Final facing direction (8-point)?
(a) South-East  (b) South-West  (c) North-East  (d) North-West
**Answer: (d) North-West**
*Solution:* North + 135° CW = South-East. Then 180° anticlockwise (i.e. opposite) of South-East = North-West.

**Q3.** A man walks 9 km towards North, then 4 km towards South. How far is he from the start?
(a) 13 km  (b) 5 km  (c) 4 km  (d) 9 km
**Answer: (b) 5 km**
*Solution:* Net displacement = 9 − 4 = 5 km towards North.

**Q4.** At 6 a.m., a man stands facing a pole; the pole's shadow falls exactly to his left. Which direction is he facing?
(a) North  (b) South  (c) East  (d) West
**Answer: (a) North**
*Solution:* At sunrise the shadow falls West. If West is to his left, he must be facing North (left of North is West).

### 🔑 Trick Box
- Right turn = clockwise; left turn = anticlockwise. Update facing after each turn.
- Collapse the path into net North–South and net East–West first.
- If the two nets are perpendicular, straight distance = √(x² + y²) — memorise 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 6-8-10.
- Sunrise → shadow West; sunset → shadow East; noon → negligible shadow.

> Sketch every problem; a 2-minute diagram beats a wrong mental rotation.
