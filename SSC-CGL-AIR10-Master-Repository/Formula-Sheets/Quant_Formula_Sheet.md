## 1. Number System

- Sum of first n natural numbers: `n(n+1)/2`
- Sum of first n odd numbers: `n²`
- Sum of first n even numbers: `n(n+1)`
- Sum of squares: `n(n+1)(2n+1)/6`
- Sum of cubes: `[n(n+1)/2]²`
- Number of factors of `N = aᵖ·bᵍ·cʳ` → `(p+1)(q+1)(r+1)`
- Sum of factors → `(aᵖ⁺¹−1)/(a−1) × (bᵍ⁺¹−1)/(b−1) × …`
- Product of factors → `N^(no. of factors / 2)`
- Divisibility 7: double last digit, subtract from rest, repeat
- Divisibility 11: (sum of odd-place digits − sum of even-place digits) divisible by 11
- Units digit cycles: 2→(2,4,8,6); 3→(3,9,7,1); 7→(7,9,3,1); 8→(8,4,2,6); cycle length 4
- `aⁿ − bⁿ` always divisible by `(a − b)`; divisible by `(a + b)` if n even
- `aⁿ + bⁿ` divisible by `(a + b)` if n odd

## 2. HCF & LCM

- `HCF × LCM = Product of two numbers`
- HCF of fractions = `HCF(numerators) / LCM(denominators)`
- LCM of fractions = `LCM(numerators) / HCF(denominators)`
- Greatest number dividing a, b, c leaving same remainder r → `HCF(a−b, b−c, c−a)`
- Least number which when divided by a, b, c leaves remainder r → `LCM(a,b,c) + r`

## 3. Percentage

- `x% of y = y% of x`
- Fraction ↔ %: 1/2=50, 1/3=33.33, 1/4=25, 1/5=20, 1/6=16.67, 1/7=14.28, 1/8=12.5, 1/9=11.11, 1/11=9.09, 1/12=8.33
- Increase by x%, then decrease by x% → net change `−x²/100` (always a loss)
- A is x% more than B → B is less than A by `x/(100+x) × 100`%
- A is x% less than B → B is more than A by `x/(100−x) × 100`%
- Successive change a% and b% → `a + b + ab/100`
- Population after n yrs: `P(1 + r/100)ⁿ`

## 4. Profit & Loss

- `Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP × 100`
- `SP = CP × (100 + P%)/100`
- `CP = SP × 100/(100 + P%)`
- Two items same SP, one at +x%, other at −x% → net **loss** `x²/100`%
- `MP × (100 − Discount%) = SP`
- Successive discounts a% & b% → equivalent `a + b − ab/100`
- Marked x% above CP, discount y% → profit `x − y − xy/100`
- Dishonest dealer (false weight): `Gain% = (Error / (True − Error)) × 100`
- Buy x get y free → discount% = `y/(x+y) × 100`

## 5. Simple & Compound Interest

- `SI = PRT/100`
- `Amount (CI) = P(1 + R/100)ᵀ`
- CI for 2 yrs = `P[(R/100)² + 2R/100]`
- Difference (CI − SI) 2 yrs = `P(R/100)²`
- Difference 3 yrs = `P(R/100)²·(3 + R/100)` = `PR²(300+R)/100³`
- Rate that doubles money in SI: `R = 100/T`
- CI doubles → ~ Rule of 72: `T ≈ 72/R`
- Half-yearly CI: R→R/2, T→2T; Quarterly: R→R/4, T→4T

## 6. Ratio & Proportion

- a:b = c:d → `ad = bc`
- Mean proportional of a & b = `√(ab)`
- Third proportional to a, b = `b²/a`
- Fourth proportional to a, b, c = `bc/a`
- If a:b and b:c known → a:b:c = `a×b : b×b : b×c` (make b common)
- Componendo-dividendo: `(a+b)/(a−b) = (c+d)/(c−d)`

## 7. Partnership

- Profit ratio = `(Capital × Time)` ratio
- Simple partnership (same time) → ratio of capitals
- Working partner gets salary/commission first, rest divided by ratio

## 8. Average

- `Average = Sum / Number of terms`
- Average of first n natural numbers = `(n+1)/2`
- Average of consecutive numbers = `(First + Last)/2`
- Speed avg (equal distance) = `2xy/(x+y)` (harmonic mean)
- New avg when one item replaced: `Δavg = (new − old)/n`

## 9. Time & Work

- If A does work in `n` days → 1 day work = `1/n`
- A & B together: `(a×b)/(a+b)` days
- Work–Wage: wages ∝ work done ∝ efficiency × time
- `M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂`
- Efficiency inversely ∝ time
- A is x% more efficient than B → time ratio `B:A = (100+x):100`

## 10. Pipes & Cisterns

- Same as time & work; outlet pipe = negative work
- Fill `1/x`, empty `1/y` → net `1/x − 1/y` per hour

## 11. Time, Speed & Distance

- `Speed = Distance / Time`
- km/hr → m/s: `× 5/18`; m/s → km/hr: `× 18/5`
- Avg speed (equal dist, speeds x,y) = `2xy/(x+y)`
- Same time, speeds x:y → distance x:y
- Two bodies, distance D, speeds opposite → meet time `D/(a+b)`; same direction `D/(a−b)`

## 12. Trains, Boats & Streams

- Train crossing pole: `Length/Speed`
- Train crossing platform: `(Train + Platform)/Speed`
- Two trains opposite: relative speed = sum; same direction = difference
- Downstream = `(b + s)`; Upstream = `(b − s)`
- Boat speed `b = (down + up)/2`; Stream `s = (down − up)/2`
- `Avg speed for to-and-fro in stream = (down × up)/boat speed`

## 13. Mixture & Alligation

- `(Cheaper qty)/(Dearer qty) = (Dearer price − Mean)/(Mean − Cheaper price)`
- Repeated replacement: after n operations `Final = Initial × (1 − r/V)ⁿ`

## 14. Algebra (identities)

- `a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b)`
- `(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²` ; `(a−b)² = a² − 2ab + b²`
- `a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a²−ab+b²)`
- `a³ − b³ = (a−b)(a²+ab+b²)`
- `a³ + b³ + c³ − 3abc = (a+b+c)(a²+b²+c² − ab−bc−ca)`
- If `a+b+c=0` → `a³+b³+c³ = 3abc`
- `a + 1/a = x` → `a² + 1/a² = x²−2`; `a³ + 1/a³ = x³−3x`
- `a − 1/a = x` → `a² + 1/a² = x²+2`; `a³ − 1/a³ = x³+3x`

## 15. Geometry

- Triangle angle sum = 180°; exterior = sum of opposite interiors
- Area = `½ × base × height` = `√[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]`, s = perimeter/2
- Equilateral area = `(√3/4)a²`; height = `(√3/2)a`
- Centroid divides median `2:1`
- Circle: angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference (same arc)
- Tangent ⊥ radius at point of contact
- Cyclic quad: opposite angles sum 180°
- Pythagoras triples: (3,4,5)(5,12,13)(8,15,17)(7,24,25)(20,21,29)

## 16. Mensuration (2D / 3D)

- Rectangle: A=`l×b`, P=`2(l+b)`, diagonal=`√(l²+b²)`
- Square: A=`a²`, diagonal=`a√2`
- Circle: A=`πr²`, C=`2πr`; Sector area=`(θ/360)πr²`
- Trapezium: A=`½(sum of parallel sides)×height`
- Cube: V=`a³`, TSA=`6a²`, diagonal=`a√3`
- Cuboid: V=`lbh`, TSA=`2(lb+bh+hl)`, diagonal=`√(l²+b²+h²)`
- Cylinder: V=`πr²h`, CSA=`2πrh`, TSA=`2πr(r+h)`
- Cone: V=`⅓πr²h`, CSA=`πrl`, l=`√(r²+h²)`, TSA=`πr(r+l)`
- Sphere: V=`(4/3)πr³`, SA=`4πr²`
- Hemisphere: V=`(2/3)πr³`, TSA=`3πr²`, CSA=`2πr²`

## 17. Trigonometry

- `sin²θ + cos²θ = 1`; `1 + tan²θ = sec²θ`; `1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ`
- Values: sin(0,30,45,60,90)=`0, ½, 1/√2, √3/2, 1`
- `sin(90−θ)=cosθ`, `tan(90−θ)=cotθ`
- Height & distance: `tanθ = height/base`
- `sin30=½, cos60=½, tan45=1, sin45=cos45=1/√2`

## 18. Data Interpretation & Statistics

- Mean = `Σx/n`; weighted mean = `Σwx/Σw`
- Median (odd) = middle term; (even) = avg of two middle
- Mode = most frequent
- `Mean − Mode = 3(Mean − Median)` (empirical)
- Variance = `Σ(x−x̄)²/n`; SD = `√Variance`
- % change = `(New − Old)/Old × 100`
- Pie chart: value = `(angle/360) × total`
