# Voice & Narration — Solved Question Bank

> Navigation: [English Master Bank](README.md) · [English Grammar Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/English_Grammar_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Rules
- **Passive voice**: object becomes subject + correct form of *be* + past participle (+ by + agent).
- Keep the **tense** the same when changing voice.
- In **indirect speech**, shift the tense back, change pronouns, and adjust time/place words (now→then, today→that day).
- Questions, commands and exclamations follow special reporting-verb patterns (asked, ordered, exclaimed).

## EASY

**Q1.** Change to passive voice: **She writes a letter.**
(a) A letter is written by her.  (b) A letter was written by her.  (c) A letter is being written by her.  (d) A letter has written by her.
**Answer: (a) A letter is written by her.**
*Solution:* Simple present active becomes "is/are + past participle". "Writes" → "is written", agent "by her".

**Q2.** Change to passive voice: **They are building a house.**
(a) A house is built by them.  (b) A house is being built by them.  (c) A house was being built by them.  (d) A house has been built by them.
**Answer: (b) A house is being built by them.**
*Solution:* Present continuous passive is "is/are being + past participle": "is being built".

**Q3.** Change to indirect speech: **He said, "I am tired."**
(a) He said that he was tired.  (b) He said that I am tired.  (c) He says that he is tired.  (d) He said that he is tired.
**Answer: (a) He said that he was tired.**
*Solution:* The reporting verb "said" is past, so present "am" shifts to "was" and "I" becomes "he".

**Q4.** Change to passive voice: **He opened the door.**
(a) The door was opened by him.  (b) The door is opened by him.  (c) The door has opened by him.  (d) The door opened by him.
**Answer: (a) The door was opened by him.**
*Solution:* Simple past active becomes "was/were + past participle": "was opened by him".

**Q5.** Change to indirect speech: **She said, "I will help you."**
(a) She said that she will help me.  (b) She said that she would help me.  (c) She said that she helped me.  (d) She says she would help me.
**Answer: (b) She said that she would help me.**
*Solution:* "Will" changes to "would" after a past reporting verb, and "you" becomes "me".

**Q6.** Change to passive voice: **The teacher praised the student.**
(a) The student is praised by the teacher.  (b) The student was praised by the teacher.  (c) The student has praised the teacher.  (d) The student praised by the teacher.
**Answer: (b) The student was praised by the teacher.**
*Solution:* Simple past passive is "was + past participle": "was praised by the teacher".

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** Change to passive voice: **Someone has stolen my bicycle.**
(a) My bicycle has been stolen.  (b) My bicycle was stolen.  (c) My bicycle is stolen.  (d) My bicycle had been stolen.
**Answer: (a) My bicycle has been stolen.**
*Solution:* Present perfect passive is "has/have been + past participle". The vague agent "someone" is usually dropped.

**Q2.** Change to indirect speech: **He said to me, "Where do you live?"**
(a) He asked me where I lived.  (b) He asked me where do I live.  (c) He asked me where I live.  (d) He told me where I lived.
**Answer: (a) He asked me where I lived.**
*Solution:* A wh-question uses "asked", drops the auxiliary, keeps statement order, and shifts "do you live" to "I lived".

**Q3.** Change to indirect speech: **She said, "Please close the window."**
(a) She requested to close the window.  (b) She requested me to close the window.  (c) She said to close the window.  (d) She ordered closing the window.
**Answer: (b) She requested me to close the window.**
*Solution:* A polite command uses "requested + object + to + verb". "Please" becomes "requested".

**Q4.** Change to passive voice: **The committee will announce the results.**
(a) The results will announced.  (b) The results will be announced by the committee.  (c) The results are announced.  (d) The results have been announced.
**Answer: (b) The results will be announced by the committee.**
*Solution:* Future simple passive is "will be + past participle": "will be announced".

**Q5.** Change to indirect speech: **The teacher said, "Do not make noise."**
(a) The teacher said do not make noise.  (b) The teacher ordered not to make noise.  (c) The teacher asked making noise.  (d) The teacher told making noise.
**Answer: (b) The teacher ordered not to make noise.**
*Solution:* A negative command uses "ordered/forbade + not + to + verb": "ordered not to make noise".

**Q6.** Change to passive voice: **People speak English all over the world.**
(a) English is spoken all over the world.  (b) English was spoken all over the world.  (c) English speaks all over the world.  (d) English is being spoken all over the world.
**Answer: (a) English is spoken all over the world.**
*Solution:* Simple present passive is "is spoken"; the general agent "people" is dropped.

## HARD

**Q1.** Change to indirect speech: **He said, "What a beautiful sight it is!"**
(a) He said that it was a beautiful sight.  (b) He exclaimed that it was a very beautiful sight.  (c) He asked what a beautiful sight it was.  (d) He told that it is a beautiful sight.
**Answer: (b) He exclaimed that it was a very beautiful sight.**
*Solution:* An exclamatory sentence uses "exclaimed", "what a...!" becomes "that...very", and "is" shifts to "was".

**Q2.** Change to passive voice: **They were repairing the road when I passed by.**
(a) The road was repaired when I passed by.  (b) The road was being repaired when I passed by.  (c) The road is being repaired when I passed by.  (d) The road had been repaired when I passed by.
**Answer: (b) The road was being repaired when I passed by.**
*Solution:* Past continuous passive is "was/were being + past participle": "was being repaired".

**Q3.** Change to indirect speech: **She said, "I have finished my work now."**
(a) She said that she has finished her work then.  (b) She said that she had finished her work then.  (c) She said that she finished her work now.  (d) She said that she had finished her work now.
**Answer: (b) She said that she had finished her work then.**
*Solution:* Present perfect "have finished" shifts to past perfect "had finished", and "now" becomes "then".

**Q4.** Change to passive voice: **One should keep one's promises.**
(a) Promises should be kept.  (b) Promises should keep.  (c) Promises are kept.  (d) Promises were kept.
**Answer: (a) Promises should be kept.**
*Solution:* With a modal, the passive is "modal + be + past participle": "should be kept". The general agent is omitted.

**Q5.** Change to indirect speech: **He said, "May God bless you!"**
(a) He said that God may bless me.  (b) He prayed that God might bless me.  (c) He asked God to bless me.  (d) He told that God would bless me.
**Answer: (b) He prayed that God might bless me.**
*Solution:* An optative (wish/prayer) uses "prayed/wished", "may" shifts to "might", and "you" becomes "me".

## SSC PYQ PATTERN
*(SSC CGL pattern)*

**Q1.** Select the correct passive form: **The chef is preparing a special dish.**
(a) A special dish is prepared by the chef.  (b) A special dish is being prepared by the chef.  (c) A special dish was prepared by the chef.  (d) A special dish has been prepared by the chef.
**Answer: (b) A special dish is being prepared by the chef.**
*Solution:* Present continuous passive is "is being + past participle": "is being prepared".

**Q2.** Select the correct indirect speech: **He said to her, "Are you coming with me?"**
(a) He asked her if she was coming with him.  (b) He asked her if she is coming with him.  (c) He told her that she was coming with him.  (d) He asked her are you coming with me.
**Answer: (a) He asked her if she was coming with him.**
*Solution:* A yes/no question uses "asked...if", shifts "are you coming" to "she was coming" and "me" to "him".

**Q3.** Select the correct passive form: **They have completed the project.**
(a) The project is completed by them.  (b) The project was completed by them.  (c) The project has been completed by them.  (d) The project had been completed by them.
**Answer: (c) The project has been completed by them.**
*Solution:* Present perfect passive is "has/have been + past participle": "has been completed".

**Q4.** Select the correct indirect speech: **The officer said to the soldiers, "March forward."**
(a) The officer told the soldiers to march forward.  (b) The officer ordered the soldiers to march forward.  (c) The officer asked the soldiers march forward.  (d) The officer said the soldiers to march forward.
**Answer: (b) The officer ordered the soldiers to march forward.**
*Solution:* An authoritative command uses "ordered + object + to + verb": "ordered the soldiers to march forward".

**Q5.** Select the correct active form: **A new bridge is being built by the government.**
(a) The government builds a new bridge.  (b) The government is building a new bridge.  (c) The government built a new bridge.  (d) The government has built a new bridge.
**Answer: (b) The government is building a new bridge.**
*Solution:* "Is being built" is present continuous passive, so the active is present continuous: "is building".

### 🔑 Memory Techniques
- For passive, remember the formula: **object + correct *be* + past participle (+ by + agent)**.
- Match the *be* form to the **original tense** (is → is being; has → has been; will → will be).
- In narration, use the right **reporting verb**: asked (question), ordered/requested (command), exclaimed (exclamation), prayed (wish).
- Apply the **back-shift** of tense and change **now→then, today→that day, here→there** in indirect speech.
