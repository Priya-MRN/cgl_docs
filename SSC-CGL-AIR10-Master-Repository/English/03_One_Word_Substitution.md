# One Word Substitution — Solved Question Bank

> Navigation: [English Master Bank](README.md) · [English Grammar Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/English_Grammar_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Rules
- A **one word substitution** replaces a whole descriptive phrase with a single precise word.
- Focus on the **key qualifier** in the phrase (e.g. "cannot be" → *im-/in-* prefix words).
- Many answers share roots: *-cide* = killing, *-vorous* = eating, *-archy* = rule.
- Eliminate options whose meaning is close but not exact.

## EASY

**Q1.** One who studies the stars and planets.
(a) Astrologer  (b) Astronaut  (c) Astronomer  (d) Geologist
**Answer: (c) Astronomer**
*Solution:* An astronomer scientifically studies celestial bodies. An astrologer links stars to human affairs, an astronaut travels in space, a geologist studies the earth.

**Q2.** A person who cannot read or write.
(a) Illiterate  (b) Ignorant  (c) Innocent  (d) Illegible
**Answer: (a) Illiterate**
*Solution:* "Illiterate" precisely means unable to read or write. Ignorant means lacking knowledge, illegible describes unreadable writing, innocent is unrelated.

**Q3.** A place where animals are kept for public show.
(a) Zoo  (b) Aquarium  (c) Sanctuary  (d) Den
**Answer: (a) Zoo**
*Solution:* A zoo houses many kinds of animals for public viewing. An aquarium holds fish, a sanctuary protects wildlife in nature, a den is an animal's home.

**Q4.** A medicine that kills germs.
(a) Antiseptic  (b) Antibiotic  (c) Anaesthetic  (d) Antidote
**Answer: (a) Antiseptic**
*Solution:* An antiseptic destroys or prevents the growth of germs. An anaesthetic numbs sensation; an antidote counters poison; an antibiotic treats bacterial infection from within.

**Q5.** A person who writes for newspapers.
(a) Author  (b) Journalist  (c) Editor  (d) Publisher
**Answer: (b) Journalist**
*Solution:* A journalist writes news and articles for newspapers. An author writes books, an editor revises content, a publisher produces and sells it.

**Q6.** Government by the people.
(a) Monarchy  (b) Democracy  (c) Aristocracy  (d) Anarchy
**Answer: (b) Democracy**
*Solution:* Democracy is rule by the people. Monarchy is rule by a king, aristocracy by the nobility, and anarchy is the absence of government.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** One who eats everything (both plants and meat).
(a) Carnivore  (b) Herbivore  (c) Omnivore  (d) Insectivore
**Answer: (c) Omnivore**
*Solution:* An omnivore eats both plant and animal food. A carnivore eats meat, a herbivore eats plants, an insectivore eats insects.

**Q2.** A speech made without preparation.
(a) Maiden  (b) Extempore  (c) Eloquent  (d) Verbose
**Answer: (b) Extempore**
*Solution:* An extempore speech is delivered without prior preparation. A maiden speech is one's first, eloquent means fluent, verbose means wordy.

**Q3.** A person who does not believe in the existence of God.
(a) Theist  (b) Atheist  (c) Agnostic  (d) Heretic
**Answer: (b) Atheist**
*Solution:* An atheist denies the existence of God. A theist believes in God, an agnostic is unsure, a heretic holds views against accepted doctrine.

**Q4.** Words written on the tomb of a person.
(a) Epitaph  (b) Epigram  (c) Epilogue  (d) Epistle
**Answer: (a) Epitaph**
*Solution:* An epitaph is the inscription on a gravestone. An epigram is a witty saying, an epilogue ends a book, an epistle is a formal letter.

**Q5.** A cure for all diseases.
(a) Antidote  (b) Panacea  (c) Tonic  (d) Sedative
**Answer: (b) Panacea**
*Solution:* A panacea is a remedy for all ills. An antidote counters a single poison, a tonic invigorates, a sedative calms.

**Q6.** One who is greedy and eats too much.
(a) Glutton  (b) Epicure  (c) Cannibal  (d) Gourmet
**Answer: (a) Glutton**
*Solution:* A glutton eats excessively and greedily. An epicure and a gourmet appreciate fine food in moderation; a cannibal eats human flesh.

## HARD

**Q1.** A person who is unable to pay their debts.
(a) Bankrupt  (b) Insolvent  (c) Pauper  (d) Beggar
**Answer: (b) Insolvent**
*Solution:* "Insolvent" describes a person unable to pay debts. A pauper is very poor, a beggar lives by begging, and bankrupt is a legal status declared by a court.

**Q2.** Something that can be heard.
(a) Audible  (b) Legible  (c) Tangible  (d) Visible
**Answer: (a) Audible**
*Solution:* "Audible" means capable of being heard. Legible can be read, tangible can be touched, visible can be seen.

**Q3.** A person who hates mankind.
(a) Philanthropist  (b) Misanthrope  (c) Misogynist  (d) Egoist
**Answer: (b) Misanthrope**
*Solution:* A misanthrope dislikes humankind. A philanthropist loves and helps people, a misogynist hates women specifically, an egoist is self-centred.

**Q4.** The study of the origin and history of words.
(a) Etymology  (b) Entomology  (c) Ecology  (d) Philology
**Answer: (a) Etymology**
*Solution:* Etymology studies word origins. Entomology is the study of insects, ecology of organisms and environment, philology of language in historical texts broadly.

**Q5.** One who talks in their sleep.
(a) Somnambulist  (b) Somniloquist  (c) Insomniac  (d) Sleepwalker
**Answer: (b) Somniloquist**
*Solution:* A somniloquist talks while asleep. A somnambulist (sleepwalker) walks while asleep, and an insomniac cannot sleep.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN
*(SSC CGL pattern)*

**Q1.** Select the one word substitution for the given phrase. **"A place where bees are kept."**
(a) Apiary  (b) Aviary  (c) Sanctuary  (d) Hive
**Answer: (a) Apiary**
*Solution:* An apiary is a place where beehives are kept. An aviary houses birds; a hive is the single structure bees live in, not the place where they are kept.

**Q2.** Select the one word substitution for the given phrase. **"That which cannot be corrected."**
(a) Incurable  (b) Incorrigible  (c) Indelible  (d) Illegible
**Answer: (b) Incorrigible**
*Solution:* "Incorrigible" means not able to be corrected or reformed. Incurable refers to diseases, indelible to marks that cannot be removed, illegible to unreadable writing.

**Q3.** Select the one word substitution for the given phrase. **"A government run by officials."**
(a) Democracy  (b) Bureaucracy  (c) Theocracy  (d) Plutocracy
**Answer: (b) Bureaucracy**
*Solution:* Bureaucracy is government by appointed officials. Theocracy is rule by religious authority, plutocracy by the wealthy, democracy by the people.

**Q4.** Select the one word substitution for the given phrase. **"One who loves all human beings."**
(a) Misanthrope  (b) Philanthropist  (c) Egoist  (d) Cynic
**Answer: (b) Philanthropist**
*Solution:* A philanthropist loves and helps humankind. A misanthrope hates people, an egoist is self-centred, a cynic distrusts others' motives.

**Q5.** Select the one word substitution for the given phrase. **"A handwriting that cannot be read."**
(a) Audible  (b) Eligible  (c) Illegible  (d) Invisible
**Answer: (c) Illegible**
*Solution:* "Illegible" means impossible to read. Audible relates to hearing, eligible to qualification, invisible to sight.

### 🔑 Memory Techniques
- Learn the common **suffixes**: *-cide* (kill), *-vorous* (eat), *-cracy/-archy* (rule), *-logy* (study).
- Pair confusable words (apiary/aviary, etymology/entomology) and drill them **side by side**.
- Spot the **prefix of negation** (*in-, il-, im-*) for "cannot be ___" phrases.
- Make a personal **glossary** and revise 10 substitutions daily.
