# Newspaper Antonyms & Thesaurus — 1 Full Year (≈365 Words)

> Navigation: [English Master Bank](README.md) · [Newspaper Vocabulary — 1 Year](12_Newspaper_Vocabulary_1_Year.md) · [Vocabulary Synonyms & Antonyms (MCQs)](01_Vocabulary_Synonyms_Antonyms.md)

## What this is
This is the **opposite-word companion** to [12_Newspaper_Vocabulary_1_Year.md](12_Newspaper_Vocabulary_1_Year.md). Every word in the year-long list appears here again — this time with its **ANTONYMS (opposite words)**, written thesaurus-style with several related opposites for each.

Knowing a word AND its opposite is the fastest way to truly own it. Exams (SSC, banking, etc.) test antonyms directly, and editorials use these contrasts all the time (*affluent vs. impoverished, transparent vs. opaque*).

## How to read each entry
**Word** *(part of speech)* — meaning
- 👉 **easiest opposite:** the one simple opposite to remember first
- 🔄 **more opposites (easy → hard):** a thesaurus list, from simple words you know to higher ones
- ⚠️ a note appears only when a word has **no true one-word opposite** (then the closest contrasts are given).

**Study tip:** Learn the **easiest opposite** first. Only when that is solid, pick up one or two of the harder opposites from the 🔄 list.

Pair this file with [12_Newspaper_Vocabulary_1_Year.md](12_Newspaper_Vocabulary_1_Year.md) (the meanings + sentences) and you have **both sides of every newspaper word** for a full year. Keep going — you are building real reading power. 💪
