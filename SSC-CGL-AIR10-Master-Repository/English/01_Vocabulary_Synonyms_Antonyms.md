# Vocabulary — Synonyms & Antonyms — Solved Question Bank

## Key Rules
- A **synonym** has a similar meaning; an **antonym** has the opposite meaning.
- Read the instruction carefully — confusing "synonym" with "antonym" is the most common slip.
- Use word roots, prefixes (e.g. *in-*, *un-*, *dis-*) and context to narrow choices.
- Eliminate options that are merely related in topic but not in meaning.

## EASY

**Q1.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Abundant**.
(a) Scarce  (b) Plentiful  (c) Empty  (d) Rare
**Answer: (b) Plentiful**
*Solution:* "Abundant" means existing in large quantities, i.e. plentiful. Scarce, empty and rare all denote shortage, so they are antonyms.

**Q2.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Brave**.
(a) Timid  (b) Cowardly  (c) Courageous  (d) Weak
**Answer: (c) Courageous**
*Solution:* "Brave" means showing courage, so courageous matches. Timid, cowardly and weak express the opposite quality.

**Q3.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Ancient**.
(a) Old  (b) Antique  (c) Modern  (d) Aged
**Answer: (c) Modern**
*Solution:* "Ancient" means belonging to the very distant past; its opposite is modern. Old, antique and aged are near-synonyms.

**Q4.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Happy**.
(a) Sad  (b) Joyful  (c) Gloomy  (d) Angry
**Answer: (b) Joyful**
*Solution:* "Happy" means feeling pleasure, so joyful is the match. Sad, gloomy and angry are unrelated negative emotions.

**Q5.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Victory**.
(a) Triumph  (b) Success  (c) Defeat  (d) Win
**Answer: (c) Defeat**
*Solution:* "Victory" is a win; its opposite is defeat. Triumph, success and win are synonyms of victory.

**Q6.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Begin**.
(a) Finish  (b) Stop  (c) Commence  (d) End
**Answer: (c) Commence**
*Solution:* "Begin" means to start; commence is a formal synonym. Finish, stop and end mean to conclude.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Diligent**.
(a) Lazy  (b) Hardworking  (c) Careless  (d) Idle
**Answer: (b) Hardworking**
*Solution:* "Diligent" describes someone who works carefully and hard, so hardworking fits. Lazy, careless and idle are antonyms.

**Q2.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Generous**.
(a) Kind  (b) Liberal  (c) Stingy  (d) Charitable
**Answer: (c) Stingy**
*Solution:* "Generous" means willing to give freely; stingy means unwilling to give, the opposite. Kind, liberal and charitable are synonyms.

**Q3.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Candid**.
(a) Deceptive  (b) Frank  (c) Secretive  (d) Cunning
**Answer: (b) Frank**
*Solution:* "Candid" means open and honest in speech, i.e. frank. Deceptive, secretive and cunning suggest the opposite.

**Q4.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Transparent**.
(a) Clear  (b) Obvious  (c) Opaque  (d) Plain
**Answer: (c) Opaque**
*Solution:* "Transparent" means able to be seen through; opaque blocks light and view, the opposite. Clear, obvious and plain are synonyms.

**Q5.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Reluctant**.
(a) Eager  (b) Willing  (c) Unwilling  (d) Keen
**Answer: (c) Unwilling**
*Solution:* "Reluctant" means hesitant or unwilling to do something. Eager, willing and keen express enthusiasm, the opposite.

**Q6.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Humble**.
(a) Modest  (b) Meek  (c) Arrogant  (d) Simple
**Answer: (c) Arrogant**
*Solution:* "Humble" means modest about oneself; arrogant means proud and self-important, the opposite. Modest, meek and simple are synonyms.

**Q7.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Vivid**.
(a) Dull  (b) Bright  (c) Faint  (d) Vague
**Answer: (b) Bright**
*Solution:* "Vivid" means producing strong, clear images; bright captures this vividness. Dull, faint and vague indicate the opposite.

## HARD

**Q1.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Ephemeral**.
(a) Permanent  (b) Eternal  (c) Transient  (d) Lasting
**Answer: (c) Transient**
*Solution:* "Ephemeral" means lasting a very short time; transient also means short-lived. Permanent, eternal and lasting denote durability.

**Q2.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Benevolent**.
(a) Kind  (b) Malevolent  (c) Gracious  (d) Caring
**Answer: (b) Malevolent**
*Solution:* "Benevolent" means well-meaning and kind; malevolent means wishing harm, the exact opposite. Kind, gracious and caring are synonyms.

**Q3.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Meticulous**.
(a) Careless  (b) Sloppy  (c) Painstaking  (d) Hasty
**Answer: (c) Painstaking**
*Solution:* "Meticulous" means showing great attention to detail; painstaking conveys the same care. Careless, sloppy and hasty are opposites.

**Q4.** Choose the ANTONYM of **Frugal**.
(a) Thrifty  (b) Economical  (c) Extravagant  (d) Sparing
**Answer: (c) Extravagant**
*Solution:* "Frugal" means careful with money; extravagant means spending lavishly, the opposite. Thrifty, economical and sparing are synonyms.

**Q5.** Choose the SYNONYM of **Audacious**.
(a) Timid  (b) Bold  (c) Fearful  (d) Cautious
**Answer: (b) Bold**
*Solution:* "Audacious" means showing a willingness to take bold risks; bold matches. Timid, fearful and cautious describe the opposite.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN
*(SSC CGL pattern)*

**Q1.** Select the most appropriate SYNONYM of the given word. **Lucid**
(a) Confusing  (b) Clear  (c) Murky  (d) Obscure
**Answer: (b) Clear**
*Solution:* "Lucid" means expressed clearly and easy to understand, so clear is correct. Confusing, murky and obscure are antonyms.

**Q2.** Select the most appropriate ANTONYM of the given word. **Optimistic**
(a) Hopeful  (b) Cheerful  (c) Pessimistic  (d) Positive
**Answer: (c) Pessimistic**
*Solution:* "Optimistic" means hopeful about the future; pessimistic, expecting the worst, is the opposite. Hopeful, cheerful and positive are synonyms.

**Q3.** Select the most appropriate SYNONYM of the given word. **Tedious**
(a) Exciting  (b) Boring  (c) Pleasant  (d) Brief
**Answer: (b) Boring**
*Solution:* "Tedious" means too long, slow and dull, hence boring. Exciting and pleasant are opposites; brief refers to length, not dullness.

**Q4.** Select the most appropriate ANTONYM of the given word. **Hostile**
(a) Aggressive  (b) Unfriendly  (c) Friendly  (d) Antagonistic
**Answer: (c) Friendly**
*Solution:* "Hostile" means unfriendly or aggressive; friendly is its opposite. Aggressive, unfriendly and antagonistic are synonyms.

**Q5.** Select the most appropriate SYNONYM of the given word. **Abolish**
(a) Establish  (b) Create  (c) Abolish-retain  (d) Eradicate
**Answer: (d) Eradicate**
*Solution:* "Abolish" means to formally put an end to something; eradicate (to root out completely) is the closest synonym. Establish and create are opposites.

### 🔑 Memory Techniques
- Group words into **synonym families** (e.g. brave–bold–audacious–valiant) so one cue recalls many.
- Learn the **prefix** signal: *mal-* = bad (malevolent), *bene-* = good (benevolent).
- For antonyms, picture the **two extremes** of a scale (frugal ↔ extravagant).
- Always re-read whether the question asks for synonym or antonym **before** marking.
