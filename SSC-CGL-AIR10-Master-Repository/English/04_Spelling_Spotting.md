# Spelling Spotting — Solved Question Bank

> Navigation: [English Master Bank](README.md) · [English Grammar Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/English_Grammar_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Rules
- Read every letter of each option carefully — errors are often a single doubled or dropped letter.
- Remember tricky rules: **"i before e except after c"** (receive, believe).
- Watch double consonants (*accommodate*, *embarrass*, *occasion*) and silent letters.
- Decide first whether the question asks for the **correctly** or the **incorrectly** spelt word.

## EASY

**Q1.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Definately  (b) Definitely  (c) Definitly  (d) Definetely
**Answer: (b) Definitely**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "definitely" (root *finite*). The others wrongly insert an 'a' or drop the 'e'.

**Q2.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Receive  (b) Believe  (c) Acheive  (d) Relieve
**Answer: (c) Acheive**
*Solution:* The correct form is "achieve" (i before e). The other three are correctly spelt.

**Q3.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Seperate  (b) Separate  (c) Seperete  (d) Sepparate
**Answer: (b) Separate**
*Solution:* "Separate" contains "a rat" in the middle (sep-a-rate). The others misplace or double letters.

**Q4.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Calendar  (b) Tomorrow  (c) Goverment  (d) Address
**Answer: (c) Goverment**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "government" with an 'n' (govern + ment). The other three are correct.

**Q5.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Begining  (b) Beginning  (c) Begginning  (d) Beginnig
**Answer: (b) Beginning**
*Solution:* "Beginning" doubles the final 'n' before -ing. The others drop or misplace the doubled consonant.

**Q6.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Friend  (b) Truely  (c) Honest  (d) Quiet
**Answer: (b) Truely**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "truly" — the 'e' of true is dropped. The others are correctly spelt.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Accomodate  (b) Acommodate  (c) Accommodate  (d) Acomodate
**Answer: (c) Accommodate**
*Solution:* "Accommodate" has double 'c' and double 'm'. All other options drop one of these.

**Q2.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Embarrass  (b) Occurrence  (c) Posession  (d) Necessary
**Answer: (c) Posession**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "possession" with double 's' twice. The other three are correct.

**Q3.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Privilege  (b) Priviledge  (c) Privelege  (d) Privilage
**Answer: (a) Privilege**
*Solution:* "Privilege" has no 'd' and uses two 'i's then 'e'. The others insert a 'd' or wrong vowels.

**Q4.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Maintenance  (b) Existence  (c) Persistance  (d) Resistance
**Answer: (c) Persistance**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "persistence" with -ence. The other words are correctly spelt.

**Q5.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Concious  (b) Conscious  (c) Consious  (d) Conscius
**Answer: (b) Conscious**
*Solution:* "Conscious" has the 'sci' cluster. The others drop the 's' or 'c'.

**Q6.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Rhythm  (b) Tomorow  (c) Bicycle  (d) Vacuum
**Answer: (b) Tomorow**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "tomorrow" with double 'r'. The other three are correct.

## HARD

**Q1.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Liesure  (b) Leisure  (c) Lisure  (d) Leasure
**Answer: (b) Leisure**
*Solution:* "Leisure" is one of the exceptions where 'e' comes before 'i'. The others reorder or drop letters.

**Q2.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Conscientious  (b) Mischievous  (c) Millennium  (d) Entreprenuer
**Answer: (d) Entreprenuer**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "entrepreneur" (-neur, not -nuer). The other three are correctly spelt.

**Q3.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Bureaucracy  (b) Bureacracy  (c) Burocracy  (d) Bureaucrasy
**Answer: (a) Bureaucracy**
*Solution:* "Bureaucracy" keeps the French "bureau" plus -cracy. The others drop the 'a' or use 's'.

**Q4.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Questionnaire  (b) Liaison  (c) Pronounciation  (d) Hierarchy
**Answer: (c) Pronounciation**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "pronunciation" (no 'o' after 'pron'). The other three are correct.

**Q5.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Occassion  (b) Ocassion  (c) Occasion  (d) Occasionn
**Answer: (c) Occasion**
*Solution:* "Occasion" has double 'c' but single 's'. The others wrongly double the 's' or 'n', or drop a 'c'.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN
*(SSC CGL pattern)*

**Q1.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Grammer  (b) Parallel  (c) Harassment  (d) Committee
**Answer: (a) Grammer**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "grammar" with an 'a', not "grammer". The other three are correctly spelt.

**Q2.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Maintainance  (b) Maintenance  (c) Maintainence  (d) Maintnance
**Answer: (b) Maintenance**
*Solution:* "Maintenance" drops the 'ai' of maintain before -tenance. The others keep extra letters.

**Q3.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Foreign  (b) Sovereign  (c) Forfiet  (d) Counterfeit
**Answer: (c) Forfiet**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "forfeit" (e before i here). The other three are correctly spelt.

**Q4.** Select the CORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Wierd  (b) Weird  (c) Werid  (d) Weild
**Answer: (b) Weird**
*Solution:* "Weird" is an exception to the i-before-e rule (e-i order). The others reorder the vowels.

**Q5.** Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
(a) Exaggerate  (b) Exhilarate  (c) Exilerate  (d) Exasperate
**Answer: (c) Exilerate**
*Solution:* The correct spelling is "exhilarate" with an 'h'. Option (c) is a misspelling; (a), (b) and (d) are correct.

### 🔑 Memory Techniques
- Use **mnemonics**: "necessary" = one Collar, two Socks (one c, two s).
- Recall **"i before e except after c"**, and memorise the exceptions (weird, leisure, seize).
- Flag your **personal spelling demons** (accommodate, embarrass) and over-practise them.
- Break long words into **syllables** (en-tre-pre-neur) before judging the spelling.
