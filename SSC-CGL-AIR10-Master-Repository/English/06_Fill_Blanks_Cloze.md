# Fill in the Blanks & Cloze — Solved Question Bank

> Navigation: [English Master Bank](README.md) · [English Grammar Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/English_Grammar_Cheat_Sheet.md)

## Key Rules
- Read the **whole sentence** to judge tone, tense and the logical connector required.
- For vocabulary blanks, match **collocation** (words that naturally go together).
- For grammar blanks, check the needed **part of speech** and agreement.
- In a cloze, use the **context of surrounding sentences**, not just the immediate words.

## EASY

**Q1.** She was so tired that she could ______ keep her eyes open.
(a) hardly  (b) easily  (c) clearly  (d) surely
**Answer: (a) hardly**
*Solution:* Being tired logically means she could "hardly" (barely) keep her eyes open. The other adverbs contradict the sense of difficulty.

**Q2.** He apologised ______ his rude behaviour.
(a) on  (b) for  (c) with  (d) at
**Answer: (b) for**
*Solution:* The verb "apologise" collocates with the preposition "for" before the reason. The other prepositions are incorrect with "apologise".

**Q3.** The sun ______ in the east.
(a) rise  (b) rises  (c) rose  (d) rising
**Answer: (b) rises**
*Solution:* A universal truth takes the simple present, and the singular "sun" requires "rises".

**Q4.** I have not seen him ______ last week.
(a) for  (b) from  (c) since  (d) by
**Answer: (c) since**
*Solution:* "Since" marks a point in past time (last week) with the present perfect "have not seen".

**Q5.** She is fond ______ classical music.
(a) of  (b) for  (c) with  (d) to
**Answer: (a) of**
*Solution:* The adjective "fond" is always followed by the preposition "of".

**Q6.** Please switch ______ the lights before leaving.
(a) on  (b) off  (c) in  (d) up
**Answer: (b) off**
*Solution:* "Before leaving" implies turning the lights "off". "Switch on" would contradict the context.

## MEDIUM

**Q1.** Despite working hard, he ______ to clear the exam.
(a) succeeded  (b) failed  (c) managed  (d) tried
**Answer: (b) failed**
*Solution:* "Despite" signals a contrast: hard work yet a negative outcome, so "failed" fits. "Succeeded" and "managed" would not contrast.

**Q2.** The new policy will ______ a positive impact on the economy.
(a) make  (b) have  (c) do  (d) take
**Answer: (b) have**
*Solution:* The standard collocation is "have an impact on". The other verbs do not pair correctly with "impact".

**Q3.** He is not only intelligent ______ also hardworking.
(a) and  (b) but  (c) or  (d) yet
**Answer: (b) but**
*Solution:* The correlative pair is "not only...but also". The other conjunctions break the structure.

**Q4.** The medicine should be taken ______ meals.
(a) after  (b) afterwards  (c) later  (d) lately
**Answer: (a) after**
*Solution:* A preposition is needed before "meals"; "after" fits both grammar and sense. The other words are adverbs.

**Q5.** Her explanation was so ______ that everyone understood at once.
(a) confusing  (b) lucid  (c) vague  (d) complicated
**Answer: (b) lucid**
*Solution:* If everyone understood at once, the explanation was clear, i.e. "lucid". The other adjectives mean the opposite.

**Q6.** We must ______ by the rules of the game.
(a) abide  (b) follow  (c) keep  (d) stay
**Answer: (a) abide**
*Solution:* The fixed phrase is "abide by" meaning to obey. "Follow" would not take "by".

## HARD

**Q1.** The minister's speech was met with ______ applause from the audience.
(a) reluctant  (b) thunderous  (c) silent  (d) faint
**Answer: (b) thunderous**
*Solution:* "Applause" collocates strongly with "thunderous" to mean very loud approval. The other adjectives weaken or contradict the sense of enthusiastic applause.

**Q2.** His arguments were so ______ that no one could refute them.
(a) weak  (b) flimsy  (c) cogent  (d) vague
**Answer: (c) cogent**
*Solution:* If no one could refute them, the arguments were "cogent" (clear and convincing). The other words suggest weak arguments.

**Q3.** The detective found a ______ clue that solved the entire case.
(a) trivial  (b) crucial  (c) minor  (d) useless
**Answer: (b) crucial**
*Solution:* A clue that solved the whole case must be "crucial" (decisive). The other options downplay its importance.

**Q4.** She remained ______ in the face of severe criticism.
(a) composed  (b) agitated  (c) furious  (d) nervous
**Answer: (a) composed**
*Solution:* "Remained ______ in the face of criticism" implies staying calm, i.e. "composed". The others denote loss of calm.

**Q5.** The two nations finally decided to ______ their long-standing dispute.
(a) aggravate  (b) prolong  (c) resolve  (d) ignore
**Answer: (c) resolve**
*Solution:* "Finally decided to" suggests ending the dispute, so "resolve" fits. The other verbs would worsen or continue it.

## Cloze Passage

Read the passage and choose the best option for each numbered blank.

> Reading is one of the best habits a person can (1)______. It not only entertains us (2)______ also broadens our knowledge. A regular reader gradually (3)______ a rich vocabulary and a sharper imagination. Moreover, books offer companionship (4)______ we feel lonely. Therefore, we should (5)______ time for reading every day.

**(1)**
(a) develop  (b) destroy  (c) waste  (d) avoid
**Answer: (a) develop**
*Solution:* A habit is something one "develops". The other verbs contradict the positive tone of the passage.

**(2)**
(a) and  (b) but  (c) or  (d) so
**Answer: (b) but**
*Solution:* The structure "not only...but also" requires "but" in the second blank.

**(3)**
(a) loses  (b) acquires  (c) forgets  (d) ignores
**Answer: (b) acquires**
*Solution:* A regular reader gains, i.e. "acquires", vocabulary. The other verbs imply loss.

**(4)**
(a) when  (b) although  (c) unless  (d) because
**Answer: (a) when**
*Solution:* "When we feel lonely" gives the time at which books offer companionship. The other conjunctions do not fit the sense.

**(5)**
(a) waste  (b) kill  (c) set aside  (d) lose
**Answer: (c) set aside**
*Solution:* We should reserve, i.e. "set aside", time for reading. The other phrases mean wasting time.

## SSC PYQ PATTERN
*(SSC CGL pattern)*

**Q1.** Select the most appropriate word to fill in the blank. The brave soldier was awarded a medal for his ______.
(a) cowardice  (b) gallantry  (c) timidity  (d) fear
**Answer: (b) gallantry**
*Solution:* A brave soldier is rewarded for "gallantry" (bravery). The other options denote the opposite.

**Q2.** Select the most appropriate word to fill in the blank. The scientist's discovery had a profound ______ on modern medicine.
(a) affect  (b) effect  (c) defect  (d) infect
**Answer: (b) effect**
*Solution:* As a noun meaning influence, "effect" is correct (with the collocation "effect on"). "Affect" is a verb.

**Q3.** Select the most appropriate word to fill in the blank. He spoke with such ______ that the whole hall fell silent.
(a) hesitation  (b) authority  (c) confusion  (d) weakness
**Answer: (b) authority**
*Solution:* Speaking "with authority" commands attention and silence. The other options would not silence a hall.

**Q4.** Select the most appropriate word to fill in the blank. We should always ______ to our elders.
(a) listen  (b) hear  (c) obey  (d) defy
**Answer: (c) obey**
*Solution:* The expected value is respect, so "obey" fits the sense and the structure best. "Defy" is the opposite.

**Q5.** Select the most appropriate word to fill in the blank. The bridge ______ down due to the heavy floods.
(a) broke  (b) breaks  (c) breaking  (d) broken
**Answer: (a) broke**
*Solution:* A completed past event ("due to the heavy floods") requires the simple past "broke".

### 🔑 Memory Techniques
- Learn high-frequency **collocations** (have an impact, abide by, fond of) as fixed units.
- In cloze tests, **read the full passage once** before filling any blank.
- Use **signal words** (despite, not only, therefore) to predict the logical answer.
- Distinguish confusable pairs like **affect (verb) / effect (noun)** with a quick test sentence.
