# Current Affairs — Master Bank

> Quick links: [GK Revision Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/GK_Revision_Cheat_Sheet.md) · [Repository Home](../README.md)

This folder is the **framework + stable-facts** layer for SSC CGL Current Affairs. SSC's General Awareness section blends *static GK* with *current affairs of roughly the last 8–12 months* before the exam. Because the "current" part changes every single month, this repo is built to be **category-organised and self-updatable** rather than a frozen snapshot that goes stale.

> ### ⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING — READ THIS FIRST
> **Current affairs change. This repo deliberately does NOT fabricate volatile monthly specifics** (e.g. "who won X award last month", "who was appointed Y this week"), because a *wrong* current-affairs fact is worse than no fact at all.
>
> - **Stable/durable facts** (Chandrayaan-3 landed near the Moon's south pole in Aug 2023; HDR is published by UNDP; PM-KISAN gives ₹6000/yr; UN HQ is in New York) are stated directly — memorise these.
> - **Volatile monthly facts** are written as **study prompts** with a `*Verify latest*` / `*(check latest)*` flag. You must fill these from a live source.
> - **Always cross-check before the exam against a live source:**
>   - **PIB** (Press Information Bureau) press releases — [pib.gov.in](https://pib.gov.in)
>   - A daily newspaper (The Hindu / Indian Express editorial + national pages)
>   - A trusted **monthly Current Affairs PDF** (and a one-shot revision PDF in the last month)
>
> Treat the MCQ bank and one-liner sheet as a **revision skeleton**: the stable facts are exam-ready as written; the flagged slots are *your* homework.

## How this folder is structured

| 1 | [SSC_CGL_Current_Affairs_MCQ_Master_Bank.md](SSC_CGL_Current_Affairs_MCQ_Master_Bank.md) | MCQs organised **by category** (Appointments, Awards, Sports, Schemes, Economy, Sci-Tech, Space, Defence, Environment, IR, Reports & Indices, Books, Summits) | Practise category-by-category; includes a **monthly template** to append Jan-2024 → present entries yourself |

## Why "by category" instead of "by month"

- Monthly arrangement rots the moment a month passes; **categories are permanent**.
- SSC repeats *types* of questions (organisation → HQ/head, report → publisher, scheme → ministry/amount). Mastering the **pattern** lets you answer even an unseen new item.
- You can drop a new fact into the correct category in seconds using the monthly template.

## Suggested study loop

1. Read the **One-Liner High-Yield block** until it is automatic.
2. Work through the **MCQ bank** one category at a time.
3. Every week, open PIB / your monthly PDF and **fill the `*Verify latest*` slots** using the template.
4. In the final 30 days, revise only the **One-Liner sheet** + your filled-in current slots.
