## About this set
A General Awareness MCQ bank built from the **Sports pages** and **Important Days** featured in Indian newspapers over **January 2025 → June 2026**. Designed for SSC CGL / banking / general competitive prep.

**What makes it different:** *every option — right AND wrong — has a one-line explanation.* So even when you pick the wrong answer, you learn what that wrong option actually was. This is the fastest way to absorb a whole cluster of related facts from one question.

### ⚠️ Please read before trusting any answer
- This was prepared by an AI whose knowledge goes up to **early 2026**, and it **cannot read live newspapers**.
- Any question marked `*Verify latest*` is a **volatile result or a fact after the cutoff** (e.g. IPL 2026, a yearly day-theme). The stem is exam-realistic, but **confirm the actual answer from a current source** (PIB / The Hindu / a monthly Current-Affairs PDF) before memorising it.
- Sports scorelines and winners change fast — treat flagged ones with care. Dates of important days and what they *commemorate* are **stable** and safe to learn.

### How to use
1. Cover the answer, read the stem, pick an option.
2. Then read **all four** explanations — the wrong ones teach you 3 extra facts each.
3. Re-test weekly; focus on the `*Verify latest*` ones only after you confirm them.

## National Important Days of India (Jan–Jun)

**Q51.** India celebrates Republic Day on 26 January every year. What does this day primarily commemorate?
(a) The day India's Constitution came into effect (1950)  (b) The day India gained independence from British rule  (c) The day the Constituent Assembly first met  (d) The day Mahatma Gandhi was assassinated

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) The day India's Constitution came into effect (1950)** — Correct — the Constitution of India came into force on 26 January 1950, replacing the Government of India Act 1935; the date was chosen to honour the 1930 Purna Swaraj declaration.
- ✖️ **(b) The day India gained independence from British rule** — That is Independence Day, 15 August 1947 — a different national day.
- ✖️ **(c) The day the Constituent Assembly first met** — The Constituent Assembly first met on 9 December 1946; the Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, not enforced then.
- ✖️ **(d) The day Mahatma Gandhi was assassinated** — Gandhi was assassinated on 30 January 1948, observed as Martyrs' Day (Shaheed Diwas), not Republic Day.

> 📌 26 Nov 1949 = adopted; 26 Jan 1950 = came into force. The 2026 Republic Day will be the 77th. Chief guest each year is a frequently-asked GK point.

- ✅ **(a) 10 December** — Correct — The UN General Assembly adopted the Universal Declaration of Human Rights on 10 December 1948; the day has been observed since.
- ✖️ **(b) 24 October** — 24 October is United Nations Day (UN Charter took effect in 1945), not Human Rights Day.
- ✖️ **(c) 1 December** — 1 December is World AIDS Day, not Human Rights Day.
- ✖️ **(d) 5 December** — 5 December is International Volunteer Day / World Soil Day, not Human Rights Day.

> 📌 Anchor: UDHR adopted 10 Dec 1948. 2023 marked the 75th anniversary of the UDHR.

**Q84.** The International Day of Peace (World Peace Day) is observed by the United Nations on which date?
(a) 21 September  (b) 16 September  (c) 24 October  (d) 2 October

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) 21 September** — Correct — The International Day of Peace is observed on 21 September, established by the UN in 1981.
- ✖️ **(b) 16 September** — 16 September is World Ozone Day, not the Day of Peace.
- ✖️ **(c) 24 October** — 24 October is United Nations Day, a separate observance.
- ✖️ **(d) 2 October** — 2 October is the International Day of Non-Violence (Gandhi Jayanti), not the Day of Peace.

> 📌 2 October (Gandhi's birthday) = International Day of Non-Violence; 21 September = International Day of Peace.

**Q85.** World Tourism Day, promoted by the UN World Tourism Organization (UN Tourism), is celebrated on which date?

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) 27 September** — Correct — World Tourism Day is observed on 27 September, marking the anniversary of the adoption of the UNWTO Statutes (1970).
- ✖️ **(b) 21 September** — 21 September is the International Day of Peace, not World Tourism Day.
- ✖️ **(c) 16 September** — 16 September is World Ozone Day, not World Tourism Day.
- ✖️ **(d) 5 October** — 5 October is World Teachers' Day, not World Tourism Day.

> 📌 27 Sep: anniversary of UNWTO Statutes. The organisation now brands itself 'UN Tourism'.

**Q86.** International Day of the Girl Child is observed by the United Nations on which date?
(a) 11 October  (b) 24 January  (c) 8 March  (d) 1 October

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) 11 October** — Correct — The UN declared 11 October as International Day of the Girl Child in 2011 (first observed 2012).
- ✖️ **(b) 24 January** — 24 January is India's National Girl Child Day, not the international observance.
- ✖️ **(c) 8 March** — 8 March is International Women's Day, a separate observance.
- ✖️ **(d) 1 October** — 1 October is International Day of Older Persons, not the Girl Child.

> 📌 Distinguish 11 Oct (UN, International Day of Girl Child) from 24 Jan (India, National Girl Child Day).

**Q87.** World Food Day, observed to mark the founding of the FAO, falls on which date?
(a) 16 October  (b) 24 October  (c) 10 October  (d) 31 October

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) 16 October** — Correct — World Food Day is observed on 16 October, marking the founding of the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) in 1945.
- ✖️ **(b) 24 October** — 24 October is United Nations Day, not World Food Day.
- ✖️ **(c) 10 October** — 10 October is World Mental Health Day, not World Food Day.

**Q88.** World AIDS Day is observed globally on which date each year?
(a) 1 December  (b) 10 December  (c) 7 April  (d) 14 November

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) 1 December** — Correct — World AIDS Day has been observed on 1 December since 1988, the first-ever global health day.
- ✖️ **(b) 10 December** — 10 December is Human Rights Day, not World AIDS Day.
- ✖️ **(c) 7 April** — 7 April is World Health Day (WHO's founding), not World AIDS Day.
- ✖️ **(d) 14 November** — 14 November is World Diabetes Day (and Children's Day in India), not World AIDS Day.

> 📌 1 Dec — coordinated by UNAIDS; the red ribbon is its symbol.

**Q89.** Universal Children's Day, the UN-designated international observance, is celebrated on which date (distinct from India's Children's Day on 14 November)?
(a) 20 November  (b) 14 November  (c) 1 June  (d) 11 October

**Answer: (a)**

*Why each option:*
- ✅ **(a) 20 November** — Correct — Universal Children's Day is 20 November, marking the adoption of the Declaration (1959) and Convention (1989) on the Rights of the Child.
- ✖️ **(b) 14 November** — 14 November is India's national Children's Day (Nehru's birthday), not the UN's Universal Children's Day.
- ✖️ **(c) 1 June** — 1 June is International Children's Day observed in some countries, but the UN's Universal Children's Day is 20 November.
- ✖️ **(d) 11 October** — 11 October is International Day of the Girl Child, not Universal Children's Day.

> 📌 20 Nov: Convention on the Rights of the Child adopted 1989. India uniquely observes Children's Day on 14 Nov.
