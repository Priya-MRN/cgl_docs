Current affairs in this file are arranged **by category** so they never go stale by date. To add a real month (Jan 2024 → the present month), copy the block below into a personal notes file or append it to the right category, fill it from a live source, then convert each line into an MCQ here.

# 5. Economy

**Q1.** Which is the rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks?
(a) Reverse Repo Rate  (b) Repo Rate  (c) Bank Rate  (d) SLR
**Answer: (b)**
*Trick:* **Repo = RBI lends to banks**; Reverse Repo = banks park with RBI.

**Q2.** CRR (Cash Reserve Ratio) refers to the portion of deposits that banks must keep —
(a) In government securities  (b) As cash reserves with the RBI  (c) As gold  (d) As foreign currency

*Trick:* **CRR → cash with RBI** (earns no interest); **SLR → in liquid/govt securities**.

**Q3.** Monetary policy in India (repo rate) is decided by the —
(a) Finance Ministry  (b) NITI Aayog  (c) Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the RBI  (d) SEBI

**Q4.** The financial year in India runs from —
(a) January to December  (b) April to March  (c) July to June  (d) October to September

*Trick:* Indian FY → **1 April to 31 March**.

**Q5.** GST in India is a —
(a) Direct tax  (b) Indirect tax  (c) Wealth tax  (d) Corporate tax only

**Q6.** The Union Budget is presented in Parliament under which Article of the Constitution (Annual Financial Statement)?
