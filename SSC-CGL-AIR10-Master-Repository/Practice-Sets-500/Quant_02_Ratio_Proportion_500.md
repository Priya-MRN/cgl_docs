## Key Formulas
- Combine `a:b` and `b:c` → make B common (LCM), giving full `a:b:c`
- Divide amount in ratio a:b:c → one part = total/(a+b+c); share = part × ratio-term
- **Mean proportional** of a, b = `√(ab)`
- **Third proportional** to a, b = `b²/a`  ·  **Fourth proportional** to a, b, c = `bc/a`
- **Componendo–dividendo:** if a:b given, `(a+b)/(a−b)` computed directly
- **Duplicate ratio** = a²:b²  ·  **Sub-duplicate** = √a:√b
- **Partnership:** profit share ∝ **capital × time** (NOT capital alone)
- **Alligation:** cheap : dear = (dear − mean) : (mean − cheap)
- **Income–expenditure:** Income−Expenditure = Savings; solve the two linear equations
