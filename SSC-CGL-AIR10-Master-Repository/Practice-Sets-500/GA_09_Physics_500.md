**Q1. (a) newton** *[M]* — Force is measured in newtons (N), where 1 N = 1 kg·m/s².

**Q3. (c) watt** *[M]* — Power = energy/time; 1 W = 1 J/s.
**Q4. (d) pascal** *[M]* — Pressure = force/area; 1 Pa = 1 N/m².

**Q6. (b) 7** *[M]* — The SI has seven base units (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd).
**Q7. (c) base quantity** *[M]* — The ampere is a base SI unit of electric current.
**Q8. (d) Temperature** *[M]* — The kelvin (K) is the SI unit of thermodynamic temperature.
**Q9. (a) mole** *[M]* — The mole (mol) is the SI unit of amount of substance.
**Q10. (b) candela** *[M]* — The candela (cd) is the SI unit of luminous intensity.
**Q11. (c) Displacement** *[M]* — Displacement has both magnitude and direction, hence a vector.
**Q12. (d) Temperature** *[M]* — Temperature is a scalar (magnitude only).
**Q13. (a) [MLT⁻²]** *[VH]* — F = ma → mass × acceleration = [M][LT⁻²].
**Q14. (b) [ML²T⁻²]** *[VH]* — Work = force × distance = [MLT⁻²][L].
**Q15. (c) [ML²T⁻³]** *[VH]* — Power = work/time = [ML²T⁻²]/[T].
**Q16. (d) [ML⁻¹T⁻²]** *[VH]* — Pressure = force/area = [MLT⁻²]/[L²].
