# Syllogism — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, Reasoning, Tier-I/II Level)

> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard (3-statement & either-or / possibility cases).
> **Every verdict is machine-verified** by a brute-force set-model checker — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50.

## How to answer
- Treat the statements as **definitely true**, even if they contradict common sense.
- A conclusion **follows** only if it must be true in EVERY situation consistent with the statements.
- If a conclusion is merely *possible* (true in some situations but not all), it does **not** follow.
- **Either-or:** when two conclusions cannot both be false (they are complementary and one must hold), the answer is *Either I or II follows*.

## Standard options legend
- (a) Only conclusion I follows
- (b) Only conclusion II follows
- (c) Both conclusions I and II follow
- (d) Neither conclusion I nor conclusion II follows
- (e) Either conclusion I or conclusion II follows  *(appears only when applicable)*
