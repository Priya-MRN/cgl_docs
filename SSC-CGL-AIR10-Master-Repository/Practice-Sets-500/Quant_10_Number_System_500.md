> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard (trap) — CGL Tier-I/II standard, modelled on actual PYQ patterns (2018–2025).
> **Every numeric answer is computed and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake values.

## Key Formulas
- **Unit digit of aⁿ**: depends only on the unit digit of a; powers repeat with period ≤ 4 (cyclicity).
- **Trailing zeros of n!** = highest power of 5 in n! = [n/5] + [n/25] + [n/125] + …
- **Highest power of prime p in n!** (Legendre) = [n/p] + [n/p²] + [n/p³] + …
- **Divisibility by 9 / 3**: digit sum divisible by 9 / 3.  **By 11**: (odd-place sum − even-place sum) divisible by 11.
- **Dividend** = divisor × quotient + remainder.
- **Least number leaving remainder r with several divisors** = LCM(divisors) + r.
- **Sum of first n naturals** = n(n+1)/2 · **squares** = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 · **cubes** = [n(n+1)/2]².
- **Number of divisors** of N = p^a·q^b… is (a+1)(b+1)… ; **sum of divisors** = Π (pᵃ⁺¹−1)/(p−1).
- **Count divisible by a or b in 1..N** = [N/a] + [N/b] − [N/LCM(a,b)] (inclusion–exclusion).
- **Perfect square**: every prime exponent in the factorization must be even.
