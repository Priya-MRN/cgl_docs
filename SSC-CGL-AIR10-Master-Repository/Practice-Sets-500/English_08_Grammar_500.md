> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first (Q1–Q125 Subject-Verb Agreement, Q126–Q250 Prepositions, Q251–Q375 Articles, Q376–Q500 Tenses); the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard — CGL Tier-I/Tier-II grammar repeated across PYQs 2018–2025.
> **Every item is hand-authored and rule-checked:** exactly ONE option is grammatically correct; the other three are common errors students actually make.
> **Each section tests one core area** — agreement of verb with subject, idiomatic prepositions, use/omission of articles, and the twelve tenses with conditionals & inversions.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50 — read the blank and the cue words (each/neither, since/for, vowel SOUND, time markers) before marking.
