# Statement & Conclusion / Assumption / Inference / Cause-Effect / Course of Action — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, Reasoning, Tier-I/II Level)

> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard.
> **Mix:** logical-deduction *Statement & Conclusion* items are machine-verified by a brute-force set-model checker (the keyed option is provably correct); the *Assumption / Inference / Cause-Effect / Course-of-Action* items are authored to conventional SSC logic with exactly one defensible answer.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50.

## How to answer (by sub-type)
- **Statement & Conclusion (logical):** treat the statements as definitely true; a conclusion *follows* only if it must be true in EVERY situation consistent with the statements. A merely *possible* conclusion does not follow. *Either-or* applies when two complementary conclusions cannot both be false.
- **Statement & Assumption:** an assumption is *implicit* only if it is necessarily taken for granted for the statement to hold. Do not bring in extra or extreme suppositions.
- **Statement & Inference:** decide what can be *definitely* concluded — *Definitely true*, *Definitely false*, *Cannot be determined*, or *Probably true but not certain*.
- **Course of Action:** an action *follows* only if it is a practical, relevant step that directly addresses the problem; extreme or irrelevant steps do not follow.
- **Cause & Effect:** identify which event brought about the other, or whether both share a common cause / are independent.

## Standard option legends
- *Conclusion items:* (a) Only I follows · (b) Only II follows · (c) Both follow · (d) Neither follows · (e) Either I or II follows *(only when applicable)*
- *Assumption items:* (a) Only I implicit · (b) Only II implicit · (c) Both implicit · (d) Neither implicit
- *Inference items:* (a) Definitely true · (b) Definitely false · (c) Cannot be determined · (d) Probably true but not certain
- *Course-of-action items:* (a) Only I follows · (b) Only II follows · (c) Both follow · (d) Neither follows
- *Cause-effect items:* (a) I is cause, II effect · (b) II is cause, I effect · (c) independent causes · (d) effects of a common cause
