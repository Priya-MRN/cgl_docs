> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium - ~60% Hard - ~20% Very-Hard (long paths / many turns) - CGL standard.
> **Every path is simulated on a coordinate grid and machine-verified** - the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is -0.50; distractors are real common-mistake values (wrong direction, off distance, ignored a leg).

## How direction sense works (quick reference)
- Set up a coordinate grid: +x = East, -x = West, +y = North, -y = South.
- **Shortest distance** between start and end = straight line = sqrt(net-x^2 + net-y^2); clean answers use Pythagorean triples (3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, ...).
- **Final direction** from start: sign of net-x gives East/West, sign of net-y gives North/South; both nonzero -> a diagonal (NE/NW/SE/SW).
- **Turns:** facing North and turning right -> East -> South -> West (clockwise); left is anticlockwise; 'about turn' is 180 degrees.
- **Shadow:** the sun in the East (morning) casts shadows to the West; sun in the West (evening) casts shadows to the East.
