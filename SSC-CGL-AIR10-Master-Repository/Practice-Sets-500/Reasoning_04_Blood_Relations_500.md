# Blood Relations — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, Reasoning / Tier-I & II Level)

> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard (longer chains, in-laws, cross-generation).
> **Every relationship is DERIVED from a family graph by a resolver and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50; distractors are real common-mistake relations (gender swap, wrong generation, in-law confusion).

## How to solve blood-relation questions
- Draw a small family tree: use ▲/△ for males, ●/○ for females; a horizontal line for marriage, a vertical line for parent→child.
- Track **gender** of every person — many distractors only differ by gender (uncle vs aunt).
- Track **generation** — grandfather vs father vs uncle differ by one generation.
- For **in-laws**, go through the spouse: wife's brother = brother-in-law, son's wife = daughter-in-law.
- **Cousin** = child of your uncle/aunt (your parent's sibling's child).

## Coded blood-relation legend (used in coded questions)
- `A + B` → A is the **father** of B
- `A - B` → A is the **mother** of B
- `A * B` → A is the **brother** of B
- `A / B` → A is the **sister** of B
- `A = B` → A is the **husband** of B
- Decode left-to-right, build the family tree, then read off the asked relation.
