# Sentence Improvement — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, PYQ Pattern)

> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first (Q1–Q500); the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end. Each item asks you to substitute the **[bracketed]** part of the sentence.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard — modelled on CGL Tier-I/Tier-II Sentence-Improvement PYQs 2018–2025.
> **One correct improvement per item:** exactly ONE option fixes the bracketed part; the other choices are plausible but grammatically wrong. Option **(d) is always "No improvement"** and is the right answer only when the sentence is already correct (~8% of items).
> **Error types covered:** subject-verb agreement, tense, prepositions, articles, parallelism, redundancy/pleonasm, degrees of comparison, conditionals, modifiers, idiomatic usage.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50 — read the bracketed part and the whole sentence before marking.
