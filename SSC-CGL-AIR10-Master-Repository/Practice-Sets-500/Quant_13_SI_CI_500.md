## Key Formulas
- Simple Interest: `SI = P*R*T/100`  ·  `Amount = P + SI = P(1 + RT/100)`
- Compound Amount: `A = P(1 + R/100)^n`  ·  `CI = A − P`
- A sum becomes k times itself at SI: `R*T/100 = (k − 1)`
- CI − SI for 2 years = **P(R/100)²**
- CI − SI for 3 years = **P(R/100)² × (3 + R/100)**
- Half-yearly: rate → R/2, periods → 2n  ·  Quarterly: rate → R/4, periods → 4n
- Equal annual installments (SI): `x*(n + R/100·n(n−1)/2) = L(1 + RnT/100)`
- Equal annual installments (CI): `L = Σ x/(1 + R/100)^k`, k = 1..n
- Population / depreciation as CI: grow `P(1 + R/100)^n`, decline `P(1 − R/100)^n`
