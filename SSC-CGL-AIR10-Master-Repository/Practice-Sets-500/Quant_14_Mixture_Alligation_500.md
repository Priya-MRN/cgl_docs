> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** Medium / Hard / Very-Hard (trap) — CGL Tier-II standard.
> **Every numeric answer is computed and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake values.

## Key Formulas
- **Alligation rule:** cheaper : dearer = (dearer − mean) : (mean − cheaper)
- Mean price = (q₁·p₁ + q₂·p₂) / (q₁ + q₂)
- **Replacement:** after n operations of removing y from x and refilling, pure left = **x(1 − y/x)ⁿ**
- Ratio milk:water after replacement = (1−y/x)ⁿ : [1 − (1−y/x)ⁿ]
- Add water to dilute: acid (or milk) amount is constant; new total = amount / target-fraction
- Dishonest seller at cost price for x% gain → water : milk = **x : 100**
- Mixing two ratios a₁:b₁ and a₂:b₂ in ratio k₁:k₂ → combine milk & water parts, then reduce
