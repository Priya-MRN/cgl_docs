> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard (multi-operation) — CGL standard.
> **Every code is generated by an exact rule and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is -0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake codes (off-by-one shift, reversed, wrong operation).

## How coding works (quick reference)
- **Letter shift:** each letter moves a fixed number of places (A=1...Z=26, wrap around Z->A).
- **Opposite letter:** position p -> 27-p (A<->Z, B<->Y, M<->N).
- **Positional value:** A=1, B=2, ..., Z=26; a word may be coded as the SUM or PRODUCT of these.
- **Number coding:** reverse the digits, or apply a fixed arithmetic operation.
- **Substitution:** real words are replaced by other (code) words; follow the chain.
- **Symbol coding:** each letter maps to a fixed symbol.
- **Mixed / multi-step (Very-Hard):** combine two rules, e.g. reverse then shift, or letter+position.
