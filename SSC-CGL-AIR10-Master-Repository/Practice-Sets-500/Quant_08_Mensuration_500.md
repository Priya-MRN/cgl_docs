> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard (trap) — CGL Tier-II standard.
> **Every numeric answer is computed and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Where stated, **π = 22/7** and radii are chosen so areas/volumes are exact.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake values.

## Key Formulas
- Square: diagonal = a√2 · area from diagonal = d²/2
- Rectangle: diagonal = √(l²+b²) · area = l×b · perimeter = 2(l+b)
- Triangle (Heron): s = (a+b+c)/2, area = √(s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c))
- Equilateral side a: area = (√3/4)a² · height = (√3/2)a
- Circle: area = πr² · circumference = 2πr · ring = π(R²−r²)
- Sector (angle θ): area = (θ/360)πr² · arc = (θ/360)·2πr
- Rhombus: area = ½d₁d₂ · side = √((d₁/2)²+(d₂/2)²)
- Trapezium: area = ½(a+b)h
- Cube: V = a³ · TSA = 6a² · space diagonal = a√3
- Cuboid: V = lbh · TSA = 2(lb+bh+hl) · diagonal = √(l²+b²+h²)
- Cylinder: V = πr²h · CSA = 2πrh · TSA = 2πr(r+h)
- Cone: V = ⅓πr²h · slant l = √(r²+h²) · CSA = πrl
- Sphere: V = 4/3 πr³ · SA = 4πr² | Hemisphere: V = 2/3 πr³ · TSA = 3πr²
- Recasting: total VOLUME is conserved (π cancels) — equate volumes
