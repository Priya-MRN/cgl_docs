> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** Medium / Hard / Very-Hard (trap) — CGL Tier-II standard.
> **Every numeric answer is computed and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake values.

## Key Formulas
- `Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP × 100`  ·  `Loss% = (CP − SP)/CP × 100`  (always on **CP**)
- `SP = CP × (100 ± profit/loss%)/100`  ·  `CP = SP × 100/(100 ± %)`
- Marked price & discount: `SP = MP × (100 − discount%)/100`
- Two successive discounts d₁, d₂ → single equiv **d₁ + d₂ − d₁d₂/100 %**
- For a target profit p% after discount d%: markup = **[(100+p)/(100−d) − 1] × 100 %**
- Same SP, +x% on one & −x% on other → always **net loss = (x/10)² %**
- False weight (sell at CP): `gain% = error/(true value given) × 100`
- CP of m articles = SP of n articles → `gain% = (m − n)/n × 100`
- Profit = Loss for two SPs → `CP = (SP₁ + SP₂)/2`
- Buy x get y free → effective discount = **y/(x+y) × 100 %**
