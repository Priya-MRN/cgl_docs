# Percentage — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, Hard / Tier-II Level)

> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** Medium / Hard / Very-Hard (trap) — CGL Tier-II standard.
> **Every numeric answer is computed and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake values.

## Key Formulas
- `x% of y = y/100 × x`  ·  `x% of y = y% of x`
- A is x% more than B → B is **x/(100+x)×100 %** less than A
- A is x% less than B → B is **x/(100−x)×100 %** more than A
- Successive a%, b% → net **a + b + ab/100 %** (use −b for a decrease)
- Price ↑x% → consumption cut to keep spend same = **x/(100+x)×100 %**
- Population after n yrs at r% = **P(1+r/100)ⁿ**
- Failed in at least one = a + b − both ; Passed both = 100 − that
