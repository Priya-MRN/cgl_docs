## Key Formulas / Quick Reference
- Standard angles: sin30°=½, sin45°=1/√2, sin60°=√3/2, sin90°=1, sin0°=0
- cos θ = sin(90°−θ); tan30°=1/√3, tan45°=1, tan60°=√3
- Pythagorean: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 · sec²θ − tan²θ = 1 · cosec²θ − cot²θ = 1
- Reciprocals: sinθ·cosecθ = cosθ·secθ = tanθ·cotθ = 1
- Complementary: sin(90°−θ)=cosθ, tan(90°−θ)=cotθ, sec(90°−θ)=cosecθ
- sec θ ± tan θ are reciprocals: (secθ−tanθ)(secθ+tanθ) = 1
- tan θ + cot θ = (sin²θ+cos²θ)/(sinθcosθ) = 1/(sinθcosθ)
- Range: −√(a²+b²) ≤ a·sinθ + b·cosθ ≤ √(a²+b²)
- a·sin²θ + b·cos²θ lies between min(a,b) and max(a,b)
- Heights & Distances: height = distance × tan(elevation); use tan30°=1/√3, tan45°=1, tan60°=√3
- Two points d apart, angles 30° & 60°: h = d·√3/2 (from h = d/(cot30°−cot60°))
