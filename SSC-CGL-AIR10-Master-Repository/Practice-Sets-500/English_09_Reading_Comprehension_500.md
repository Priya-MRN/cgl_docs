# Reading Comprehension — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, PYQ Pattern)

> **Format:** 50 passages each followed by 10 questions (50 × 10 = 500). All passages and questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Question types:** Each passage tests a mix of main idea, inference, vocabulary-in-context, detail, and tone/purpose — exactly the blend SSC CGL Comprehension has used across PYQs 2018–2025.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium · ~60% Hard · ~20% Very-Hard — inference and tone/purpose items are the Very-Hard ones.
> **Every item is answerable strictly from its passage:** exactly ONE option is correct; the other three are plausible but contradicted by or unsupported in the text.
> Negative marking in CGL is −0.50 — read the passage carefully and confirm each answer is supported before marking.

> Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises over time, reducing the purchasing power of money. A moderate, predictable rate of inflation is often seen as a sign of a healthy, growing economy, because it encourages people to spend and invest rather than hoard cash whose value would otherwise stay fixed. > Coral reefs, sometimes called the rainforests of the sea, occupy less than one percent of the ocean floor yet support roughly a quarter of all marine species. Living within the coral's tissue are microscopic algae that, through photosynthesis, supply the coral with food and give it its vivid colours. This partnership is delicate.
