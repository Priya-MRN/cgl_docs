# Number & Alphabet Series — 500 Question Practice Set (SSC CGL, Reasoning / Tier-I & II Level)

> **Format:** All 500 questions appear first; the full **Answer Key & Explanations** is at the very end.
> **Difficulty skew:** ~20% Medium - ~60% Hard - ~20% Very-Hard (2nd-level / interleaved / wrong-term) — CGL standard.
> **Every series is generated by an exact rule and machine-verified** — the keyed option is provably correct.
> Negative marking in CGL is -0.50; the distractors are real common-mistake values (off-by-one, wrong rule, adjacent term).

## How series questions work (quick reference)
- **Arithmetic:** constant difference between consecutive terms.
- **Geometric:** constant ratio (each term = previous x r).
- **2nd-level differences:** the differences themselves form a pattern (AP, or x constant).
- **Squares / cubes +/- k:** terms are n^2 or n^3, possibly shifted by a constant.
- **Interleaved:** two independent series alternate position by position.
- **Mixed operations:** alternate +n and xm, or term = previous x m + k.
- **Fibonacci-type:** each term = sum of the previous two.
- **Alphabet:** letters move by fixed/varying skips (A=1...Z=26); pairs like AZ, BY, CX use opposite letters (positions sum to 27).
- **Wrong-term:** all terms but one fit an exact rule; identify the one that breaks it.
