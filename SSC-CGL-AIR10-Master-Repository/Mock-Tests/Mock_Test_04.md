## Solutions & Explanations

**Reasoning**

- **Q1.** Each term multiplied by 3: 3→9→27→81→**243**.
- **Q2.** Each letter shifts +1: D→E, E→F, L→M, H→I, I→J → DELHI=EFMIJ. So M→N, U→V, M→N, B→C, A→B, I→J → **NVNCBJ**.
- **Q3.** 121, 144, 169 are perfect squares (11², 12², 13²); **150** is not.
- **Q4.** "My father's only son" = Seema's brother; the boy is the son of that brother, so he is Seema's **Nephew**.
- **Q5.** Gaps +3 each: C→F→I→L→**O**.
- **Q6.** Apply substitutions (×→−, ÷→+, −→÷, +→×): 12 − 6 + 3 ÷ 1 × 2. BODMAS: 3÷1 = 3, 3×2 = 6 → 12 − 6 + 6 = **12**.
- **Q7.** B→F (+4), D→H (+4); J→N (+4), L→P (+4) → **NP**.
- **Q8.** Pattern ×2 − 1: 5→9 (×2−1), 9→17, 17→33, 33→65, 65×2−1 = **129**.
- **Q9.** Infant → Child → Youth → Adult → Old = **1, 2, 5, 3, 4**.
- **Q10.** Yesterday Sunday → today Monday. After 100 days: 100 mod 7 = 2 → Monday + 2 = **Wednesday**.
- **Q11.** Position from left = (35 − 20) + 1 = **16th**.
- **Q12.** Pattern n × (n+1): 6 × 7 = 42, so 9 × 10 = **90**.
- **Q13.** Copper, Iron, Zinc are pure metals (elements); **Bronze** is an alloy.
- **Q14.** P is mother of Q; R is daughter of S and sibling of Q; so S is the father, married to P → S is P's **Husband**.
- **Q15.** Letters mapped to alphabet position: B=2, R=18, U=21, S=19, H=8 → **2-18-21-19-8**.
- **Q16.** 16, 36, 64, 100 are even-square numbers (4²,6²,8²,10²); **81** = 9² is odd → odd one out.
- **Q17.** Differences 2, 4, 6, 8, 10: 21 + 10 = **31**.
- **Q18.** East 4, North 3 (left from East), West 4 (left from North): East and West cancel; net = 3 km North → **3 km**.
- **Q19.** "Some flowers are red" does not guarantee any rose is red → conclusion **Invalid**.
- **Q20.** @ gives a²+b²: 5²+2² = 25+4 = **29**.
- **Q21.** Gaps +2, +3, +4, +5: B→D→G→K→**P**.
- **Q22.** Differences −4, −8, −12, −16, −20: 60 − 20 = **40**.
- **Q23.** An author creates a book; a composer creates **music**.
- **Q24.** A 2×2 grid has 4 small + 1 large = **5** squares.
- **Q25.** Each term ×3: 2→6→18→54→**162**.

**Quantitative Aptitude**

- **Q51.** Speed = 200/8 = 25 m/s = 25 × 18/5 = **90 km/h**.
- **Q52.** Average of 7 consecutive = middle (4th) = 20 → numbers 17,18,19,20,21,22,23 → smallest **17**.
- **Q53.** 30% = 90 → number = 300; 50% of 300 = **150**.
- **Q54.** Triples in 16 yrs (SI) → interest = 2 × principal → R = 200/16 = **12.5%**.
- **Q55.** 72 in ratio 5:7 (12 parts) → one part = 6 → smaller = 5×6 = **30**.
- **Q56.** CP 100 → MP 150 → 20% discount → SP = 120 → profit = **20%**.
- **Q57.** Form a³−b³ over a²+ab+b² = a−b = 0.7 − 0.2 = **0.5**.
- **Q58.** 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6 → time = **6 days**.
- **Q59.** CI = 8000(1.1² − 1) = 8000 × 0.21 = **₹1,680**.
- **Q60.** CP of 15 = SP of 12 → SP/CP = 15/12 = 1.25 → profit **25%**.
- **Q61.** Downstream = 40/4 = 10 km/h, upstream = 40/8 = 5 km/h → stream speed = (10−5)/2 = **2.5 km/h**.
- **Q62.** πr² = 616 → r² = 616 × 7/22 = 196 → r = **14 cm**.
- **Q63.** BODMAS: 12÷4 = 3, 3×2 = 6 → 20 − 6 + 6 = **20**.
- **Q64.** SI = 6000 × 5 × 4 / 100 = **₹1,200**.
- **Q65.** Net factor 1.25 × 0.8 = 1.00 → **No change**.
- **Q66.** Ratio 1:2:3 (6 parts) = 180° → one part = 30° → largest = 3×30 = **90°**.
- **Q67.** From −20% to +5% is a 25% jump = ₹100 → 25% of CP = ₹100 → CP = **₹400**.
- **Q68.** 42² = 1764 → √1764 = **42**.
- **Q69.** Other = (LCM × HCF)/given = (60 × 5)/15 = **20**.
- **Q70.** Average speed = 2·80·20/(80+20) = 3200/100 = **32 km/h**.
- **Q71.** cos θ = 12/13 → 5-12-13 right triangle → sin θ = 5/13 → **5/13**.
- **Q72.** Increase = 20/(100−20) × 100 = 20/80 × 100 = **25%**.
- **Q73.** Equal SP with +20%/−20% gives a loss = (20²)/100 = **4% loss**.
- **Q74.** 25% of 800 = 200; 20% of 200 = **40**.
- **Q75.** Side = 64/4 = 16 → area = 16² = **256 cm²**.
