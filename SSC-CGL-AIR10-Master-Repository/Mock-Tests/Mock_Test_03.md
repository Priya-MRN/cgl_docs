## Solutions & Explanations

**Reasoning**

- **Q1.** Differences increase by 1: +1, +2, +3, +4, +5, **+6** → 16 + 6 = **22**.
- **Q2.** RED = 18+5+4 = 27 (position sum). GREEN = 7+18+5+5+14 = **49**.
- **Q3.** January, March and July have 31 days; **June** (option c) has 30 days, so it is the odd one out.
- **Q4.** "Son of the brother of her mother" = son of her maternal uncle = her **cousin**.
- **Q5.** Step −3: Z, W, T, Q, **N**.
- **Q6.** Replace symbols: 6 + 4 ÷ 2 − 3 → 6 + 2 − 3 = **5**. (× means +, − means ÷, + means −.)
- **Q7.** A cup rests on a saucer; a pen is covered by a **cap**.
- **Q8.** Pattern n³ − 1: 2³−1=7, 3³−1=26, 4³−1=63, 5³−1=124, 6³−1 = **215**.
- **Q9.** Infant → Child → Adolescent → Adult → Old = **1, 2, 5, 3, 4**.
- **Q10.** January has 31 days; 31 = 4 weeks + 3 days → 1 Feb is 3 days after Monday = **Thursday**.
- **Q11.** Total = (7 + 14) − 1 = **20**.
- **Q12.** The relation is the square root: √16 = 4, so √81 = **9**.
- **Q13.** 121, 144, 169 are perfect squares (11², 12², 13²); **150** is not.
- **Q14.** P's mother is Q; Q and R are siblings; S is R's son → P and S are **cousins**.
- **Q15.** C=3, A=1, B=2 → 3 + 1 + 2 = **6**.
- **Q16.** Powers of 3: 3, 9, 27, 81, **243**.
- **Q17.** Differences −4, −8, −12, −16, **−20** → 60 − 20 = **40**.
- **Q18.** At 3:00 the hands are 3 hours apart × 30° = **90°**.
- **Q19.** All pens are pencils, and no book is a pen → those pencils (the pens) are not books → "Some pencils are not books" is **Valid**.
- **Q20.** East 10, North 10, West 10: East and West cancel → net 10 km North → **10 km**.
- **Q21.** Gaps +2, +3, +4, +5: B, D, G, K, **P**.
- **Q22.** ⊕ = a²+b²: 6² + 7² = 36 + 49 = **85**.
- **Q23.** Hunger is satisfied by food; thirst is satisfied by **water**.
- **Q24.** A rectangle split into two halves: the 2 small halves + 1 whole = **3** rectangles.
- **Q25.** Fibonacci-style (each = sum of previous two): 8 + 13 = **21**.

**Quantitative Aptitude**

- **Q51.** Relative speed (opposite) = 40 + 32 = 72 km/h = 20 m/s; total length = 200 m → time = 200/20 = **10 s**.
- **Q52.** Sum of 6 = 150; remove 40 → 110 over 5 = **22**.
- **Q53.** 60% = 90 → number = 150; 75% of 150 = **112.5**.
- **Q54.** SI = 1920 = 6000 × 8 × t / 100 → 480t = 1920 → t = **4 years**.
- **Q55.** Men-days = 3 × 8 = 24 → 4 men take 24/4 = **6 days**.
- **Q56.** SP = 720 at 10% loss → CP = 720/0.9 = **₹800**.
- **Q57.** (a³ − b³)/(a − b) = a² + ab + b² with a = 0.6, b = 0.4: 0.36 + 0.24 + 0.16 = **0.76**.
- **Q58.** 1/6 + 1/12 = 2/12 + 1/12 = 3/12 = 1/4 → **4 hours**.
- **Q59.** CI = 4000(1.25² − 1) = 4000(0.5625) = **₹2,250**.
- **Q60.** SP = 450 at 25% gain → CP = 450/1.25 = **₹360**.
- **Q61.** Downstream 16/2 = 8 km/h, upstream 16/4 = 4 km/h → stream = (8 − 4)/2 = **2 km/h**.
- **Q62.** CSA = 2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = **440 cm²**.
- **Q63.** BODMAS: (8−3)=5, 5×5=25, 12÷4=3 → 25 + 3 = **28**.
- **Q64.** Interest for 2 years (5−3) = 2600 − 2400 = 200 → per year 100 → for 3 years 300 → principal = 2400 − 300 = **₹2,100**.
- **Q65.** Area ∝ r²; doubling r multiplies area by 2² = **4**.
- **Q66.** Angle = complement + 30 → x = (90 − x) + 30 → 2x = 120 → x = **60°**.
- **Q67.** CP 100 → MP 150 → 20% discount → SP = 120 → profit **20%**.
- **Q68.** √144 + √169 = 12 + 13 = **25**.
- **Q69.** LCM × HCF = product of numbers = 200 → other = 200/20 = **10**.
- **Q70.** Distance = 5 × 6 = 30 km → at 6 km/h time = 30/6 = **5 hours**.
- **Q71.** cos 0° = 1, sin 90° = 1 → sum = **2**.
- **Q72.** (x + 1/x)² = x² + 1/x² + 2 → 16 = x² + 1/x² + 2 → x² + 1/x² = **14**.
- **Q73.** Sum 200 over 10 parts = 20/part; largest = 5 × 20 = **100**.
- **Q74.** Net factor 1.1 × 1.1 = 1.21 → **21% increase**.
- **Q75.** Area = (√3/4)·6² = (√3/4)·36 = **9√3 cm²**.
