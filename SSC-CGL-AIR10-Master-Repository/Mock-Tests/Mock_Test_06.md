## Solutions & Explanations

**Reasoning**

- **Q1.** Each term doubles: 2→4→8→16→32→**64**.
- **Q2.** Letters mapped to alphabet position: H=8, E=5, A=1, D=4 → **8-5-1-4**.
- **Q3.** 16, 25, 36 are perfect squares; **30** is not.
- **Q4.** "Only sister of my father" = Anil's aunt; her daughter is Anil's **Cousin**.
- **Q5.** Gaps +2, +3, +4, +5, +6: B→D→G→K→P→**V**.
- **Q6.** Apply substitutions (×→+, +→÷, ÷→−, −→×): 6 × 4 + 2 − 3 ÷ 1 → 6 + 4 ÷ 2 × 3 − 1. BODMAS: 4÷2 = 2, 2×3 = 6 → 6 + 6 − 1 = **11**.
- **Q7.** Pattern n³: 2³ = 8, so 4³ = **64**.
- **Q8.** Pattern ×2 + 1: 2→5→11→23→47→**95**.
- **Q9.** Egg → Larva → Cocoon → Butterfly = **2, 3, 1, 4**.
- **Q10.** Day before yesterday = Monday → today = Wednesday → day after tomorrow = **Friday** (Wed + 2).
- **Q11.** Position from right = (45 − 22) + 1 = **24th**.
- **Q12.** Rule n² + n: 3² + 3 = 12, so 5² + 5 = 25 + 5 = **30**.
- **Q13.** Rose, Lotus, Jasmine are flowers; **Oak** is a tree.
- **Q14.** W is husband of Y; Y is X's child (daughter, since she is a mother) → W is X's **Son-in-law**.
- **Q15.** Letters to alphabet position: B=2, L=12, A=1, C=3, K=11 → **2-12-1-3-11**.
- **Q16.** 11, 22, 33, 44 are multiples of 11; **54** is not.
- **Q17.** Pattern ×2 + 2: 1→4→10→22→46→**94**.
- **Q18.** East 9, then right (South) 12 → right triangle → √(9² + 12²) = √225 = **15 km**.
- **Q19.** All cars are vehicles; no vehicle is a tree → no car is a tree → **Valid**.
- **Q20.** Δ gives a × b − a: check 3×2−3 = 3 ✓, 5×4−5 = 15 ✓, 6×2−6 = 6 ✓ → 7 × 3 − 7 = 21 − 7 = **14**.
- **Q21.** Gaps +2, +3, +4, +5, +6: C→E→H→L→Q→**W**.
- **Q22.** Pattern ×2 + 1: 4→9→19→39→79→**159**.
- **Q23.** A ship is commanded by a captain; an aeroplane by a **pilot**.
- **Q24.** A square with both diagonals contains 4 small triangles + 4 larger half-square triangles = **8** triangles.
- **Q25.** Each term doubles: 3→6→12→24→48→**96**.
