## Solutions & Explanations

**Reasoning**

- **Q1.** Rule ×2+1: 4→9→19→39→79→**159** (79×2+1).
- **Q2.** Each letter shifts +1: M→N, A→B, N→O, G→H, O→P → **NBOHP**; same shift on APPLE → **BQQMF**.
- **Q3.** 121=11², 144=12², 169=13² are perfect squares; **150** is not.
- **Q4.** "My mother's only son" = Sita's brother; the boy is the son of that brother, hence Sita's **Nephew**.
- **Q5.** Gap +3 each: C→F→I→L→**O**.
- **Q6.** Apply (+→×, −→+, ×→÷, ÷→−): 12 × 3 + 6 − 2 ÷ 1 = 36 + 6 − 2 = **40**.
- **Q7.** B,D and F,H differ by +4 in starting letter; J,L → next pair **NP**.
- **Q8.** Rule ×2+1: 2→5→11→23→47→**95**.
- **Q9.** Butterfly life cycle: Egg → Caterpillar → Pupa → Butterfly = **2, 1, 4, 3**.
- **Q10.** Yesterday Sunday → today Monday. 100 days = 14×7+2 → 2 days after Monday = **Wednesday**.
- **Q11.** Position from right = (35 − 20) + 1 = **16th**.
- **Q12.** Rule n×(n+1): 6×7 = 42, so 9×10 = **90**.
- **Q13.** Copper, Iron, Gold are pure metals (elements); **Brass** is an alloy.
- **Q14.** P is Q's mother, R is Q's sibling and S's daughter, so S is married to P → S is P's **Husband**.
- **Q15.** R=18,E=5,D=4 sum=27 ✓; B+L+U+E=2+12+21+5=40 ✓; SUN = 19+21+14 = **54**.
- **Q16.** Multiples of 12: 12,24,36,48,60; **50** is not a multiple of 12.
- **Q17.** Differences 2,4,6,8,10: 21+10 = **31**.
- **Q18.** North → 90° CW → East → 90° CW → South → 45° ACW → **South-East**.
- **Q19.** "Some flowers are red" does not guarantee roses are red → **Invalid**.
- **Q20.** East 4, North 3, West 4: East/West cancel, net 3 km North → **3 km**.
- **Q21.** Gaps +2,+3,+4,+5: B→D→G→K→**P**.
- **Q22.** Rule a@b = 2(a+b): 2(3+5)=16, 2(4+6)=20, 2(7+2)=18 → 2(8+5) = **26**.
- **Q23.** An author creates a book; a composer creates **music**.
- **Q24.** 4×4 grid squares = 16+9+4+1 = **30**.
- **Q25.** Cubes: 2³,3³,4³,5³,**6³ = 216**.

**Quantitative Aptitude**

- **Q59.** CI = 20000(1.05² − 1) = 20000 × 0.1025 = **₹2,050**.
- **Q60.** CP of 15 = SP of 12 → SP/CP = 15/12 = 1.25 → profit **25%**.
