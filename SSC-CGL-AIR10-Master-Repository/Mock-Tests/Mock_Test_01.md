## Solutions & Explanations

**Reasoning**

- **Q1.** Each term doubles: 7→14→28→56→**112**.
- **Q2.** Letters mapped to position: C=3, A=1, T=20. So D=4, O=15, G=7 → **4-15-7**.
- **Q3.** 25, 36, 49 are perfect squares; **50** is not.
- **Q4.** "Only daughter of my mother" = the woman herself; she is the man's mother → **Mother**.
- **Q5.** Gaps +2, +3, +4, +5: A→C→F→J→**O**.
- **Q6.** Apply substitutions (+→÷, ÷→−, ×→+): 16 ÷ 4 − 2 × 8 = 4 − 16 = **−12**.
- **Q7.** A→B (+1), Z→Y (−1); so C→D, X→W → **DW**.
- **Q8.** Differences are 3, 5, 7, 9, **11**: 27 + 11 = **38**.
- **Q9.** Seed → Plant → Tree → Flower → Fruit = **1, 2, 5, 4, 3**.
- **Q10.** Day before yesterday = Thursday → today = Saturday → day after tomorrow = **Tuesday**.
- **Q11.** Position from right = (40 − 13) + 1 = **28th**.
- **Q12.** Pattern n × (n+1): 5×6 = 30, so 8×9 = **72**.
- **Q13.** Triangle, Square, Rectangle have straight sides; **Circle** has none.
- **Q14.** A is brother of B; B and C are siblings; C is D's father → A is D's **Uncle**.
- **Q15.** M=1, O=2, N=3, D=4, A=5, Y=6 → MAY = **1-5-6 = 156**.
- **Q16.** 8, 27, 64, 125 are perfect cubes; **100** is not.
- **Q17.** Pattern n²+1: 1+1, 4+1, 9+1, 16+1, 25+1, **36+1 = 37**.
- **Q18.** Each direction rotates 135° anticlockwise. West (270°) − 135° = 135° = **South-East**.
- **Q19.** All pens → books → tables, so all pens are tables → **Valid**.
- **Q20.** North 5, East 3, South 5: the North and South cancel; net displacement = 3 km East → **3 km**.
- **Q21.** Reverse alphabet, step −2: Z, X, V, T, **R**.
- **Q22.** Rule: a # b = 2 × (a + b). Check 2(5+4)=18, 2(7+3)=20, 2(6+5)=22 → 2(8+6) = **28**.
- **Q23.** A doctor works in a hospital; a teacher works in a **school**.
- **Q24.** A triangle with all three medians drawn contains **16** triangles in total (6 smallest plus larger combinations).
- **Q25.** Perfect squares: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, **36**.

**Quantitative Aptitude**

- **Q51.** Speed = 150 m / 10 s = 15 m/s = 15 × 18/5 = **54 km/h**.
- **Q52.** Average of 5 consecutive numbers = middle number = 30 → numbers 28,29,30,31,32 → largest **32**.
- **Q53.** 25% = 75 → number = 300; 40% of 300 = **120**.
- **Q54.** Doubles in 8 yrs (SI) → interest = principal → R = 100/8 = **12.5%**.
- **Q55.** 56 in ratio 3:4 → parts 24 and 32 → larger = **32**.
- **Q56.** CP 100 → MP 140 → 25% discount → SP = 105 → profit = **5%**.
- **Q57.** Form a³+b³ over a²−ab+b² = a+b = 0.5 + 0.3 = **0.2**.
- **Q58.** 1/12 + 1/18 = 3/36 + 2/36 = 5/36 → time = 36/5 = **7.2 days**.
- **Q59.** CI = 10000(1.1² − 1) = 10000 × 0.21 = **₹2,100**.
- **Q60.** CP of 20 = SP of 16 → SP/CP = 20/16 = 1.25 → profit **25%**.
- **Q61.** Downstream 30/3 = 10 km/h, upstream 30/5 = 6 km/h → still water = (10+6)/2 = **8 km/h**.
- **Q62.** πr² = 154 → r² = 154 × 7/22 = 49 → r = **7 cm**.
- **Q63.** BODMAS: 8÷2 = 4, then 4×3 = 12 → 12 + 12 − 4 = **20**.
- **Q64.** SI = 5000 × 8 × 3 / 100 = **₹1,200**.
- **Q65.** Net factor 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 → **4% decrease**.
- **Q66.** Ratio 2:3:4, sum 9 parts = 180° → one part = 20° → largest = 4×20 = **80°**.
- **Q67.** Loss 10% then +₹40 gives 10% gain → ₹40 = 20% of CP → CP = **₹200**.
- **Q68.** 48² = 2304 → √2304 = **48**.
- **Q69.** Other = (LCM × HCF)/given = (48 × 8)/24 = **16**.
- **Q70.** Average speed = 2·60·40/(60+40) = 4800/100 = **48 km/h**.
- **Q71.** tan θ = 3/4 → 3-4-5 right triangle → sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse = **3/5**.
- **Q72.** Reduction = 25/(100+25) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = **20%**.
- **Q73.** Equal SP with +10%/−10% always gives a loss = (10²)/100 = **1% loss**.
- **Q74.** 40% of 500 = 200; 15% of 200 = **30**.
- **Q75.** Side = 48/4 = 12 → area = 12² = **144 cm²**.
