# Revision Notes — Final Phase

> Back to the main repo: [../README.md](../README.md)

This folder holds your end-game revision plans. Unlike the learning notes (which teach concepts), these are **execution plans** — they tell you exactly what to touch, what to drop, and how to spend each remaining day so you walk into the SSC CGL exam at peak readiness.

You are working full-time in IT and started at beginner level. That is fine. The plan below is built around **high-yield prioritisation**: in the final phase you do NOT try to learn everything — you lock in what scores marks and protect your accuracy against the −0.5 negative marking.

## The 4 Plans

| Plan | Use it when | Core idea |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| [Last 30 Days — Revision Plan](Last_30_Days_Revision.md) | ~1 month to exam | Cover full syllabus but prioritise; 2 full mocks/week; kill weak areas |
| [Last 15 Days — Revision Plan](Last_15_Days_Revision.md) | ~2 weeks to exam | Pure high-yield; 1 mock/day + analysis; formula + last-6-months current affairs |
| [Last 7 Days — Revision Plan](Last_7_Days_Revision.md) | Final week | Only highest-probability topics; cheat-sheet recall; 1 mock/day; error log |
| [Last 1 Day — Revision Plan](Last_1_Day_Revision.md) | Day before exam | Formula sheets + one-liners + light recall; NO new topics; exam strategy + calm |

## How to use these as the exam approaches

1. **Pick the plan that matches your remaining time** and follow it day by day. Each plan flows naturally into the next — finish the 30-day plan and you roll straight into the 15-day plan.
2. **Mocks are non-negotiable.** The single biggest score jump in the last 2 months comes from mock analysis, not from new content. Log every wrong answer.
3. **Keep an error log.** A simple notebook/sheet: question type, why you got it wrong (concept gap / silly mistake / time pressure / guess), and the correction. Review it before each mock.
4. **Touch every section every day.** Tier-1 is 60 min for 100 questions across Quant, Reasoning, English, GA. You cannot afford to go cold on any one section.
5. **Respect the negative marking.** Every wrong answer costs you the mark you got plus another 0.5. In the final phase, accuracy beats attempts. The plans repeatedly push you toward a high-accuracy attempt strategy.
