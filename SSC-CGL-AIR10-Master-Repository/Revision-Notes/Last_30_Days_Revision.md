**Your situation:** full-time IT job, beginner base, ~30 days left. You have time to still cover the full syllabus, but you must **prioritise ruthlessly** and convert weak areas into reliable scorers. Target: 3–3.5 focused hours on weekdays (1.5 morning + 1.5–2 evening), and one full 60-min mock + 2 hours of analysis on weekends.

---

## What to Study

- **Full syllabus, but weighted.** Spend ~50% of time on the highest-return topics (listed below), ~30% on consolidating weak areas surfaced by mocks, ~20% keeping strong areas warm.
- **Quant:** Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Profit-Loss-Discount, Time & Work, Time-Speed-Distance, Algebra (identities + linear/quadratic), Geometry (triangles, circles), Mensuration (2D + 3D), Data Interpretation. Number System and Simplification for speed.
- **Reasoning:** Coding-Decoding, Series (number + alphabet), Syllogism, Blood Relations, Direction & Distance, Non-verbal (mirror/paper folding/figure series), Analogy, Classification.
- **English:** Error Spotting, Vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms/one-word/idioms), Cloze test, Reading Comprehension, Sentence Improvement, Spelling, Fill in the blanks.
- **GA:** Polity, Modern History, Geography (India + World physical), Economy basics, Science (Physics/Chemistry/Biology static), and the last 6 months of Current Affairs. GA is high-volume but quick — protect it.

## What to Ignore

- Obscure, low-frequency Quant chapters: heavy Permutation-Combination/Probability, complex Mensuration of frustums, advanced trigonometric proofs. Learn only the standard exam patterns.
- Reasoning: extremely rare puzzle types (complex floor+box double-constraint puzzles) — SSC rarely asks these at Tier-1 weight.
- English: deep grammar theory you will never be tested on. Stick to the rule patterns that appear in PYQs.
- GA: ancient/medieval history minutiae, very old current affairs (>1 year), trivia with near-zero PYQ hit rate.
- Do **not** start any brand-new subject area you have never touched. 30 days is for prioritising, not first-time learning of an entire domain.

## High Probability Topics

- **Quant:** Percentage (and its links to Profit-Loss, SI/CI, DI), Time-Speed-Distance + Time-Work, Ratio/Proportion/Partnership/Ages, Geometry + Mensuration, DI (tables, bar, pie). These cover the bulk of the 25 quant questions.
- **Reasoning:** Coding-Decoding, Series, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Direction, Analogy/Classification, Non-verbal — together ~70% of the 25 reasoning questions.
- **English:** Vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms/idioms/one-word — huge cluster), Error Spotting, Cloze, Sentence Improvement, RC.
- **GA:** Polity (Constitution, Articles, Schedules, Amendments), Modern History (freedom struggle), Static Geography, Economy (budget/banking terms), Science static, plus current affairs.

## Expected Question Types

- **Quant:** direct application sums (percentage change, successive discount, work-time, relative speed, boats-streams), 1 DI set (4–5 Qs), geometry/mensuration numericals, algebra identity-based.
- **Reasoning:** "find the missing term" series, coded language, conclusion-follows syllogism, family-tree relations, shortest distance/direction, odd-one-out, mirror images.
- **English:** spot-the-error sentence, choose synonym/antonym, fill the blank (cloze passage), improve the underlined part, RC inference + vocabulary-in-context.
- **GA:** one-line factual MCQs (which Article, which committee, who is the current X, date/year, scientific unit, river/dam).

## Week-by-Week Plan

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Diagnose + High-Yield Quant/Reasoning
- **Day 1:** Take a full 60-min diagnostic mock. Analyse honestly. Build your error log. Identify your 2 weakest sections.
- **Day 2:** Quant — Percentage + Ratio/Proportion (theory recall + 40 problems).
- **Day 3:** Quant — Profit-Loss-Discount + SI/CI (40 problems). Reasoning — Coding-Decoding.
- **Day 4:** Quant — Time-Work + Pipes-Cisterns. Reasoning — Series.
- **Day 5:** Quant — TSD (trains/boats/relative speed). English — Vocabulary block.
- **Day 6:** Reasoning — Syllogism + Blood Relations. English — Error Spotting.
- **Day 7 (weekend):** Full mock #2 + 2-hr analysis. GA — Polity revision.

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Geometry/Mensuration/Algebra + English core
- **Day 8:** Quant — Algebra (identities, equations).
- **Day 9:** Quant — Geometry (triangles, circles, lines/angles).
- **Day 10:** Quant — Mensuration 2D + 3D. Reasoning — Direction + Analogy.
- **Day 11:** Quant — DI (2 sets). English — Cloze + Sentence Improvement.
- **Day 12:** Reasoning — Non-verbal (mirror/paper/figure). English — RC practice.
- **Day 13:** GA — Modern History + Geography. Revisit error log.
- **Day 14 (weekend):** Full mock #3 + analysis. Re-attempt previously wrong questions.

### Week 3 (Days 15–21): Consolidate weak areas + speed
- **Days 15–18:** Target your 2 weakest sections from mock data. Mixed daily set: 30 Quant + 20 Reasoning + 20 English + 30 GA MCQs, timed. Daily formula-sheet skim.
- **Day 19:** Topic-wise speed drills — solve known types against the clock to push attempt count.
- **Day 20:** GA full revision (Polity + History + Economy + Science) using the [GK Cheat Sheet](../Cheat-Sheets/GK_Revision_Cheat_Sheet.md). Current affairs last 6 months.
- **Day 21 (weekend):** Full mock #4 + analysis. Compare score trend across mocks 1–4.

### Week 4 (Days 22–30): Mock-heavy + transition to 15-day plan
- **Days 22–27:** One sectional or mini-mock every weekday + analysis; rotate through all sections. Daily cheat-sheet + formula recall. Light current affairs daily.
- **Days 28–30:** Two full mocks; refine attempt order and time allocation per section. Begin the [Last 15 Days plan](Last_15_Days_Revision.md) mindset — stop adding new material, start locking in.

**Mock cadence this month:** minimum 2 full mocks/week (Days 1, 7, 14, 21, plus daily mini-mocks in Week 4). Every mock = attempt + same-day analysis + error log update. No analysis = wasted mock.
