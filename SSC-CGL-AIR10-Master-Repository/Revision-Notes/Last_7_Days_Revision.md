## What to Study

- **Only the highest-probability topics** (below). Nothing else gets your time this week.
- **Cheat-sheet recall daily** — Quant formulas, Reasoning shortcuts, English rules, GK one-liners. Read, recall, repeat.
- **One mock per day** + a focused 45–60 min analysis. Quality of analysis > quantity of mocks.
- **Error log review every day** — your wrong answers are the highest-value study material you own right now.

## What to Ignore

- Any new topic, any hard low-frequency topic, any long theory or full textbook. Zero new material this week.
- Marathon study sessions that wreck your sleep. Routine and rest protect your exam-day brain.
- Other people's scores and last-minute "important question" forwards that create panic.
