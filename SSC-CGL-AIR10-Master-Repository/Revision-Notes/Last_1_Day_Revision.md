## Exam-Day Strategy

### Section time allocation (Tier-1: 100 questions, 60 minutes total)
Aim for roughly:
- **General Awareness (25 Q): ~6–8 min** — fastest section; you either know it or you don't, so don't linger.
- **Reasoning (25 Q): ~10–12 min** — high-accuracy, quick scoring; bank these marks.
- **English (25 Q): ~10–12 min** — fast if your vocab and grammar are solid.
- **Quant (25 Q): ~25–30 min** — most time-hungry; do it with the time you have left.
- Keep ~2–3 min buffer at the end to review flagged questions.

### Attempt order
- Start with your **strongest / fastest section** to build momentum and bank easy marks early. A common, effective order is **GA → Reasoning → English → Quant**, but use the order you locked in during your mocks. Don't change your proven order today.
- Do **two passes per section** mentally: grab all the easy ones first, flag the time-consuming ones, come back if time allows. Never let one hard question eat 3 minutes.

### Accuracy vs negative marking (−0.5)
- Each correct answer = **+2**, each wrong = **−0.5**. A wrong answer effectively costs you 2.5 marks versus skipping.
- **Rule of thumb:** answer only when you are confident or can confidently eliminate to a 50/50 or better. Blind random guessing across many questions hurts you.
- If you've eliminated 2 of 4 options, an educated guess is usually worth it. If you have no idea, **leave it blank.**
- High accuracy on fewer attempts beats low accuracy on many. Protect your marks.

### OMR / login / logistics tips
- This is a **computer-based test** — confirm your **roll number / registration ID and password** are correct before you go; carry the admit card printout and a valid photo ID.
- Reach the centre **early** to handle frisking, biometrics, and seat allocation without stress.
- At your terminal: **read the on-screen instructions**, confirm your name/photo/roll number on the login screen, and note the question palette (answered / not answered / marked-for-review colours).
- Use **"Mark for Review"** for questions you want to revisit; don't leave the easy ones flagged.
- Keep an eye on the **on-screen timer**, but don't obsess over it — glance every ~10 questions.
- The exam **auto-submits** when time ends; do a final review pass before that, then submit calmly.
