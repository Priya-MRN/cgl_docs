## What to Study

- **Only revise what you already know.** Strengthen retention and speed, do not learn fresh concepts.
- **Daily formula revision** from the [Quant Formula Sheet](../Formula-Sheets/Quant_Formula_Sheet.md) — read it cover-to-cover every 2–3 days.
- **Daily cheat-sheet recall** for Reasoning, English grammar rules, and GK one-liners.
- **Current affairs of the last 6 months** — this is the most efficient GA scoring lever in the final stretch.
- **One mock per day**, fixed time of day if possible, followed by deep analysis.

## What to Ignore

- Any topic you still find genuinely hard and that is low-frequency — let it go, don't burn days on it.
- Long theory reading. If it's not a formula, a rule, or a one-liner, skip it.
- Full textbooks/long PDFs. Only cheat sheets, formula sheets, error log, and mocks now.
- New question types you haven't seen — do not introduce uncertainty this close to the exam.

## High Probability Topics

- **Quant:** Percentage, Profit-Loss-Discount, Ratio/Proportion, Time-Work, TSD, DI set, Geometry + Mensuration core formulas, Algebra identities. (These reliably produce the most quant marks.)
- **Reasoning:** Coding-Decoding, Series, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Direction, Analogy/Classification, Non-verbal.
- **English:** Vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms/idioms/one-word), Error Spotting, Cloze, Sentence Improvement, RC.
- **GA:** Polity (Articles/Schedules/Amendments), Modern History highlights, Static Geography, Economy terms, Science static one-liners, and last-6-months current affairs.

## Expected Question Types

- **Quant:** quick percentage/ratio application, one DI set, work & speed problems, a couple of geometry/mensuration numericals, algebra simplification.
- **Reasoning:** missing-term series, coded language, syllogism conclusions, family relations, direction-distance, odd-one-out, mirror/figure series.
- **English:** spot-the-error, synonym/antonym pick, cloze fill, sentence improvement, RC comprehension + vocab-in-context.
- **GA:** single-line factual MCQs — Article numbers, committees, current office-holders, dates, scientific facts, geography.
